FAQ

AI Chat for Flooring: Common Questions

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on your actual language, how you describe your services, your pricing structure, and your personality. When a customer asks about water damage restoration, they get an answer that sounds like it came from your team, not a generic bot.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It flags the question, saves the conversation, and routes complex requests to you. You review it and decide. Over time, the AI learns from those interactions and handles similar questions on its own.

Does this replace my receptionist?

It replaces voicemail. If you have a receptionist, they now focus on scheduling, customer follow-up, and other work that matters. If you don't have one, you suddenly have a reliable front desk that never takes a day off.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. Calls at 11 PM on a Saturday get answered. Texts come in during dinner and get queued for your team. You decide when you want to be notified.

How long does setup take?

10–14 days from discovery call to live. You spend maybe 30 minutes reviewing the agent before launch. The rest is on us.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

It's fine. We're transparent in the greeting. Most customers don't care as long as their appointment gets booked and their question gets answered. Transparency builds trust.
