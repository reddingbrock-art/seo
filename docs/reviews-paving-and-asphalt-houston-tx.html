FAQ

Google Review Automation Questions for Houston Paving Contractors

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests only to customers you've actually completed work for. The system just automates the ask—the review has to be genuine and come from a real person.

What if a customer is unhappy?

They won't leave a positive review, and that's fine. The system's designed to reach customers shortly after completion while they're satisfied. If something goes wrong on a job, they won't be in that satisfied window anyway.

Which platforms does this work on?

We focus on Google, which is where your Houston customers are looking. That's where the visibility happens.

How does the timing work?

The request goes out within 48 hours of job completion—that's the window when satisfaction is highest. We handle all the timing automatically based on when your crew marks the work done.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll work around it. If you've got something running, we'll either integrate with it or replace it with something more automated. No overlap, no confusion.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

That depends on your job volume and customer base. Most companies see a significant increase because they're asking consistently instead of sporadically. Some jobs won't convert—that's normal. But you'll see reviews accumulating faster than before.
