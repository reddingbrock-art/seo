FAQ

Review Automation for General Contractors in Henderson—Common Questions

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We only request reviews from customers whose jobs you've actually completed. Google's system filters out anything suspicious, and authentic reviews from real customers are what actually move the needle.

What if a customer is unhappy?

We send requests after completion, so you'll catch problems before anyone posts publicly. If a customer isn't satisfied, you'll have the chance to make it right—and that's infinitely better than a negative review.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google Business Profile is the main priority—that's where customers look first in Henderson. We can expand to other platforms based on where your customers actually search.

How does the timing work?

You tell us when a job is complete (via your system or via a quick text), and the request goes out at the best moment. Timing matters—too soon and they're still covered in dust, too late and they've forgotten you.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll integrate with what you're doing. If it's working, we'll keep it. If it's not, we'll replace it with something that actually generates results without adding work to your plate.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

That depends on your job volume and how many customers you're currently asking. Most contractors see significantly more reviews within the first 60 days because they're actually requesting them systematically for the first time.
