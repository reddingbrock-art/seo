FAQ

AI Chat Agent for Plumbing: Questions Answered

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We configure it with your tone, service offerings, and common phrases your team uses. It doesn't sound robotic or generic—it sounds like someone familiar with your company answering the phone.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It recognizes the limit, captures the customer's info, and flags the conversation for your team to follow up on. You get everything you need to call them back informed.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. If you have a receptionist, they move into scheduling management, vendor coordination, or crew support instead of fielding the same four questions all day. If you don't have one, you're not hiring one now.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. Calls come in at midnight, 6 a.m., during crew lunch breaks—whenever. The AI answers every one.

How long does setup take?

You're live and taking calls in 10-14 days. Most of that is us configuring your specific workflows; your time commitment is about an hour total.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

Transparency builds trust. The AI can acknowledge it's an automated system while assuring them their request is being routed to a real person or handled accurately. Most customers care about getting scheduled, not whether a human or system did it.
