FAQ

Common Questions About Lead Follow-Up Automation for Commercial Cleaning

How many follow-ups does it send?

We customize the sequence based on your process. Most commercial cleaning businesses run 3–5 touches over 7–10 days: initial response, confirmation, reminder, and a final check-in before we mark it stale. You control the timing and message for each.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. We write the initial templates based on your voice and sales process, then you can edit them anytime. Every message sounds like your company, not a robot.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can opt out, and we honor it immediately. The system removes them from future sequences. You'll never spam someone into a sale.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Within minutes of the lead arriving—usually 1–5 minutes depending on how you set it. The goal is to be first to respond, not fastest to forget.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in your dashboard marked as unresponsive after the sequence runs. You can see which leads fell through the cracks and decide whether to reach out manually or move on. No mystery, no lost opportunities.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. It plugs into where your leads currently land and enhances what you're already doing. If you're using another tool or method, we integrate it or run parallel—whatever keeps your current rhythm.
