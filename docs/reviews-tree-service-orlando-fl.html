FAQ

Google Review Automation Questions for Tree Service Owners

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. The system sends requests only to customers whose jobs are actually completed. You only get real reviews from real work—that's what builds authority with Google and with customers reading your profile.

What if a customer is unhappy?

The system requests reviews after job completion; it doesn't filter or suppress anything. If a customer leaves a negative review, that's data you can use to improve. Most unhappy customers won't leave a review at all—automation just increases your overall volume.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google reviews. That's where customers look first and where Google ranks your business. We focus there because it's the lever that moves.

How does the timing work?

You set when requests go out—same day as completion, next day, or within a few hours. Most tree service owners see better response when the request arrives that evening or the next morning while the work is still fresh.

What if I already have a review process?

We build around what you're doing. If you're already asking for reviews sometimes, the system automates and scales that—it doesn't replace your process, it makes sure nothing slips through.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Most tree service businesses see significantly more reviews within 60 days of going live because they're finally asking consistently. The exact number depends on your job volume and how receptive your customer base is, but the floor is: zero reviews you would've gotten manually if you'd never set the system up.
