FAQ

Septic & Drain Review Automation: Questions Answered

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests only to customers tied to completed jobs in your system. They're real people who actually worked with you. Whether they leave a review is entirely their choice.

What if a customer is unhappy?

If someone had a bad experience, they'll either ignore the request or leave honest feedback. That's part of the process. You want to know what went wrong so you can fix it. Unhappy customers who stay silent cost you more than reviews that tell you what to improve.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google is our focus because that's where customers search for service and make decisions. Google reviews also feed into local search rankings, so that's where your effort matters most.

How does the timing work?

Requests go out the same day a job closes, or the next morning. The timing is automated based on your job completion data, so you don't have to think about it.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll talk about it during the discovery call. If you're doing something that's working, we build around it. Usually, you're asking customers manually and losing people in the process. Automation just makes what you're already trying to do actually happen.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

It depends on your job volume and customer base. Most owners see a significant increase because they're capturing reviews that were always possible—customers just never got reminded. We'll show you what realistic looks like for your size and market during the discovery call.
