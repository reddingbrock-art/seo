FAQ

Questions About Review Automation for HVAC Businesses

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. Requests only go to actual customers after you've completed a job for them. Google verifies review sources—we're just making sure your real customers actually see the ask.

What if a customer is unhappy?

They don't get a review request if they mark the job incomplete or flag an issue. The system only reaches customers who've paid and finished. If someone leaves a negative review, that's their genuine feedback—and it matters more than silence.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google is where most customers search for HVAC. That's where we focus. Reviews on Google are what show up when someone in San Diego looks for a tech on their phone.

How does the timing work?

Requests go out the same day or the next morning after job completion, when the customer still remembers the work you did. That window matters—too late and they've forgotten you exist.

What if I already have a review process?

We can work with what you've got or replace it entirely. Most shops ask inconsistently or forget altogether. We make it automatic, so nothing falls through the cracks.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

It depends on your job volume. If you're closing 30 jobs a month and getting zero reviews, you'll see significantly more once customers are actually asked. Most shops see growth start within the first month.
