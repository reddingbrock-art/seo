FAQ

Lead Follow-Up System FAQs for Plumbing in Tucson

How many follow-ups does it send?

Typically 3–5 touches over the first 48 hours—a text, maybe an email, a second text if they don't respond. We customize the sequence to match your business, but the goal is contact without spam.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. We write the initial messages based on your voice and process, and you can adjust them. The system is yours. It should sound like your company, not a template.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can opt out at any time, and they're automatically removed. We respect that. Compliance is built in—no gray area.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Within 2–5 minutes of a lead coming in. If it's after hours, it goes out immediately when your business opens. No lead sits overnight.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in the system and get the follow-up sequence we designed. After that window closes, they go into a nurture list and get touched monthly so when they actually need a plumber, you're top of mind.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. We integrate with whatever you're already using—your calendar, phone system, forms. The automation sits on top. Your team doesn't change how they work.
