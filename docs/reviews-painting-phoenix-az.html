FAQ

Google Review Automation Questions for Painting Contractors

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests only to customers whose jobs actually closed in your system. The reviews that come back are real feedback from real work you completed.

What if a customer is unhappy?

We send review requests after job completion. If a customer had a bad experience, that's between you and them to resolve before the job closes. We don't filter or suppress anything—we just send the request at the right moment.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google reviews are the main target because that's where homeowners look first. The system integrates with your existing job management platform to trigger requests automatically.

How does the timing work?

Requests go out automatically once a job is marked complete. You set the delay—immediately after, 24 hours later, whatever works best. The goal is to catch customers while the work is still fresh in their mind.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll work around it. If you're already asking manually or have a template, we can automate what you've got or replace it entirely with something more consistent. Either way, the manual effort stops.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

It depends on your job volume and customer response rate, but most contractors see a significant jump in review count within 60–90 days just because the request is hitting customers at the right moment instead of getting forgotten.
