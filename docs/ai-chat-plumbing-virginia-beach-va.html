FAQ

AI Chat for Plumbing Calls in Virginia Beach: Common Questions

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We script it based on how your team talks, your service area, your pricing, and the problems you actually solve. It's trained on your intake process so it asks what matters and skips the fluff.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It says so. It'll tell the customer 'I don't have that info, but I'm logging your question and someone from our team will call you back within 30 minutes.' Then it flags the question for you. No pretending. No frustration.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It replaces the phone ringing unanswered while your receptionist is helping another customer, or after hours when nobody's there. If you have a receptionist, this makes them more effective—they spend time on relationship stuff, not answering 'Do you service Ocean Lakes?'

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. Calls and chats come in anytime. Your dispatcher sees them anytime. You decide your response windows—some owners respond to leads during business hours only, some handle evening calls too. The AI never sleeps, but your team works your schedule.

How long does setup take?

10–14 days from discovery to live. We need your input on how you talk, your service area boundaries, and your calendar integration. Most of that time is us building and testing. The actual 'go live' happens in an afternoon.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

Some will figure it out. We recommend being transparent in your greeting: 'Hi, this is [Your Company's] AI assistant. I'll help you find a time that works.' Customers care about getting booked fast, not whether a human or machine did it. Your crew will still be human. That's what matters.
