FAQ

Google Review Automation for Contractors: Common Questions

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send review requests only to customers whose jobs are actually complete. Google flags fake review patterns and penalizes accounts. We don't do that.

What if a customer is unhappy?

They get the same request as everyone else. But here's the thing—unhappy customers rarely leave reviews. The ones who do respond positively are your advocates. Negative feedback is rare, and when it happens, it's usually because something was missed. That's a business conversation, not a review automation problem.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google is where it counts for contractors. That's where homeowners search, compare, and decide. We focus there because it's where the ROI is.

How does the timing work?

A request goes out the day your crew marks the job complete in your system. Customers get a simple text or email asking them to leave a review. No delay. No forgetting.

What if I already have a review process?

Most contractors do—it just doesn't work because it's manual and inconsistent. We take whatever process exists and make it automatic so it actually happens every time.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Most contractors see a significant increase in the first 30 days because reviews that should've happened are finally happening. The exact number depends on your job volume and response rate, but you'll see the difference fast.
