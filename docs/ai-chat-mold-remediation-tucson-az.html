FAQ

AI Chat Agent Questions for Mold Remediation Companies

Will the chat widget actually sound like my business?

Yes. We write it using your tone, your service language, and the way you actually talk to customers. It won't sound corporate or generic.

What happens when the chat can't answer something?

It hands off smoothly to a human. The conversation thread transfers so you pick up context, not confusion. You know exactly what they asked.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It handles the questions that come at 2 a.m., during jobs, or on weekends when nobody's at the desk. It frees your team to focus on closing and execution, not fielding repetitive questions.

What hours does the chat widget work?

24/7. It responds to visitors whenever they land on your site. You control business hours for human handoff if you want, so off-hours chats can queue until morning.

How long does setup take?

10-14 days from discovery to live. We handle the technical side. You just approve the chat personality and make sure your calendar integrations are clean.

What if a customer wants to call instead of chat?

Your phone number is visible in the chat. They can call anytime. The chat is there for people who prefer to type first—not to force messaging on everyone.
