FAQ

Review Automation Questions for Tree Service Owners

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests only to customers whose jobs you've actually completed. Real work, real customers, real reviews.

What if a customer is unhappy?

We request reviews from every job completion. If someone had a bad experience, that's between you and them—not our system's job to filter. Fix the problem, then ask for the review.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google is the primary focus. Requests point customers to your Google Business Profile where it matters most for local search.

How does the timing work?

You decide when the request goes out—immediately after job completion, or a few hours later. We send it automatically based on your preference.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll map out what you're doing now and show you where automation fills the gaps. Most owners are doing it manually or inconsistently; we just remove that friction.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

It depends on your current baseline and job volume. Most owners see significantly more reviews within the first 60 days because the requests are consistent and timely. You're capturing reviews that were already possible—just not happening.
