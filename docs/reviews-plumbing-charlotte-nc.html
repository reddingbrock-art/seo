FAQ

Common Questions About Plumbing Review Automation in Charlotte

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send review requests only to customers who actually completed jobs with you. Real work, real customers, real reviews.

What if a customer is unhappy?

You'll find out fast. Unhappy customers won't leave a review, so you can address the issue before it becomes a public complaint. The system only automates the request—it doesn't filter responses.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google, primarily. That's where Charlotte customers look first when they need a plumber. We can discuss other platforms on your call.

How does the timing work?

Requests go out within hours of job completion—when the customer is still thinking about the work and most likely to respond. We adjust the timing based on your schedule.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll work with what you've got. If your team is already asking for reviews manually, we'll replace that burden with automation so they stop having to remember.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Most plumbers see a significant increase within the first month because you're asking way more often and at the right moment. The exact number depends on your job volume, but the difference is noticeable.
