FAQ

Common Questions About Review Automation for Gutters Companies

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We only send requests to customers whose jobs are marked complete in your system. The reviews that come back are from people you actually worked for.

What if a customer is unhappy?

Unhappy customers don't leave reviews just because you ask. If someone had a bad experience, they're unlikely to respond to the request at all. This system requests reviews—it doesn't suppress or redirect negative feedback.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google is where most of your customers search and where reviews matter most for visibility. We focus there, though we can support other platforms depending on your needs.

How does the timing work?

The request goes out the same day the job is closed in your system, or the next business day. That's when the work is fresh and customers are most likely to respond.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll talk through what you're doing now. If your current process is working, great—but most contractors we talk to aren't capturing anywhere near the review volume they could be. We usually find gaps worth filling.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

It depends on how many jobs you close and your baseline response rate. Most businesses see a significant jump in the first 60–90 days just from consistent, timely requests. Book a call and we'll give you a realistic picture based on your numbers.
