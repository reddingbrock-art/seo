FAQ

Google Reviews & Automation for Waterproofing Contractors in Los Angeles

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send review requests only to customers whose jobs you've actually completed. Google flags fake reviews quickly. Our system just asks the people you've already served—the ones most likely to leave honest feedback.

What if a customer is unhappy?

They get the same review request as everyone else. If they're unhappy, they'll leave a negative review—and that's information you need. We don't filter or suppress unhappy customers. You handle service recovery the right way.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google, primarily—that's where homeowners search for waterproofing companies in Los Angeles. We focus there because that's where it moves the needle.

How does the timing work?

You tell us when a job is complete, and the system sends a review request automatically that same day or the next morning. We can adjust the timing to match your workflow—whatever feels natural to your operation.

What if I already have a review process?

We integrate with what you've got. If you're manually asking sometimes, we fill the gaps and make sure every completed job gets asked. If you've got nothing, we build it from scratch. Either way, reviews stop falling through the cracks.
