Inbound calls and form submissions get routed and answered instantly. Appointment booking happens without manual entry. Text and email confirmations go out automatically. Review requests are sent after job completion. Your team stops handling intake—they focus on jobs and sales.

Do I need technical skills to run this?

No. Your dispatcher uses a dashboard to manage appointments just like they do now, but faster and without double-bookings. We handle the technical setup. You handle the business.

Most resistance comes from fear of complexity. This system makes their job easier—fewer calls to answer manually, fewer forms to chase, fewer appointment conflicts. Once they see it working, they're sold. We train them and stay available if questions come up.

How is this different from buying software myself?

What does the setup process look like?

Discovery call, then we configure your system—call routing, form handling, automations, integrations with your calendar and text system. We test it, train your team, and launch. Total time: 10–14 days from contract to live.

What happens if something breaks after launch?
