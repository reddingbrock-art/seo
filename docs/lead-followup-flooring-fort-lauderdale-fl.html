FAQ

Lead Follow-Up FAQs for Fort Lauderdale Flooring Companies

How many follow-ups does it send?

We design the sequence based on your process, typically 3-5 touches over 7-10 days if a lead doesn't respond. You control the cadence and messaging.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. We write it to match your voice and ask the right questions for flooring jobs—square footage, damage type, timeline, etc. You approve every message before it goes live.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can opt out anytime. The system respects that and stops contacting them immediately. We handle compliance so you don't have to worry about it.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Within minutes of the lead landing—typically 2-5 minutes depending on your setup. Not hours. Not the next business day.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in the system and get the full sequence. After that, they're marked as a non-responder. You can reach back out manually later if you want.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. It feeds qualified leads into your sales process. Your team still closes them. We just make sure they don't die before you get the chance.
