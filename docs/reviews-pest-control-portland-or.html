FAQ

Review Automation for Pest Control: Questions Answered

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests to customers you've actually serviced. What they write is their honest experience—good, bad, or in between.

What if a customer is unhappy?

They can leave an honest review. Negative reviews happen, and they're less damaging than no reviews at all—plus they show you where to improve. Most of the time, satisfied customers are the ones who actually take time to post.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google, Yelp, Facebook, and other major review sites. We make sure requests go to the platforms where your customers are actually looking and leaving feedback.

How does the timing work?

Requests go out a few hours after job completion—when the service is fresh in their mind but before they've moved on to the next thing. That's when response rates are highest.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll replace it. Manual processes get skipped, and staff have enough to worry about. Automation is faster and more consistent.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Most companies see a significant jump in volume within the first month. The exact number depends on your job volume, but the consistency is what matters—you'll see reviews showing up every week instead of sporadically.
