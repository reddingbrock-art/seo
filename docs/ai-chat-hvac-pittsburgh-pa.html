FAQ

AI Chat for HVAC: Common Questions About 24/7 Lead Capture in Pittsburgh

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on your real language, your service offerings, and how your team talks to customers. It answers like someone from your shop, not a robot reading a script.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It tells the customer the truth—'Let me grab someone who can help with that'—and transfers the call to your team or books a time for a callback. No fake answers, no bad handoffs.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It handles the calls your receptionist misses while they're on other tasks—during lunch, when they're on another line, or when volume spikes. It frees them up for the work that actually requires a human.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. Calls at 10 PM on a Sunday get answered the same way as calls at 9 AM on a Monday. You're catching leads your competitors let go to voicemail.

How long does setup take?

10-14 days from discovery call to live. We handle the technical work. You just need to tell us how your business runs.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

Most don't. But if they ask, the AI says so upfront. Customers care more about getting their problem solved and getting on the schedule than whether they talked to a human or a machine. If they want a human, they get transferred.
