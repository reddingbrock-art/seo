FAQ

AI Chat for Gutter Companies: Answers to Common Questions

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We record a brief intro from you or use your existing voicemail greeting, and we configure the agent to answer questions the way your company would. It uses your terminology for gutter types, pricing structure, and service areas.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It's transparent about it. It'll say something like 'That's a good question—I want to make sure you get the right answer. Let me have someone from the team reach out to you.' Then it captures their contact info and flags it for your crew. No dead ends.

Does this replace my receptionist?

Not if you don't want it to. If you have an office person, they stop answering phones and start focusing on follow-ups, scheduling details, and customer service. If you don't have an office person, this fills that gap entirely.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. Calls and texts come in at midnight, 6 a.m., or during your busiest season—they all get answered the same way. You can set it to book same-day, next-day, or give customers a range to choose from.

How long does setup take?

Discovery to live is 10-14 days. We handle the configuration, testing, and training. You're not learning a new platform or training a new hire.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

Most don't notice. The ones who do? They're fine with it because they got their question answered instantly and got booked. If someone asks, the AI doesn't hide it—it just explains that it's helping get them scheduled faster.
