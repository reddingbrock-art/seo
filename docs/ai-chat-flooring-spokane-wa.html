FAQ

Common Questions About AI Chat for Flooring Companies

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on your tone, your service types, and how you actually talk to customers. It doesn't sound robotic—it sounds like someone from your team who knows your work.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It qualifies the customer and flags the conversation as needing a callback. Your team gets the full context—what they asked, what the AI couldn't resolve—so you're ready when you call back.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It handles the intake and qualification that usually kills them during peak season. If you have office staff, they get to focus on scheduling, invoicing, and management instead of being glued to the phone.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. It takes calls and messages at midnight on Sunday, 6 AM Monday, whenever. You set the booking rules so it doesn't schedule outside your service hours.

How long does setup take?

10-14 days from kickoff to live. We handle the technical setup—you just give us your service menu and approval on how it sounds.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

Most don't care as long as they get their answer and a scheduled appointment. If they want a human, the AI offers a callback and routes them immediately. It's transparent without being awkward.
