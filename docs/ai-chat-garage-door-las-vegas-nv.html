FAQ

Garage Door Chat Widget FAQ for Las Vegas Owners

Will the chat widget actually sound like my business?

Yes. We interview you and your team about how you talk to customers, what you prioritize, and what matters most. The widget uses your language and tone, not corporate bot speak.

What happens when the chat can't answer something?

It says so. The chat transfers the conversation to your team inbox with full context—what they asked, who they are, where they're located. Your staff reads the whole thread and responds from there.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It replaces the part of your receptionist's day spent answering the same five questions from website visitors. Your receptionist handles callbacks, manages the schedule, and focuses on the work that actually drives revenue.

What hours does the chat widget work?

Around the clock. Visitors chat at night, on weekends, whenever they land on your site. You choose which hours your team responds live, and outside those hours, the chat collects their info and marks it urgent for first thing in the morning.

How long does setup take?

10–14 days from discovery call to live deployment. We handle the integration, testing, and training. You're not doing technical work.

What if a customer wants to call instead of chat?

Your phone number is there. The chat doesn't hide it. But most people who start a chat finish in chat—it's faster and easier than dialing. You're just giving them the option they actually prefer.
