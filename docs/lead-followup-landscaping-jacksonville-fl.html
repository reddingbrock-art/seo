FAQ

Lead Follow-Up Questions for Jacksonville Landscaping Businesses

How many follow-ups does it send?

That's up to you. Most landscaping companies run 3-5 touches over a week or two—text, call, email, repeat. You set the cadence and we execute it.

Can I customize what it says?

Completely. You write the message or we help you craft it. The system just handles the timing and delivery.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can opt out anytime. The system respects that immediately and stops sending to them.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Immediately or within minutes, depending on how you configure it. A lead fills a form at 3 PM on a Wednesday—they can get a text the same minute.

What happens to leads that never respond?

The sequence runs through your set timeline and stops. You can review them in the system anytime or re-engage them later in the season if you want.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Yes. If you're already using a form, phone system, or CRM, we plug into it. The system works with what you've got.
