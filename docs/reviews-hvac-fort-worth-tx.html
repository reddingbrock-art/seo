FAQ

Google Review Automation Questions: HVAC Edition

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. The system sends review requests only to actual customers you've completed work for. Google detects fake reviews quickly anyway. Real requests to real customers—that's how you build a legitimate presence.

What if a customer is unhappy?

They won't leave a five-star review. You'll still get honest feedback, which is better than silence. Some will ignore it. That's fine. You're asking everyone equally, so your average reflects reality.

Which platforms does this work on?

We send review requests that direct customers to Google, where your search ranking actually matters. That's the platform that drives calls.

How does the timing work?

The request goes out the same day the job's marked complete in your system, or the next morning—whatever you configure. Fast timing means better response rates because the experience is fresh.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll talk through what you're doing now. If it's working, great. Most HVAC shops aren't actually systematic about it, though—it falls to whoever remembers. Automation closes that gap.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Most shops see a significant increase in the first 30 days because you're actually asking consistently. The exact number depends on how many jobs you close per month. We'll show you the math on a call.
