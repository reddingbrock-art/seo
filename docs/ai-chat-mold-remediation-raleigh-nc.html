FAQ

AI Chat Automation for Mold Remediation Companies: Common Questions

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We write the voice and conversation flows based on how you actually talk to customers. It greets them with your company name, uses your language, asks your qualifying questions. It's you answering the phone, not a generic bot.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It tells them the truth and gets their contact info so you can follow up. If they ask something outside your scope, it acknowledges it and says you'll call them back with details. No fake answers.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It replaces the phone ringing and going to voicemail. Your team still closes, schedules inspections, and manages relationships. This just makes sure nothing gets missed while you're in the field.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. Calls and texts come in at midnight, 3 a.m., Sunday afternoon—they all get answered immediately. You decide during setup which appointments actually go into your calendar and which get held for you to review.

How long does setup take?

One call with us, one review of your intake flow, then 10-14 days to go live. You're not waiting months. By mid-week you'll be getting booked appointments from the AI.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

It doesn't hide. If they ask, it tells them straight up and moves to booking. Most don't ask—they're just happy someone answered and got them scheduled. If they want to talk to you instead, it captures that too.
