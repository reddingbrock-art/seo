Automation for Garage Door Companies: Common Questions

What exactly gets automated?

Call answering and qualification, form submissions converted to scheduled appointments, customer follow-ups, text message responses, and review requests after job completion. Basically everything that isn't the actual service work.

Do I need technical skills to run this?

No. You need to log in, check your calendar, and see your jobs. We handle the backend setup and ongoing support. If you can use a phone, you can run this.

What if my team resists new tools?

This doesn't add work—it removes it. Your office person isn't answering phones all day. Your technicians show up with complete job details. Once they see that, resistance usually disappears fast.

How is this different from buying software myself?

We build it and run it for you. You don't buy licensing, don't figure out integrations, don't troubleshoot why tools aren't talking to each other. We're responsible for it working, not you.

What does the setup process look like?

Discovery call, we build your system (10–14 days), we train your team, and we monitor the first month. Most of it happens behind the scenes. You're not doing the heavy lifting.

What happens if something breaks after launch?

We support you. If a call isn't routing right, a form isn't converting, or something else stops working, you reach out and we fix it. That's part of the service.
