AI Chat for Generator Services: Common Questions

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on your language, your service philosophy, how you talk to customers. It doesn't sound robotic or generic—it sounds like someone from your team answering the phone.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It's honest. It says 'I'm going to have my team look into that and get back to you by tomorrow'—and then it actually triggers that handoff. Your team gets a flag to follow up.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It replaces the moment your receptionist can't be available—nights, Sundays, lunch breaks, when they're helping on other jobs. Your team stays focused on what they do best.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. Every inbound call and message gets answered immediately. You decide what happens during your team's working hours versus after-hours—we can route differently based on time.

How long does setup take?

Live in 10-14 days from the discovery call. You're not waiting weeks for a massive implementation. It's fast because we've done this before.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

Some will ask. The AI tells them honestly, then says 'but I can get you booked with a technician right now.' Customers care more about being heard and handled fast than whether it's AI or human—you prove that by answering instantly.
