FAQ

Google Review Automation FAQs for Tampa Roofing Contractors

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests only to actual customers who've completed jobs with you. Google detects and removes fake reviews anyway—this is real customer requests, done automatically.

What if a customer is unhappy?

The system sends review requests to everyone by default. If you mark a job as problematic or a customer complains, you can exclude that request before it goes out. You stay in control.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google is the main focus because that's where Tampa homeowners look first. We can integrate other platforms depending on your setup—discuss options on the discovery call.

How does the timing work?

Requests go out automatically a few days after job completion, when the customer's still impressed but remembers the work. Timing adjusts based on your job cycle.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll replace it or layer on top of it, depending on what's working. If you're already getting decent reviews, we'll just make sure you're not leaving any on the table.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Most roofing companies see 3–5x more reviews once requests are automated, because they're consistent and timely. Your baseline depends on how many jobs you're running and your current response rate.
