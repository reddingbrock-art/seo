FAQ

Review Automation Questions for Philadelphia Appliance Repair Owners

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests only to actual customers who completed jobs through your system. Real person, real service, real review.

What if a customer is unhappy?

We're not filtering or blocking unhappy customers. If someone had a bad experience, they won't leave you a five-star review—and you probably want to know that. But most customers who had decent service will leave a review if you ask at the right moment.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google is the priority. That's where customers search for appliance repair and where they see your reviews first. We can configure for other platforms based on your business.

How does the timing work?

Requests send automatically after job completion. Usually same day or next day, depending on what time the tech closes out the call. Customers see it while the experience is fresh.

What if I already have a review process?

We take over. If you're manually emailing customers or texting them, we replace that with automation so you don't have to think about it.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Depends on your job volume and how many customers ignore the request. Most shops see a significant increase in the first month—often double or more—because you're asking everyone instead of hoping some will volunteer.
