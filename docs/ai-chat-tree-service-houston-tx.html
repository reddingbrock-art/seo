FAQ

AI Chat Widget for Tree Service: Questions Answered

Will the chat widget actually sound like my business?

Yes. We customize it based on how you talk to customers—your language, your tone, your service area rules. It sounds like someone from your crew, not a bot.

What happens when the chat can't answer something?

It routes to your inbox or can trigger a notification to your phone so you can jump in and take over the conversation. You stay in control.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It handles the stuff that happens outside office hours and qualifies incoming leads so your team can focus on selling and climbing. Your receptionist gets better-qualified inbound.

What hours does the chat widget work?

24/7. It's live whenever someone lands on your site—nights, weekends, holidays. You respond when it makes sense for your schedule.

How long does setup take?

From initial call to live on your site: 10-14 days. We handle everything. You just review and approve before launch.

What if a customer wants to call instead of chat?

The chat can direct them to call your number. We're not forcing phone calls away—we're just giving customers the option to chat if they prefer it.
