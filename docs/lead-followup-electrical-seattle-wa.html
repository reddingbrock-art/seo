FAQ

Questions About Lead Follow-Up Automation for Electrical Contractors

How many follow-ups does it send?

You decide. Most electrical companies start with 3–5 touches over 48 hours: first call, text at 30 minutes, second call at 2 hours, third text at 4 hours, final call at 24 hours. You set the exact sequence.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. Every message is yours. You write the scripts—the system just delivers them on schedule. Nothing generic, nothing that doesn't sound like your company.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can opt out at any time. The system respects their preference and stops contacting them. You stay compliant and they get what they asked for.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Immediately. The moment a lead submits your form or calls in, the system starts the sequence. No waiting for someone to remember to call back.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in the system and keep getting your follow-up touches until the sequence completes or they opt out. After that, they're logged as a contact attempt so you know you reached out. You can re-engage them later if you want.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Yes. It layers in with however you're currently taking calls, scheduling, and managing jobs. Your crew still handles the calls—this just makes sure the calls happen and nothing slips through the cracks.
