FAQ

Google Review Automation for EV Charger Installation Contractors: FAQs

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We're sending review requests to actual customers who completed jobs with you. Google can tell the difference between real reviews from real jobs and fake ones—you're just asking people who already know your work to share their experience.

What if a customer is unhappy?

They won't leave a review if they don't want to. The system sends a request, but customers choose whether to respond. Unhappy customers usually won't go to Google anyway—they'll call or text you directly, which is how you catch issues early.

Which platforms does this work on?

We focus on Google reviews because that's where Richmond homeowners and property managers look first. Google Local Pack results drive the most calls for service businesses.

How does the timing work?

The request goes out 24 hours after you mark the job complete. That's when the customer is most likely to remember the install and feel good about it. Early enough to be fresh, late enough that they've had time to use the charger.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll integrate with what you're doing instead of replacing it. If you're already asking some customers, we can fill in the gaps and automate the parts that are currently manual.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Most installers see 3–5 additional reviews per month once the system is running, depending on your job volume. You're not creating new reviews out of thin air—you're converting customers who would've left one anyway but forgot to.
