FAQ

Automation Questions From Riverside Fire & Water Restoration Owners

What exactly gets automated?

Incoming calls and form submissions get answered with a quick response and an appointment link. Appointment confirmations, job reminders, and post-completion review requests all go out automatically on schedule. Your crew still does the actual restoration work—the system just makes sure nothing falls through the cracks before, during, or after.

Do I need technical skills to run this?

No. You log in, you see your leads and scheduled appointments. That's it. The system does the work. If something needs to change—a message, a time, a workflow—you tell us and we update it.

What if my team resists new tools?

They won't see a new tool. They'll see appointments already booked, confirmations already sent, and less time spent on the phone. The system makes their job easier, not harder. Most crews feel the difference within the first week.

How is this different from buying software myself?

You could buy call software, form software, text software, and a calendar—then spend weeks figuring out how to connect them and train your team. We do all that. We build it, we connect it, we run it. You just use it.

What does the setup process look like?

One call with you to understand your business. Then we integrate your phone, website, and any other tools you use. You review it, we make adjustments, and we go live within 10 to 14 days. There's no long implementation or IT setup.

What happens if something breaks after launch?

We monitor the system and we're here. If a call doesn't route, a text doesn't send, or something's acting weird, you let us know and we fix it. We don't hand it off to you on day 15—we maintain it.
