FAQ

Review Automation for Locksmiths: Your Questions Answered

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests to actual customers after real jobs are completed. Google flags fake reviews and kills your credibility. We don't do that.

What if a customer is unhappy?

That's their choice. We send the request; they decide what to write. An honest negative review is better than no review at all—it keeps you honest and shows you're real.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google is the priority. That's where search happens. We can add Yelp and Facebook if you want, but Google is where Colorado Springs customers are looking.

How does the timing work?

The request goes out within a few hours of job completion. Close enough that the customer remembers you. Not so fast that it feels creepy.

What if I already have a review process?

If you're getting steady reviews, that's rare. Most shops talk about asking but never actually do it systematically. We're the 'actually doing it' part. If you want to keep your process, we can audit it first.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Most shops see 15-25 new reviews in the first 90 days, depending on job volume. Some customers won't respond. That's normal. The ones who do care enough to take the time.
