FAQ

Common Questions About Google Review Automation for Septic & Drain

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests only to customers whose jobs are actually marked complete in your system. Google flags fake review patterns, and we're not generating anything artificial—just asking real customers at the right moment.

What if a customer is unhappy?

The request goes out after job completion, so it only reaches satisfied customers. If a customer marks a job incomplete or leaves notes about a problem, the request doesn't fire. You control the trigger.

Which platforms does this work on?

We integrate with the most common field service software. During discovery, we'll confirm your setup is compatible and map out the connection.

How does the timing work?

The request goes out within minutes of a job being marked complete in your system. No delays, no manual intervention needed. It lives on your Google Business Profile the same way any customer review would.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll work with it. If you've got a manual system running, we'll show you during the discovery call how automation replaces the work without disrupting what's working. You might stop doing the manual part entirely.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

It depends on your job volume. Owners typically see a significant increase because requests are going out consistently instead of sporadically. You're capturing reviews that were always possible but never asked for.
