FAQ

Review Automation for Houston Appliance Repair—Common Questions

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. The system asks only customers who actually booked with you and completed a job. Google's detection catches fake reviews anyway—we don't need them, and neither do you.

What if a customer is unhappy?

That's the point. You only ask after the job's done and marked complete. If a customer isn't satisfied, they shouldn't be marked complete. The system respects your quality gate.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google is the priority because that's where customers look first. The system focuses on getting reviews there, where they actually move the needle.

How does the timing work?

The request goes out the same day or the next morning after you close the job in your system. That's when the customer memory is freshest.

What if I already have a review process?

We build around what you're doing. If you've got a manual system that's working, we automate it. If it's not working, we replace it with something that does.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Most shops see 2–4 new reviews per week after automation kicks in, depending on your call volume. You'll notice the difference inside 30 days.
