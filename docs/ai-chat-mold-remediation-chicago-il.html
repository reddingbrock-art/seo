Yes. We train it on how your team talks, what you care about, and your actual intake process. It uses your company name, references your service area, and handles objections the way you would. Customers don't hear generic chatbot—they hear your business.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It transfers to you or your team with full context—job details, customer info, everything the AI already captured. You're never left cold. And over time, the more edge cases that happen, the smarter it gets.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. If you have a receptionist, they're freed up for callbacks, follow-ups, and the work that actually makes you money. If you don't, you're no longer bleeding leads because nobody answered.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. Nights, weekends, holidays, 3 a.m. water emergencies—it's always on. You can set it to quiet hours if you want, but most owners let it run all the time and sleep better knowing no call goes unanswered.
