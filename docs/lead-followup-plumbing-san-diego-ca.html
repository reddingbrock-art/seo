FAQ

Lead Follow-Up System FAQs for Plumbing Companies

How many follow-ups does it send?

It depends on your process. We typically set it up to send 3 to 5 touches over 48 hours—text, email, and a callback attempt—unless the lead responds sooner. You decide the cadence.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. We write it to sound like your business, not like a robot. You can edit everything before it goes live, and you can change sequences anytime.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They're removed immediately. We handle opt-outs the right way—no legal risk, no headaches. It's built in.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Within 2 to 5 minutes of the lead coming in. Most leads are contacted before they've even finished looking at your competitor's website.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in the system. Some leads take a week to respond—a burst pipe at 11 PM, a hot water heater that dies on a Saturday. You don't lose them.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. If you're already using a scheduling tool or CRM, we integrate with it. The system doesn't replace your crew—it makes your crew more efficient.
