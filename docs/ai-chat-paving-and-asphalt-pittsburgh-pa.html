FAQ

Common Questions About AI Chat for Asphalt & Paving Companies

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on your pricing, your service areas, how many crews you run, and the way you actually talk to customers. It doesn't sound corporate or robotic—it sounds like someone from your team.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It tells the customer the truth: 'I need to get you to someone who can help with that,' and either logs the question for your team or transfers to a live person if you want. No pretending. No making up answers.

Does this replace my receptionist?

It replaces the part of the job that's answering phones and scheduling. If you have a receptionist, they're now doing higher-value work—following up on estimates, managing crews, handling logistics. If you don't, you're not hiring one.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. Your phone rings at 11 p.m. on Saturday? It answers. A customer texts during your crew's lunch? Answered. You pick which hours are 'live booking' (appointment lands automatically) and which are 'log the lead for follow-up.'

How long does setup take?

Full deployment—from discovery call to live—is 10 to 14 days. You're taking new leads from the AI within two weeks.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

It doesn't try to hide. If asked, it'll say 'I'm an automated booking system designed to get your job scheduled.' Most customers don't ask. They're just happy their appointment got booked without waiting on hold.
