FAQ

AI Chat & Call Handling for Sacramento Foundation Repair—Common Questions

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We write the agent's personality based on how you talk to customers and what your business values are. It won't sound robotic or generic—it sounds like someone who knows your service area and your foundation repair expertise.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It gets the customer's information and tells them a team member will follow up shortly. Then it sends you the details so you can call them back with the answer. It never leaves a customer hanging.

Does this replace my receptionist?

Not unless you want it to. Most owners use it to handle the calls their receptionist can't pick up—nights, weekends, and during busy service days. It frees your team up to focus on work that requires a human touch.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. Calls at 2 AM on a Tuesday get answered the same way as calls during business hours. You set your own availability—the AI knows when you're open and can still take messages outside those times.

How long does setup take?

Full deployment in 10-14 days from our first call. We handle all the technical setup. You just approve how it sounds and make sure your calendar is synced.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

It doesn't hide it. If asked, the AI will let them know they're talking to an automated system. Most customers don't ask—they just appreciate getting answered. And if they prefer a human, the AI transfers them or schedules a callback.
