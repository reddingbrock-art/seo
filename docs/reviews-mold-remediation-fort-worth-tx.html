FAQ

Review Automation Questions for Mold Remediation Owners

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send review requests only to customers you've actually worked for, after they've received your service. They choose whether to leave a review—we just make sure they get asked.

What if a customer is unhappy?

They can leave an honest review, just like always. We're not filtering or hiding anything. You get real feedback, good and bad, which is what builds trust.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google is the primary focus—that's where Fort Worth homeowners search for mold remediation. We can also integrate with other platforms depending on your setup.

How does the timing work?

Review requests go out automatically after a job is marked complete. Timing is configurable based on your typical job cycle—same day, next day, or a few days out.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll work with what you've got. If you're already collecting reviews manually, we take over the heavy lifting so you don't have to think about it.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Most businesses see significantly more reviews once they're asking consistently. The exact number depends on your job volume, but automation means you're asking after every job instead of sporadically.
