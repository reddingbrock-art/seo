FAQ

Lead Follow-Up Questions for San Antonio Painting Contractors

How many follow-ups does it send?

We customize the sequence based on what works for your business. Typically 2–4 touches over the first week if someone doesn't respond, then we back off.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. We write all the messages and you approve them. They'll reflect your voice and your process.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can opt out with one click, and the system respects that immediately. No more contact.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Within minutes of the lead coming in. If they call, text goes out while you're still on the phone. If it's a form, they hear from you before the day ends.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stop getting messages after the sequence runs. They stay in your system so you can see them, but we don't spam them.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. It fills the gap where leads slip through now. You keep doing what you're doing—the system just makes sure nothing falls through.
