FAQ

Lead Follow-Up Questions for Roofing Companies

How many follow-ups does it send?

That depends on your process. Most roofing companies use 3-5 touches over 5-7 days (a text or call within minutes, then email and SMS reminders). We customize the sequence based on what's worked for you before.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. We write the messages in your voice, and you approve them before they go live. You control the tone, the timing, and exactly when each contact happens.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can unsubscribe or opt out. The system respects that immediately—no more messages go to them. You stay compliant and they're marked in your CRM.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Usually within 2-5 minutes of a form submission or incoming call. Fast enough that you're reaching them before they've closed their browser tab or finished thinking about who else to call.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in your system. You can see the full history of who you've contacted and when. If you want to re-engage them later, you can. They don't disappear.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. We integrate with the tools you're already using—your CRM, your phone system, your website. Nothing gets ripped out or replaced. It just fills the gap where follow-up was slipping.
