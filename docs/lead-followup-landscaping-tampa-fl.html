FAQ

Lead Follow-Up Questions for Tampa Landscaping Owners

How many follow-ups does it send?

It depends on your process. Most landscaping companies use 3-5 touchpoints across text, email, and calls over 7-10 days. We customize it to match what actually converts for you.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. Every message gets your voice, your questions, your offer. We write it with you, not for you.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can opt out at any time, and we honor that immediately. It's built in—completely compliant.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Within an hour of the lead submitting their info, they're contacted. Most of the time, it's within 15 minutes.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in the sequence until they've received all the follow-ups you've designed. Some convert weeks later. We don't throw anyone away.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. It plugs into your website, forms, phone system, and calendar. No rip-and-replace. You keep what works.
