FAQ

Lead Follow-Up Questions for Orlando Locksmiths

How many follow-ups does it send?

Typically three to five touches over 7-10 days, but we customize the sequence to your business. More aggressive follow-up early on, tapering off if they don't engage.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. We write the messaging with you, and you approve every template before a single lead sees it. It sounds like your business, not a bot.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They unsubscribe and we stop contacting them immediately. The system respects their request automatically.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Within 15 minutes of form submission, usually faster. Depends on your settings—we can make it instant or delay it a couple hours if you prefer.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in the system until they unsubscribe or you manually close them out. Some come back weeks later when their lock actually breaks. You keep reaching out until they opt out.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. We integrate with your forms and pull leads in automatically. Your dispatcher and techs keep doing what they're doing—this just makes sure nothing falls through the cracks.
