FAQ

Questions About AI Chat for Pressure Washing in Tampa

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on your language, your service offerings, how you talk to customers, and what matters to your specific market. It'll reflect your professionalism and personality.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It either transfers the call to your team in real time or collects their info and flags the message for you to follow up on personally. You never lose a lead.

Does this replace my receptionist?

If you have one, it replaces the repetitive parts—answering the phone at 6 a.m., taking basic info, scheduling appointments. Your team focuses on quality conversations and closing.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. It answers calls, texts, and messages whenever they come in—during crew hours, after hours, weekends, holidays. You never miss a lead because it's outside business hours.

How long does setup take?

Setup happens in 10-14 days from your discovery call. We handle the configuration, training, and testing. You don't have to touch any technology.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

Most don't. But if they ask or prefer a human, the AI transfers them to your team immediately. It's built to feel natural, not to trick anyone.
