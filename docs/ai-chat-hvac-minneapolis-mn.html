FAQ

Common Questions About AI Chat Agents for HVAC Companies

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on how you talk to customers, your service philosophy, and the personality your team brings to the job. It answers like someone who knows your business, not like a robot reading a script.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It flags the call and routes it to your team with full context. The customer never feels passed around — they transition straight to a human who already knows what they need.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It handles intake so your receptionist (or you) can do higher-value work — managing the schedule, handling callbacks, coordinating with techs. It's force multiplication, not replacement.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. It answers calls at midnight on a Sunday just as well as it does at 9 AM on Tuesday. Customers get a response instantly, every time.

How long does setup take?

10-14 days from discovery to live. We move fast because we've built dozens of these. You're not waiting months for a custom tool.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

It's transparent about what it is, and most customers don't care — they just want their problem solved and an appointment booked. If they want to talk to a human, they get routed instantly.
