FAQ

EV Charger Installation Lead Follow-Up: Questions Answered

How many follow-ups does it send?

Depends on your preferences. Most installers use three to five touches over 10-14 days—a call, a text, an email, and maybe a second text if they don't respond. We customize the cadence to match your sales cycle.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. We write and refine your messages based on your voice and your process. If you want to change a follow-up message later, you can, or we'll do it for you.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They're removed from future follow-ups immediately. The system respects opt-outs, and you stay compliant with text and email regulations.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Within minutes of the lead coming in—usually under five. Phone calls can be routed to your team right away, or sent as a callback reminder depending on how you set it up.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in your follow-up sequence until you mark them as closed or disqualified. After the final touch, they're archived so your team isn't chasing ghosts.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. It slots into how you already work—your calendar system, your dispatch, your team. It adds follow-up, not replacement.
