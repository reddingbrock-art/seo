FAQ

Lead Follow-Up Questions for Landscaping Business Owners

How many follow-ups does it send?

We build your sequence based on your sales cycle. Most landscaping companies use 3–5 touchpoints over 7–10 days. Text, email, phone call—whatever works for your market. You design it; we automate it.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. We write your first draft based on what you tell us about your business, then you approve every message. It sounds like you, not like a robot.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can opt out with one reply or click. We respect that. They're removed from the sequence immediately, but you can still reach out manually if you want.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Within minutes of the lead coming in. No delays. No overnight queue.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in your system. You can see they didn't engage and decide to call them yourself, or let them age out. The choice is yours.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. It plugs into your current tools and phone number. Your team doesn't have to change anything. The system just fills in the gaps.
