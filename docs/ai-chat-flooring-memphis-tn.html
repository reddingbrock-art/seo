FAQ

AI Chat Agent for Flooring: Questions Answered

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on your language, your service descriptions, your pricing, and your process. It sounds professional and knowledgeable because it knows your business—not generic.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It tells the customer it'll have you follow up on that specific question, captures their info, and sends you a note so you can callback with the answer. No dead ends.

Does this replace my receptionist?

It handles the calls and chats your team can't pick up. If you have a receptionist, they can focus on scheduling, billing, or field coordination instead of answering the same questions all day.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. Calls and messages get answered at 2 AM on a Sunday the same way they do at noon on Tuesday. You set your availability windows so appointments only book during your working hours.

How long does setup take?

We go live in 10-14 days from discovery. You'll be answering leads before the end of the month.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

We don't hide it. The AI introduces itself and offers to transfer to you or schedule a call. Most customers don't care as long as they get an answer fast and their appointment is locked in.
