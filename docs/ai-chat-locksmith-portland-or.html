FAQ

AI Chat for Locksmith Companies: Questions Answered

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on how you talk to customers—your tone, your pricing, your service area, and your policies. Customers won't know the difference between the AI and a real dispatcher.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It flags the call and logs the customer's number. You can call them back with the answer, or the customer can stay on the line and talk to you directly. You're always in control.

Does this replace my receptionist?

It replaces the phone-answering part of the job—the repetitive "What's your address? What's the issue? What time works?" questions. Your team still handles callbacks, special requests, and anything complex. You save them for actual conversations.

What hours does the AI work?

All of them. 24/7, every day of the year. It answers calls at 3 AM on Sunday just as quickly as it does during business hours.

How long does setup take?

We get it live and answering calls in 10-14 days. You'll have a working system within two weeks.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

Some will figure it out, and that's fine. The AI tells customers it's automated and can transfer them to a person if they want. Most customers just want their problem solved and their appointment booked. The AI does both.
