FAQ

Review Automation for Mold Remediation: Questions Answered

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests only to actual customers whose jobs you've completed. Google catches fake reviews fast, and you don't want them anyway. Real reviews build real trust.

What if a customer is unhappy?

You handle it the same way you do now. The system asks for reviews; it doesn't filter out unhappy customers or silence them. Your unhappy customers can still leave feedback—and you can respond to it publicly.

Which platforms does this work on?

We focus on Google reviews because that's where customers search for mold remediation in Charlotte. Google is where leads turn into calls.

How does the timing work?

The request goes out automatically after job completion—usually the same day or next day. Customers see it while the work is still fresh in their mind, which is when they're most likely to respond.

What if I already have a review process?

Tell us what you're doing. If it's working, we build around it. If it's mostly talk and no action, we replace it with something that actually runs without you thinking about it.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

It depends on your job volume. If you close five jobs a month, you'll get significantly more reviews than you are now. Most mold companies close many more than five, so the number climbs fast.
