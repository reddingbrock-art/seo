FAQ

Lead Follow-Up System FAQs for Tampa Generator Services

How many follow-ups does it send?

It depends on your business. We typically set up a sequence of 3-5 touches over a few days, spaced so you're staying visible without being annoying. You control exactly how many and when they go out.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. We work with you to write the exact messages that match your voice and your close. It's not a generic template—it's your words, automated.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can opt out, and they're removed from future sequences. We handle that automatically. You stay compliant, no hassle.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Within minutes of the lead coming in. If it's a call or form, you're reaching back out before they've even hung up with the next contractor.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in your system. You can see who's engaged and who's gone cold. Some shops follow up again the next month. You decide what makes sense.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. We're not replacing how you work—we're automating the part that was falling through the cracks. Your team still closes deals. The system just makes sure nothing gets missed.
