FAQ

Common Questions About Review Automation for Solar Installation

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests to real customers who actually hired you. They choose whether to leave a review or not. If they don't want to, they skip it. What changes is the timing and consistency—you're asking everyone, at the right moment, instead of asking some people randomly or forgetting entirely.

What if a customer is unhappy?

The request still goes out. What they do with it is up to them. Some unhappy customers won't respond at all. Others will leave honest feedback. That's not a flaw in the system—that's your real reputation showing. The difference is you're now collecting feedback from happy customers too, instead of only hearing from the frustrated ones who take time to complain.

Which platforms does this work on?

We send review requests to Google, Yelp, and other platforms your customers actually use. The requests are simple and direct—usually a text or email with a link. Customers decide where to leave the review, and most default to Google because it's the easiest.

How does the timing work?

You tell us when a job is truly complete. That might be when the final inspection passes, when the customer signs off, or when the invoice is paid. The moment that happens, we send the request. It goes out within minutes, not days.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll look at what you're doing now and either integrate with it or replace it entirely. If you're already getting results, we'll build on that. If requests are slipping through the cracks, we stop the bleeding and get consistent instead.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

It depends on your current response rate and how many jobs you're completing each month. Most companies see a significant jump in requests getting responses—sometimes 3x or more—just because timing shifts from 'whenever' to 'right now.' We'll talk through your specific numbers on a call.
