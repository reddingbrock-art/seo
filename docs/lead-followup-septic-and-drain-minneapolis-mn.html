FAQ

Common Questions About Lead Follow-Up for Minneapolis Septic & Drain Contractors

How many follow-ups does it send?

It depends on your sequence. Most contractors use 3-5 touches over the first week: an instant reply, a second check-in at 2 hours, then spaced follow-ups at 24 and 48 hours. We customize this based on your sales cycle.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. We write the copy with you so it sounds like your business. If you want to mention your emergency service window or warranty, we bake that in. It stays on-brand and personal.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They're unsubscribed immediately. The system respects their preference, and they won't receive any further follow-ups from you.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Within 2-3 minutes of the lead coming in. If it's a form submission or text, they get a response almost instantly while they're still shopping.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in your sequence until the cycle ends, then they sit in your history. You can review them, call them manually, or let them go. The system doesn't discard anything.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. It integrates with your current tools and doesn't force you to change how you schedule or dispatch. It plugs in and fills the gap between lead and callback.
