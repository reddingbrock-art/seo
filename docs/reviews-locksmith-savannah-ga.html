FAQ

Common Questions About Review Automation for Locksmiths

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send review requests only to customers you've actually worked for. Real jobs. Real customers. Real reviews.

What if a customer is unhappy?

We send the request after job completion. If a customer had a bad experience, they're unlikely to respond. This isn't a filter—it's just how it works. Unhappy customers rarely write reviews without being specifically prompted, and we're not pushing them to do it.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google primarily. That's where Savannah customers look when they need a locksmith at night. We focus on the platform that moves the needle for your business.

How does the timing work?

The request goes out the same day the job is completed, or the next morning. We time it so it hits when the memory is fresh but the customer has had time to settle after the service.

What if I already have a review process?

We take it over. If you're asking manually or via random texts, this system replaces that and scales it. No double-asking. No overlap.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Depends on your job volume. A locksmith doing 40-50 jobs a week typically sees significantly more reviews within the first two months. Some customers won't respond, but the response rate is much higher when the ask is timely and automatic.
