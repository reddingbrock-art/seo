FAQ

Lead Follow-Up System FAQs for Savannah Pressure Washing Owners

How many follow-ups does it send?

You decide. Typically it's an immediate acknowledgment, a second touch after a few hours, and a third reminder the next day—but we customize it to your process. Some owners do one or two. You're in control.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. Every message is written for your business, your service area, your pricing structure, and your voice. It doesn't sound like an automated system—it sounds like you.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can opt out at any time, and the system respects it immediately. You stay compliant and they stay happy.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Instantly. Within minutes of the lead submitting a form or being logged in the system. The faster the first touch, the higher your conversion rate.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They get the full follow-up sequence you set up—then they're marked as inactive. You can revisit them manually if you want, but the system stops chasing dead leads after your sequence runs.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Yes. It integrates with whatever you're already using—Google Forms, your website, your CRM. It doesn't replace anything. It just makes sure nothing falls through the cracks.
