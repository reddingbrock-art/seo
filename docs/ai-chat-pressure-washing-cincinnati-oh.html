FAQ

AI Chat Agent FAQs for Pressure Washing Business Owners

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We record a call or two with you, listen to how you talk to customers, and train the voice to match your tone—friendly, straightforward, professional. Customers interact with it like they'd interact with a receptionist in your office.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It's honest. If a customer asks about something outside the scope, the AI says so and offers to get a real person on the call. You stay in control and never look unprepared.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. If you have a receptionist, this frees them up for higher-value work—following up with leads, managing customer relationships, handling billing. If you don't have one, this fills that gap without the hiring headache.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. Calls and texts come in anytime. You control which time slots get booked into your calendar—so a midnight call might go into the queue for your Monday morning schedule instead of booking something impossible.

How long does setup take?

10-14 days from discovery call to live. Most of that is us configuring the system and running tests with you. The actual switch-over is seamless—your number routes to the AI, and you get alerts for every lead.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

Some will ask. The AI tells them straight up, explains that their information is being captured for the right person on your team, and they'll get a callback. Most customers don't care if they got answered fast and their appointment is booked.
