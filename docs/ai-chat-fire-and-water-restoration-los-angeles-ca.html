FAQ

Common Questions About AI Chat for Restoration Companies

Will the chat widget actually sound like my business?

Yes. We write the chat responses based on how your team talks and what you actually ask customers. It sounds like someone from your company—because it's trained on your process.

What happens when the chat can't answer something?

It escalates to your team. If a question's too complex or the visitor needs a person, the chat hands them off to a real team member or books a callback. Nothing falls through the cracks.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It handles the work that happens before your receptionist even knows about it—late-night questions, form fills, basic qualification. Your team stays focused on jobs. Your receptionist handles the calls that actually come in.

What hours does the chat widget work?

24/7. It answers questions and qualifies leads whether it's 3 a.m. or during business hours. Your team gets the qualified lead whenever they're ready to work.

How long does setup take?

Live in 10–14 days. We handle discovery, build, and testing. You don't touch the code. We install it on your site, and it starts working.

What if a customer wants to call instead of chat?

They can. The chat shows your phone number. Some people will call, some will chat—both routes work. You've just made it possible to capture the ones who prefer chat.
