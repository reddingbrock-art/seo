FAQ

Common Questions About AI Chat for Septic & Drain Services

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on your real call recordings, job descriptions, and how your team talks to customers. It uses your terminology, your tone, even your running jokes if that matters to you. Customers don't notice it's AI—they notice it answered their call.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It flags the call for your team and sends it to your dispatcher or office manager with a summary of what the customer asked. No data gets lost. You can call them back or have the AI transfer to a live number if that's your preference.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It replaces the phone ringing into dead air at 6 a.m., the missed texts at midnight, and the form submissions you never see. Your team stays focused on running jobs. The AI handles the volume.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. Calls at 2 a.m. on a Sunday get answered the same way as calls at 2 p.m. on a Tuesday. You choose what gets booked automatically and what gets flagged for your dispatcher to handle in the morning.

How long does setup take?

Discovery to live is 10 to 14 days. We need your phone number, your calendar system, and about 20 minutes of your time to understand your process. After that, we handle the rest.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

They usually don't. But if they ask, the AI tells them straight—'I'm an automated system designed to make sure your appointment gets booked accurately.' Most customers don't care as long as their call got answered and their job got scheduled.
