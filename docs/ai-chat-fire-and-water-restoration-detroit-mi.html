FAQ

AI Phone Answering for Detroit Fire & Water Restoration—Common Questions

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on your terminology, your tone, how your team actually talks to customers. It'll reference your service areas by name, mention your certifications, and handle objections the way you would.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It says so and captures the customer's info for a callback. No guessing. No bad information. Your team calls them back with the real answer.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It handles the calls your receptionist can't—nights, weekends, during peak season surges. If you have a receptionist, they stop answering routine intake calls and start handling VIP customers and relationship work.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. It's on during business hours, after hours, holidays, and 3 AM on a Tuesday. You choose when it routes calls to your team versus capturing info for a callback.

How long does setup take?

10–14 days from our first call to going live. One discovery session, then build and deploy while you keep running your jobs.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

Most don't notice. But if they ask, transparency is fine—you can have the AI acknowledge it and ask if they'd prefer to wait for a human callback. Either way, their info is captured and routed to your team.
