FAQ

AI Chat for Electrical Contractors: Common Questions

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on your language, your pricing structure, how you describe your services, and your tone. When a customer calls, they're talking to something that sounds like your operation, not a generic bot.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It tells the customer it needs a moment and transfers the call to you or your team. It never pretends to know something it doesn't. It also logs the question so we can train it on that scenario next time.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. If you have one, it frees them up for follow-up work and complex calls. If you don't, it does the job of answering and qualifying inbound calls—which a human receptionist would do anyway.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. It answers at midnight, at 6 AM before your office opens, and on Sundays. You decide what it can do during those hours—book jobs, take emergency calls, or just collect information for you to follow up on.

How long does setup take?

About 10-14 days from discovery call to live. We handle all the configuration. You spend maybe an hour reviewing and approving the final version.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

Most don't notice. But if they ask, the AI tells them upfront. Customers are usually fine with it—they're more annoyed by unanswered phones than by talking to a system that actually helps them.
