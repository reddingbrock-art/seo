FAQ

Review Automation Questions for Septic & Drain Contractors

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests only to customers you've actually served. Real jobs, real customers, real reviews. Google's system filters out anything suspicious anyway.

What if a customer is unhappy?

The request goes out, and the customer can leave a review—good or bad. Unhappy customers are a business problem, not an automation problem. We don't filter or suppress anything. You deal with service issues directly.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google is the priority—that's where homeowners search for septic service and make calls. Other platforms can be added depending on your needs.

How does the timing work?

The request goes out a few hours after you mark the job complete, when the experience is fresh but they're not in crisis mode. Timing matters.

What if I already have a review process?

We can work with what you've got, improve it, or replace it entirely. Either way, we handle the execution so you don't have to.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Most contractors see significantly more reviews within 60 days because they're actually asking. The exact number depends on your job volume and customer response rate—both of which improve over time.
