What exactly gets automated?

Lead capture from your website and Google, instant qualification of inquiries, automatic estimate scheduling, appointment booking, and post-job review requests. Everything that's currently taking manual labor stops being manual.

Do I need technical skills to run this?

No. You won't touch the backend. Your team logs in, checks their dashboard, confirms appointments, and that's it. If something changes in your business, we adjust it for you.

What if my team resists new tools?

We build it so your team does less work, not more. They'll see fewer repetitive tasks in their day—that's usually enough. We also train everyone before launch and stay available for questions.

How is this different from buying software myself?

You could buy five tools and stitch them together, or hire someone to manage them. We do that work. You get one integrated system built specifically for your cleaning business, maintained and updated by us.

What does the setup process look like?

Discovery call, then we build everything in 7–10 days and go live. You won't be without your systems during transition—we work alongside what you've got now and cut over cleanly.

What happens if something breaks after launch?

We monitor the system and fix issues on our side. You've got direct support for the first month, and ongoing access after that. It's our system—we keep it running.
