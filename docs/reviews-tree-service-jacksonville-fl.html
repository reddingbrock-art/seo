FAQ

Reviews Automation Questions for Jacksonville Tree Service Owners

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. The system requests reviews only from customers associated with completed jobs in your dispatch records. Every review comes from someone who actually worked with your crew.

What if a customer is unhappy?

Unhappy customers typically won't respond to the request. The system is designed to capture reviews from satisfied customers at the moment they're most likely to leave one. We don't filter or suppress anything—the timing and simplicity just naturally favor positive responses from happy clients.

Which platforms does this work on?

Reviews flow directly to your Google Business Profile. That's where Jacksonville customers look first, and that's where search visibility matters most for tree service.

How does the timing work?

Requests go out automatically the moment a job is marked complete in your system. You decide the exact timing and message, but the delivery happens without any action from your crew or office.

What if I already have a review process?

This replaces it with something that actually runs without your team thinking about it. If you're manually asking or sending reminders, this cuts that work out entirely and gets faster response rates because the timing is automated and consistent.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

It depends on your job volume and current review rate, but most tree service operations see significantly more reviews within the first 60 days because requests are captured at the right moment. We'll show you exactly where you are now during the discovery call.
