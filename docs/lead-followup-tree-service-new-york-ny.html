FAQ

Common Questions About Lead Follow-Up Automation for Tree Service

How many follow-ups does it send?

It typically sends three touches: immediate reply, next-day message, and a final check-in. We customize the sequence based on how your leads usually convert.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. We write the messages to sound like your business—voice, tone, questions asked. You approve before it goes live.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can reply STOP anytime. The system respects opt-outs immediately and removes them from the sequence.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Within two minutes of form submission or missed call. Most leads see it before they've even closed your website.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in your account marked as inactive after the final touch. You can still see them, review them, or reach out manually if you want.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. It plugs into your current phone line, forms, and email. No ripping out infrastructure. No retraining anyone.
