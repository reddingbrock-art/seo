FAQ

Lead Follow-Up Questions for Milwaukee Paving Contractors

How many follow-ups does it send?

We set it based on your process. Most paving shops use 2–3 touches over 7 days—enough to reach someone, not so much that it feels spammy.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. We write the first draft in your voice, you approve it, and you can change it anytime. It stays consistent with how you'd respond if you had time.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can opt out with one click, and they're immediately removed from all future messages. We handle that automatically—no manual work on your end.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Typically within 5 minutes of the lead coming in, depending on when it arrives. If it's 11 p.m., we can schedule it for 8 a.m. the next day instead—your call.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in your system. You'll see them in the dashboard marked as inactive, and you can follow up manually or leave them for later if they circle back.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. If you're already using a CRM or taking calls a certain way, we integrate around it. It's an extra set of hands, not a replacement.
