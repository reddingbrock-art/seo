FAQ

Common Questions About Electrical Automation in Dallas

What exactly gets automated?

Phone answering, job qualification, appointment scheduling, calendar syncing, and review requests after completion. You pick up the phone and talk to customers—we handle the routing, routing logic, and calendar management behind the scenes.

Do I need technical skills to run this?

No. We set it up and manage it. Your job is to answer calls and send crews. That's it.

What if my team resists new tools?

Your team doesn't interact with new tools—they use the same phone and the same calendar they already have. The system works in the background. Nothing changes for them.

How is this different from buying software myself?

You'd buy software, train your admin to use it, troubleshoot issues, update it, and handle calls yourself during gaps. We do all of that. You get a turnkey operation, not a tool to manage.

What does the setup process look like?

One discovery call, then we build and brief your team—usually happens in 3-5 days. You go live within two weeks. No long onboarding, no weeks of waiting.

What happens if something breaks after launch?

We monitor it and fix it. You call us, we respond. It's part of what we run for you. You're not on your own.
