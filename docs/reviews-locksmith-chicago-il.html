FAQ

Google Review Automation for Locksmiths: Common Questions

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We only send requests to customers you've actually serviced. Google can tell the difference between real reviews and fake ones. We're asking real customers—the ones who already have a reason to review you.

What if a customer is unhappy?

They'll get the same request as everyone else. But unhappy customers are the minority on most jobs, and a negative review is better than no review at all—it's honest, and Google's algorithm rewards genuine feedback. You'll still have significantly more positive reviews to balance it out.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google primarily. That's where locksmith customers look for you. Other review sites matter less for service area businesses.

How does the timing work?

The request goes out the same day the job closes, usually within minutes. That's when the customer remembers the interaction best. The sooner you ask, the higher your response rate.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll work around it. If you're already asking some customers manually, we'll make sure the automation doesn't duplicate requests. Most shops find that automation fills gaps your manual process can't reach.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Depends on your job volume, but most shops see 2-3x more reviews within the first 90 days because you're now asking customers you would've forgotten about. The exact number shows up in your dashboard in real time.
