FAQ

AI Chat Widget for Painting Companies: Common Questions

Will the chat widget actually sound like my business?

Yes. We write every response based on how you talk to customers. You approve the tone, the questions, the pitch—it feels like talking to your team, not a robot.

What happens when the chat can't answer something?

It offers a time to talk directly with you, or collects their info and tells them you'll follow up. Nothing gets lost—it all goes into your dashboard.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It replaces the gap when your receptionist isn't there. Early morning, lunch, after hours, weekends—the chat qualifies leads 24/7 so your team only handles serious conversations.

What hours does the chat widget work?

All day, every day. You set your availability window for booking, but the chat itself is always live and answering questions.

How long does setup take?

Installation and customization takes 10–14 days from kickoff. You'll see it live on your site within two weeks.

What if a customer wants to call instead of chat?

The chat can offer a callback button, or they can find your number on your site as usual. We don't force chat—we just make sure you don't miss the people who prefer it.
