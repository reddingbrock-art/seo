No. We send requests only to actual customers whose jobs are complete. Google's algorithm spots fake reviews fast—we're after real ones from real work you've done.

If they're unhappy, they won't leave a review. The system requests reviews after job completion, so you're asking people who've already experienced your work. We're not suppressing negative feedback—we're asking satisfied customers to share their experience.

Google is the primary focus. That's where homeowners search for mold remediation in Phoenix and make decisions.

How does the timing work?

The request goes out the day you mark the job complete in your system. Customers have a few days to respond, and reviews typically show up within a week.

What if I already have a review process?

We build on what you're doing. If you have existing follow-ups, we fill the gaps and automate what's been falling through the cracks.
