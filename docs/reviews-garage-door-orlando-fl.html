FAQ

Garage Door Review Automation—Answers to Your Questions

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. The system sends review requests to actual customers after completed jobs. Whether they leave a review is up to them—we're just making sure they're asked at the right moment.

What if a customer is unhappy?

Unhappy customers won't leave reviews. That's not our problem to solve—that's your service quality. The system just asks. Real feedback, good or bad, comes from real customers.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google reviews primarily. That's where homeowners look for garage door companies in Orlando, and where your local ranking matters most.

How does the timing work?

The request goes out within an hour of the job being marked complete in your system. Early enough to catch them while you're fresh in their mind, but not intrusive.

What if I already have a review process?

If you're already getting consistent results, great. Most garage door shops aren't—they ask sporadically or not at all. If you want to know whether your current process is actually working, book a call and we'll show you the gap.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

That depends on your job volume and customer satisfaction. If you're doing 20 jobs a month and converting 30% to reviews, you're looking at 6 new reviews monthly. The system's job is to make sure you're asking everyone—the rest is on your service.
