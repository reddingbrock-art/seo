FAQ

Common Questions About Review Automation for EV Charger Installation

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We only send requests to customers you've actually completed work for. We're asking real people who know your real work.

What if a customer is unhappy?

They don't have to leave a review. The request is just an ask. Unhappy customers typically don't respond, and that's fine—we're not forcing anything.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google, primarily. That's where Tucson commercial and residential customers search and decide. We can configure for other platforms depending on your market.

How does the timing work?

The request goes out after job completion—usually within a few hours. That's when the experience is fresh and customers are most likely to respond.

What if I already have a review process?

This replaces it. If you're manually asking or forgetting to ask, we automate it. If you're already getting tons of reviews, we'll look at the data together.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Most installation companies see significantly more reviews once requests are automated. The exact number depends on your current ask rate and your team's responsiveness to customers. We'll show you the baseline during the call.
