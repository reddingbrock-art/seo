FAQ

Review Automation Questions for Generator Service Owners

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests only to customers you've actually worked for. Real jobs, real customers, real reviews—or no reviews. Nothing forced or artificial.

What if a customer is unhappy?

We send requests after job completion, so timing-wise you've already delivered the work. If feedback is negative, that's genuine and worth hearing. We're automating the ask, not filtering responses.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google, Yelp, and other review platforms where generator service customers actually look. We prioritize where your customers are searching.

How does the timing work?

Requests go out within hours of job completion, when the experience is still fresh. That window is when you're most likely to get a response.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll look at what you're doing and upgrade it. Most manual processes miss jobs or have gaps. Automation fills those holes without replacing anything that's working.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Most shops are capturing 20–30% of review opportunities with manual processes. Automation typically brings that significantly higher because nothing falls through. The exact number depends on your job volume and customer base.
