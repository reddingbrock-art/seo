FAQ

Common Questions About Review Automation for Pressure Washing Businesses

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests only to customers after they've actually completed a job with you. Google's been cracking down on fake reviews for years, and we don't touch that. Real jobs, real customers, real feedback.

What if a customer is unhappy?

That's the point. You find out immediately instead of weeks later. You can reach out, fix it, and address it before it becomes a public problem. Some unhappy customers will still leave reviews—that's real feedback you can learn from.

Which platforms does this work on?

The system focuses on Google reviews, which is where most of your Henderson customers are looking. That's where the visibility happens.

How does the timing work?

The request goes out automatically the day after a job is marked complete in your system. Customers get one prompt. If they don't respond in 10 days, nothing else happens. No spam, no follow-up.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll look at what you're doing. If you're manually asking every customer and it's working, great. Most owners tell us they're hitting maybe 10% of their customers. This system gets 40–60% response rates because it's automatic and timely.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Depends on your job volume. A crew running 15–20 jobs weekly typically sees 6–10 new reviews per month after launch. More jobs, more reviews. The consistency is what changes your ranking.
