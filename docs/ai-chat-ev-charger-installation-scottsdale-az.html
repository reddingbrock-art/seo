FAQ

Common Questions About AI Chat for EV Charger Installation

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on your actual messaging, your crew names, your service areas, and how you talk to customers. It's not a template—it's you, available 24/7.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It tells the customer you'll get back to them and sends the question directly to your inbox or team member. The conversation doesn't break—it escalates.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It handles the volume your receptionist can't—calls before hours, after hours, and during busy field time. If you have a receptionist, they now handle the complex stuff and relationship calls while the AI owns the scheduling and FAQs.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. Calls and texts come in whenever they come in. You set business hours for scheduling, so customers can't book a 4 a.m. install—but they can leave a message or request a callback anytime.

How long does setup take?

10–14 days from discovery to live. We need your install process, pricing, crew schedule, and service boundaries. Once you give us that, we build and deploy fast.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

Some will. The agent doesn't hide it—but it's clear, professional, and fast. Most customers care more about getting booked same-day than whether a human or AI did it. If they want to talk to you, the agent transfers them or schedules a callback immediately.
