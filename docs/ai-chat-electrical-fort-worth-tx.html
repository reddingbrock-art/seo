FAQ

Common Questions About AI Chat for Electrical Service in Fort Worth

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We customize the voice, tone, and responses to match how you talk to customers. It answers questions the way you'd answer them—no corporate robot energy.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It says so. If it's urgent (gas smell, electrical fire), it routes the call to your cell immediately. For other questions, it collects the info and flags it so you can follow up.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It replaces the voicemail and the missed calls. If you have a receptionist, they can focus on more complex work—invoicing, material orders, customer follow-up—instead of just answering the phone.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. Your system works nights, weekends, and holidays. You can set business hours for scheduling, but the AI always answers.

How long does setup take?

We go live in 10–14 days from your discovery call. You'll be on the phone with a working AI system in about two weeks.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

Most don't. But if they ask, the AI is honest about it and transfers them to you immediately. Customers care more about getting an answer fast than who gives it.
