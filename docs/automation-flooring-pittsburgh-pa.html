Common Questions About Flooring Automation in Pittsburgh

What exactly gets automated?

Incoming calls get answered with a greeting that qualifies the job. Website forms trigger instant confirmations and calendar booking links. Text inquiries get responses. All leads—qualified or not—land in one organized feed for your team to follow up on if needed.

Do I need technical skills to run this?

No. Your team checks your calendar and takes appointments like normal. We handle the automation side. You never log into a platform or manage software.

What if my team resists new tools?

There's nothing new for them to resist. Their job stays the same—answer calls and show up to jobs. The system just makes sure leads don't slip through cracks before it gets to them.

How is this different from buying software myself?

You'd be buying a platform and teaching yourself and your team how to use it. We build it, customize it to your flooring business, run it, and support it. You just benefit from better lead flow.

What does the setup process look like?

Discovery call to understand your operation. We handle the configuration offsite—takes about two weeks. You get a walkthrough, then we flip it on. Most owners see results in their first week.

What happens if something breaks after launch?

You have direct support. If a call doesn't route right or a form stops working, you tell us and we fix it. You're not troubleshooting on your own.
