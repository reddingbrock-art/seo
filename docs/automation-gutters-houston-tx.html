What exactly gets automated?

Inbound calls, chat on your website, form submissions, appointment booking, and review requests after job completion. Anything that was eating your time but didn't require your judgment to handle gets handed off.

No. You log in, look at your appointments, and work. The system handles the backend. If something needs adjusting, we adjust it for you.
