FAQ

Common Questions About Review Automation for EV Charger Installation

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests only to actual customers you've completed work for. Google flags fake review patterns instantly, so this protects you as much as it protects them.

What if a customer is unhappy?

The request goes out, but unhappy customers either don't respond or leave honest feedback. That's real data. You'd rather know the problem than pretend it didn't happen.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google is the primary focus—that's where EV charger prospects search in Baltimore. We can extend to other review sites depending on your goals. Let's discuss during the call.

How does the timing work?

The request fires 24 hours after a job is marked complete. Customers have the highest intent to review at that moment, so response rates are significantly faster than email or manual follow-up.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll look at what you're doing and either replace it or layer in automation where you're dropping the ball. Most crews forget the ask on 40% of jobs. We catch those.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Most businesses see 2–3x more reviews in the first three months because the ask is consistent and timely. The exact number depends on your install volume and customer overlap, which we'll map during the call.
