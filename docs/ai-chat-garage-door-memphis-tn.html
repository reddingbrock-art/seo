AI Chat for Garage Door Contractors—Common Questions

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We write the conversation scripts based on how you actually talk to customers—your language, your process, your policies. If you say 'I'll get a tech out tomorrow morning,' the AI says the same thing.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It collects the information, thanks the customer, and immediately flags you with the full conversation so you can follow up. Nothing gets lost.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It handles the volume your receptionist can't—nights, weekends, during service calls, peak season overload. It frees them up to do higher-value work instead of answering the same questions 40 times a day.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. You set business hours if you want—it can take calls during off-hours and queue them, or it can work around the clock. Your call.

How long does setup take?

You're live in 10-14 days from the initial call. We handle the heavy lifting—scripting, integration, testing. You don't touch the technical side.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

Most won't. But if they ask directly, it tells them the truth and transfers them to you immediately. No deception. No awkward moments.
