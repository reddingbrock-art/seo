FAQ

Google Review Automation Questions for Flooring Owners

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send the request only to customers who actually completed jobs with you. What you get are real reviews from real customers. That's the whole point—capturing reviews from people who already know your work.

What if a customer is unhappy?

They can choose not to leave a review, or they can leave an honest one. We're not filtering reviews or hiding bad ones. If there's a problem with the work, that's something to fix with the customer directly—not something to hide from future prospects.

Which platforms does this work on?

We focus on Google reviews, where most flooring customers actually search for you. That's where your profile has the most impact.

How does the timing work?

The request goes out a day or two after the job's marked complete. It's close enough that the customer remembers the work and the experience, but not so fast it feels like you're rushing them.

What if I already have a review process?

We can work around it or replace it. If you're already doing something that works, we can automate it. If you're not getting the results you want, we'll show you a better way.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

It depends on your job volume and how many customers you're currently asking. Most flooring shops see their review count climb significantly within 60 days. The real win is consistency—reviews coming in regularly instead of in clumps.
