FAQ

Common Questions About AI Chat Agents for Nashville Restoration Companies

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We build it to match your voice, your terminology, and the way you qualify jobs. During setup, you send us recordings or notes on how you talk to customers. The AI mirrors that tone—not robotic, not generic.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It transfers the call to your team or takes a detailed message and routes it to you immediately. We design the conversation so the AI handles the 80% of questions it can answer, and escalates the rest in seconds.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. If you have a receptionist, this removes the overflow so they can focus on follow-ups, scheduling callbacks, or other tasks. If you don't have one, this does the job of answering and qualifying so your team isn't interrupted constantly.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. It answers at 2 a.m. on a Saturday the same way it answers at 2 p.m. on a Tuesday. Fire and water damage don't wait for business hours.

How long does setup take?

10-14 days from discovery call to going live on your main number. We need a week to build and refine, then a few days for you to test it before we flip the switch.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

It's fine. Most don't notice. But if they ask, the AI confirms it, thanks them for being clear, and keeps the conversation moving. Homeowners care more about getting help fast than whether a human answered—especially at 3 a.m.
