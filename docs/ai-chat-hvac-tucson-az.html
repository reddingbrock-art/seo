FAQ

HVAC AI Chat Agent FAQs for Tucson Owners

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on your scripts, your service names, your tone, and your common questions. It sounds like your team—professional and knowledgeable about HVAC.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It routes to your team immediately with the full context of what the customer asked. You take it from there without the customer repeating themselves.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It handles the first call and books the appointment. Your receptionist now focuses on dispatch coordination, callbacks, and customer service instead of being chained to the phone.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. Calls at 2 a.m. on a Sunday get answered just like calls at 2 p.m. on a Tuesday. Your calendar controls availability; the AI won't book outside your service windows.

How long does setup take?

You're live in 10–14 days. We handle all the integration and training. You just hand us your calendar and dispatch details.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

It's not a problem. You can be transparent about it from the first message if you want, or let them discover it naturally. Either way, they're getting their appointment booked faster than any human could do it.
