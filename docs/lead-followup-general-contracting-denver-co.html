FAQ

Lead Follow-Up Questions for Denver General Contractors

How many follow-ups does it send?

Typically 3–5 touches over 7–10 days, depending on lead source and response. We customize the sequence based on your sales cycle.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. We write the follow-up messages to match your voice and business. You review and approve everything before it goes live.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They're removed from future sequences immediately. The system respects opt-outs and compliance rules.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Within minutes of a lead coming in. If it's a phone call you miss, the first follow-up text or call can go out in under 5 minutes.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in your CRM with a complete follow-up history. You can see who's been contacted, how many times, and when. No lead disappears.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. The system plugs into however you're taking leads now—phone, forms, texts, whatever. Your team's workflow doesn't change except they get better organized.
