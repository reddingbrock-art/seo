Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests only to customers with completed jobs in your system. Real customers, real transactions, real reviews. Nothing artificial.

What if a customer is unhappy?

You still get the feedback—and you get it fast enough to respond. An unhappy customer leaving a negative review is better than an unhappy customer telling ten people. You can address real problems before they spread.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google is where the search happens. That's where we focus the automation. That's where your next customer is looking.

How does the timing work?

The request goes out the day the job is marked complete. Customers receive it while the experience is still fresh. Most respond within 24-48 hours.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll audit what you're doing now and build around it. If you're already collecting some reviews manually, we automate the rest so nothing falls through.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

It depends on your job volume. If you're doing 40 jobs a month and currently getting 2-3 reviews, you should see that number grow significantly within 60 days. Not every customer will respond, but automation captures most of the ones who would if asked.
