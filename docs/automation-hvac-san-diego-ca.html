What exactly gets automated?

Incoming calls get answered and qualified. Forms and text inquiries trigger instant confirmations and booking links. Follow-up reminders go out on schedule. Review requests send automatically after job completion. Your dispatcher and team see jobs pre-organized and ready to dispatch.

Do I need technical skills to run this?

No. You'll never touch code or log into a backend. You see a simple dashboard. You answer questions from your team about when jobs were booked and who called. That's it. Everything else is managed for you.

What if my team resists new tools?

The system makes their job easier. Dispatchers see appointments already confirmed. Service techs don't deal with vague or missing customer details. No one's spending time on follow-up calls or trying to decode old voicemails. Most teams see the benefit in the first week.

How is this different from buying software myself?

You'd be installing software, training yourself and your team, and monitoring it daily. We handle setup, training, ongoing optimization, and support. You don't hire someone to manage it. You just use it. Big difference in time and stress.

What does the setup process look like?

We take an inventory call to understand your workflow. Then we integrate your phone, website, and messaging channels. We script your voice into the system so it sounds like your business. You review it. We go live in 10 to 14 days.

What happens if something breaks after launch?

You have a dedicated support contact. If a call isn't routing, a form isn't converting, or a follow-up isn't firing, you tell us and we fix it. You're not troubleshooting. We are.
