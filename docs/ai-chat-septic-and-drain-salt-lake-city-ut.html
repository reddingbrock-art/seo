Yes. We build it from your existing language, tone, and how you actually talk to customers. It's not robotic. It sounds like someone from your company answering questions.

It captures the customer's info and the question, then hands it off to you with full context. You respond when you have time. Nothing falls through the cracks.

No. It replaces the chaos of unanswered phones and delayed callbacks. If you have a receptionist, they now handle callbacks and complex discussions instead of fielding ten repetitive questions.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. It answers at midnight on a Saturday just as fast as it does on Tuesday at 9 AM. Your customers get a response whenever they reach out.

10-14 days from discovery call to live. We handle everything—you don't need to touch code or learn a platform. It just works.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?
