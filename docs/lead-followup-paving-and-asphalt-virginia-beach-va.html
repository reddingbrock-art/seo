FAQ

Common Questions About Lead Follow-Up Automation for Paving Contractors

How many follow-ups does it send?

Usually 3–5 touches over 7–10 days, depending on what you choose. More for hot leads, fewer for people who opt out early. You control the sequence.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. Every message gets your voice and brand. We'll write drafts and you approve them before anything goes live.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They're removed immediately. The system logs it, and you'll never contact them again through automation.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Usually within 2–5 minutes of the lead coming in, depending on the source. Fast enough that they remember they submitted something.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in the system until they opt out or the sequence ends. You'll still see them in your dashboard so you can follow up manually if you want to.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. It automates the initial touches so you can focus on actual conversations and closing. Fits around how you already work.
