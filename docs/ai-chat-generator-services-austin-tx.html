Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We record you handling a call or two, train the AI on your language and tone, and it answers as your company. Customers don't notice the difference until you tell them.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It collects the question, captures the customer's info, and routes the call or details to you immediately. You always get the last word.

Does this replace my receptionist?

It handles inbound calls and scheduling so your team doesn't have to. If you have a receptionist, they can focus on follow-ups and customer service instead of answering phones all day.

24/7. It answers at midnight, 6 a.m., weekends, holidays—whenever your phone rings.
