FAQ

Review Automation for Pool Service: Common Questions

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests only to customers you've actually serviced. Google's system catches and removes fake reviews anyway. This is just automating the legitimate ask.

What if a customer is unhappy?

We send requests to everyone. Some won't respond. Some might leave feedback that's not five stars—and that's honest. Your real reviews, both positive and constructive, build more credibility than a profile full of nothing.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google is where pool service customers look first. That's where we focus. Your profile there is the one that shows up in local search and matters for your ranking.

How does the timing work?

Request goes out the day after service completion. You set the message and tone. It comes from your business, not a third party. Customers recognize it's from you.

What if I already have a review process?

We can supplement it or replace it entirely. If you're already asking manually and getting 5-10 reviews a month, this system typically doubles that volume because it never forgets and it hits the right timing window.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Most pool service businesses see significant increase within the first 60 days once this runs consistently. The exact number depends on your job volume and customer list size. Your discovery call will give us a realistic picture.
