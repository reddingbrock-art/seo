Automation Questions for Albuquerque Home Security Companies

What exactly gets automated?

Lead intake from all sources (phone, web form, text), qualification questions, appointment scheduling, customer notifications, job dispatch to technicians, and post-job review requests. Anything repetitive and time-sensitive.

Do I need technical skills to run this?

No. Your team operates it the same way they use email or their phone. We handle the backend. If something needs tweaking, we make the change.

What if my team resists new tools?

We build it into the workflow they already use, not on top of it. And we train everyone before launch. Most teams see the time saved and buy in immediately.

How is this different from buying software myself?

You'd spend weeks shopping, learning, configuring, and troubleshooting. We do all of that for you and deliver a working system in 10–14 days. You don't manage it—we do.

What does the setup process look like?

One discovery call to understand your business, then we build and test the system on our end, integrate your phone and calendar, train your team, and go live. You show up for the call and the training.

What happens if something breaks after launch?
