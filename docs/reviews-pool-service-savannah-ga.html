FAQ

Common Questions About Review Automation for Pool Service

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests only to customers who actually completed work with you. Real service equals real reviews. The system just removes the friction of asking.

What if a customer is unhappy?

You'll see unhappy feedback come in, which is useful. It tells you where service broke down. The system doesn't filter or hide negative reviews—it just asks everyone equally. Most businesses discover problems they can actually fix.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google is the priority because that's where pool service customers search. We set up for Google review requests, and the system integrates with how your jobs are currently tracked.

How does the timing work?

Requests go out a few hours after a job is marked complete in your system. Customers are still thinking about the work and the experience is fresh, so response rates are highest.

What if I already have a review process?

If you're manually sending review links or relying on crew to ask, automation removes that burden. If you've got something else running, we'll look at what you have and build around it.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

It depends on your current volume and how many customers you service monthly. Most see a noticeable increase within 30 days because you're asking consistently. The bigger impact is the velocity—reviews arrive faster, and your profile stays fresh.
