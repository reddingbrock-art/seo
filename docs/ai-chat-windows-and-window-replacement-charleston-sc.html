FAQ

AI Chat Questions for Window & Door Replacement Owners

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We feed it your real language, your process, your neighborhoods, and your service area boundaries. When a customer asks about vinyl vs. wood frames, it answers the way you would—not like a generic chatbot.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It says so honestly and escalates to you or your team with full context. The customer doesn't get stuck in a loop. You get a hand-off that's actually useful because the AI already gathered their info.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. If you have a receptionist, this frees them from answering the same questions over and over. If you don't, it means you're not bleeding leads anymore when the phone rings and no one answers.

What hours does the AI work?

All of them. It works nights, weekends, holidays. You set your own business hours for scheduling estimates, but the chat never stops listening.

How long does setup take?

Live in 10-14 days from your discovery call. We handle the heavy lifting. You don't need to learn new software or hire a tech person.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

They usually don't care. They got an instant answer. But if it comes up, it's fine—transparency builds trust. The point isn't to trick anyone; it's to make sure nobody waits three hours for a response.
