FAQ

Google Review Automation Questions for Cincinnati Contractors

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. The system sends requests only to customers tied to completed jobs in your system. Google can see the legitimate work history behind every review.

What if a customer is unhappy?

They still get the request like anyone else. If they leave a negative review, it's authentic feedback—and you can respond publicly and fix it. Suppressing unhappy customers isn't our model.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google My Business is the priority. Most leads in Cincinnati come from local search there, and that's where your profile matters most.

How does the timing work?

The request triggers after a job is marked complete in your system. Typically 1–3 days after the final walkthrough, when the experience is fresh.

What if I already have a review process?

We can integrate with what you're doing or replace it entirely. Either way, automation means requests actually go out consistently instead of being skipped when your team gets busy.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Most contractors see 60–70% of customers respond when asked at the right moment. The exact number depends on your job volume and current request rate. We'll show you the math on a call.
