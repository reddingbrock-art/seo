FAQ

Pest Control Lead Follow-Up: Questions Answered

How many follow-ups does it send?

That depends on your setup. Most pest control businesses run 3–5 touches per lead over 7–10 days. We customize the sequence based on your booking cycle and how your team wants to work.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. We write templates together so the messages sound like your business, not generic automation. You're in control of the tone and the offer.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can opt out anytime. The system respects that immediately. You're always compliant.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Usually within the first 2–5 minutes of the lead arriving. The timing is configurable based on your preference.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in the system for your team to follow up on manually if you want. Or they drop off after your sequence ends, depending on your settings. You decide the rules.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. It plugs into your current lead sources and works with however your team is already organized. We integrate with your phone system, forms, and dispatch tools so nothing changes except your response speed.
