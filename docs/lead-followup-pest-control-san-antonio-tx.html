FAQ

Lead Follow-Up Questions Answered

How many follow-ups does it send?

We build it to match your process. Typically 3–5 contacts spread across calls and texts over 48–72 hours. You decide the sequence.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. Every message is yours. We write based on your voice and business style so leads feel like they're talking to you, not a system.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can opt out at any time, and they're removed from future contact. It's built in and compliant.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Within minutes of form submission or missed call. Most leads hear from you before they've hung up the phone.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in your system and get the full sequence. If they don't convert after the sequence runs, you can manually follow up or they sit in your database for later outreach.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. It integrates with how you already work. Your team still closes deals. The system just makes sure nothing falls through the cracks on the front end.
