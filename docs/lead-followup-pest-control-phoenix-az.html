FAQ

Lead Follow-Up for Pest Control: Questions Answered

How many follow-ups does it send?

That depends on your business model and what works best for your market. We typically set up 3–5 touchpoints over 7–10 days: an initial call or text, a second contact 2–3 hours later, and one or two more if the lead hasn't responded. You control the sequence.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. We write the scripts and messaging based on your brand voice and what your customers respond to. You approve everything before it goes live. If you want to tweak it later, we make those changes.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can opt out at any time. The system honors that request immediately and removes them from the sequence. You stay compliant with regulations and don't burn relationships with people who aren't interested.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Usually within 2–5 minutes of the lead coming in. You set the timing based on your operation—some teams prefer the first contact to hit immediately, others want a short delay so they can screen first.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in the sequence until they opt out or you manually mark them closed. After the final touchpoint, they sit in your system so you can re-contact them later if you want. Dead leads don't disappear—you just stop wasting time chasing them.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. If you're already using a CRM or dispatch system, we integrate with it so leads flow in automatically. If you're not, we set up a simple intake method that feeds the system. Either way, it runs parallel to what you're already doing—no disruption.
