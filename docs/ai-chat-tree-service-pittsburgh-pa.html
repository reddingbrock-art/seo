FAQ

AI Chat Agent for Tree Service: Questions Answered

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on your website, past communications, and how you talk to customers. It uses your business name, service areas, and brand voice. Customers don't hear a corporate script—they hear someone who knows your operation.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It escalates to you or your team with full context—customer name, property details, the question asked. You respond once with the answer, and the AI learns it for next time. Complex estimates and insurance claims always route to a human.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It replaces the repetitive call-taking and scheduling. Your team shifts to higher-value work—managing crews, refining estimates, handling callbacks on complex jobs. Most tree service owners report their office staff actually works less during peak season.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. It answers calls, texts, and chats anytime—nights, weekends, holidays. During business hours, it can book estimates immediately. After hours, it captures the lead and notifies you first thing in the morning.

How long does setup take?

10 to 14 days from discovery call to live. We handle the technical setup, training, and testing. You don't need to learn software or manage servers. We maintain and optimize it ongoing.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

We're transparent about it if asked. Most customers prefer getting through to someone immediately instead of leaving a voicemail. The AI's job is to solve the problem fast—not to hide. If they want a human, the escalation happens instantly.
