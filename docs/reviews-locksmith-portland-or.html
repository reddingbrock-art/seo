FAQ

Google Reviews for Locksmiths—Common Questions Answered

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We only send requests to customers you've actually serviced. The system validates the job completion before the request goes out. Real work, real reviews.

What if a customer is unhappy?

We send the request after every job. If a customer had a bad experience, they have the option to leave feedback. That's their right, and it's honest. Our focus is making sure satisfied customers actually leave reviews instead of forgetting.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google reviews are the primary focus. That's where customers search for locksmiths and where reviews matter most for your visibility. We can discuss other platforms on your call.

How does the timing work?

Requests go out as soon as you mark a job complete in the system. Usually within minutes. The customer gets a simple text or email asking them to leave a review.

What if I already have a review process?

We can work around what you're doing or replace it entirely. Most locksmiths have manual processes that depend on them remembering to ask. This removes the memory part and makes it consistent.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

It depends on your job volume and current response rate. Most locksmiths see significantly more reviews because the timing is right and the process is automatic. We'll give you realistic projections during your call based on your numbers.
