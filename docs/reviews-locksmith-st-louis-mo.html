FAQ

Review Automation FAQs for St. Louis Locksmiths

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests only to customers you've actually worked for. Google's algorithm can tell the difference, and so can your reputation.

What if a customer is unhappy?

They receive the request like anyone else. If they leave a negative review, that's feedback you need to see. We don't filter or suppress reviews—we just make sure you ask for them consistently.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google Business Profile is the primary focus. That's where your customers are searching and where reviews matter most for local locksmith searches.

How does the timing work?

The request goes out automatically shortly after job completion—when the customer's still thinking about the work you just did. No manual timing required.

What if I already have a review process?

We replace manual processes entirely. If you're currently texting or emailing review requests, this automation does it faster and more consistently without eating up your time.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

It depends on your current job volume and request rate, but most locksmiths see a significant uptick in review velocity within the first month simply because requests are going out consistently.
