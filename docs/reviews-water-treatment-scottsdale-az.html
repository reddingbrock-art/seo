FAQ

Common Questions About Review Automation for Water Treatment

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We only send requests to actual customers who booked and completed a job through your business. Real work, real feedback.

What if a customer is unhappy?

They get the same request as everyone else. Some won't respond. Some might leave honest feedback. That's data you can act on—it beats never hearing about the problem at all.

Which platforms does this work on?

We focus on Google, where most customers look first. We can also route requests to other platforms depending on your priorities.

How does the timing work?

The request goes out the day of or day after job completion—when the experience is fresh. You set the exact timing based on your schedule.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll talk through what you're doing now and where the gaps are. Most owners have sporadic ask rates or long delays between job completion and request. We tighten that up significantly.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

That depends on your volume and current ask rate. Most owners see 2-4 new reviews per week once the system's running, compared to a few per month before.
