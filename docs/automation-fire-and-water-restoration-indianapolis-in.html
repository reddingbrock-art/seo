FAQ

Questions About Automation for Fire & Water Restoration

What exactly gets automated?

Incoming calls and web forms are captured and routed to the right person with full context. Follow-up messages and review requests go out automatically after jobs complete. Everything else—the actual restoration work, pricing decisions, customer conversations—stays in your hands.

Do I need technical skills to run this?

No. Your dispatcher or office manager uses it the same way they'd use a phone or text message. We handle all the backend setup and integrations.

What if my team resists new tools?

Most crews push back on software because it feels like extra work. This one actually removes work—fewer manual callbacks, fewer missed messages, less hunting for customer info. They usually adopt it in the first week because it makes their day easier.

How is this different from buying software myself?

You could buy a phone system or CRM and wire it together yourself. That takes months, requires IT help, and breaks when updates happen. We build, test, and run it for you. You just use it.

What does the setup process look like?

We schedule a discovery call, connect your existing phone lines and tools, configure routing and messaging to match your business, test with your team, and go live. Most shops are running live within two weeks.

What happens if something breaks after launch?

You have direct support. If a call doesn't route right or a message doesn't send, we fix it. You're not troubleshooting on your own or waiting for a software company's support queue.
