FAQ

AI Chat Widget for Pest Control: Common Questions

Will the chat widget actually sound like my business?

Yes. During setup we learn your voice, your service areas, and how you talk to customers. The chat reflects that—it's not generic. It answers questions the way you would if you had time to answer every visitor.

What happens when the chat can't answer something?

It tells the visitor honestly that it needs your team's input, collects their details, and sends the conversation to you. No fake answers. No frustration.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It replaces the work of answering repetitive questions from website visitors. Your team still closes jobs, still schedules complex situations, still builds relationships. It just eliminates the time spent on 'Do you service my area?' and 'What's your availability?'

What hours does the chat widget work?

24/7. It captures visitors whenever they land on your site—midnight on a Tuesday, Sunday morning, holidays. You define response expectations so customers know when your team will follow up on complex requests.

How long does setup take?

Live in 10–14 days from your discovery call. We handle the technical work. You just need to spend about an hour reviewing the chat responses and making sure they match your business.

What if a customer wants to call instead of chat?

They can. We include your phone number in the chat, and they can click to call anytime. The chat isn't forcing them into text—it's offering them an option when they'd rather type than talk.
