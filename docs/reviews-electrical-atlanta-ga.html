FAQ

Review Automation FAQs for Atlanta Electrical Contractors

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send review requests only to customers whose jobs are marked complete in your system. Real work, real request, real reviews. That's it.

What if a customer is unhappy?

The request still goes out—that's on you to handle. If a customer had a bad experience, no automation can fix that conversation. But most of your jobs are solid; unhappy ones are the exception, not the rule.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google is the primary platform for local search and customer discovery. We can set up requests to flow there automatically, and reviews post directly to your Google Business Profile.

How does the timing work?

Requests go out within 24 hours of job completion. Customers have a direct link to leave a review, and most respond within 3–7 days if they're going to respond at all.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll audit what you're doing and either integrate with it or replace it with something faster and more reliable. If your current process is working, we'd rather improve it than start over.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Most electrical companies see 40–60% of customers respond when asked at the right time. If you're completing 20 jobs a week, expect 8–12 new reviews per week once the system is running. Results vary based on your current review count and customer base.
