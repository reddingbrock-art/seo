FAQ

Review Automation FAQs for Paving & Asphalt Contractors

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We only send requests to actual customers you've completed work for. Every review you get is from someone who paid you and saw the finished job.

What if a customer is unhappy?

Happy customers leave reviews. Unhappy ones usually won't. You're not forcing anyone to write something they don't mean—you're just making it easy for satisfied customers to share their experience.

Which platforms does this work on?

We push reviews to Google Business Profile, which is where homeowners search for paving contractors. That's where it counts for visibility.

How does the timing work?

The request goes out the same day or next morning after you mark a job complete in your system. Customers see it when they're thinking about the finished work, not months later.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll integrate with what you're doing or replace it entirely—whichever makes sense. If your current process isn't bringing in reviews consistently, this will fix that.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Depends on your job volume. Most contractors see significantly more reviews within the first 30 days because the ask actually reaches customers. You're not creating new customers—you're capturing reviews from the ones you already have.
