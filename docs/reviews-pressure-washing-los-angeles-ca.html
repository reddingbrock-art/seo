FAQ

Review Automation for Pressure Washing Companies: Your Questions Answered

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests only to customers whose jobs you've actually completed. You can only get a review from someone who actually worked with you.

What if a customer is unhappy?

The request goes out after job completion, so timing is your control. If you know a job didn't go well, you can skip that one. The system doesn't suppress negative reviews—it just asks happy customers to share their experience.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google, Yelp, Facebook, and industry-specific review sites. We direct customers to whichever platform makes sense for your business.

How does the timing work?

Requests go out within 24 hours of job completion. That's when customers are most likely to remember the clean results and take 60 seconds to post.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll sync with it or replace it, depending on what's working. Most owners find they were asking for reviews inconsistently—this removes the guesswork.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

It depends on your current request rate. Most shops find they were asking for reviews on maybe 20% of jobs. Automating that typically surfaces 3–4 new reviews per week once it's running.
