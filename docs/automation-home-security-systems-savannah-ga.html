What exactly gets automated?

Lead capture and qualification, appointment scheduling and confirmations, customer communication (estimates, contracts, payment reminders), crew notifications and job details, and follow-up sequences. Anything that used to require manual work stops requiring it.

Do I need technical skills to run this?

No. You manage the business side—what you want customers to see and when. We handle the technical wiring and updates. Your crew gets notifications and jobs; your admin gets a clean inbox.

What if my team resists new tools?

We design this around what your team already does, not what some vendor thinks they should do. Your crew still sees jobs the way they expect to see them—just with less confusion and faster turnaround. Most resistance disappears in the first week.

How is this different from buying software myself?

Software gets installed and abandoned. We build the workflow, integrate it with your existing systems, and manage it for you. When something changes in your business, we update it. You're not learning a platform; you're running your business faster.

What does the setup process look like?

One call to understand your process, then we build and test while you run business as usual. You give feedback, we adjust, then we flip the switch. Total time: 10-14 days from first conversation to live.

What happens if something breaks after launch?

We monitor and maintain the whole system. If a lead isn't going through, if confirmations aren't sending, or if your crew's notifications stop working, we fix it and let you know. This is ours to keep running, not yours.
