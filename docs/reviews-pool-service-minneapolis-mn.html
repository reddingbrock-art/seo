FAQ

Google Reviews for Pool Service: Questions Answered

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests only to customers you've already completed work for. Google's system verifies the customer exists, the email is real, and they actually use Google. Fake reviews violate Google's policy and don't help your business anyway.

What if a customer is unhappy?

The system flags negative feedback before it becomes public, so you can reach out and resolve it. Only satisfied customers move forward to leave a public review.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google is the primary focus because that's where 85% of your search traffic comes from. We can also route requests to Yelp, Facebook, and other platforms depending on where your customers are looking.

How does the timing work?

The request goes out 2–4 hours after a job is marked complete—when satisfaction is highest but the customer still remembers the work. You choose the exact window that fits your operation.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll audit what you're doing now and either integrate with it or replace it if it's not producing results. Most owners realize their current process isn't consistent enough to move the needle.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Most pool service operations see 40–60% of customers leave a review when the request is timed right and frictionless. If you're closing 50 jobs a month, that's 20–30 new reviews. In a competitive market, that changes your ranking significantly.
