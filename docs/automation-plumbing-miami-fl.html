What exactly gets automated?

Inbound calls—they get answered and either routed to your team or scheduled directly. Web forms and text messages get replied to immediately. After a job's done, review requests go out automatically. Everything else—the actual work, the sales calls, the decisions—that stays with you.

Do I need technical skills to run this?

No. If you can use your phone and check a calendar, you can use this. We handle setup. Your job is checking in on calls, managing jobs, and running your business.

What if my team resists new tools?

They won't resist something that removes their phone duty. Your team will like this because it lightens their load, not adds to it. We train everyone on day one.

How is this different from buying software myself?

You'd have to set it up, integrate it with your calendar and phone, figure out what to say to customers, and manage it alone. We do all that. It's done-for-you, not DIY. You get a system that works on day one.

What does the setup process look like?

Discovery call to understand your flow. We configure everything—your phone number transfers over, forms connect, responses are written. Training for your team. Then we flip the switch. Two weeks, start to finish.

What happens if something breaks after launch?

We support it. If a call doesn't route right, if a form integration hiccups, if something needs adjustment—you contact us and we fix it. You're not troubleshooting alone.
