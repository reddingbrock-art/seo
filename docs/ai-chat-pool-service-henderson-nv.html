FAQ

AI Chat Widget FAQs for Pool Service Owners

Will the chat widget actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on your voice, your service details, and the way you actually talk to customers. It sounds like someone from your team, not a robot.

What happens when the chat can't answer something?

It tells the customer the truth—'I'll have [your name] reach out to you about that'—and logs the conversation so you know exactly what they asked. You follow up when you're free, but you're not waiting for an initial response anymore.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It handles the visitors who would otherwise disappear before you could answer. It frees your team to focus on jobs and relationships instead of answering 'Do you service my zip code?' fifty times a day.

What hours does the chat widget work?

24/7. Visitors can chat whenever they land on your site—even at midnight on Sunday. The chat captures them and books them, and you see everything in the morning.

How long does setup take?

About 10-14 days from the first call to going live. We handle the technical side; you just answer questions about your business and approve the final version.

What if a customer wants to call instead of chat?

We can add a phone option in the widget, or they can find your number on your site as usual. The chat doesn't prevent phone calls—it just makes sure someone's always there when they want to chat instead.
