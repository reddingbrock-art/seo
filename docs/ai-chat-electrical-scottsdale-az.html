FAQ

AI Chat for Electrical: Common Questions Answered

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on your voice tone, your service language, and how your team actually talks to customers. It's not robotic; it sounds like someone answering your phone who knows electrical work.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It gathers the details—the customer's name, address, problem description, callback number—and flags the conversation for you to review. You reach out with the full context already captured.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. If you have one, it frees them from call intake so they handle scheduling, invoicing, and customer follow-up—higher-value work. If you don't have one, this eliminates the need to hire.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. Calls come in at midnight, 6 a.m., or 2 p.m. on Sunday—the AI answers all of them. You decide when to respond, but no lead ever hits voicemail.

How long does setup take?

10–14 days from discovery call to live. One call with us to map your process, then we build and train the system while you keep working. No downtime on your end.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

It's fine. Most customers don't care—they got their problem logged, their job scheduled, and their questions answered. The AI isn't trying to hide. It's honest and fast, and that's enough.
