FAQ

Automation for Tucson Plumbing: Questions & Answers

What exactly gets automated?

Inbound calls, text routing to your team, job dispatch to the field, customer status updates, and review requests after completion. Everything between the phone ringing and the invoice getting paid stays in the system.

Do I need technical skills to run this?

No. You use it like you'd use a text message or email. If a call comes in or a job needs reassigning, you see it on your phone and tap once. We handle all the wiring.

What if my team resists new tools?

Most crews resist because they haven't seen it before, not because it's hard. Once they realize they're not writing down addresses on a notepad anymore and customers can see their ETA, adoption is fast. We train them during setup.

How is this different from buying software myself?

You'd spend weeks setting it up, integrating it with your current tools, training your team, and dealing with breakdowns. We do that work and stay on the backend so you never have to. You own the automation, we maintain it.

What does the setup process look like?

Discovery call, then 7-10 days of build on our end while you keep running. We connect your phone lines, set up your dispatch rules, and load your service area. No downtime for your business.

What happens if something breaks after launch?

You get a dedicated support contact. If a call doesn't route or a text doesn't send, you message us and we fix it—usually within hours. You're never on your own troubleshooting.
