FAQ

Lead Follow-Up Questions for Flooring Contractors

How many follow-ups does it send?

We typically run 3–5 touches per lead over 7–10 days, depending on your business model. You decide the sequence, timing, and message for each one.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. We'll set up the messaging with you so it sounds like you, not a robot. Every text, email, and follow-up reflects your voice and your offer.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can opt out at any time, and the system respects that request immediately. No further messages go out to that contact.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Most leads are contacted within 5–15 minutes of submitting a form or inquiry. We can adjust timing based on your schedule if you prefer.

What happens to leads that never respond?

After the sequence completes, they stay in your system. You can re-engage them seasonally or when you're running a promotion. Nothing gets deleted.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Absolutely. If you're already using a scheduling tool, CRM, or dispatch system, we integrate with it. This just fills the gap in your follow-up.
