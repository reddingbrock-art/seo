FAQ

Lead Follow-Up System FAQs for Mold Remediation

How many follow-ups does it send?

That depends on your process. We typically build sequences that touch a lead 3–5 times over 7–10 days, but you control how many messages go out and on what schedule.

Can I customize what it says?

Completely. We write templates that match your voice and process, and you approve every message before it runs. You can adjust any time.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can opt out with one click. The system respects that immediately and stops all automated outreach to that contact.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Typically within 2–5 minutes of a form submission or call. Phone leads can be reached even faster if you set it up that way.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in your database tagged as 'no response.' You can re-engage them manually later or let them sit. The system doesn't force anything—you decide what to do.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Yes. It integrates with the tools you already use. The system fills gaps in your current workflow instead of replacing it.
