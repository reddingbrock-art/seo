FAQ: AI Chat for EV Charger Installation Companies

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We record a brief sample of how you talk about your work and build the AI to match that voice. It'll reference your crew, your service area, and your specific process so it sounds like someone from your shop answered the phone.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It tells the customer it'll have someone follow up and flags the question for you. You get the call transcript and can respond within a few hours. The AI learns from your answer and handles similar questions better next time.

Does this replace my receptionist?

Not if you have one. It works alongside them—picking up overflow, answering after hours, and handling the qualification questions they'd normally ask. If you're running without a receptionist now, you won't need to hire one.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. Calls come in at midnight on a Sunday? It answers. You get the summary the next morning. Most of your actual scheduling conversations will happen during business hours, but leads aren't lost to voicemail anymore.

How long does setup take?

10-14 days from your first call. That includes discovery, building the AI to match your voice, testing it with your install process, and going live. You're not waiting months for this to work.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

It doesn't hide what it is. The AI's clear it's helping qualify the project so your crew shows up prepared, and customers generally appreciate getting their questions answered immediately instead of waiting for a callback. Most of them never care—they just want the install booked.
