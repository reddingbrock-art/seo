FAQ

AI Chat for Septic & Drain: Questions & Answers

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We write the scripts, set the tone, and train it on your specific service offerings and pricing. It sounds professional and knowledgeable about septic and drain work—not robotic.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It says so and offers to have you call the customer back. It collects their number and notes the question so you know exactly what they need. Nothing gets lost.

Does this replace my receptionist?

It doesn't replace them—it frees them up. If you have a receptionist, they can focus on coordination and follow-up instead of answering the same intake questions all day. If you don't have one, this handles the volume you couldn't before.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. It answers at 2 a.m. just like it answers at 2 p.m. A lot of emergency calls come in after hours—you capture those now.

How long does setup take?

10–14 days from your first call to going live. We handle everything—you just confirm the scripts and voice tone.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

They'll figure it out eventually, and that's fine. Customers care more about whether their problem gets solved and their call gets logged than whether they're talking to a person. The AI is fast, friendly, and gets the job done. Most don't ask.
