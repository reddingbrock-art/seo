FAQ

AI Chat Agent FAQs for Chicago Junk Removal Companies

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on your actual language, your service areas, your pricing structure, and how your team closes deals. It answers questions the way you would—not like a generic chatbot.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It escalates to your team immediately with the customer's info and the conversation history. You jump in and finish what the AI started, no context switching.

Does this replace my receptionist?

It handles the jobs a receptionist shouldn't—answering at midnight, qualifying leads during your jobs, and booking appointments while your team is working. If you have a receptionist, they now focus on closing and operations instead of answering the same questions 50 times a day.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. It answers leads at 3 a.m. on a Saturday just as fast as it does at noon on a Tuesday. Your competitors go to voicemail—you don't.

How long does setup take?

You're live in 10-14 days from your discovery call. We handle everything—no technical work on your end, no training your team on new software.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

Most don't care as long as they get instant answers and a booked appointment. But if they ask or prefer to talk to a human, the AI hands them off to your team immediately. The point is you never miss the moment—the AI buys you time to respond like a professional.
