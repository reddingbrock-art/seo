FAQ

Review Automation for Electrical Companies: Common Questions

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests only to customers you've actually completed jobs for. Google filters out fake reviews anyway, and we're not asking for anything dishonest—just asking real customers to share their real experience.

What if a customer is unhappy?

The system sends a review request after job completion. If a customer's unhappy, they're less likely to leave a review—that's their choice. What matters is that satisfied customers, who'd normally forget to leave one, now get asked when they're most likely to say yes.

Which platforms does this work on?

We focus on Google reviews because that's where Tampa customers are looking and where search ranking matters most. Google reviews are also the hardest to fake and the most trusted by new customers.

How does the timing work?

The request goes out automatically when a job's marked complete in your system. The customer gets asked within hours, when they're still thinking about the work you just did.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll audit what you're doing now and either enhance it or replace it with a fully automated version. Most shops are asking inconsistently or at the wrong time—we just make it consistent and automatic.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

It depends on your job volume, but most electrical contractors see a significant increase once they're asking everyone systematically instead of sporadically. You're removing the friction that stops satisfied customers from leaving reviews.
