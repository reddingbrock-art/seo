FAQ

FAQ: AI Chat for EV Charger Installation Websites

Will the chat widget actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on your language, your service approach, the way you actually talk to customers. It reflects your professionalism, your technical knowledge, and your local roots—not generic corporate tone.

What happens when the chat can't answer something?

It's honest. If a question's outside its knowledge base, it tells the visitor and connects them to the right next step—usually booking a call with you for specifics.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It does what a receptionist can't do: work 24/7, answer the same question for the 50th visitor without frustration, and qualify leads before they reach you. Your team stays focused on installations and customer relationships.

What hours does the chat widget work?

All hours. It's live whenever someone visits your site—nights, weekends, holidays. You pick when and how responses get routed to your team.

How long does setup take?

10-14 days from first call to live on your site. We handle the technical work. You just give us the information about your business, your services, and your booking preferences.

What if a customer wants to call instead of chat?

They can. The chat widget doesn't lock them into messaging. If someone prefers to talk, they book a call slot or we can include your number. We're not forcing them into chat—we're making it available for those who prefer it.
