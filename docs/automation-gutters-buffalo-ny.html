Automation Questions for Gutter Companies in Buffalo

What exactly gets automated?

Lead capture from your website and phone, instant responses to inquiries, appointment scheduling and confirmation, customer reminders before jobs, and review requests after completion. Your crew gets a dispatch board with customer details already loaded. Everything that was manual before now runs on its own.

Do I need technical skills to run this?

No. You don't build or maintain anything. We handle all the backend work. You log in, see your leads and appointments, and dispatch your crew. If you can use email, you can operate this.

What if my team resists new tools?

We train your team on day one and stick around for the first week. Most crews embrace it once they see that appointments are pre-confirmed and they're not answering the same intake questions 10 times a day. It makes their job easier, not harder.

How is this different from buying software myself?

You'd have to research platforms, sign contracts, integrate systems yourself, and manage it all. We do that. We customize everything to your business, connect all your tools, and support you ongoing. You own the relationships with your customers. We own the operational headache.

What does the setup process look like?

We do a discovery call, map your current process, build the system over about a week, test it with you, train your team, and launch. You don't have to do technical work. We handle integration with your phone, email, website, and dispatch software.

What happens if something breaks after launch?

You get direct support. If a lead doesn't route correctly or an automation doesn't trigger, we fix it. You're not troubleshooting alone or waiting for a support ticket to queue. We monitor and maintain the system so you can focus on running jobs.
