FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions About Review Automation for Tree Service

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send review requests to real customers after real jobs. Google filters out fake reviews anyway. We're just making sure the people who should be reviewing you actually get asked.

What if a customer is unhappy?

The system doesn't force anyone to leave a review. If a customer's unhappy, they won't post—and that's better than them posting something negative because you pestered them. The automation just makes sure the ask reaches them; they decide whether to respond.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google, primarily. That's where tree service customers search and make decisions. We focus on the platform that actually drives calls and estimates.

How does the timing work?

Requests go out the day after a job is marked complete in your system. Timing is automatic, so you're never early or late—just consistent.

What if I already have a review process?

If it's working, great. Most owners we talk to have a process that works 40% of the time—requests get sent late or forgotten entirely. We make sure it hits 90%+ of jobs, consistently.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Depends on your current volume and how many jobs you're currently not requesting on. If you're sending requests on 30% of jobs and converting 15% of those, automating coverage typically gets you 2-3x more reviews within 60 days.
