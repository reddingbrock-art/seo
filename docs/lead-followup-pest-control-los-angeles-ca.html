FAQ

Lead Follow-Up Questions for Pest Control Owners in Los Angeles

How many follow-ups does it send?

Typically 3–5 touches spread over 5–7 days: one immediately after they book, then spaced reminders. We customize the sequence based on how your sales cycle actually works.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. We write the messages to match your voice and your process. Every follow-up includes a direct call-to-action that makes sense for where the lead is in the journey.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can opt out anytime, and they're immediately removed. The system respects that. We keep everything compliant so you don't have to worry about it.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Immediately. Once a lead confirms or you add them to the system, the first touch goes out within minutes—not hours.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They're documented in your system so you see who's cold. You can flag them for later follow-up, or let them age out if they don't convert. Nothing's lost.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. We integrate with your scheduling software and your dispatch. This sits on top of what you're already doing—no rip-and-replace, no retraining your team.
