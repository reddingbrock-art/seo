FAQ

Questions About Google Review Automation for Locksmiths

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. The system sends review requests only to customers who've actually completed a job. Google flags fake reviews and kills your profile. We're not doing that.

What if a customer is unhappy?

The request still goes out—it's their choice whether to leave a review. You'll see all reviews, good and bad. That's actually how you know the system is legit.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google is the one that matters for locksmith calls. That's where your customers are looking. That's where we focus.

How does the timing work?

The request goes out after a job is marked complete in your dispatch system. You decide the timing—immediately, or after 24 hours. Most owners pick immediately while the job's fresh.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll look at what you're doing now and either replace it or layer the automation on top. Either way, we're removing the manual work.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Most locksmiths see a significant increase once the system is asking consistently. You'll get more reviews because you're asking automatically after every job, not randomly.
