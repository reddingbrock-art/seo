FAQ

Automation for Water Treatment: Questions Answered

What exactly gets automated?

Lead intake (forms, calls, texts), initial response, callback routing, customer info delivery to dispatch, and post-job review requests. Basically everything between a customer reaching out and your team having a booked appointment with full context.

Do I need technical skills to run this?

No. We build it and hand it to you. Your team uses it like any other tool—answer texts if they come in, check the calendar, mark jobs complete. That's it.

What if my team resists new tools?

We design it around how you already work, not against it. If your team uses text and paper dispatch now, the system sits in the middle and feeds them information in the format they actually use.

How is this different from buying software myself?

You'd buy the platform, spend weeks setting it up, figure out which tools connect, train your team, and troubleshoot when something breaks. We do all of that. You get a working system in 10–14 days.

What does the setup process look like?

One discovery call with us, then we build behind the scenes. We integrate with your existing tools, test everything, and onboard your team. You're involved for feedback, not implementation.

What happens if something breaks after launch?

We handle it. You get support—not a ticket queue where you wait for third-tier tech support. If something stops working, we fix it.
