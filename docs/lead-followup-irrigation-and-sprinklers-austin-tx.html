FAQ

Lead Follow-Up Questions for Irrigation & Sprinklers Owners

How many follow-ups does it send?

Typically 3–5 touches over a week, depending on how you want it set up. We customize the number and timing based on your sales cycle.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. We write the first version to match your voice, and you can adjust any message before it goes live. Changes are easy to make anytime.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can opt out with one click, and they're removed from future touches. It's built in and compliant.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Within 2–5 minutes of them submitting a form or calling in. The goal is to be first.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in the system for the full sequence. After that, they're flagged so you know whether to try a different angle or let them go.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. It fills the gap between when a lead comes in and when you have time to call them back. Your team keeps doing what they do—this just makes sure nothing falls through.
