FAQ

Lead Follow-Up System FAQs for Irrigation & Sprinklers

How many follow-ups does it send?

We typically set up 3–5 touches over 7–10 days: first response same-day, then touchpoints spaced to catch people at different times. You decide the exact sequence based on what works for your business.

Can I customize what it says?

Completely. We write the initial messaging for you based on your business, but you approve and can adjust every message before it goes live. It should sound like you, not a robot.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can reply STOP or opt out, and the system automatically removes them from the sequence. You're compliant and they're respected.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Usually within 5–15 minutes of the lead coming in, depending on time of day. If they call during business hours, it's nearly instant.

What happens to leads that never respond?

The system sends the full sequence, then stops. You can review them in your lead list and decide if you want to reach out differently, or let them sit until they circle back.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Yes. It doesn't replace your dispatch, your scheduling, or your crew communication—it just makes sure every lead gets touched consistently while your team handles the rest.
