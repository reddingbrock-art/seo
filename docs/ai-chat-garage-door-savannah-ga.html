FAQ

Chat Widget Questions for Savannah Garage Door Businesses

Will the chat widget actually sound like my business?

Yes. We interview you about how you talk to customers, your service offerings, and your tone—then the chat reflects that. It's your voice, not a generic bot.

What happens when the chat can't answer something?

It's designed to handle the most common questions—spring replacements, emergency service windows, service area, appointment booking. If something falls outside that, it collects their info and routes it to you for follow-up.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It replaces the problem of missed website inquiries. If you have office staff, they now handle fewer basic questions and more complex scheduling. If you don't, you get a 24/7 front desk for your site.

What hours does the chat widget work?

24/7. Whether someone visits your site at midnight or 6 AM, they get an answer and can book a time that works for you.

How long does setup take?

Start to finish, 10–14 days. One discovery call, we build it, test it with you, and deploy it live.

What if a customer wants to call instead of chat?

The widget includes your phone number. Customers can call anytime. The chat is just another way for them to reach you—some prefer it, and you capture all of them.
