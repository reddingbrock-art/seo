FAQ

Review Automation for HVAC: Common Questions Answered

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. The system only sends requests to customers associated with completed jobs in your system. Every review that comes back is from someone who actually paid you.

What if a customer is unhappy?

You don't ask for reviews from every job—you decide which completions trigger the request. If a customer reported an issue that day, you can skip the automation for that service call.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google, primarily. That's where Tucson customers are searching for HVAC contractors. We focus there because that's where it matters most for your phone.

How does the timing work?

The request goes out the same day the job is marked complete in your system. If you finish at 2 PM, the request is live by 3 PM. Follow-ups happen automatically over the next week if they don't respond.

What if I already have a review process?

Most manual processes slip—someone forgets, timing's off, or it takes three reminders to actually send it. Automation makes your current intention actually happen every time, consistently.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

You'll see significantly more reviews than you're getting now, but the real number depends on your job volume and current response rate. Most contractors see reviews start appearing within the first two weeks.
