FAQ

Review Automation for Generator Services: Questions Answered

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests only to customers you've actually serviced. Google catches fake reviews fast and tanks your credibility. We don't go there.

What if a customer is unhappy?

They still get asked, same as everyone else. That's actually good—you find out about problems before they become public. You respond, make it right, and show future customers you stand behind your work.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google, primarily. That's where your customers search for generator services and where Google shows star ratings in local results. We focus there because that's where it counts.

How does the timing work?

The request goes out the same day the job completes, when they're still thinking about the service. Most responses come in within 48 hours. Your review count updates weekly.

What if I already have a review process?

We take over and systemize it. If you're sending manual requests now, this eliminates that work entirely. If you're doing nothing, we build it from zero.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Depends on your volume—if you're doing 20 jobs a month, you're potentially capturing 15-18 new reviews instead of 2-3. The automation significantly increases your request response rate compared to leaving it to chance.
