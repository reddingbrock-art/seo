FAQ

Common Questions About Junk Removal Review Automation in Memphis

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. The system sends requests only to customers after their job is actually complete. You only get reviews from people who hired you.

What if a customer is unhappy?

Unhappy customers won't leave you a review just because the request arrives. If someone had a real problem, you'll handle it the same way you do now—directly. The automation doesn't suppress or filter feedback.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google, Yelp, Facebook, and other review platforms where your customers are looking. The system routes requests to the ones that matter most for your business.

How does the timing work?

You tell us when to send. Most owners prefer within a few hours of job completion—while the work is fresh. You control the timing.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll incorporate whatever you're already doing and automate the parts that are falling through the cracks. If you have no process, we build one that fits your operation.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

It depends on your job volume and how many customers actually see the request. Most owners see significantly more reviews within the first 30 days than they were getting before—just because the ask is now consistent.
