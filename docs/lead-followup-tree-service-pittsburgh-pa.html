FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions About Lead Follow-Up for Tree Service

How many follow-ups does it send?

It depends on your preferences. Most tree service owners use a 3–5 message sequence over 7–10 days. The first goes out immediately, then spaced out to stay top-of-mind without being pushy.

Can I customize what it says?

Completely. We write the scripts together so they sound like you, not like an automated system. You approve every message before it goes live.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can unsubscribe anytime, and they're removed immediately. Compliance is built in—no gray area.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Within 5–10 minutes of the lead submitting their information. The faster you respond, the higher your conversion rate.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They're tagged in your system so you can see who went cold. Some owners circle back manually after 30 days; others move on. It's your call.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Yes. It integrates with whatever tools you're already using—your CRM, your scheduling app, your website. It supplements your team, doesn't replace it.
