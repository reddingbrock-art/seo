FAQ

AI Chat for Painting Companies: Questions About the System

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. During discovery, we learn how you talk to customers, what matters to you, what jobs you take. The AI uses your words, knows your service areas, and answers like someone from your team would.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It says so and captures the question. You get notified and it goes to your inbox. The lead doesn't bounce around confused—you follow up from a position of strength because the AI already qualified them.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. If you have a receptionist, the AI handles calls when they're busy or it's after hours. If you don't, the AI becomes your first line. Either way, you're never missing bids again.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. Calls at 6 AM, calls at midnight—they all get answered the same way. You only hear from the ones worth your time.

How long does setup take?

Live in 10-14 days. One discovery call, we build it, you review it, we launch it. No months of implementation. You're taking calls through the system before your next busy season hits.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

Most won't ask. The ones who do—the AI doesn't hide it, just moves them forward to get what they need. It says something like, 'I'm the automated scheduling system. Let me make sure I get your job details right so the team can help you.' Transparency beats surprise every time.
