FAQ

Google Review Automation Questions for Seattle Painting Contractors

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests only to customers whose jobs you've actually completed. Google's review system verifies that they're real customers. Real jobs, real reviews.

What if a customer is unhappy?

The request goes out to everyone who finished a job. If someone leaves a negative review, you handle it like you normally would—by responding and fixing the issue. We're not filtering who gets asked.

Which platforms does this work on?

This system focuses on Google Reviews, which is where your customers are searching and where it matters most for local paint work.

How does the timing work?

The review request goes out the same day your crew marks the job complete in your system. No delay, no manual step. Timing is automatic.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll integrate with what you're doing. If you've got something working, we build on top of it. If you're starting from scratch, we set it up from zero.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Most painting businesses see a significant increase once requests are getting out consistently. You're capturing reviews that would've happened anyway—you're just asking at the right moment instead of not asking at all.
