FAQ

Google Review Automation FAQs for Dallas Tree Service Owners

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We're only sending review requests to customers you've actually worked for. Google's system verifies all reviews—fake ones don't stick, and you don't want them anyway. We're asking real customers for real feedback.

What if a customer leaves a bad review?

That's on you to handle, not us. We just send the request. Bad reviews are actually useful intel—they tell you what went wrong. You can respond publicly and fix it. Our job is making sure you get asked; what people say is up to them.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google is where it counts for tree service. That's where customers search for you, and that's where reviews move the needle. We focus there because it's where your revenue lives.

How does the timing work?

The request goes out within hours of job completion—or whenever you mark a job as done in your system. You can adjust the timing so it hits during business hours or whenever your customer is most likely to respond.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll build around what you're doing. If your crew is already asking, we can sync the automation to avoid double-asking. If you've got nothing, we start from scratch. Either way, the goal is consistency—no more hit-or-miss requests.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Depends on your job volume and how many customers you're actually capturing contact info for. Most owners see a significant jump because they're finally asking systematically. One month you're getting 0–2 reviews; six months in, you're getting 4–8 or more if you're running high volume.
