FAQ

HVAC Automation in Charleston: Your Questions Answered

What exactly gets automated?

Call answering, appointment booking, text reminders, and review requests. Anything that doesn't require a technician in the field gets handled by the system. Your dispatcher still makes the final call on job details—the system just removes the friction.

Do I need technical skills to run this?

No. You check your dashboard and respond to what's there. If you can check email, you can use this. Your dispatcher might spend the first few days getting used to where things are, but it's not complicated.

What if my team resists new tools?

Most resistance disappears after day one because the system makes their job easier, not harder. Fewer calls are missed. Fewer customers are frustrated. Your team has breathing room instead of panic.

How is this different from buying software myself?

You're buying done-for-you, not DIY. We build it, configure it for your workflows, and keep it running. You don't hire someone to learn it or spend three months figuring it out.

What does the setup process look like?

We take a call with you and your dispatcher to map out how you work now. We build the system on our end. You test it. Then it's live. The whole thing takes about two weeks.

What happens if something breaks after launch?

We're on call. If a call doesn't route right or a form isn't working, you reach out and we fix it. It's part of keeping your system running.
