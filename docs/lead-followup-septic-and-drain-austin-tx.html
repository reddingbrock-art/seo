FAQ

Lead Follow-Up System FAQs for Septic & Drain Owners

How many follow-ups does it send?

We customize this based on what works for your business. Most septic and drain companies see best results with 3-5 touches spread across a few days. We'll set it up and adjust based on what your leads respond to.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. We write the messages based on your voice and business, so they sound like you, not a robot. If you want to tweak anything after go-live, we handle that.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can opt out with a single reply, and we stop contacting them immediately. It's built in and compliant.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Usually within minutes of the lead coming in. The system doesn't wait for your team to notice—it's instant.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in the follow-up sequence until it completes. After that, you'll see them marked as non-responsive so you know which ones are worth manual outreach later.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Yes. It sits on top of whatever you're already doing—doesn't replace anything, just fills in the gaps where leads are falling through.
