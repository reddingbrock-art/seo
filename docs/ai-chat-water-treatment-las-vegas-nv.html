FAQ

Questions About AI Chat for Your Water Treatment Business

Will the chat widget actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on how you talk to customers, what you service, and what matters to your market. It answers like your team would, not like a generic bot.

What happens when the chat can't answer something?

It knows when it's outside its lane and escalates to you or your team with full context. The visitor doesn't get stuck—they get help.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It replaces the problem of not being able to talk to everyone. Your team still handles the real work. The chat just makes sure nobody's invisible.

What hours does the chat widget work?

24/7. Your website's open all the time—so is the chat. You decide when it escalates to a person or takes a message.

How long does setup take?

We go live in 10–14 days. One discovery call, a few days of build, then it's on your site. Most of that time is us working, not you.

What if a customer wants to call instead of chat?

The chat has your phone number and hours. If they want to call, they can. The widget's not replacing your phone—it's filling the gap when the phone doesn't work.
