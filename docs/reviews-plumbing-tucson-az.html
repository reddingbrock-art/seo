FAQ

Review Automation for Plumbing: Common Questions Answered

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. The system sends review requests only to customers you've actually completed work for. Google flags and removes fake reviews—this isn't that. You're asking real customers for honest feedback.

What if a customer is unhappy?

You handle it directly. If a customer reaches out with a complaint or concern, you address it before they post publicly. The system just makes sure the ask happens at the right moment. Bad jobs don't suddenly turn into good reviews because you asked faster.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google primarily. We send requests that link directly to your Google Business profile, which is where customers actually search for plumbers in Tucson anyway.

How does the timing work?

Your crew marks a job complete in your dispatch system. The system waits a few hours (so the customer isn't bothered while you're still on the job), then sends a review request. You can adjust the timing or the message to match your workflow.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll look at what you're doing now and either integrate with it or replace it with something more reliable. Most shops have a process on paper that doesn't actually execute consistently—we fix that part.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

It depends on your job volume and current ask rate. Most plumbing shops see significantly more reviews once requests start going out automatically and consistently. A crew doing 5-6 jobs a day will see a noticeable difference within the first month.
