FAQ

Automation for Electrical Contractors in Pittsburgh: Your Questions Answered

What exactly gets automated?

Call answering and qualification, form submissions to scheduling, appointment confirmations via text, and review requests after job completion. Your team stops doing manual intake work and starts focusing on production and service delivery.

Do I need technical skills to run this?

No. Your team doesn't manage the system—we do. They log in to see scheduled jobs and customer info. It works the same way your current dispatch software does, except leads show up pre-qualified and booked.

What if my team resists new tools?

This one reduces their workload instead of adding to it. They stop answering phones and manually scheduling. They just see jobs appear in their calendar. Most teams are on board within the first week because their day got easier.

How is this different from buying software myself?

You could buy a platform and spend weeks configuring it, training staff, and figuring out what you don't know. We do all that for you. You own the outcome—more leads booked, faster response times, less admin burden. The technical setup is on us.

What does the setup process look like?

One discovery call to understand your current process. We build and test for 1-2 weeks. You run parallel with your old process for a week so nothing slips. Then it goes full live. Total time to operation: 10-14 days.

What happens if something breaks after launch?

We monitor it 24/7 and handle all technical support and fixes. If a call doesn't route properly or a text doesn't send, we catch it and fix it before you even notice. We're responsible for keeping it running.
