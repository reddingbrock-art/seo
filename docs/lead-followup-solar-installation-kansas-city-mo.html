FAQ

Common Questions About Lead Follow-Up for Solar Installation

How many follow-ups does it send?

We typically set up 4-6 touches over 10-14 days depending on how aggressive you want to be. Text first, then email, then a check-in call if they've shown interest but haven't booked. You control the sequence.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. We write it to sound like your business and your team. We don't do generic solar scripts—every message comes from your voice, with your selling points and your call-to-action.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can opt out anytime with a single click or text reply. The system respects that immediately and stops all contact. It's built in from day one—no compliance headaches.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Instantly for form submissions. If a lead calls, the text goes out within 2-3 minutes once they're logged. Most leads get a response before they've even finished talking to the next company.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in your pipeline on a longer cycle so they don't disappear. If they engage again in the future—even 60 days later—the system recognizes them and re-engages. Dead leads stay accessible if they warm up.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Yes. It plugs into whatever you're already doing—your CRM, your scheduling, your dispatcher. We're not replacing your system, we're automating the part that's manual and slow.
