FAQ

Lead Follow-Up Questions for St. Louis Pest Control Owners

How many follow-ups does it send?

As many as you want. Most pest control owners run 3–5 touches over 7–14 days before a lead goes cold. We build it to match your timeline.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. You tell us what your message is—how you position yourself, your service areas, your offer. We write it into the system.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can opt out at any time. We respect that. The system logs it, and we don't send them anything else.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Within minutes of the lead landing. You set the timing—immediate, 5 minutes later, or whenever works best for your operation.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in the system until your sequence runs its course. You can see them anytime and follow up manually if you want. Nothing is lost.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. It integrates with how you take leads now. We're not replacing anything—we're automating the part that's been falling through the cracks.
