FAQ

Review Automation FAQs for Indianapolis Flooring Contractors

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests only to customers whose jobs you actually completed. Real customers, real experiences, real reviews.

What if a customer is unhappy?

The system sends to everyone post-job. If someone had a bad experience, they may leave an honest negative review—and that's the point. You get real feedback, not filtered praise. Most unhappy customers never leave reviews anyway; you're just asking the satisfied ones who will.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google, Yelp, Home Advisor, and others. We can set it up to request reviews on the platforms that matter most to your flooring business in Indianapolis.

How does the timing work?

You tell us when a job is complete—either we pull that from your scheduling system or you log it manually. The request goes out at the optimal moment based on industry data. Usually within 24-48 hours of completion.

What if I already have a review process?

If you're doing it manually, this just removes that burden and makes sure it actually happens every time. If you're not asking at all, we're starting from scratch. Either way, we build around what you've got.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

It depends on your job volume and how many customers were satisfied but never got asked. Most flooring contractors see a noticeable uptick within the first month once the system is live.
