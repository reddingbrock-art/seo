FAQ

AI Chat for Water Treatment — Common Questions

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We listen to how you talk to customers — your language, your pace, your concern for detail — and write the AI's personality to match. It sounds like someone from your company, not a robot.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It says so, captures the customer's info and question, and flags it for you immediately. You call them back with the answer. No lost lead.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It replaces the moment your receptionist can't pick up. It handles call intake, scheduling, and qualification. Your team stays in the loop and owns the relationship.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. Calls and texts coming in at midnight get answered the same way as calls at 9 AM. You set your response hours for human follow-ups if you want them different.

How long does setup take?

10-14 days from discovery to live. We need your calendar system access and about 30 minutes of your time to map your intake process. That's it.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

We're transparent about it. The AI introduces itself clearly. Customers don't mind — they just want to be heard and scheduled fast. Speed and accuracy matter way more than pretending to be human.
