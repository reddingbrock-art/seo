FAQ

Lead Follow-Up Questions for HVAC Companies

How many follow-ups does it send?

Typically 3-5 touches across the first 7 days, spaced so you're contacting leads at different times without feeling pushy. You control the exact sequence.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. Every message reflects your voice and your business specifics. We help you write it so it sounds like your team reaching back out, not a generic bot.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can opt out anytime, and the system stops. You're always in compliance with how they want to be contacted.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Within minutes of the lead coming in. You can set it to go immediately or wait until business hours if you prefer.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in your system and can be re-contacted during peak seasons or added to a different nurture sequence. No lead gets permanently lost.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. It works with whatever CRM, form provider, or phone system you're using now. We integrate with what you've already got.
