FAQ

Lead Follow-Up Questions for HVAC Owners

How many follow-ups does the system send to each lead?

We customize the sequence to your business. Most HVAC shops use 3–5 touchpoints: instant auto-response, qualification attempt, reminder text, and re-engagement if they don't book. We adjust based on what converts best for your market.

Can I customize what the AI says to my customers?

Absolutely. We write the scripts around your brand voice and service offerings. You review and approve everything before it goes live. You own the conversation.

What happens if a customer asks to stop getting messages?

They can opt out with one text or voice command. The system respects it immediately and logs it. You stay compliant and never annoy a customer who's trying to unsubscribe.

Does this integrate with the CRM or software we're already using?

Yes. The system lives in GoHighLevel, which syncs with most dispatch and accounting platforms—ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro, your email, your calendar. If it doesn't integrate directly, we build the bridge.

How long does it take to get live?

10–14 days. We handle the setup, testing, and training. You just approve the scripts and point your phone number and forms at the system. Your first automated lead response happens the day we flip the switch.

What if a lead needs to talk to a human right away?

They can press a button to reach your office or an on-call tech immediately. The system doesn't block humans—it frees your team to answer the calls that matter instead of playing phone tag with cold leads.
