FAQ

AI Chat Agent Questions for HVAC Business Owners

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on your language, your service offerings, your neighborhood focus, and how you actually talk to customers. It's not a generic bot—it's your business answering the phone.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It recognizes the limit, acknowledges the question, and hands it off to your team with full context. A tech or dispatcher gets a note with the complete conversation so they can follow up informed.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It replaces the moment when the phone rings and nobody answers. Your receptionist or dispatcher handles the complex calls, the callbacks, and the relationship building—the AI just makes sure nothing falls through the cracks.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. Nights, weekends, holidays—whenever a customer reaches out, they get an answer. You can configure when it books appointments versus just collects information for morning follow-up.

How long does setup take?

Live in 10–14 days from your first call. Discovery, configuration, and testing happen fast because we've done this for HVAC teams before. There's no long onboarding process.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

It doesn't hide. If they ask, the AI tells them straight up. Most customers don't care—they just want an answer. The ones who want a human get transferred fast. Honesty builds trust.
