FAQ

Lead Follow-Up System FAQs for Pool Service Owners

How many follow-ups does it send?

Typically 3–4 touches over the first week: one same-day text, one email the next morning, and a final reminder a few days out. We customize the sequence based on what works best for your business.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. We write everything in your voice and tailor it to your service area, pricing, and brand. You approve the language before it goes live.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can opt out, and we honor that immediately. It's built into every message—no compliance issues.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Usually within 5–15 minutes of the lead submitting a form or texting in. The goal is to be first, not perfect.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in your system. You can see the full history of who got contacted and when, so you can decide if you want to reach out manually later.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. It's designed to catch leads the moment they come in while you're running jobs. Your team keeps doing what they're doing—this handles the waiting gap.
