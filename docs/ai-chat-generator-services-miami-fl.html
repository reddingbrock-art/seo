FAQ

AI Chat Widget Questions for Miami Generator Service Owners

Will the chat widget actually sound like my business?

Yes. We write every response based on how your team talks to customers. It mirrors your language, your tone, your process—not generic bot speak.

What happens when the chat can't answer something?

It tells the customer you'll follow up personally and collects their info. Your team gets the message and reaches out during business hours.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It replaces the part of your receptionist's day spent answering 'Do you service my area?' and 'What's your availability?' The complex calls still come to your team.

What hours does the chat widget work?

24/7. It's always on your site, capturing leads at 2 a.m. or 3 p.m. on Sunday. You decide which messages get responses immediately and which trigger follow-ups during business hours.

How long does setup take?

10-14 days from discovery to live. Most of that is us building and you reviewing. Installation on your site takes less than an hour.

What if a customer wants to call instead of chat?

The widget includes your phone number. Some people will always prefer calling—that option is there. But most visitors who won't call will chat, and that's where you're gaining leads.
