Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on your language, your neighborhoods, your pricing tiers, and your service type. It sounds like someone from your dispatch team who knows your market.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It collects the customer's info, confirms the appointment or callback window, and flags it for a human to review. No customer gets left hanging.

It replaces the need to hire one. If you already have someone, they move to higher-value work instead of answering 'do you service my area?' for the 50th time.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. It answers at 2 AM just as fast as 2 PM. Hurricane season emergencies don't care about business hours—your system doesn't either.

How long does setup take?

10-14 days from discovery call to live. Most of that's us building and refining. You're typically taking calls from the system by week two.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?
