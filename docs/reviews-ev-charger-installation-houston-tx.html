Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send review requests only to customers you've actually completed jobs for. Google knows the difference between real and fabricated reviews—we're just automating the ask to real customers who've already hired you.

What if a customer is unhappy?

You still want to know. If someone's upset, they're more likely to leave a negative review if you don't hear from them. Early contact gives you a chance to fix it before it lands online.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google Reviews is the one that matters for EV installers in Houston. That's where customers search, compare, and decide. We focus there.

How does the timing work?

Requests go out the same day the job's marked complete in your system. Customers typically respond within 24-48 hours. You'll start seeing reviews land almost immediately.

What if I already have a review process?

Most manual processes leak. If you're calling or texting, you're also forgetting, getting interrupted, or missing the window. This replaces that and runs consistently every single day.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

It depends on your volume and how many customers you're currently asking. Most teams see significantly higher response rates once requests are sent the same day the work's done instead of days later.
