FAQ

Lead Follow-Up Automation Questions for Home Security Companies

How many follow-ups does it send?

That's your call. Most security companies send 3-5 touches over 5-7 days. We build what matches your sales cycle, not a template everyone uses.

Can I customize what it says?

Completely. The messages come from you and sound like you. You approve the copy before any lead sees it.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can opt out with one click or text STOP. The system respects it immediately and flags them in your CRM so you know not to call manually either.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Within 60 seconds of the lead coming in. If it's outside your business hours, it goes out the first thing the next morning at the time you set.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in your follow-up sequence for as long as you've set it. After that, they move to a "dormant" list you can circle back to seasonally or manually if you want.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Yes. It plugs into whatever CRM or system you're using now. Your team keeps doing what they're doing—this just fills in the gaps where leads used to fall through.
