FAQ

Electrical Business Automation: Questions Answered

What exactly gets automated?

Lead capture from all sources (phone, text, web form, email), instant customer response, job assignment to field crew, appointment scheduling, and review requests after completion. Everything that makes a lead move from 'inquiry' to 'scheduled' gets faster and stops relying on someone remembering to follow up.

Do I need technical skills to run this?

No. If you can use your phone and dispatch software, you can use this. We handle the setup, integrations, and troubleshooting. Your team interacts with a simple interface—like texting or checking a to-do list.

What if my team resists new tools?

Resistance usually stops once they see the time it saves. We build this specifically around how your team works now, not forcing them to change their habits. Most teams are sold within the first week when they realize they're not answering the same question twice.

How is this different from buying software myself?

You could buy five tools and spend two months connecting them yourself. Or we do that for you, configure it for your actual workflow, train your team, and support it. You get a working system in under two weeks instead of a project that never quite comes together.

What does the setup process look like?

Discovery call, then we build and test everything while you watch. We connect your phone line, your dispatch system, and your customer database. By day 14, you're live and your team knows how to use it. Zero training burden on you.

What happens if something breaks after launch?

We stay on support for the first month and beyond. If a lead doesn't route correctly or a customer response fails, we catch it and fix it. You're never left managing a broken system on your own.
