Automation for Painters in Philadelphia: Common Questions

What exactly gets automated?

Inbound calls get answered or logged. Form submissions get instant replies. Text and email follow-ups happen automatically. Review requests launch after job completion. Your calendar syncs with availability. You still sell, estimate, and close—we just handle the first four touches so nothing falls through.

Do I need technical skills to run this?

No. You don't build anything. We set it up, train you on what you need to know (usually 15 minutes), and you run it from your phone or computer like you normally would. If something needs adjusting, you tell us—we handle the backend.

What if my team resists new tools?

Most crews don't notice because this system takes work off their plate—they're not managing inbound volume anymore. Your office staff (if you have any) usually see it as relief. We also onboard everyone on the first day so there's no mystery around how it works.

How is this different from buying software myself?

You'd have to connect the pieces yourself, figure out the logic, train your team, and troubleshoot when something breaks. We do all of that. You're paying for the done-for-you setup and ongoing support, not just a license. That's why it works in 10–14 days instead of three months.

What does the setup process look like?

We gather your phone number, email, booking links, and form details. We ask about your crew schedule and availability windows. Then we build the flows on our end and test with you. Once you approve, it goes live. The whole process is 1–2 weeks of light back-and-forth.

What happens if something breaks after launch?

You reach out. We fix it. That's included. If a call doesn't route right or a text goes to the wrong number, we troubleshoot and get it working again. You're not managing this alone.
