How many follow-ups does it send?

Typically 3–5 touches over 14 days, depending on what you want. Could be more or fewer—we customize it for your business.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. We write the messages based on how you talk to customers. They sound like they're coming from you, not a robot.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can opt out anytime with one click. We comply with their request immediately and remove them from the sequence.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Within 5–10 minutes of the lead coming in. Sometimes faster depending on how they reached you.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in your CRM so you can re-engage them later—maybe they'll be ready in a few weeks. Nothing is lost.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. If you're already taking calls and booking jobs, this just plugs in and catches the ones you'd normally miss.
