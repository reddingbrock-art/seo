FAQ

Review Automation FAQs for Columbia Paving Contractors

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests only to customers who actually worked with you. Reviews come from real jobs your crew completed.

What if a customer is unhappy?

The system requests reviews from everyone, but unhappy customers rarely leave reviews when asked directly—they just don't respond. You're not filtering anyone out; you're just giving satisfied customers the easiest way to share.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google Reviews is where your customers look first. That's where this lives.

How does the timing work?

Requests go out automatically after a job is marked complete. Your crew finishes Friday, the customer gets a request over the weekend when they're thinking about the work they just had done.

What if I already have a review process?

If you're already asking manually, this automates what you're doing and removes the steps you're skipping. If you don't have one, we build it from scratch.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Most businesses see significantly more reviews within the first 60 days because the ask is consistent and timely. The exact number depends on your job volume and completion rate.
