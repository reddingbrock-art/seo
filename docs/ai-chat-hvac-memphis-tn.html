FAQ

AI Chat for HVAC in Memphis: Questions Answered

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on your service area, your pricing structure, your team's availability, and the tone you want. It answers like someone who knows your neighborhood and your business, not a call center script.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It escalates. The customer gets offered a callback from your team within a timeframe you set, or transferred to a live person if you want that option. Nothing falls through the cracks.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. If you have a receptionist, the AI handles overflow and after-hours calls. If you don't, the AI becomes your front-end intake system—your team spends less time on phones, more time on actual work.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. Nights, weekends, holidays. An emergency AC call at midnight gets answered immediately, not tomorrow morning.

How long does setup take?

Discovery to live is typically 10-14 days. We need a few details from you, and we handle the rest.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

They find out because you tell them, or because they ask. Some customers prefer it—they get instant answers and no wait. Most don't care as long as the job gets scheduled and a tech shows up. Transparency builds trust, not the other way around.
