FAQ

Common Questions About Review Automation for Sacramento Water Treatment Businesses

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send review requests only to actual customers whose jobs are completed in your system. We can't control whether they leave a review, but we're asking real people to share their real experience.

What if a customer is unhappy?

The system asks for feedback just like you would. If they had a bad experience, they're not obligated to leave a review. This doesn't change your customer service—it just makes sure happy customers actually get asked.

Which platforms does this work on?

We integrate with your scheduling and CRM system to pull completed jobs and trigger requests. Google Reviews is the primary focus, though the system can be configured for other platforms too.

How does the timing work?

Requests can be set to go out the same day the job completes, or you can customize the delay. Most businesses see better results when the request lands within 24 hours.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll work around what's already working. If you're getting decent review velocity manually, we can still layer in automation for the jobs that slip through the cracks. If your manual process is spotty, we replace it entirely.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

That depends on your job volume and current review-ask rate. Most businesses see a significant jump because they're finally asking consistently at the right moment. Some go from getting 2-3 reviews monthly to 8-12. Your mileage will vary.
