FAQ

Common Questions About Lead Follow-Up Automation for Virginia Beach Restoration Crews

How many follow-ups does it send?

That depends on your business. We typically set up a sequence of 3–5 touches over 7–10 days, but you decide the cadence and messaging. Some owners want more aggressive follow-up; others prefer slower. We build it around what works for your crew.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. You control the message templates. Every text, email, and call script reflects your voice. We handle the timing and delivery; you own the words.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

The system respects that immediately. They're removed from the sequence, and you'll never contact them again on that number or email.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Typically within 5–15 minutes of the lead coming in. If it's a phone call, you get an answer attempt right away. Form submissions trigger a message almost instantly.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They complete the follow-up sequence and then stay in your system tagged as 'inactive.' If they call in six months, you'll have their history and can pick up where you left off.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. It feeds leads to your crew the same way—through text, email, or your existing dispatch tool. It just makes sure nothing falls through the cracks while they're busy.
