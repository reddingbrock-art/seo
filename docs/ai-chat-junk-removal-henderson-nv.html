FAQ

AI Chat Widget for Henderson Junk Removal: FAQs

Will the chat widget actually sound like my business?

Yes. We listen to how you talk to customers, learn your service areas and job types, and build conversations that match your voice. It won't sound like a robot or a generic chatbot.

What happens when the chat can't answer something?

It escalates to you. If a customer asks something outside the widget's training—a weird question, a job edge case—it captures their contact info and hands it off so you can follow up directly.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It does the job your receptionist can't do: stay on your website 24/7 and capture visitors who won't call. It frees your team to focus on scheduling and customer relationships, not fielding initial inquiries.

What hours does the chat widget work?

Around the clock. People search for junk removal at midnight, on weekends, on holidays. Your chat is always there. You respond during your business hours.

How long does setup take?

10-14 days from kickoff call to live. We handle the technical setup. You just need to tell us how you work.

What if a customer wants to call instead of chat?

The widget includes your phone number and they can call anytime. But most won't—they'll book through chat because it's faster and they get instant confirmation.
