FAQ

AI Chat and Phone Automation for Commercial Cleaning: Questions Answered

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on your language, your service descriptions, your tone. When a prospect talks to it, they're getting a real conversation—not a robot menu. They don't realize it's not a person until we tell them.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It escalates to you via text or email in real time. You respond once, and the AI learns that answer for next time. No dropped calls, no awkward silence.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. If you have a receptionist, this frees them from repetitive calls and lets them focus on customer follow-up, quality control, or operations. If you don't have one, this does the intake work so you don't have to.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. It answers calls at midnight, takes bookings on Sunday, responds to chat at 6 a.m. You control the availability calendar—so if you're closed Sundays, it only books Monday through Saturday.

How long does setup take?

We go live in 10 to 14 days from your first call. You're taking qualified bookings while your competitors are still hitting voicemail.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

It tells them upfront: 'This is our automated booking line.' Transparency builds trust. They still get their question answered, their job booked, and they move forward. No friction.
