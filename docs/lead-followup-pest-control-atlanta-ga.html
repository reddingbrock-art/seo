FAQ

Common Questions About Lead Follow-Up Automation for Pest Control

How many follow-ups does it send?

The sequence runs three touches by default—an immediate response, a timed follow-up 24 hours later, and a review request post-job. We customize it based on how your sales cycle works.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. We write the messages to match your voice, your brand, and your process. You review and approve everything before it goes live. It should sound like you're sending them.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can opt out at any time, and the system respects that. You stay compliant. They're removed from all future sequences.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Immediately. If a form comes in at 2 p.m., the prospect gets an acknowledgment within seconds—not hours. Phone calls get acknowledged just as fast.

What happens to leads that never respond?

The system runs through the sequence, then stops. They stay in your system so you can follow up manually later if you want, but they don't get spammed with endless messages.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. It sits on top of whatever you're doing now—your CRM, your scheduling tool, your phone system. We integrate it into your workflow, not replace it.
