FAQ

Review Automation for Fire & Water Restoration: Questions Answered

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. The system only sends requests to customers you've actually served. We're asking real people who completed jobs with you to share their real experience.

What if a customer is unhappy?

The request goes to them like everyone else. If they leave a negative review, that's honest feedback—and it's far better to see it and respond than to have them tell others privately. Most unhappy customers won't leave a review at all if never asked.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google is the priority—that's where most customers look. We can also push requests to other platforms depending on your setup. We'll walk you through what makes sense for your market during the call.

How does the timing work?

Requests send automatically on the day you mark a job complete, or on a schedule you choose. The goal is to catch customers while satisfaction is highest.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll look at what you're doing and either enhance it or replace it entirely with automation. Most owners find they're asking too infrequently or at the wrong time—we fix that.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

It depends on your job volume and how many customers you're currently asking. Most restoration companies see a significant jump in the first 90 days once automation is live—often 2-3x more reviews than before, assuming you're doing 15+ jobs monthly.
