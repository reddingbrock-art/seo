Automation FAQs for Fire & Water Restoration Companies

What exactly gets automated?

Incoming calls, web forms, and messages get captured automatically and routed to your dispatcher with job details pre-filled. Customers can confirm availability and book jobs directly. Follow-up review requests go out after completion without manual work.

Do I need technical skills to run this?

No. Your dispatcher or admin logs in, sees all inbound leads in one dashboard, and responds the same way they do now—just faster and without missing anything.

What if my team resists new tools?

The system lives in your existing workflow. Your team isn't learning new software—they're using one unified inbox instead of juggling calls, texts, and forms. It makes their job easier, not harder.
