FAQ

Lead Follow-Up System Questions for Indianapolis Window Contractors

How many follow-ups does it send?

Typically three touches: a call, then a text if no answer, then an email. We customize the sequence based on your process.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. We write the initial messaging and you approve it. You can change scripts anytime without rebuilding anything.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They're removed from the follow-up sequence immediately. The system respects opt-outs.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Within 5 minutes of form submission or phone intake. Most calls connect before your team even gets the notification.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in a secondary nurture sequence. You can reach out manually later, or they re-enter if they show new interest.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. It catches leads your team would otherwise miss. Everything integrates with your current CRM and calendar.
