FAQ

AI Chat Agent FAQs for EV Charger Installation Businesses

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on your actual tone, your service details, and your process. When it answers, customers are talking to your business—not some generic bot. They won't know the difference until you want them to.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It tells the customer it's getting your team to follow up, collects their info, and sends you a notification immediately. It never pretends to know something it doesn't. That's when you jump in.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. If you have a receptionist, this covers the calls they can't get to and handles the repetitive questions. If you don't have one, this means you never need to hire one. Either way, you're not paying for labor to do the same thing over and over.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. It answers at 2 AM, 8 AM, and Sunday morning. You set when it books appointments—maybe your crew doesn't work weekends, so it won't schedule Sundays. But the AI is always listening and always working.

How long does setup take?

Discovery to live is 10-14 days. You spend about 30 minutes in a call with us, then we build while you keep running your business. No months of onboarding or technical setup from you.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

Some will. That's fine. You built an AI because you're professional and you're keeping up. Most customers don't care if it's AI or human—they just care that someone answered and got them scheduled. Being transparent about it is actually good for your brand.
