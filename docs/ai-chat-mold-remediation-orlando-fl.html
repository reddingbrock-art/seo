FAQ

AI Chat Widget for Mold Remediation: Questions & Answers

Will the chat widget actually sound like my business?

Yes. We write the chat personality based on how you talk to customers, your service philosophy, and the tone your prospects expect from a local Orlando mold company. It's your voice, not a generic corporate chatbot.

What happens when the chat can't answer something?

The chat tells the prospect you'll follow up with specifics and collects their contact info. The lead goes straight to your team so you can take it from there. No dropped conversations.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It handles the visitors your receptionist never gets to—people messaging your site at 10 PM, during lunch, or when all your lines are busy. It qualifies them and gets them in your pipeline. Your team still closes.

What hours does the chat widget work?

24/7. It answers questions and collects leads around the clock. You decide when your team follows up, but prospects can reach you anytime.

How long does setup take?

10-14 days from discovery call to live. We handle the writing, integration, and testing. You don't touch the technical side.

What if a customer wants to call instead of chat?

They can. The chat includes your phone number and encourages them to call if that's easier. But most won't—that's the whole point. This captures the ones who prefer to message first.
