FAQ

Common Questions About Review Automation for Roofing

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests only to customers whose jobs you actually completed. Real work, real people, real reviews—or no reviews. That's it.

What if a customer is unhappy?

They don't have to leave a review. The request is an invitation, not a demand. Unhappy customers usually don't respond—they just don't leave anything. We're not filtering or suppressing anything; we're asking at the right moment when satisfaction is highest.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google, Facebook, and industry-specific review sites. We focus on Google first because that's where homeowners search for roofing companies in Atlanta, but the system adapts to wherever your leads come from.

How does the timing work?

You mark the job complete in your system. Within 2 hours, the review request goes out via text or email. The sooner the ask, the higher the response rate—that's what we've built for.

What if I already have a review process?

We replace the manual part—the reminding, the chasing, the forgetting. If you've been asking for reviews but it's inconsistent, we automate the consistency. If you haven't been asking at all, we start the machine.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

That depends on your job volume and current response rate. Most roofing companies see significantly more reviews within 60 days because the requests are happening consistently, on time, every single day. The exact number is yours to discover.
