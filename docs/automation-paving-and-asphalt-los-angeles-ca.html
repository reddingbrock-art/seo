What exactly gets automated?

Incoming calls and form submissions get answered immediately. Customers get qualified through a short conversation or automated questions. If they're a fit, they're booked directly into your calendar. You focus on the job; the system handles the capture.

Do I need technical skills to run this?

No. Your office manager or whoever handles scheduling already knows what to do. We set it up so you're managing appointments, not troubleshooting software.

What if my team resists new tools?

Most resistance comes from adding more complexity. This removes steps from their day. They spend less time on the phone and scheduling, more time on actual work. We handle the training and stay accessible if questions come up.

How is this different from buying software myself?

You'd buy a platform, figure out how to set it up, integrate it with your phone and calendar, and then troubleshoot when something doesn't work. We do all of that and build the actual process around your business. You get a working system, not a tool.

What does the setup process look like?

Discovery call to understand your flow. We build and configure your system off-site. You get a training session. Then we go live and monitor for the first week. Total time from start to live is 10-14 days.

What happens if something breaks after launch?

You've got direct support. If a lead isn't being routed correctly or your calendar gets out of sync, you reach us and we fix it. This isn't a self-service platform where you're on your own.
