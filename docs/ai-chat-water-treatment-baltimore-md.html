FAQ

AI Chat Agent for Water Treatment: Common Questions

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on your real language, service descriptions, and how your team talks to customers. It uses your company name, your pricing, your scheduling rules. Customers should feel like they're talking to your shop—because they are.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It says so honestly and transfers the call to your team with a full transcript of what the customer already said. No loops, no "I didn't understand that." It knows its limits and gets your people involved when it matters.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It replaces the waiting, the voicemail tag, and the repetitive intake questions. Your team handles the real work—selling, scheduling, and service. The agent just makes sure no call or lead gets lost in the gap.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. It answers calls and web messages at 2 a.m. on Sunday the same way it does at 10 a.m. on Tuesday. You decide if it books appointments for all hours or holds them until your office opens.

How long does setup take?

10 to 14 days from kickoff to go-live. We need about an hour of your time upfront to gather your service details and pricing. The rest is build and testing on our end.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

They'll figure it out pretty fast—and most won't care as long as they get what they need. The agent is honest, helpful, and transfers them to a human if they ask. Transparency builds trust more than pretending it's your nephew.
