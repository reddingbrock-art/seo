No. We send requests only to real customers after their job is complete. Real customers, real reviews—that's it.

They're not going to leave a review if they're unhappy—and that's fine. This system reaches out to people who just finished a job with you. If there's a problem, they'll tell you first.

Google, Yelp, your website—anywhere customers can leave reviews. We target the platforms that matter most to your business.

How does the timing work?

Requests go out automatically after job completion. Customers are most likely to respond when the work is fresh, so timing is built into the system.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll build on what's working and fill in the gaps. Most flooring companies have no process at all—but if you do, we make sure this complements it.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

That depends on your job volume. If you're finishing 20 jobs a month and getting nothing now, you should see a significant increase. We'll show you exactly what to expect during a discovery call.
