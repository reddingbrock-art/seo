FAQ

Lead Follow-Up Questions for Milwaukee Pest Control Owners

How many follow-ups does it send?

That's up to you. Most pest control shops we work with use 3-5 touchpoints over 7-10 days. You decide the sequence, the timing, and when to stop.

Can I customize what it says?

Completely. You write the messages, set the timing, and we plug them in. Your voice, your process, your brand.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can opt out anytime. Once they do, they're out of the sequence immediately. It's automatic and compliant.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Within minutes of the lead coming in. If they submit a form at 2 p.m., they hear from you by 2:05. Before your team even sees the lead sheet.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in your CRM. You can see the full history of follow-ups sent, when they were sent, and what you've tried. You decide if you want to circle back later.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Yes. It plugs into how you work now. You keep your dispatch, your scheduling, your routing. This just makes sure no lead falls through the cracks between the inquiry and the callback.
