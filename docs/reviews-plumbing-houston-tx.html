FAQ

Review Automation FAQs for Plumbing Companies

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We only send requests to customers you've already served. Google's system is smart enough to flag suspicious patterns—and we don't trigger them. Real customers, real work, real reviews.

What if a customer is unhappy?

The system can be set up to redirect negative feedback to you first—giving you a chance to make it right before it lands on Google. Unhappy customers are rare when the work is done well, and this approach respects that.

Which platforms does this work on?

We focus on Google reviews because that's where homeowners search for plumbers. Google local pack results drive most of your calls in Houston.

How does the timing work?

Requests go out 24–48 hours after a job is marked complete. That's the sweet spot—the work's fresh in their mind, but they've had time to see that everything's working.
