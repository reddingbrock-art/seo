FAQ

Review Automation for Plumbers: Common Questions

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests only to customers whose jobs you actually completed. Real customers, real jobs, real feedback. Google's algorithms catch fake reviews fast, and that hurts you more than helps.

What if a customer is unhappy?

They can leave a negative review through any channel. This system doesn't suppress unhappy customers—it just asks satisfied ones to share their experience. If something went wrong, that feedback is valuable.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google is the priority—that's where customers search for plumbers. We can extend to other platforms based on what makes sense for your business.

How does the timing work?

The request goes out automatically when a job is marked complete in your system. The customer gets the ask while the work is still fresh. No manual scheduling, no delays.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll talk about what's working and what isn't. Most processes fail because they depend on someone remembering to act. Ours doesn't. We can supplement or replace what you're doing now.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

It depends on your volume and your customer satisfaction. Most plumbing businesses see a significant increase because they're capturing reviews they're already earning but never asking for. We'll show you the potential during a demo.
