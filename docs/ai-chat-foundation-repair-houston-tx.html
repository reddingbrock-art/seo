FAQ

FAQs: AI Chat for Foundation Repair Companies in Houston

Will the chat widget actually sound like my business?

Yes. We write the chat in your voice—using your language, your service area, your typical questions. It sounds like someone from your office, not a robot.

What happens when the chat can't answer something?

It tells the visitor 'A specialist will get back to you within 2 hours' and logs the conversation so your team sees it immediately. Nothing gets lost.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It removes the routine stuff—'What's your address? When did the crack start?'—so your team focuses on the phone calls that matter and closing jobs faster.

What hours does the chat widget work?

24/7. Visitors can chat at night, weekends, holidays. Urgent inquiries get logged and flagged so your team sees them the moment your office opens.

How long does setup take?

10–14 days from discovery call to going live. Most of that is us training the chat. Your technical lift is minimal.

What if a customer wants to call instead of chat?

The chat offers your phone number, and if they choose to call, they can. We're not forcing anyone into chat—we're just making sure nobody falls through the cracks if they prefer to type.
