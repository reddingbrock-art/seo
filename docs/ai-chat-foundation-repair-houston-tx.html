FAQ

AI Chat Agent Questions for Houston Foundation Repair Owners

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on your language, your process, and your tone. A homeowner talking to your AI should feel like they're talking to someone who knows foundation repair—not a robot reading a script.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It tells the customer it'll get you the answer and transfers the call to you, or takes their number for a callback. It never guesses or makes something up.

Does this replace my receptionist?

It replaces the part of your receptionist's job that's sitting at a desk fielding phone calls. If you have someone managing scheduling, follow-ups, and customer relationships, they still do that—the AI just handles the ringing phone.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. Nights, weekends, holidays—whenever your phone rings, it answers.

How long does setup take?

10–14 days from your discovery call to go-live. We get you trained and live fast so you start capturing calls you're missing now.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

Some do. When they ask, the AI is honest about it. Most customers don't mind—they're just happy someone picked up the phone and understood their problem.
