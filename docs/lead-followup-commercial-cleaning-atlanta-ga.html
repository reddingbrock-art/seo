FAQ

Lead Follow-Up Questions for Atlanta Cleaning Companies

How many follow-ups does it send?

That depends on your preference. We typically recommend 3-5 touches over 7-10 days for cold leads, with more frequent outreach for warm prospects who've already engaged. We customize the sequence to your sales cycle.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. We write the initial messages based on your business, then you approve and refine them. Your leads hear language that matches your voice and your specific service offerings.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can unsubscribe at any time. The system honors those requests immediately and removes them from future sends. Your compliance is automatic.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Within minutes of them submitting a form or missing a call. The goal is to reach them before they call another cleaning company.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in your system. You can review them anytime, re-engage them manually if you want, or let them cycle out after the sequence completes. Nothing gets deleted—everything's logged.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. It plugs into your current phone number, email, forms, and calendar. Your team keeps doing what they do. The system just makes sure no lead falls through the cracks while you're handling operations.
