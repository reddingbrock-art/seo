What exactly gets automated?

Do I need technical skills to run this?

No. You don't configure or maintain anything. Your team logs in, assigns jobs, and dispatches techs. The system handles everything behind the scenes. We handle all the setup and support.

What if my team resists new tools?

Your team won't be learning new software—they'll just see appointments show up on their dispatch board and jobs confirmed. The change is invisible to them. You'll train them once on how to monitor and adjust, and it becomes routine.

How is this different from buying software myself?

You'd buy a license, spend weeks configuring it, train your team, troubleshoot when something breaks, and manage it yourself. We do all of that. You get a working system in 10 days, ongoing support, and someone accountable when something needs to change.

We ask you questions about your current workflow, integrate with your existing tools, test everything with your team, then flip the switch. You'll see it working before it takes over your real calls. The whole process takes 10–14 days from first call to live.

What happens if something breaks after launch?
