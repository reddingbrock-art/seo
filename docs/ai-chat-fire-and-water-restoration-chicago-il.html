FAQ

AI Chat Questions for Chicago Restoration Owners

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on your intake process, your language, how you talk to customers, and your policies. When a customer talks to the chat, they're talking to your company—not a generic bot.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It tells the customer straight up: "I'm not sure about that—let me get someone on the team to answer." Then it flags the question for your team and provides the customer's contact info so you can follow up immediately.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It handles the intake questions and form fills that happen outside business hours or when your team is busy. Your receptionist can focus on scheduling, coordination, and customer service instead of repeating the same ten questions all day.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. It's answering questions at midnight on a Sunday just as well as it does at 2 p.m. on a Tuesday.

How long does setup take?

We deploy live in 10-14 days from our first call. We handle all the setup—you just give us the information we need and review the chat before it goes live.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

Most don't ask. But if they do, the chat tells them: "You're talking with our automated intake system to get your information down and get you scheduled—a real person from our team will follow up." Transparency builds trust, not the opposite.
