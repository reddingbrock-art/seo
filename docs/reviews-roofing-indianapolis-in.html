FAQ

Common Questions About Review Automation for Roofers

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests only to actual customers with completed jobs. Google flags fake reviews and penalizes you for it. We don't do that.

What if a customer is unhappy?

They can leave whatever review reflects their experience. We can't and won't filter or suppress negative feedback. Your job is to earn positive reviews by doing good work—we just make sure you ask for them.

Which platforms does this work on?

We focus on Google Reviews because that's where homeowners search for local roofers and where reviews drive the most visibility. Other platforms can be added depending on your needs.

How does the timing work?

Requests go out the day after a job is marked complete in your system. You control the timing and frequency so it fits your workflow.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll look at what you're doing and either strengthen it or replace it entirely. Most roofing teams have a process on paper but not in practice—we make it actually happen.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

It depends on your job volume and how responsive your customers are. Most roofing companies see a significant lift when they ask consistently instead of sporadically. We'll show you what to expect during your call.
