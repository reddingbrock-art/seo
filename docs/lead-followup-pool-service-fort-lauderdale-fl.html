FAQ

Lead Follow-Up System FAQs for Pool Service Owners

How many follow-ups does it send?

Typically 3-5 touches over 7-10 days, depending on your process. You control the schedule and messaging—we customize it to match how you actually work.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. We write your initial messages, but you approve everything before it goes live. Changes are easy once it's running.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can opt out anytime. We handle compliance and unsubscribe requests automatically.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Within 5-15 minutes of a lead coming in. You set the timing based on your workflow.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in the system on a nurture sequence and get one final touch after a few days. If they still don't respond, they're marked as cold but stay on file.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. It plugs into how you already take calls and forms. No system to replace—just fills the gaps while you're busy.
