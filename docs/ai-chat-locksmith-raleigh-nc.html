FAQ

FAQ: AI Chat Widget for Locksmith Websites in Raleigh

Will the chat widget actually sound like my business?

Yes. We write the chat personality based on how you actually talk to customers—your tone, your terminology, your pace. It's trained on your business, not a generic template.

What happens when the chat can't answer something?

It hands off to you or a team member in real time, or it captures the customer's info and routes the conversation to your phone or email. You're never leaving a customer stranded.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It handles the visitors who would've never called in the first place—late-night browsers, people who prefer typing, prospects comparison shopping. Your receptionist focuses on phone calls and existing customers.

What hours does the chat widget work?

24/7. Nights, weekends, holidays. The chat is there when you're sleeping. If someone needs an emergency lockout at 2 AM and reaches you via chat, you get notified and can respond or hand it off.

How long does setup take?

Live in 10–14 days from your discovery call. We handle the technical setup, customization, and testing. You don't need to touch code or fiddle with integrations.

What if a customer wants to call instead of chat?

The widget has your phone number displayed. They can call anytime. Chat is the option for people who prefer it—you're not forcing anyone into text-only conversation.
