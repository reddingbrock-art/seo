FAQ

Review Automation FAQs for Windows & Window Replacement Companies

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests to customers you've actually worked for. Google filters out fake reviews anyway. This just makes sure the real customers who had a good experience actually leave one.

What if a customer is unhappy?

They won't leave a review. We're not forcing anything. The system asks—they respond or they don't. If someone had a bad experience, a review request won't change that.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google. That's where homeowners search for window companies. That's where the request goes, and that's where the review shows up.

How does the timing work?

The request goes out the day the job completes. We can adjust that timing based on your workflow—some teams prefer a day later, some prefer the same day. You decide.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll map out what you're doing now and either enhance it or replace it entirely. Either way, you'll send more requests faster and stop relying on memory.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Most of your happy customers never leave a review because nobody asked. When you ask consistently, you'll see significantly more. The exact number depends on your current job volume and completion rate.
