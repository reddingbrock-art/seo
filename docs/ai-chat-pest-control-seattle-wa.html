FAQ

AI Chat for Pest Control: Common Questions About 24/7 Lead Response

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on your service descriptions, how you talk about pricing, your neighborhoods, your guarantees. A prospect can't tell the difference between your AI and a receptionist on your team—that's the goal.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It's honest about it. If a question falls outside your services or needs a decision only you can make, it collects the customer's info and flags it for you. No fake answers, no guessing.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It replaces voicemail and callback delays. If you have a receptionist, they focus on customer follow-up and operations instead of answering the same questions 50 times a day. If you don't have one yet, you're not stuck hiring one.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. Calls, texts, and web forms get answered around the clock. You set your service windows and availability—the AI knows them and books accordingly.

How long does setup take?

Discovery to live is 10–14 days. That's one call with us, training and integration, then launch. You're not waiting months to start answering leads.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

Most don't notice. But if they do, it doesn't matter—they've already gotten their question answered and booked their appointment. The proof is in the result, not the reveal.
