FAQ

Google Review Automation FAQs for Roofing Companies

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests to actual customers after they've paid and the job is complete. Real customers, real reviews. Google's system catches fake reviews anyway—this keeps you clean.

What if a customer is unhappy?

If they don't want to leave a review, they don't leave one. We never pressure anyone or manipulate the process. We're just making it easy for satisfied customers to do what they already wanted to do.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google primarily. That's where roofing leads search in Savannah. We can expand to other platforms depending on your needs—discuss that on your discovery call.

How does the timing work?

The request goes out after you mark the job complete in your system. You control when that happens. Most roofing companies set it for 3-7 days after the customer pays, when the job is still fresh in their mind.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll look at what you're doing and either replace it or improve it. If you're getting reviews consistently, that's great—we can scale it. Most companies have a process that works sometimes. We make it work every time.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Depends on your job volume and your current review rate. If you're doing 15 roofs a month and getting 1-2 reviews, expect significantly more once requests are automatic and timely. We'll set realistic expectations on your call.
