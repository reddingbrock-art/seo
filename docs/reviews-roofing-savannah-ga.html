FAQ

Common Questions About Review Automation for Roofing

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. The system sends requests to actual customers from your completed jobs. We only request reviews from customers you've worked for—nothing synthetic, nothing purchased.

What if a customer is unhappy?

The system sends requests to all customers automatically. If someone's not satisfied, they can leave honest feedback or ignore the request. You're not filtering out negative reviews—you're asking everyone fairly.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google, primarily. That's where homeowners search for roofing contractors and make decisions. We focus there because that's where your leads actually look.

How does the timing work?

Requests go out after you mark a job complete in your system. The timing's automatic—you don't have to remember to send anything.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll talk through what you're doing now and either enhance it or replace it with something that doesn't require manual work. Either way, the goal's consistent, automated requests tied to job completion.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

It depends on how many jobs you're closing monthly and your current review request rate. Most roofing companies see significantly more reviews once requests become automatic. We'll show you what's realistic for your operation during a demo.
