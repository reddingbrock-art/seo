Do I need technical skills to run this?

No. We set it all up and run it for you. You log in to check your booked appointments and lead details. That's it. No coding, no configuration, no training required.

This doesn't require your team to learn anything. The system handles the answering and booking—they just show up to appointments that are already scheduled. Most crews see it as a relief, not a headache.
