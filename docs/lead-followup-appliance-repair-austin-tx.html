FAQ

Lead Follow-Up System FAQs for Appliance Repair Owners

How many follow-ups does it send?

We typically set up 3–4 touches over 5 days: immediate text, email, a second text, and a callback offer. You control the sequence and can adjust based on what converts best in your market.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. We write all messaging to match your voice and include your company details, service area, and availability. Changes take minutes, not hours.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can opt out with a single reply. They're removed immediately, and no further messages go out. Full compliance with texting regulations.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Within 2–5 minutes of form submission or missed call. Your team can adjust the delay if you want them staggered for some reason, but faster usually wins.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They move through the full sequence, then get tagged as unresponsive. You can resend them an offer in 30 days or segment them out. Nothing falls into a void.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. If you're already using a CRM or dispatch software, we integrate with it. If you're not, this becomes your lead management layer — no new logins required from your team.
