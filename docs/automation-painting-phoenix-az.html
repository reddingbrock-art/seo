No. Your team uses it the same way they'd use their phone or email. We handle the setup and integration. If something needs adjusting, we adjust it—you don't touch the backend.

What if my team resists new tools?

Most resistance comes from complexity. This system cuts work for your crew—they see fewer redundant calls and more qualified leads waiting. It takes a 15-minute orientation, and within a week it's just part of the flow.
