FAQ

Lead Follow-Up System FAQs for Indianapolis Landscapers

How many follow-ups does it send?

You decide. Most set up 2–4 touches over 5–7 days: one immediate, one at 24 hours, one at 72 hours. You can run more or fewer depending on your process.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. You write the message—or we help you craft it. It goes out under your name, in your voice, with whatever offer or call-to-action you want.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can unsubscribe or reply 'stop,' and the system removes them immediately. Full compliance with regulations.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Instantly, or on a delay you set. Most owners pick 5–15 minutes after the lead comes in, so it feels natural and fast.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in the system for the full sequence, then drop off. You can see them in your history and follow up manually later if you want.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. It pulls from your forms, calls, or CRM and works in parallel with whatever you're already doing. It's addition, not replacement.
