Automation for Solar Installers in San Diego: Common Questions

What exactly gets automated?

Inbound phone calls get answered and routed to appointment booking. Web form submissions trigger instant confirmations and appointment scheduling. Follow-up texts are sent automatically after jobs are completed for review requests. Everything ties to your calendar and your team's workflow.

Do I need technical skills to run this?

No. You don't touch the backend. You use it the same way you use your phone or email now. Your team answers questions, qualifies leads, and closes jobs. The system handles the repetitive first-contact work.

What if my team resists new tools?

There's no new tool for your team to learn. They get organized lead lists and confirmed appointments in their inbox, just cleaner and faster. If anything, they resist going back to the old chaos once they see how much time they save.

How is this different from buying software myself?

When you buy software, you get a login, a manual, and a support ticket. You figure out the setup, integrate it with your calendar, train your team, and maintain it. We do all that. You just get the results.

What does the setup process look like?

One call with us about your current process. We spend 5–7 days configuring your system. You approve it. We go live and stay involved during your first week of calls. Total time investment from you: about 30 minutes spread across 10 days.

What happens if something breaks after launch?

We don't just turn it on and disappear. We monitor the system during your first week of operation. If something needs adjusting, we handle it. You've got a real person to call, not a support queue.
