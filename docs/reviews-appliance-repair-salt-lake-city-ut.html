FAQ

Google Review Automation FAQs for Appliance Repair Companies

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We're asking real customers who just paid you to share their real experience. Google flags fake review patterns instantly; we're the opposite—legitimate requests to legitimate customers at the moment they're most likely to respond honestly.

What if a customer is unhappy?

The request still goes out. Whether they leave a review or not is their choice. Unhappy customers rarely respond to automated requests anyway. What matters is you're asking satisfied customers consistently—and those are the ones who write.

Which platforms does this work on?

We focus on Google Business profiles because that's where most customers search for appliance repair and that's where reviews affect your ranking most. Other platforms are secondary.

How does the timing work?

The request goes out the same day the job is marked complete, typically a few hours after the customer gets home. You choose the exact window that fits your workflow.

What if I already have a review process?

Most shops ask inconsistently or forget. This replaces guessing with automation. If you're already getting reviews, this will significantly increase your volume without adding work to your team.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Most appliance repair shops see a meaningful uptick in the first 30 days. The exact number depends on your job volume and customer quality, but the consistency is what changes—you're asking everyone, not just the ones you remember.
