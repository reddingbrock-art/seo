FAQ

Chat Widget Questions from Tampa Gutter Companies

Will the chat widget actually sound like my business?

Yes. We build it around how you actually talk to customers—your language, your process, your pace. It's not generic chatbot energy.

What happens when the chat can't answer something?

It acknowledges the question and escalates it to you with the full conversation history. Your team picks it up with full context, not starting from zero.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It replaces the moment when nobody answers because you're on the truck. A receptionist works 9-5. The chat works 24/7 and never forgets a conversation.

What hours does the chat widget work?

Around the clock. Visitors chat at midnight, 6 AM, weekends—whenever. During your business hours, conversations flow to your team in real time.

How long does setup take?

About 10-14 days from first call to live. We do the configuration. You just provide context on how you book jobs and what questions matter most.

What if a customer wants to call instead of chat?

They can. The chat includes your phone number and they can call anytime. But most don't have to—they book through the chat because it's faster and they don't have to wait.
