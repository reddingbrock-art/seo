FAQ

AI Chat Agent FAQs for Water Treatment in Austin

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We customize every response, greeting, and workflow to match your voice and how you actually talk to customers. It pulls your real service area, pricing tiers, and past customer history to sound like someone from your office.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It knows its limits. If a customer asks something outside its training, the AI tells them the truth — usually something like 'Let me have one of our water specialists call you back in the next hour' — and it flags the call for your team with notes on what the customer asked.

Does this replace my receptionist?

Not unless you want it to. Most owners keep their team and use the AI to absorb after-hours volume, back-to-back calls, and peak season overload. It frees your staff to focus on logistics, dispatch, and follow-up instead of being chained to the phone.

What hours does the AI work?

All of them. 24/7. It answers calls at midnight on Sunday, 6 AM on a holiday, and every minute in between. Your business never stops, and neither does your AI.

How long does setup take?

Deployment is 10-14 days from the time we kick off. We integrate with your calendar, pull your customer data if you've got it in a CRM, and test everything before it touches a real call.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

They'll figure it out eventually if they're paying attention — and that's fine. The AI doesn't pretend to be a person. It's honest and helpful. Most customers care more about speed and getting booked than whether they're talking to flesh or code.
