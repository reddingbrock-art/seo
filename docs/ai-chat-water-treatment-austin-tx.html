FAQ

AI Chat Widget for Water Treatment: FAQs

Will the chat widget actually sound like my business?

Yes. We write the chat personality and responses based on how you actually talk to customers. It sounds like your team, not a bot.

What happens when the chat can't answer something?

It tells the visitor a team member will follow up via phone or email, and it books them into your calendar so you have context when you call.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It replaces the moment when a prospect gives up trying to reach you. Your team still handles all the real conversations and closes.

What hours does the chat widget work?

24/7. After hours, it takes their information and schedules a callback for your first available time.

How long does setup take?

You're live in 10–14 days from the discovery call. Most of that is us building and you reviewing—the install itself takes a few minutes.

What if a customer wants to call instead of chat?

The widget has a button to call you directly. Some people will use it, and that's fine. You're just not losing the people who don't.
