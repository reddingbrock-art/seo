FAQ

Google Review Automation: Common Questions from Spokane Roofing Owners

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests only to customers who actually completed jobs with you. Whether they leave a review or not is their choice. You're asking real people for real feedback.

What if a customer is unhappy?

That's between you and them. We send the request after the job is done. If something goes wrong before that, you handle it directly. This system doesn't filter out unhappy customers or hide negative reviews—it just asks everyone for honest feedback.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google is where most homeowners search and leave reviews. That's where we focus the requests. Your Google Business Profile is the core—that's what shows up in local search results for roofing in Spokane.

How does the timing work?

You tell us when a job is complete—either through your CRM, a quick email, or a form. The request goes out within 24 hours. Most customers see it while the job is still fresh in their mind.

What if I already have a review process?

We can work alongside it or replace it. If you're already asking manually and getting responses, we can automate what you're doing and remove the busywork. If you're not asking at all, we start from scratch.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

That depends on your job volume and how many of your past customers never left a review. Most roofing companies see a significant jump in the first 60 days simply because they're asking consistently. After that, it becomes your steady flow of new jobs feeding new reviews.
