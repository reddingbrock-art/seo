FAQ

AI Chat Widget FAQs for Commercial Cleaning Businesses

Will the chat widget actually sound like my business?

Yes. We write it using your brand voice, service descriptions, and the way your team actually talks to customers. It'll feel like someone from your office is typing back.

What happens when the chat can't answer something?

It collects the customer's information, explains that your team will follow up, and creates a task for your office. Nothing falls through the cracks.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It handles incoming questions and books qualified leads into your schedule. Your team still manages the relationship, confirms jobs, and handles anything complex. It frees your receptionist from answering the same questions all day.

What hours does the chat widget work?

24/7. It works while your office is closed, on weekends, and during holidays. Your team reviews and responds to qualified leads during business hours.

How long does setup take?

You'll be live in 10-14 days from your discovery call. That includes building the chat, training it, testing it with you, and deploying it on your site.

What if a customer wants to call instead of chat?

The widget displays your phone number prominently. If someone prefers to call, they can. But most won't—they'll chat because it's faster and they don't have to wait.
