FAQ

Review Automation Questions for Cincinnati Commercial Cleaning Companies

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests only to customers with actual completed jobs in your system. Google verifies every review that comes through. Fake reviews violate their policy and ours—we don't build that way.

What if a customer is unhappy?

An unhappy customer won't leave a review if they didn't want to. The system asks; they choose to respond or not. If someone's upset, they're unlikely to do it anyway. You get the honest feedback you need.

Which platforms does this work on?

We focus on Google Business Profile, which is where facility managers and property managers actually search for cleaning companies. That's where your customers are looking.

How does the timing work?

Requests go out 24 hours after a job is marked complete in your system. That's when the customer's happiest and most likely to remember the specifics of the work.

What if I already have a review process?

We can work around it or replace it, depending on what you've got. If you're manually sending emails and getting spotty results, we'll automate it. If you've got something working, we'll talk through the details first.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

That depends on your job volume and how many customers actually respond to requests. Most commercial cleaning companies see a significant increase from where they were manually asking. We'll track results from day one so you can see the difference.
