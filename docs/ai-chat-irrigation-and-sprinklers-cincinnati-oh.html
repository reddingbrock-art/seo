FAQ

AI Chat for Irrigation & Sprinkler Contractors: Questions Answered

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on your actual operations, your service areas, seasonal patterns, and how you talk. A customer calling won't feel like they're talking to a generic bot—it'll sound like someone who knows your company.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It flags the conversation and passes it to you or your team with full context. You jump in, answer the question, and we learn from it so the AI handles similar questions next time.

Does this replace my receptionist?

It replaces the intake function—answering phones, taking details, and booking appointments. If you have a receptionist, they're now freed up for customer service, upsells, or follow-ups instead of being chained to the phone.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. Calls and chats get answered at 2 a.m. just like they do at 2 p.m. You decide if appointments book for off-hours or if the AI just captures the info and schedules them for your first available slot.

How long does setup take?

Live in 10–14 days from your discovery call. We handle the build, training, and integration. You don't need to touch any code.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

It's fine. The AI is upfront if asked. Most customers don't care as long as their problem gets solved and their appointment gets booked. They're calling to get on your schedule, not to have a philosophical conversation.
