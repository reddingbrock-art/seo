FAQ

Automation for Locksmith Services in Henderson, NV

What exactly gets automated?

Lead capture from your website and forms, initial qualification (address, lock type, urgency), availability matching, booking confirmation, and delivery of job details to your dispatcher. You're automating the entire front-end handoff so your team starts with confirmed, qualified jobs.

Do I need technical skills to run this?

No. You don't manage the platform or update settings. We build it, we manage it, you just watch the leads come through qualified and ready to dispatch.

What if my team resists new tools?

Your team doesn't learn a new tool. They receive jobs the way you decide—text, email, or dashboard. The system removes work from their plate; it doesn't add friction.

How is this different from buying software myself?

You're not buying a license and learning a platform. We design, build, and run the entire automation for you. If something needs adjusting, we fix it. You pay for the result, not the software.

What does the setup process look like?

One discovery call, about a week of build and testing on our end, then a brief walkthrough with your team before launch. You're live and capturing leads within 10–14 days.

What happens if something breaks after launch?

We monitor the system daily and handle any issues. If a lead isn't routing correctly or a booking field needs adjustment, we fix it and let you know it's done.
