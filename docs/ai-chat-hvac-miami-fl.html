FAQ

HVAC Chat Widget Questions: Miami Setup, Customization & Results

Will the chat widget actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on your language, your service areas, your pricing structure, and how you close jobs. It doesn't sound like a robot—it sounds like someone from your team who knows HVAC.

What happens when the chat can't answer something?

It flags the conversation and routes it to you. Your team picks it up from there, or the system tells the customer you'll follow up within a specific window. No one gets ghosted.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It handles the visitors your receptionist never gets to—the ones who won't call or who land on your site at 2 AM. It reduces the noise your team handles, not the conversations that matter.

What hours does the chat widget work?

24/7. It responds instantly during business hours and lets people know your response time during off-hours. Visitors still get helped whether they land on your site at noon or midnight.

How long does setup take?

The widget is live and working in 10-14 days from our first call. You're not waiting months to see results.

What if a customer wants to call instead of chat?

The chat offers your phone number anytime. Some people prefer to call—that's fine. The point is you're not losing people who prefer to chat because you weren't there to respond.
