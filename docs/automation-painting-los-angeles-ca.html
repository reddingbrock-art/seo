What exactly gets automated?

Lead capture from forms and calls, initial qualification questions, appointment scheduling into your calendar, and follow-up outreach to leads who haven't booked yet. Your estimators still do the estimate — this just makes sure they're not wasting time on disqualified leads or double-booking.

Do I need technical skills to run this?

No. You don't touch the backend. We build it, you use it. Your team logs in, sees their schedule, and gets the info they need. That's it.

What if my team resists new tools?

We make sure your team actually wants to use this because it makes their day easier, not harder. You get fewer bad leads, clearer calendars, and better notes. Most crews embrace it within the first week because they're not spinning their wheels anymore.

How is this different from buying software myself?

You could buy a CRM, hire someone to configure it, and spend months getting it right. Or we build the whole thing for you — installed, trained, and running on day 10. We own the setup. You own the results.

What does the setup process look like?

Discovery call. We identify your intake channels and current bottlenecks. Build phase: we connect everything and configure your flows. Review and refinement. Live launch. Total time is roughly two weeks.

What happens if something breaks after launch?

You've got support. If a lead isn't being routed right, a callback didn't send, or something's acting weird, you reach out and we fix it. This doesn't stop working because we built it to keep working.
