FAQ

Lead Follow-Up Automation: FAQs for Garage Door Businesses

How many follow-ups does it send?

We typically set up a 5-7 touch sequence over 10-14 days—text and email combined. You can customize how many touches you want and the timing between them.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. We write the messages based on your style and business, then you review and approve before it goes live. You control the voice and the offer.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They get unsubscribed immediately. The system respects opt-outs the same way you do—no pushback, no tricks.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Typically within 5-15 minutes of the lead coming in. If it's a phone call, you can set a delay so you have time to answer first.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in the system and get the full sequence. After that, you can see them in your dashboard and decide if you want to follow up manually or let them go.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. It picks up where you are now—handling the early reach-out while you stay focused on estimates, installs, and customer service.
