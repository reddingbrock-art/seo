FAQ

Lead Follow-Up Questions for Raleigh Garage Door Businesses

How many follow-ups does it send?

Typically 3–4 touches over 5 days: text, email, text again if no response. We customize the sequence based on your process and how your leads typically respond.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. We write the initial templates, you approve them, and we build everything so you can update messaging anytime without rebuilding the whole system.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They're immediately removed from the sequence. We respect opt-outs the moment they happen.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Within 60 minutes of the initial contact. If a lead comes in at 2 p.m., they're getting a message before 3 p.m.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in your CRM tagged as 'no response.' You can run manual follow-up campaigns, sell them to a partner, or circle back in 30 days. No leads are discarded.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. It runs in parallel, feeding qualified leads into your dispatcher and CRM. Nothing replaces what you're already doing—it just fills the gaps between first contact and first callback.
