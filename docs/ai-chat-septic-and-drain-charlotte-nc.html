FAQ

Septic & Drain Chat Widget Questions

Will the chat widget actually sound like my business?

Yes. We write every response based on how you talk to customers and the specifics of your service area. It sounds like someone from your team, not a bot.

What happens when the chat can't answer something?

The conversation hands off to your team with full context so they can take it from there. Nothing gets lost.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It handles the visitors who won't call and gets them to a decision point—book, ask for a callback, or hand off to your team. It makes your receptionist's job easier, not redundant.

What hours does the chat widget work?

24/7. Visitors can chat anytime. During your off-hours, conversations get queued so your team sees them first thing.

How long does setup take?

You're live in 10-14 days from the discovery call. We handle everything—no technical work on your end.

What if a customer wants to call instead of chat?

Your phone number is visible in the chat window. They can call whenever they want. The chat is an option, not a barrier.
