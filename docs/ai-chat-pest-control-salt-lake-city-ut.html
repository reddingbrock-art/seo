FAQ

AI Chat Agent for Pest Control: Common Questions

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on your real customer conversations, service language, and how your team talks. It'll use your business name, quote your actual service areas, and handle objections the way you would.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It escalates to your team immediately and transcribes the full conversation so there's no context loss. You get the full story, not a game of telephone.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It does the work a receptionist shouldn't have to do—answer every call even at 2 a.m. and schedule routine appointments. It frees your team up for the work that actually makes money.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. Nights, weekends, holidays. The system never clocks out.

How long does setup take?

10-14 days from your discovery call to going live. We need time to understand your business and tune the system properly.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

Our system is trained to be upfront about it. Customers actually appreciate the instant response. The moment they need a human, they get transferred immediately. Transparency builds trust.
