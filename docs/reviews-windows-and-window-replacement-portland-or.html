FAQ

Google Review Automation FAQs for Windows & Window Replacement in Portland

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests only to customers after they've actually completed a job with you. They're real people who did real work with your crew. Google's system verifies that.

What if a customer is unhappy?

Unhappy customers won't leave a review, and that's fine. We're not filtering or hiding anyone—we're just making it easy for satisfied customers to share their experience. If someone has a problem, you'll hear about it separately.

Which platforms does this work on?

Primary focus is Google, where most of your local search happens. The requests direct customers to your Google Business Profile, which is where homeowners are looking.

How does the timing work?

You tell us when a job's complete. The request goes out within hours—timing matters because the experience is fresh. Customers have 5-7 days to respond before a follow-up gentle reminder.

What if I already have a review process?

Most shops have good intentions but no system. This replaces the 'we'll ask for reviews later' approach with automation that actually sends them. We'll work around your existing workflow.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Most shops see significantly more reviews in the first 90 days because the request actually reaches customers. The exact number depends on your job volume—but the difference is night and day compared to manual asking.
