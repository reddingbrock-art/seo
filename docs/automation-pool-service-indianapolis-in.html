FAQ

Automation Questions for Indianapolis Pool Service Owners

What exactly gets automated?

Incoming calls get answered with a greeting and basic qualifying questions. Web form submissions get an instant response asking for their address and preferred appointment window. Text inquiries are acknowledged immediately. All of this happens while you're servicing pools, and your team gets notified with context so they can follow up intelligently.

Do I need technical skills to run this?

No. You don't log into the system to manage calls or forms. Your dispatcher gets a simple dashboard showing all leads and their status. If something needs to change—your hours, your service area, your questions—you tell us and we update it.

What if my team resists new tools?

This isn't a tool they have to learn. It's a system that answers the phone and fills in the paperwork before work reaches them. Most teams like it because it removes the chaos of missed calls and unanswered emails.

How is this different from buying software myself?

When you buy software, you're responsible for setup, configuration, troubleshooting, and updating your team. With us, we build it, monitor it, and fix it. You show up and run your service business.

What does the setup process look like?

You'll talk to us about how your business works, we ask questions about your busiest times and most common inquiries, we build the system over 7–10 days, train your dispatcher in one session, and go live. That's it.

What happens if something breaks after launch?

We monitor the system 24/7. If there's an issue—a call not recording, a form not sending a response—we catch it and fix it. You're not on the hook to troubleshoot.
