FAQ

Review Automation FAQs for Fort Lauderdale Fire & Water Restoration Companies

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests only to customers who actually completed jobs with you. Google detects fake reviews instantly and penalizes profiles for it. We're requesting reviews from real customers; whether they leave one is up to them.

What if a customer is unhappy?

The review request goes out after job completion. If a customer had a problem, they'll either leave honest feedback or skip the request. We're not filtering or hiding unhappy customers—we're asking everyone consistently so your profile reflects reality.

Which platforms does this work on?

The primary focus is Google reviews, where most Fort Lauderdale customers search for restoration companies. We can discuss other platforms during your call if needed.

How does the timing work?

Requests send 24 hours after you mark a job complete in your system. That's when satisfaction is highest and customers are most likely to respond. You control when jobs close; the system handles the rest.

What if I already have a review process?

If it's working, great. If it's manual, inconsistent, or forgotten half the time, we can replace it entirely. We'll talk through what you've got and where the gaps are during a quick call.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

That depends on your job volume and customer responsiveness. Most owners see their review velocity increase significantly once requests go out consistently. A company doing 40 jobs a month typically sees 8–12 new reviews monthly within 60 days.
