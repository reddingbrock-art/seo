Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on your team's actual language, your rates, your service guarantees, and even the jokes your sales guy makes. Callers won't know they're talking to AI unless you want them to know.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It flags the call for you and sends a transcript. You follow up with context already in hand. No leads fall through the cracks.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It replaces the phone ringing unanswered at 8 AM and the three callback attempts you never get to. If you have a receptionist, she stops being glued to the phone and can focus on scheduling, follow-ups, or job site coordination.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. Calls at midnight on Saturday? Answered. Storm hits Denver at 2 AM and homeowners start calling? You've got leads queued up before sunrise.

How long does setup take?

10–14 days from discovery call to live. We handle everything. You just review the training calls and approve when it's ready.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?
