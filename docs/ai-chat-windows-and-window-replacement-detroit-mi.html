FAQ

AI Chat for Windows & Window Replacement: Common Questions

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We build it to answer the way you would—your tone, your pricing, your service areas, your timeline expectations. It's trained on your business, not a generic template.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It flags the conversation for you and asks the customer if you can follow up. You'll see these in your dashboard and can respond within hours. The customer never feels abandoned.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It replaces the part of your day where someone's answering routine questions about scheduling, service areas, and timelines. If you have a receptionist, they handle more complex calls and relationship work. If you don't, you get your time back.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. It answers on your website, Google Business Profile, and anywhere else you want it connected. Most of your leads come in after hours anyway—this catches them.

How long does setup take?

10-14 days from discovery call to live. You spend a couple hours with us upfront explaining your business. We handle the rest.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

The AI tells them upfront: 'I'm an AI assistant for [Your Company]. I can help you schedule or answer common questions. Would you like to book an estimate?' Transparency builds trust. Customers are fine with it—they just want a quick response.
