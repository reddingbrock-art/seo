FAQ

Lead Follow-Up Questions for Sacramento Pest Control Operators

How many follow-ups does it send?

That depends on your process. Most pest control teams we work with run 3–5 touches over 48 hours. You decide the sequence and timing based on what's worked for your business.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. We write it in your voice the first time, and you can adjust the messaging anytime. It stays consistent across every lead, but it sounds like your company, not ours.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can opt out with one click, and the system respects that immediately. You're always compliant, and your reputation stays clean.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Within minutes of the lead submitting a form or calling your number. We're talking faster than your team can even see the notification.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in the system and get your full sequence. After that, they're tagged so you know who's already gotten every follow-up. Your team can decide if a manual outreach makes sense, but the automation's done its job.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. We're not replacing your team or your CRM. We're automating the part that falls through the cracks—the immediate response and the consistent follow-up while your guys are busy.
