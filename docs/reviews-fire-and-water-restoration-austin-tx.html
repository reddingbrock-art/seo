FAQ

Review Automation Questions for Fire & Water Restoration Owners

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests only to customers you've actually completed work for, using their real contact information. Google detects and removes fake reviews. We don't generate them.

What if a customer is unhappy?

We only send review requests to customers after job completion—not before. If someone's unhappy, they typically tell you directly. We don't suppress or filter negative feedback; we just ask satisfied customers to share their experience.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google is the priority—that's where homeowners search for restoration companies in Austin. We focus there first because it's what moves the needle for your visibility and inbound calls.

How does the timing work?

You set the trigger. Most owners request reviews 1–3 days after job completion, when satisfaction is highest and the experience is still fresh. The system sends at that exact moment, every time.

What if I already have a review process?

We take over the manual work—no more checklists, no more following up with crews to remember who asked. If you're already getting reviews, automation just means you'll get significantly more without adding staff.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Most restoration owners collect 2–3 reviews per week once the system is running. That's 100+ annually instead of 20–30. Your actual number depends on your job volume and customer contact quality.
