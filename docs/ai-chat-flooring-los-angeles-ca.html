FAQ

AI Chat for Flooring Companies: Common Questions

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on your actual service process, your language, your pricing approach, and how you actually close jobs. It sounds like a real person from your team because it's answering the same questions the same way you would.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It hands off to you with full context—the customer's name, their project, what they asked. No game of telephone. You get the warm handoff and you take it from there.

Does this replace my receptionist?

It replaces the parts of the job that waste time: answering the same questions over and over, scheduling back-and-forth, handling after-hours calls. If you have a receptionist, they suddenly have time for follow-ups, upsells, and running the business instead of being on hold.

What hours does the AI work?

Around the clock. It answers calls and chats during business hours and after. During your peak seasons, you're not losing leads because it's 6 p.m. on Friday.

How long does setup take?

Discovery to live is 10–14 days. We handle the technical side. You just have to answer questions about your business and review the AI once it's trained.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

Most don't. It's natural, it answers questions accurately, and it sounds like a person. If they ask, the AI tells them the truth—and it still books the appointment. Transparency doesn't kill the deal.
