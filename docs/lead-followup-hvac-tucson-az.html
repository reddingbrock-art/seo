FAQ

Lead Follow-Up Automation FAQs for HVAC Businesses

How many follow-ups does it send?

That's up to you. Most HVAC shops use a sequence of 2-4 touches over 3-5 days. We design it around your close timeline and what your team can actually convert.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. You write the messages or we write them based on your voice and process. Every follow-up gets your approval before it goes live.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can opt out at any time. The system respects that and stops contacting them. You're never violating their preference.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Immediately—within minutes of the lead arriving. You can set it for instant or delay it a few hours if you prefer, but most shops go with instant.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in your system and complete the sequence you've set up. After that, they're marked as inactive. You decide if you want to re-engage them later with a different message.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. If you're already using a scheduling tool or CRM, we integrate with it. The system works inside whatever setup you've got.
