FAQ

AI Chat for EV Charger Installation: Common Questions

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We configure it with your business name, service areas, typical pricing, and how your team talks about installations. It doesn't sound robotic or generic—it sounds like someone from your office picking up the phone.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It says so honestly and captures the customer's contact info and question. The lead gets routed to you so a real person can follow up with the actual answer. You still own the conversation.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It replaces the missed calls. If you have a receptionist, they'll spend their day on higher-value work instead of managing call volume. If you don't, you suddenly have one without the payroll.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. Calls come in at night, on weekends, during holidays—and they get answered. Your appointment calendar fills while you sleep.

How long does setup take?

10–14 days from discovery call to live. Most of that is us gathering your service details and training the system. Your team doesn't need to do much.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

If they ask directly, the AI tells them. But most won't—the conversation feels natural. And honestly, most customers don't care how their appointment got booked as long as it's confirmed in their calendar and someone shows up on time.
