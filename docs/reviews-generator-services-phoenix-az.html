FAQ

Generator Services Review Automation: Common Questions

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests only to customers after they've completed a real job with you. Google and other platforms detect fake reviews—we're not doing that. We're just making sure the customers you actually served get asked.

What if a customer is unhappy?

They're free to write whatever they want, just like any review. Our job is to ask—not to filter or suppress. A real review, positive or negative, is more valuable than silence.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google is the primary channel. We can also integrate with industry-specific review platforms and Yelp depending on your market. We discuss your priorities in discovery.

How does the timing work?

Requests go out the same day a job is marked complete in your system. If you close work on a Tuesday, they see the request Wednesday morning. That timing is what makes the difference.

What if I already have a review process?

We can work with it, replace it, or run parallel. Most owners we work with were asking manually or sporadically. We systematize it so nothing falls through.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

It depends on your monthly job volume and customer email/phone data quality. Most owners see a significant increase in the first 90 days once the system runs consistently. We'll map your baseline during discovery.
