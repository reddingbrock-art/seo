FAQ

Questions About AI Chat Agents for Fire & Water Restoration

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on your language, your service details, your coverage area, and how you talk to customers. It's not a generic bot—it's built specifically for your restoration company.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It escalates to your team immediately. If a question's outside its scope, it collects what it can and flags the call for human follow-up. Nothing falls through the cracks.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It replaces the voicemail. If you have a receptionist, they're freed up to handle callbacks and scheduling instead of playing phone tag. If you don't, suddenly you have one available 24/7.

What hours does the AI work?

All 24 hours, every day. Nights, weekends, holidays—whenever your phone rings, it answers. That's the whole point.

How long does setup take?

10-14 days from your approval. We build it, you review it, we train your team on the backend, then we go live. It's fast.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

Most don't. But if they ask or figure it out, the AI tells them straight—'You're chatting with our automated intake system, and here's what we're doing to get you help fast.' Honesty builds trust. Customers care about getting a crew out, not whether a human typed their information.
