FAQ

AI Chat System FAQs for Columbus Painting Businesses

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on how you talk, what you care about, and how you price. When a customer calls, they're talking to someone who sounds like they work for you.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It flags the call and gets it to you immediately so you can jump in. It doesn't pretend to know—it escalates intelligently.

Does this replace my receptionist?

It handles what a receptionist does when they're not there—answering phones, taking messages, qualifying leads. If you have a receptionist, they focus on higher-value work.

What hours does the AI work?

All of them. It answers calls 24/7/365. You decide which hours it takes full calls versus which hours it just captures the message and your team gets back to them.

How long does setup take?

10-14 days from discovery call to live. Most of that's us building it right the first time, not you doing work.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

It doesn't hide what it is. Customers appreciate that it answered instantly and got their info right. The quality of the experience matters more than the label.
