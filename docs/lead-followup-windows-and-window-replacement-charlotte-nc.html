FAQ

Common Questions About Lead Follow-Up for Charlotte Window Companies

How many follow-ups does it send?

That depends on your process. We typically set up a sequence of 3-5 touches over 7-10 days, but we customize it based on what's worked for you in the past and how aggressive you want to be.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. Every message is written to match your voice and process. We don't use generic templates. You approve exactly what goes out before it's live.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can unsubscribe, and the system honors that. We also make sure you're compliant with messaging laws so you're never at risk.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Usually within 5-15 minutes of a lead coming in, depending on the time of day and how you want to set it up. The goal is to be first.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in the system and get the full sequence you set up. After the sequence ends, they sit in your database for re-engagement later or you can exclude them manually if they've gone quiet.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

It integrates with how you're already taking leads. If you're getting them from forms, calls, or a lead source, we connect the system so it follows up automatically without changing your workflow.
