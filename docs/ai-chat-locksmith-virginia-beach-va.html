FAQ

Common Questions About AI Chat for Virginia Beach Locksmiths

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on how you talk to customers, your service descriptions, your neighborhoods, even your personality quirks. When someone calls, they're hearing your voice and process, not a generic bot script.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It says so. If the question falls outside what it knows about your services, hours, or pricing, it collects the customer's info and tells them you'll follow up. Nothing gets lost.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. If you have one, she can focus on dispatch, follow-ups, and customer issues that need a human touch. If you don't have one, you suddenly have 24/7 coverage without the payroll.

What hours does the AI work?

All of them. Nights, weekends, holidays. It doesn't sleep, doesn't call in sick, doesn't need time off.

How long does setup take?

About 10-14 days from your discovery call to go-live. Most of that is us building and training, then we need a few days for testing before your number switches over.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

Some will figure it out. But most won't, because it handles the call like a real person—asking follow-ups, taking notes, getting clarity. Even if they do realize it, the fact that they got answered and booked matters more than who answered.
