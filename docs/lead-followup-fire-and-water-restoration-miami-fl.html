FAQ

Common Questions About Lead Follow-Up for Fire & Water Restoration

How many follow-ups does it send?

Depends on your workflow. We typically start with 3–5 touches across calls, texts, and email over 5–7 days. You decide the sequence and timing based on what's worked for your business.

Can I customize what it says?

Completely. Every message is written in your voice. We work with you to nail the language so it sounds like your team, not a bot.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can opt out at any time, and the system respects that immediately. No messages go out to anyone who's asked to stop.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Within minutes of the lead coming in. You set the exact timing—could be immediate, could be within 15 minutes. Your call.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They complete the sequence and sit in your system. You can review them later, change the approach, or move them to a different follow-up track if circumstances change.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Yes. It integrates with how you're already taking calls and managing jobs. We're adding systematic follow-up, not replacing what's working.
