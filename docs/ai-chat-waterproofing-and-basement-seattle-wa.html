FAQ

Questions About AI Chat for Waterproofing & Basement Contractors

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on your tone, how you describe projects, and the way your best salespeople actually talk to homeowners. It'll sound familiar to your existing customers.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It says so, collects the customer's info, and routes the question directly to you with full context. No dead ends, no dropped threads.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It handles the intake and qualification—which frees your team to focus on closing and fulfillment. If you have a receptionist, they now have time to do actual work instead of just answering the phone.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. Calls come in at midnight, 6 a.m., or Sunday—the AI answers, qualifies, and books. Your team gets notified when it's time to wake up and work.

How long does setup take?

10 to 14 days from your discovery call to going live. Most of that's us building and training—you're only involved for the initial conversation and any fine-tuning mid-week.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

The system's transparent about it early—"Hey, I'm an AI assistant here to help get you booked." Most homeowners don't mind as long as the experience is fast and painless. It's actually faster than waiting on hold.
