FAQ

Questions About AI Chat for Painting Contractors in Charleston

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We interview you, review how you talk to customers, and train the AI on your language, service area, and pricing philosophy. It doesn't sound like a chatbot—it sounds like someone from your office who knows your work.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It tells the customer you'll follow up within a specific timeframe and logs the conversation so you see exactly what was asked. Nothing falls through the cracks.

Does this replace my receptionist?

It replaces the repetitive part of phone duty—answering the same questions 30 times a week about service areas, paint types, and availability. Your team focuses on sales and operations instead.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. It answers at midnight, 6 AM, and during your lunch break. You pick which hours show on your website—but the AI's always there to capture inquiries when they come in.

How long does setup take?

10–14 days from discovery call to fully live. Most of that's training and refinement. You're not waiting months for this to work.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

Most don't. But if they ask, the AI tells them the truth and offers to connect them to a real person. Honesty builds trust. Pretending doesn't.
