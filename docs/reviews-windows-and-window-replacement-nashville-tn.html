FAQ

Google Reviews for Window Companies: Common Questions

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send review requests only to customers whose jobs are actually completed. The reviews that come back are genuine—they're from people who had your crew in their home.

What if a customer is unhappy?

They can write whatever they want. We're not filtering or suppressing anything. Unhappy customers can still leave a review—most just won't, which is why the request has to go out when satisfaction is highest.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google is the priority—that's where most searches happen in Nashville. We focus there because that's where your revenue is moving.

How does the timing work?

You set it. Most crews finish jobs in the afternoon or early evening, so requests go out same-day or next morning. You control when the ask happens so it feels natural to the customer.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll work with it. If you're already asking manually but inconsistently, we can automate the part you're missing. If you've got nothing in place, we build it from scratch.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Most window companies see a significant increase once the requests go out consistently. You'll get more reviews because you're actually asking—most yards aren't. The exact number depends on your job volume, but the uptick is fast.
