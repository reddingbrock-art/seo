FAQ

Common Questions About Automation for Irrigation Contractors

What exactly gets automated?

Lead capture from calls, web forms, and texts. Appointment scheduling and confirmation. Job dispatch to crew phones with all details. Review requests after completion. Reminder texts to customers. Anything manual and repetitive that slows you down gets handled by the system.

Do I need technical skills to run this?

No. Your admin manages it like email. Your crew sees jobs on their phone. If something needs tweaking, our support handles it. You don't touch code or databases.

What if my team resists new tools?

Most crews resist the idea, not the tool. Once they see jobs land perfectly formatted in their pocket instead of getting a phone call, they prefer it. We handle the onboarding so the resistance doesn't stick.

How is this different from buying software myself?

When you buy software, you own the setup problem. You don't know what to connect to what. We do. We build it for your exact workflow, train your team, and stay on the other end when things change.

What does the setup process look like?

Call with us, a few hours of configuration, one or two training sessions with your team, then it's live. Most customers are fully running in two weeks. We do the heavy lifting; you just use it.

What happens if something breaks after launch?

You've got support. If a lead stops flowing or a tool disconnects, you contact us and it's fixed the same day. We monitor the system, so most issues we catch before you do.
