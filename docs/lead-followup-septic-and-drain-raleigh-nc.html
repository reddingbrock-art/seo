FAQ

Lead Follow-Up System FAQs for Septic & Drain Businesses

How many follow-ups does it send?

You decide. Most businesses we work with send 3–5 touches over 7–14 days. Each one's customizable, and you can pause or adjust the sequence anytime.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. We build the sequence with your messaging. Whether it's a friendly first text, a reminder, or a final win-back offer, the words are yours.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can unsubscribe or reply STOP, and they're off the list immediately. You stay compliant, and we handle the removal.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Instantly. The moment a lead submits a form or gets added to your system, the first message goes out within seconds.

What happens to leads that never respond?

You see them in your dashboard marked as unresponsive after the sequence ends. You can decide to re-engage them later or retire them.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Absolutely. It plugs into whatever lead source you have now—website forms, phone calls, text inquiries—and handles the follow-up while your team focuses on closing.
