FAQ

Common Questions About AI Chat for General Contracting

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on your real language, your pricing structure, your service areas, and how you typically respond to customers. It doesn't sound like a corporate bot—it sounds like someone who works for you.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It flags the conversation, collects the customer's contact info and question, and routes it to your team immediately. Complex or unusual requests get a human on the phone—the AI just makes sure nothing falls through the cracks.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It replaces the voicemail, the unanswered phone, and the 45-minute callback delay. Your team still handles estimates, scope clarification, and relationship-building. The AI just makes sure those conversations actually happen.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. It answers estimate requests and calls at midnight, 5 AM, or Sunday night. You configure when it books appointments—most contractors set it to book same-day or next available, and your team handles the scheduling details.

How long does setup take?

The AI is live and handling traffic within 10-14 days of your discovery call. We handle all the backend work—you just need to give us your service details, pricing, and scheduling preferences.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

Most don't. But if they ask, your AI tells the truth straightaway and offers to connect them with your team or book an appointment. Transparency actually builds trust—customers appreciate that you're responsive and organized.
