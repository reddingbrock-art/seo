FAQ

AI Chat Agent Questions for Irrigation & Sprinkler Businesses

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on how you talk to customers, the neighborhoods you serve, your service names, and your pricing. It references local Jacksonville details and seasonal patterns specific to your operation.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It acknowledges the question and transfers the customer to your team, or offers to have someone call them back within a specific timeframe. Either way, the lead stays warm.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It replaces the voicemail and missed-call problem. Your receptionist (or dispatcher) still handles the calls that need a human, but now they're not drowning in callback stacks or repeating the same questions over and over.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. It answers calls and texts at 2 a.m. on a Saturday just as well as it does at 9 a.m. on Monday. You set the hours for automatic booking; anything outside that gets flagged for your team to handle the next business day.

How long does setup take?

10-14 days from discovery call to live. That's discovery, building and training the AI, connecting your systems, and testing. You're taking calls with it by day 15.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

Most don't. But if they ask, the AI is upfront about it. Transparency actually builds trust, especially when the AI is solving their problem faster than a human ever could.
