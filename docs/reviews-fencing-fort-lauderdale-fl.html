FAQ

Review Automation Questions for Fort Lauderdale Fence Companies

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We only send requests to customers you've actually completed jobs for. The system pulls from your job records, not a list. Real customers, real jobs, real feedback.

What if a customer is unhappy?

We only send the request after the job is marked complete. If you notice a customer's unhappy before that, don't mark it done. The timing is yours to control.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google, Facebook, and your industry-specific directories. We configure requests to go where your customers are actually looking for fence contractors.

How does the timing work?

You tell us when to send—next day, two days out, whenever makes sense for your crew. It goes out automatically on the schedule you set.

What if I already have a review process?

We take over from there. If you're getting 2-3 reviews a month manually, we'll typically get you 3-5 per week without any effort on your end.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Most contractors see 15-25 new reviews in the first 60 days. It depends on job volume, but the ceiling is significantly higher than what happens when you ask manually.
