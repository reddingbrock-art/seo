FAQ

AI Chat for Pressure Washing: Questions Answered

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on how you talk to customers—your rate, your service language, your personality. It's not a generic bot voice. Customers hear your business, not a corporate script.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It flags it and transfers the call to you, or lets the customer know you'll call them back within a specific timeframe. We don't leave anyone hanging.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It replaces the missed calls and the 'I was in the field' excuse. If you have a receptionist, it actually gives them time to do real work instead of answering the same 'Do you service my area?' question 20 times a day.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. Calls come in at 11 PM on a Saturday? Answered. Early morning inquiry Tuesday before you're even awake? Logged and waiting for you.

How long does setup take?

10–14 days from kickoff to live. We move fast because we know you're losing leads every day it's not running.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

Some will figure it out, especially on the second conversation. That's fine. The point is they got answered immediately and their information was captured correctly. Transparency beats ghosting every time.
