FAQ

Common Questions About Review Automation for Gutters Companies

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests only to customers whose jobs actually completed. Google can verify the work happened. Real customers, real reviews.

What if a customer is unhappy?

They still get the request—and they have the choice to review honestly or not respond. Unhappy customers rarely leave reviews anyway. Happy ones do. The system doesn't hide or suppress anything.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google is the main driver for local search. We focus there. We can also send requests for other platforms, but Google is where Atlanta homeowners find you.

How does the timing work?

The request goes out the day after job completion. That's when the work is fresh in their mind and they're most likely to respond.

What if I already have a review process?

If you're getting enough reviews, great. If not, this automates it so nothing falls through the cracks. Most contractors we talk to realize they're only asking verbally—which means 90% of customers don't follow through.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Depends on your job volume. If you're closing 15 jobs a month and getting 1 review now, you'll likely see 6–9 per month once requests are automated. Most customers who finish a job will respond if you ask at the right time.
