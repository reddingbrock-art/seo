FAQ

Google Review Automation for Landscapers: Common Questions

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests to customers after jobs are completed. What they write is entirely their choice. You're just making sure the ask reaches them at the right moment.

What if a customer is unhappy?

They see the request and decide whether to respond. If they don't leave a review, nothing happens. We're not filtering or hiding unhappy customers — we're simply automating the ask to happy ones.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google primarily. That's where most customers search for landscapers and where your ranking matters most for Fort Lauderdale area searches.

How does the timing work?

The request goes out automatically once you mark a job complete in your system. You control the exact timing and frequency so it never feels spammy.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll look at what you're doing now and either improve it or replace it entirely. Most owners find they're leaving reviews on the table because the process is too manual or too slow.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

It depends on your job volume and how many customers actually see the request. Most owners see a significant jump in the first 60 days just from making the ask consistent and timely.
