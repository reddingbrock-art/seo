FAQ

Review Automation FAQs for Detroit EV Charger Installation

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests only to customers whose jobs you've actually completed. Google flags artificially inflated profiles immediately, so we never go there. All reviews that come in are genuine customer responses.

What if a customer is unhappy?

The system requests a review from every customer, but unhappy ones typically don't leave one—they'll call or email you directly. You handle those conversations as usual. A few negative reviews alongside positive ones actually builds credibility with Google's algorithm.

Which platforms does this work on?

We focus on Google—that's where EV charger shoppers in Detroit are looking. Your reviews show up on Google Maps, Search, and your business profile. That's where the traffic comes from.

How does the timing work?

The request goes out automatically the day the job is marked complete in your system. Customers get a simple link; if they respond, their review posts to your Google profile within days. You see everything tracked in your dashboard.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll work with your current workflow—whether you're emailing, texting, or using another tool. We replace the manual step with automation so you don't double up on requests. One process, zero extra work.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Most installers see significantly more reviews once requests are automated—customers who wanted to leave feedback just needed the prompt. You'll notice the difference within 4-6 weeks. Real volume depends on your install count, but the jump is always noticeable.
