FAQ

AI Chat for Commercial Cleaning Companies: Common Questions

Will the chat widget actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on your language, your service descriptions, and how your team actually talks to customers. It represents your brand, not some generic corporate voice.

What happens when the chat can't answer something?

It tells the visitor exactly that, captures their info, and flags the conversation for you to follow up. Nothing falls through the cracks.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It replaces the moment your receptionist isn't available. It qualifies leads, books appointments, and handles routine questions so your team focuses on service and sales.

What hours does the chat widget work?

24/7. Nights, weekends, holidays—whenever someone's on your site, the chat's ready. You respond to qualified leads during your business hours.

How long does setup take?

10-14 days from your first call to going live. Discovery, build, refinement, and deployment. You're handling chats by week two.

What if a customer wants to call instead of chat?

They can. The chat offers both options. Some visitors prefer typing, others want your voice. You get both, and you're not forced into one channel.
