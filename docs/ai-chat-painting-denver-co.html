FAQ

AI Chat for Denver Painting Contractors: Common Questions

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on your tone, your service language, and the way you actually talk to customers. It won't sound corporate or generic. It sounds like someone from your office who knows your jobs.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It says so. It won't make up an answer. If a customer asks something outside its knowledge, it grabs their contact info and tells them a real person will follow up within an hour. Transparency builds trust.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It handles the phones so your people can focus on jobs, estimates, and running the business. Your team still closes deals and manages relationships. The AI just removes the dead time between ring and answer.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. It answers calls at midnight on a Sunday just as well as it does at 9 AM on Tuesday. You don't miss leads because the office is closed.

How long does setup take?

10–14 days from kickoff to live. We handle everything. You're not configuring software or learning new tools.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

Most don't, because it sounds natural. If they do, they don't care — they got an answer and booked an estimate in two minutes instead of waiting for a callback. That's the win.
