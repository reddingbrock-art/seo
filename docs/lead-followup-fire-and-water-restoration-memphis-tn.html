FAQ

Lead Follow-Up Questions for Memphis Fire & Water Restoration

How many follow-ups does it send?

Typically three: one immediately, one within a few hours, and one the next day if they haven't responded. You control the timing and messaging.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. The messages are yours—your name, your phone number, your offer. We build it to sound like you're responding, not like a robot.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can reply to stop, and the system removes them from future sends. That's built in—no compliance headaches.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Within minutes of form submission. Most leads get contacted before they've even closed their browser.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in your system. You can pull a report anytime and see who's been contacted but hasn't engaged, so your team can try a different approach.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. It plugs into your site and your workflow. Your team still answers calls and closes jobs the way they do now—this just makes sure leads don't slip through the cracks.
