Questions About Automation for Riverside Window Replacement Companies

What exactly gets automated?

Lead capture and immediate response, qualification questions, appointment scheduling, crew notifications, and review requests after job completion. Everything that happens between a lead coming in and your estimator actually meeting them.

Do I need technical skills to run this?

No. You don't touch the back end. You set your availability, review leads, and manage your estimators the way you do now. The system handles the rest.

What if my team resists new tools?

This isn't a tool your team has to learn. It makes their job simpler—they see a calendar full of qualified appointments instead of a pile of callbacks. Most teams feel the relief immediately.

How is this different from buying software myself?

You'd have to set it up, integrate it, train your team, and debug it when it breaks. We do all that. We also keep it running and adjust it based on what actually works in your business. You get the result, not the headache.

What does the setup process look like?

One discovery call, usually 2-3 weeks of build and testing on our end, then a soft launch where we watch it with you for 30 days. Total time before you're live and running: 10-14 days from when we start building.

What happens if something breaks after launch?

You have direct support. If a lead doesn't get a response, a booking breaks, or something's off, you contact us and we fix it. You're not troubleshooting—we are.
