FAQ

AI Chat Questions for Seattle Fire & Water Restoration Owners

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on your processes, your language, and your service area specifics. It answers like someone who actually works for you — mentions Seattle neighborhoods, knows your service areas, and responds to emergencies with the right tone.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It tells the customer it needs to transfer them to a team member and offers to take their information and callback time. You stay in control of complex questions or disputes.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It frees your receptionist from answering the same intake questions over and over. They focus on relationship building, follow-ups, and customer service instead of call volume.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. It answers calls at midnight, 3 a.m., and weekends — whenever your customers have an emergency. You configure business hours and overflow rules however you need.

How long does setup take?

10–14 days from discovery call to live. We handle the technical heavy lifting. You just tell us how you work.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

It doesn't hide it. If asked, it's transparent: 'I'm an automated assistant handling your intake so we can book you faster.' Most customers don't ask — they just want the appointment scheduled and are relieved someone answered at all.
