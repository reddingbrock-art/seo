FAQ

Lead Follow-Up Questions for Flooring Owners

How many follow-ups does it send?

Typically 3–5 touches across SMS and email over 7–10 days, but we customize the sequence based on your process and how fast you want to move leads.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. We write the initial copy to match your voice, and you approve it before anything goes out. You own the messaging.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can unsubscribe at any time—the system respects that immediately. We handle compliance so you don't have to.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Usually within 2–4 hours of the lead coming in. You can set the timing based on when your team is typically available to take calls.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They drop off after the final touch, but they stay in your CRM. You can always reach back out manually if you want to circle back later.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. It runs in parallel with whatever you're doing now—it's designed to fill the gaps, not replace your current workflow.
