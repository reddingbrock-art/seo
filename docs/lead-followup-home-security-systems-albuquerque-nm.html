FAQ

Common Questions About Lead Follow-Up for Home Security Companies

How many follow-ups does it send?

That depends on your sequence. Most home security companies use 3-5 touches over 7-10 days. We build it based on what your sales cycle actually looks like.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. We write the messages in your voice, and you approve them before anything goes out. Every step reflects how you actually talk to customers.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can opt out anytime, and they're immediately removed. Compliance is built in—you're not spamming anyone.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Typically within 15 minutes of the lead arriving. The faster you contact someone, the higher your odds of reaching them before a competitor does.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in the sequence until they either convert, opt out, or the sequence completes. Nothing disappears. If they ignore everything, you know they weren't ready.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. It runs in parallel with however you're currently selling. Your team's process doesn't change; the follow-up just happens without them having to manage it.
