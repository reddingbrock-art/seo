FAQ

Common Questions About Review Automation for Appliance Repair

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests only to customers who actually completed jobs with your company. Google's system detects fake reviews and penalizes your business. We don't do that.

What if a customer is unhappy?

They won't leave a review—or if they do, it'll be honest. That's actually good. Real feedback helps you improve. The system requests reviews from all customers equally. You handle the unhappy ones like you normally would.

Which platforms does this work on?

The system requests reviews on Google, and we can configure it for other platforms your customers use. Google is where most appliance repair searches happen in Los Angeles, so that's the priority.

How does the timing work?

Requests go out the day after a job is marked complete in your system. That's when the experience is fresh but the customer has had time to see the repair hold up.

What if I already have a review process?

We can build around it or replace it. If you're already doing manual follow-ups, we automate what you're doing so your team stops carrying that load. If you have nothing, we start from scratch.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

It depends on your job volume and how many customers are actually willing to leave reviews. Most appliance repair shops see significantly more reviews within the first month because they're asking consistently. We'll show you the trend as it builds.
