FAQ

AI Chat Agent for Junk Removal: FAQs

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We learn your neighborhoods, your truck sizes, your pricing, how you talk about jobs. It responds the way you'd respond if you had time to answer every chat.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It flags the conversation and passes it to you with context. You jump in and handle it. The customer never knows it switched—they're already warm and qualified.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It replaces the missed calls and dead forms. If you have a receptionist, now they're handling callbacks and confirmations instead of frantically answering phones. If you don't, you finally have someone answering 24/7.

What hours does the AI work?

All of them. It's live whenever a chat comes in—midnight, 6 AM, Sunday morning. You control your booking window so customers can't book times you're not working.

How long does setup take?

Discovery to go-live is 10-14 days. You're answering chats before the end of week two.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

They usually don't. But if they ask, the agent tells them straight: "I'm an automated system that handles scheduling and initial questions." No deception. Customers are fine with it—they're just happy someone answered at 10 PM.
