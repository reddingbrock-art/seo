FAQ

Roofing Review Automation: Common Questions Answered

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. The system requests reviews only from real customers after completed jobs. Real people, real work, real feedback. Anything else violates platform terms and hurts your credibility.

What if a customer is unhappy?

The system doesn't filter or suppress unhappy customers. It asks everyone for feedback. If someone has a complaint, that's information you need—and your response to honest criticism is often more powerful than the complaint itself.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google, Facebook, and other major review platforms where roofing prospects actually search. The system handles the routing so you don't have to manage multiple logins.

How does the timing work?

You tell us when a job is complete. The system waits a day or two, then sends the review request when the customer is still in the 'positive experience' window. No guessing on your end.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll work around it. If you're manually asking some customers, the system can fill the gaps and scale what you're already doing. More requests, more reviews, same effort on your side.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

It depends on your job volume and which customers actually respond. Most roofing crews see a significant increase within 60 days—often 2–3 new reviews per week instead of sporadic ones. The momentum compounds because newer reviews attract more customer attention.
