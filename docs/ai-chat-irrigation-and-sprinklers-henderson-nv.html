FAQ

Chat Widget FAQs for Irrigation & Sprinkler Companies

Will the chat widget actually sound like my business?

Yes. We build it from scratch using your language, your service types, your coverage areas, and your tone. It sounds like someone from your office who knows irrigation.

What happens when the chat can't answer something?

It's honest about it. If a visitor asks something outside the scope, the widget collects their info and notes the question so your team can follow up with the right answer.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It handles the first conversation and qualification. Your team still closes the sale and manages the relationship. It just means fewer dropped leads and fewer "I left a message" situations.

What hours does the chat widget work?

24/7. It's live whether your office is open or closed. That Friday night prospect? The widget is already engaging them while you're off the clock.

How long does setup take?

Live in 10-14 days from discovery to deployment. We handle the build, integration, and testing. You just tell us what you do.

What if a customer wants to call instead of chat?

The widget can offer a phone option or your team's hours for calling. Some visitors will choose chat; some will call. The widget doesn't force either one.
