FAQ

Foundation Repair AI Chat: Questions Answered

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We write the personality and tone based on your team's voice and how you actually talk to customers. It sounds like someone from your company, not a robot reading a script.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It tells the customer the truth and offers to have someone from your team follow up. It's trained to know the edge of its knowledge and hand off gracefully, so you never look unprepared.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It replaces the part of your receptionist's job that's handling routine lead intake. Your team stops fielding basic questions and starts focusing on selling and scheduling.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. It answers at 2 a.m. on a Tuesday the same way it answers at 9 a.m. on Saturday. Your customers get a response whenever they reach out.

How long does setup take?

10–14 days from your discovery call to go-live. We handle all the training and integration. You don't install anything or manage technical details.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

The AI is upfront about it if asked. Most customers don't care as long as they get their questions answered fast and end up talking to a real person when they need to. Speed and clarity matter more than the label.
