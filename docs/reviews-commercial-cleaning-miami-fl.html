FAQ

Common Questions About Commercial Cleaning Review Automation in Miami

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests only to customers you've actually serviced. Everything is tied to completed jobs in your system. Authentic requests to real customers—that's it.

What if a customer is unhappy?

The request still goes out. What customers do with it is up to them. You're asking; they're not obligated to respond. The system doesn't filter or suppress anything—it just asks at the right time.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google Reviews, which is where the vast majority of your prospects are looking. That's where it matters most.

How does the timing work?

The moment a job is marked complete in your system, the request triggers. You control the exact timing—same day, next morning, whatever works for your workflow.

What if I already have a review process?

We integrate with it or replace it, depending on what you're doing now. Either way, automation takes over the repetitive part so your team isn't managing it manually.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Most cleaning companies see a significant increase because they're asking at the moment customers are most likely to say yes. The exact number depends on your current ask rate and job volume. We'll show you the baseline during your call.
