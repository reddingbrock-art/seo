FAQ

Common Questions About AI Chat for Roofing

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on your actual language, your pricing, your service area, and your process. Customers hear your tone—professional but approachable, whatever that means for you.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It escalates. If a customer asks something outside its training, it collects their info and lets you know they need a callback. You stay in control of the tough conversations.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It replaces the part of the day when nobody's picking up the phone—nights, weekends, storms, while you're on jobs. If you have a receptionist, this makes them more productive by filtering and pre-qualifying leads.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. Calls and texts get answered at 3 AM on a Tuesday just as fast as they do at noon. You control your callback windows—customers know when they'll hear back from a real person.

How long does setup take?

About 10-14 days from discovery to live. You'll have it running before the next storm hits.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

They won't mind if it's working for them. It qualifies them fast, answers their questions, and books their appointment. Transparency is built in—we're not trying to trick anyone. Customers care about speed and results, not whether a human or AI handled the intake.
