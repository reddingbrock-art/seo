What exactly gets automated?

No. You check a dashboard and respond to booked jobs. We manage the backend, updates, and any integrations with your other tools. If something needs adjusting, you tell us and we handle it.

What if my team resists new tools?

They won't see a 'new tool' to learn. They see a cleaner job queue in the morning with pre-qualified leads, phone numbers, and notes already filled in. Less data entry, not more.

How is this different from buying software myself?

You'd be setting it up, training your team, and troubleshooting when something breaks. We do all that. You pay for automation that actually works, not a tool you're managing in your spare time.

What does the setup process look like?

One call with us to understand your workflow. We build and test everything behind the scenes over about a week. Then we go live and you start getting booked jobs.

What happens if something breaks after launch?

We monitor it and fix it. If a call doesn't route right or a form isn't sending, you let us know and we handle the repair. You're not debugging anything yourself.
