FAQ

Lead Follow-Up System FAQs for Pest Control Owners

How many follow-ups does it send?

Typically three to five touches over 72 hours—initial response, qualification check, and appointment reminder. We customize the sequence during setup based on your typical sales cycle.

Can I customize what it says?

Completely. We write your messages during setup so they sound like your business and your team. You approve every word before it goes live.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can opt out with a single reply. The system respects that immediately and logs it so you don't accidentally follow up again.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Within seconds of the lead coming in through your website, phone, or text. If it's 11 p.m., they get a response at 11 p.m.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in your CRM tagged as non-responsive. You can pull reports on them anytime or manually follow up later if you want.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Yes. It sits on top of whatever you're already doing—your calendar, your texting, your appointment book. It feeds leads and responses into your workflow, not replacing it.
