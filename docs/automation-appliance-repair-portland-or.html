FAQ

Automation for Appliance Repair: Common Questions Answered

What exactly gets automated?

Lead capture from all sources (phone, web forms, texts, social), instant routing to your team, job data flowing into your dispatch board, technician notifications, and review requests after completion. We automate the intake-to-assignment process so nothing disappears.

Do I need technical skills to run this?

No. Your dispatcher and office staff operate it like they'd use email or your current phone system. It's built for field service teams, not IT departments. The learning curve is measured in minutes, not weeks.

What if my team resists new tools?

The system doesn't add work—it removes it. Your team spends less time digging for information, less time answering questions about job details, and more time serving customers. Resistance usually evaporates when they see fewer calls repeating the same question.

How is this different from buying software myself?

We don't sell you software and leave you to figure it out. We build a custom automation tailored to your business, integrate it with your existing tools, set it up, train your team, and support it. You're buying a working system, not a license.

What does the setup process look like?

A discovery call to map your process, 7–10 days of building and integration, a training session with your team, and then go-live with 24/7 support from us for the first month. Most shops are fully operational within two weeks.

What happens if something breaks after launch?

You have direct access to our support team. If a lead isn't routing correctly or a system isn't syncing, we fix it the same day. You're not troubleshooting on your own or waiting for a help desk ticket to move through a queue.
