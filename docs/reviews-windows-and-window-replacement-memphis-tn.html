FAQ

Questions About Review Automation for Windows & Window Replacement in Memphis

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests only to customers you've actually completed work for. Google filters out fake reviews anyway — and you'd get caught eventually. We're asking your real customers to leave real feedback.

What if a customer is unhappy?

Then they leave honest feedback. Unhappy customers were going to leave a review or tell their friends anyway. The upside — getting reviews from your satisfied customers — outweighs the occasional negative one. And negative reviews often give you a chance to respond and show how you handle problems.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google primarily, since that's where Memphis customers are searching for window companies. We can discuss other platforms on a call if you want to expand later.

How does the timing work?

Requests go out within 24 hours of a job being marked complete in your system. Most customers respond within 48 to 72 hours. You see the review appear in your Google profile within days, not weeks.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll look at what you're doing now and either integrate with it or replace it with something that actually runs on its own. If you're manually sending requests or asking crews to handle it, you'll see the difference immediately.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Most businesses see a significant increase because they're asking consistently instead of sporadically. You'll notice the difference in your profile within 60 days — and the momentum builds from there. Exact numbers depend on your job volume, but you're capturing reviews you were leaving on the table before.
