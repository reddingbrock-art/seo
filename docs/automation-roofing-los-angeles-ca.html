Common Questions About Roofing Automation in Los Angeles

What exactly gets automated?

Lead capture from your website, Google, text, and chat. Qualification via automated questions and photo uploads. Scheduling directly into your calendar. Review requests after job completion. Callback routing to the right person on your team. Nothing happens without your team being notified and in control.

Do I need technical skills to run this?

No. You don't log in, configure, or troubleshoot. We handle the setup, updates, and backend. Your team gets a simple interface—they answer calls, texts, and chats that come in, and they check the calendar for booked jobs.

What if my team resists new tools?

The system removes work, not creates it. Your dispatch stops chasing leads—they're already qualified. Your crew stops cold-calling—appointments are already booked. Most pushback disappears once they see their first week of callbacks handled without them doing anything extra.

How is this different from buying software myself?

You'd be buying a platform and running it yourself. We build it for you, connect it to everything, train your team, and keep it running. It's not a tool you own—it's a system we own and operate for you.

What does the setup process look like?

We schedule a discovery call, confirm how you take leads now and where they break down, then build your system over 7–10 days. You review, we iterate, we go live with your team trained and ready. Most roofing companies are live and seeing leads in under 2 weeks.

What happens if something breaks after launch?

We monitor the system daily. If something stops working, we fix it and notify you. You're not managing uptime or chasing bugs—that's our job. You focus on roofs.
