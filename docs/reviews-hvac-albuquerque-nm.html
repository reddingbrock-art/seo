FAQ

Review Automation FAQs for HVAC Companies in Albuquerque

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We only send requests to customers you've actually completed work for. Google can verify that. We're asking your real customers to leave real reviews about real jobs.

What if a customer is unhappy?

The request still goes out—but an unhappy customer is far more likely to tell you than to sit silently. If you get negative feedback before they review, you have a chance to fix it. Silence is worse than a conversation.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google, primarily. Google reviews are what drive ranking and what most customers read first. We can discuss other platforms on your discovery call based on your goals.

How does the timing work?

The request goes out the same day the job is marked complete in your system. You set the trigger—usually when the invoice closes or the tech marks it done. Customer gets the request within minutes of job completion.

What if I already have a review process?

Tell us what you're doing. If you're manually asking every customer and getting solid response, the system can augment that. Most shops are asking inconsistently or not at all—this fills that gap.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

It depends on your job volume and how many customers you're currently asking. If you're completing 10 jobs a week and asking nobody, automating requests typically surfaces 2–4 new reviews weekly within 30 days. We'll map it out on your call.
