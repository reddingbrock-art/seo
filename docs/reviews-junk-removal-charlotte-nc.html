FAQ

Google Review Automation for Junk Removal: Questions Answered

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send review requests only to customers whose jobs you've actually completed. The reviews you get back are real—they're from people who hired you and used your service.

What if a customer is unhappy?

We're asking customers for reviews after the job's done. If they're unhappy, that's information you need. You can reach out and make it right before a negative review shows up. This is about capturing honest feedback, not filtering it.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google reviews. That's where Charlotte customers are looking when they search for junk removal.

How does the timing work?

The request goes out the day after a job is marked complete in your system. Customers get a text or email asking them to leave a Google review if they're happy with the work.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll look at what you're doing now and either integrate with it or replace it with something faster. If you're already collecting reviews, we'll analyze the gaps and fill them.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

It depends on your job volume and how many customers are willing to leave reviews. Most junk removal companies see a significant increase in the first month because they're asking consistently for the first time. We'll give you realistic numbers based on your crew count and monthly job completion rate.
