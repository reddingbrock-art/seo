FAQ

Lead Follow-Up FAQs for Fire & Water Restoration Companies

How many follow-ups does it send?

Typically three to five over a 7-10 day window, spaced so it stays top-of-mind without feeling spammy. You control the sequence and timing.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. You write the message templates, or we help you build them based on lead type—water damage, fire, mold, etc. It sounds like your business.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They're removed immediately. The system respects opt-outs and complies with regulations. No arguments, no friction.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Within minutes of the lead arriving. Not hours. Minutes.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in your system with a status showing no engagement. You can review them, try a different approach, or move on. Nothing disappears.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. It fills the gaps in your current workflow—faster first touch, automatic qualification, better tracking. Your team keeps doing what works.
