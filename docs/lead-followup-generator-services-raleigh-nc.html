FAQ

Lead Follow-Up Questions for Generator Services Companies

How many follow-ups does it send?

That depends on your lead source and how many no-responses you're willing to chase. Most sequences run 3–5 touches over 5–7 days. We customize it based on what your team can handle on callback.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. We write the initial sequence, you approve it, and we adjust the tone and questions to match how your team sells. Every follow-up stays on brand.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can opt out at any time, and they're removed immediately. We handle compliance—you don't have to worry about it.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Within 2–5 minutes of a lead coming in. If it's a form submission or web inquiry, the response is nearly instant.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in the sequence until they respond, opt out, or the sequence ends. After that, they move to a separate nurture list if you want to circle back later with seasonal offers or maintenance reminders.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. It runs separately from your CRM and dispatch system. We integrate with whatever you're using so leads flow in automatically, but your team's workflow doesn't change.
