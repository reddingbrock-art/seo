FAQ

Common Questions About Review Automation for Pressure Washing Businesses

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests to real customers after real jobs. They choose whether to leave a review or not. We never generate, fake, or manipulate reviews in any way.

What if a customer is unhappy?

The system sends requests to customers after job completion, but there's no forced mechanism. If someone's unhappy, they won't leave a review. This isn't a tool to suppress or filter negative feedback—it's a reminder to satisfied customers to share their experience.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google is the priority because that's where Baltimore homeowners search for pressure washing contractors. Other review platforms can be added depending on your strategy.

How does the timing work?

Requests go out automatically after a job is marked complete in your system. The timing is built around when customers are most likely to remember the work and take 30 seconds to leave feedback.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll integrate with what you're already doing or replace it entirely if it's manual or inconsistent. Either way, the goal is to make sure no customer slips through without being asked.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

It depends on your current job volume and how many customers actually respond. Most owners see a significant increase because they're no longer relying on customers to remember to leave feedback on their own.
