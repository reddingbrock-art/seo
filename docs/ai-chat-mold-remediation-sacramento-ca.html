FAQ

AI Chat Agent FAQ for Mold Remediation Owners in Sacramento

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on your real language—how you describe remediation, what you care about, the way you close jobs. It sounds like someone from your team, not a robot.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It says so. It tells the customer 'I need to get one of our team members on this—you'll hear from them in 30 minutes.' It collects everything it needs and hands it to you as a complete lead summary.

Does this replace my receptionist?

It replaces what a receptionist does for incoming calls and forms. If you have office staff, they now focus on scheduling, follow-up, and running the business instead of answering the phone for the 100th time that day.

What hours does the AI work?

All of them. Nights, weekends, holidays. It's available whenever your customers are searching for mold remediation—which in Sacramento is often after hours or during the wet season when you're already booked solid.

How long does setup take?

You're live and answering leads in 10-14 days. We handle configuration, training, and testing on our end. You approve the voice and process. Then it's running.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

Most don't—the conversation feels natural. If they ask, the AI tells the truth: 'You're talking to our automated assistant. I'm here to make sure we get all your information and get you scheduled quickly.' Transparency builds trust.
