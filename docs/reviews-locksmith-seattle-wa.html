FAQ

Google Review Automation FAQs for Locksmiths

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We're asking your actual customers—the people you just worked for—to leave genuine reviews. Google catches fake reviews fast and penalizes you for it. We don't go there.

What if a customer is unhappy?

The system asks everyone equally. If someone had a bad experience, they're unlikely to respond or will leave honest feedback. That's real. It's also rare when your work is solid. The point is you're not hiding from honest reviews—you're asking for them consistently so the good ones outnumber outliers.

Which platforms does this work on?

We focus on Google because that's where Seattle customers search for locksmiths. We can also set up requests for other platforms depending on your setup—ask during your discovery call.

How does the timing work?

The request goes out after payment clears, usually within an hour or two of job completion. That's when satisfaction is highest and memory is fresh. No waiting weeks for a customer to remember.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll integrate with what you're doing or replace it entirely if it's not working. Either way, you'll have something that actually runs on its own instead of relying on you or your team to remember.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Depends on your volume, but most owners see a significant jump in the first month just from asking consistently. If you do 20 jobs a week and capture even 30% response rate, that's 6 new reviews weekly—something you're likely getting zero of right now.
