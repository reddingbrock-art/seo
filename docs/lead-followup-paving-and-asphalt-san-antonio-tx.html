FAQ

FAQs About Lead Follow-Up for Paving Contractors

How many follow-ups does it send?

It depends on your sequence, but typically 3–5 touches over 7–10 days. We build it based on what works for paving companies and tune it based on your response rates.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. We write the initial messaging based on your voice and business, and you can edit or approve every message before it goes out.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They're immediately removed from the sequence. It's built in and happens automatically. Compliance is non-negotiable.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Within the same business day, typically within a few hours of the lead coming in. The exact timing is configurable based on your preferences.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They complete the full sequence, then move to a nurture track. You still see them in your dashboard so you can follow up manually if you want.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Absolutely. It's designed to slot in. If you're already using a CRM or scheduling tool, we integrate with it. If not, we set up a simple dashboard you can use to track everything.
