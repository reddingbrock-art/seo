FAQ

Common Questions About Review Automation for HVAC Companies

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests only to customers who've actually had work done. Google's system catches fake reviews anyway. This is just making sure real customers who had a good experience actually get asked to share it.

What if a customer is unhappy?

They'll still get the request — and that's honest. Real reviews, even critical ones, matter more to Google and to customers than a profile full of silence. If someone's genuinely upset, their feedback helps you fix what's broken.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google is the priority — that's where customers search and where new leads make decisions. We can expand to other review sites, but Google's where the ROI lives for HVAC shops.

How does the timing work?

Requests go out automatically within hours of job completion — when customers are satisfied and remember the work they just had done. No manual scheduling, no delays, no forgotten requests.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll look at what's working and what's not. Most shops are doing it manually or inconsistently. We'll replace the broken parts and keep what's actually landing reviews.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

It depends on your job volume and current review rate. Most shops see a significant increase because they're asking consistently and at the right moment. We'll show you the math on your first call.
