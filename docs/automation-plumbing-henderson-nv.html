FAQ

Automation Questions for Henderson Plumbing Companies

What exactly gets automated?

Inbound call answering, call qualification (emergency vs. non-emergency, service type, location), customer data capture, voicemail transcription and routing, and automated review requests after job completion. Your team still does the plumbing and closes the jobs—the system handles the intake and follow-up.

Do I need technical skills to run this?

No. We build it, test it, and hand it to your dispatcher or office person. They log in, see the queue, grab the qualified leads, and assign them. It's meant to be simple enough that your team uses it without thinking.

What if my team resists new tools?

Most push back on complexity. This one saves them time and makes their day easier—fewer phone interruptions, pre-filled job details, smarter dispatch. Resistance usually goes away once they see the first week of calls getting handled without their help.

How is this different from buying software myself?

You could buy generic software and spend weeks setting it up, training your team, troubleshooting integrations with your scheduling system, and figuring out what call flows actually work. We do that. You get a system built to plumbing, tested, and live in 10–14 days.

What does the setup process look like?

Discovery call to understand your operation. We build and test the system with you over a few days. Training call with your team. Go live. We're in your corner for the first week if anything needs adjustment.

What happens if something breaks after launch?

You have direct support from us. We monitor the system, handle technical issues, and fix bugs. If a call doesn't route right or a qualification rule misfires, we catch it and correct it. You're not alone on this.
