Questions About Automation for Septic & Drain Companies

What exactly gets automated?

Lead capture from your website and messaging channels, instant qualification questions, appointment booking, and review requests after job completion. Everything that can happen without a human right now gets automated. Your team handles only the sales conversation and the work itself.

Do I need technical skills to run this?

No. We set it up, train your team on how to monitor it, and keep it running. You log in, see your booked appointments, and manage from there. If something needs adjusting, you call us.

What if my team resists new tools?

Your team won't be managing it—they'll be benefiting from it. They'll see warm leads and confirmed appointments instead of callback lists. The resistance usually drops fast once they see the workload shift away from callbacks and toward actual jobs.

How is this different from buying software myself?

You could buy tools, hire someone to integrate them, train your team, and hope it works. Or we do all of that—setup, integration, training, and ongoing support—so you're not troubleshooting at 10 PM when something breaks.

What does the setup process look like?

Discovery call, we build your system in the background, your team does a training session, and it goes live in 10-14 days. You're not spending weeks configuring or learning software.

What happens if something breaks after launch?

We monitor it. If something's not working, we fix it and let you know. You've got our support on speed dial for the first month, and ongoing support after that. You're never stuck troubleshooting alone.
