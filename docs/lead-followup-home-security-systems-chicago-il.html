FAQ

Lead Follow-Up Questions for Home Security Contractors

How many follow-ups does it send?

We typically set 3–5 touchpoints over 7–10 days depending on response. You control the sequence and timing so it matches your sales cycle.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. We write the first draft based on your brand voice and offer. You review and approve every message before it goes to a single lead.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can opt out at any time with a single reply. The system removes them immediately. Compliance is automatic.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Within 15–30 minutes of form submission or lead capture. You're getting in while they're still thinking about security.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They cycle through the full sequence and then stop. No spam, no wasted effort. They're logged for your team to review or follow up manually if needed.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. It integrates with your current CRM, forms, and phone system. Automation handles follow-up while your team closes deals.
