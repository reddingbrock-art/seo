FAQ

Questions About AI Chat for Water Treatment Businesses in Jacksonville

Will the chat widget actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on your actual service descriptions, tone, and how your team talks to customers. It reflects your brand and service standards, not generic water treatment copy.

What happens when the chat can't answer something?

It's honest about it. It'll say 'that's a great question for my team—here's how to reach them' or offer to capture their info so your team can follow up. No made-up answers.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It replaces the silence your website has right now. Your receptionist still handles phone calls and complex conversations. This just captures and qualifies the leads you're currently losing to crickets.

What hours does the chat widget work?

24/7. That's the whole point—your site answers questions and books appointments when your team's asleep, at dinner, or on a service call. You wake up to qualified leads sitting in your system.

How long does setup take?

10-14 days from our first call to live. We gather info about your services, build and train the widget, test it, and flip the switch. Then it starts converting.

What if a customer wants to call instead of chat?

The chat can direct them to your phone number and hours. Some people will always prefer calling—that's fine. The widget's job is to make sure the ones who want to chat can actually reach you and get answers.
