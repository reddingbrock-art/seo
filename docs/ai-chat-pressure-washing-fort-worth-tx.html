FAQ

Questions About AI Chat for Your Pressure Washing Business

Will the chat widget actually sound like my business?

Yes. We write responses based on how you talk to customers, the questions you actually get asked, and your service style. It's trained on your business, not generic templates.

What happens when the chat can't answer something?

It hands off gracefully. If someone asks something outside the chat's scope, it collects their info and tells them you'll follow up—which you will, because they're already in your system.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It handles first contact and qualification so your team can focus on scheduling, jobs, and customer relationships. It makes your receptionist more valuable, not obsolete.

What hours does the chat widget work?

24/7. It's there when you're sleeping, on jobs, or booked solid. That's exactly when most of your website traffic happens.

How long does setup take?

10–14 days from discovery call to going live. We handle all the configuration; you just review and approve.

What if a customer wants to call instead of chat?

The chat can offer your phone number, or it can just take their info and have you call them. Either way, you stay in control of how contact happens.
