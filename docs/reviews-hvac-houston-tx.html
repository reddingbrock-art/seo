Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We only request reviews from actual customers in your job history. Google's algorithm is sophisticated enough to catch fake activity—and so are we. Every review has to be genuine, or it hurts you more than it helps.

What if a customer is unhappy?

We intercept that before it becomes a public review. When someone indicates they're not satisfied, you get notified so you can follow up and solve it. You'll convert some into five-stars, and you'll prevent the rest from damaging your profile.

We focus on Google because that's where Houston homeowners look first. It's also where your search ranking matters most. Other platforms—Yelp, Angie's List—can be added, but Google is the priority.

How does the timing work?
