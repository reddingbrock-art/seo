FAQ

Review Automation Questions for Houston HVAC Businesses

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send review requests only to customers who actually completed a job with you. What you get back is genuine feedback from real people.

What if a customer is unhappy?

We only send the request after the job's marked complete. If a customer had a problem, that gets handled through your normal service recovery process—this doesn't change that.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google, Yelp, and the major review platforms your Houston customers are actually using. We set up requests to the ones that matter most for your business.

How does the timing work?

The request goes out the same day the job closes, when the work is fresh in the customer's mind. That's when they're most likely to respond.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll look at what you're doing now and show you how to integrate this without creating duplicate requests or friction for your customers.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Most HVAC companies see significantly more reviews once the requests are actually going out consistently. The number depends on your job volume, but you'll notice movement within the first month.
