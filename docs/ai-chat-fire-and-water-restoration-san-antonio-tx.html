FAQ

Common Questions About AI Chat for Fire & Water Restoration

Will the chat widget actually sound like my business?

Yes. We write the conversation scripts based on how you actually talk to customers and what questions you hear every day. It's your voice, not a generic bot.

What happens when the chat can't answer something?

It tells the customer the truth—hands them off to your team with context about what they already discussed. No pretending or looping. Your staff picks up a pre-qualified conversation, not a cold inquiry.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It replaces the moment when someone tries to reach you and can't. Your receptionist still handles calls, estimates, and relationship work. This just makes sure website visitors aren't invisible.

What hours does the chat widget work?

24/7. It's running all night, all weekend, all holiday. You set the booking availability—if you don't take emergency calls at 3 a.m., the chat tells them when you're available instead.

How long does setup take?

10 to 14 days from discovery to live. We handle the heavy lifting. You mostly review and train your team.

What if a customer wants to call instead of chat?

The chat gives them your number right there. We're not forcing anything. But most won't need to call because the chat answered their question already.
