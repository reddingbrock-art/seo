Yes. We write every response based on how you talk to customers—your language, your standards, your tone. It's you, not a bot.

No. It replaces the silence. Your team still closes jobs and builds relationships. The chat just makes sure no one disappears while you're busy.

What hours does the chat widget work?

24/7. It's always there, whether you're sleeping, on a job, or in a meeting. Evenings and weekends are when you'll see the biggest impact.

How long does setup take?

From discovery call to live widget: 10–14 days. We need a short call to understand your business, then we build and install.

What if a customer wants to call instead of chat?
