FAQ

Lead Follow-Up Questions for Portland Pressure Washing Businesses

How many follow-ups does it send?

It depends on what you set. Most owners use 2–4 follow-ups spaced over 5–7 days. You control the number, timing, and message.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. We write the initial templates based on your voice, then you can tweak them anytime. It'll sound like you, not a bot.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can opt out with one click, and we respect that immediately. You stay compliant and build trust with the people you do reach.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

It goes out within minutes of the lead coming in — usually before you even know they've submitted a form.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in your system. You can reach back out manually whenever you want, or they'll get re-engaged if they show interest later.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. It plugs into whatever lead sources you're using now — your website, Google, Facebook, referrals. Nothing changes except your response time.
