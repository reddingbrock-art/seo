FAQ

Common Questions About Lead Follow-Up for Home Security Companies

How many follow-ups does it send?

You decide. Most home security companies use 4-7 touchpoints over 10-14 days: two calls, two texts, one email, maybe a second call. You control the sequence.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. Every message—call scripts, text templates, emails—is yours to write and approve. We build it, you own it.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They're immediately removed from the sequence. The system respects opt-outs automatically.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

You decide. Most companies set the first call or text for 15 minutes after the lead comes in. You can go faster or adjust based on your team's capacity.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in your database tagged by their last interaction. You can run re-engagement campaigns later, or export the list to your sales team.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Yes. If you're already using a CRM or taking calls manually, this adds a layer that catches leads your team would miss. It works around your current operation.
