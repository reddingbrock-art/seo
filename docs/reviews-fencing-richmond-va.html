FAQ

FAQs About Review Automation for Fencing Contractors

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests only to customers you've actually worked for. Real jobs, real customers, real reviews. Google's algorithm knows the difference.

What if a customer is unhappy?

The system requests reviews after job completion. If there's a problem, handle it directly with the customer first. We're not forcing anyone to review—we're just asking the satisfied ones to share their experience.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google Business Profile, the main driver of local search visibility for fencing companies. That's where prospects are looking.

How does the timing work?

You tell us when a job is complete—usually the day work finishes or first thing the next morning. The request goes out immediately. Customers respond within 24-72 hours typically.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll look at what you're doing now and fill the gaps. If you're only getting reviews occasionally, the system automates what's working and catches the jobs you're missing.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Most fence companies see significantly more reviews once requests are automatic. It depends on your job volume, but expect a noticeable jump within 60 days if you're completing 8+ jobs per month.
