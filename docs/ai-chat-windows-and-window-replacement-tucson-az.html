FAQ

FAQs About AI Chat for Window Replacement Companies

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on your scripts, your common answers, your tone, even your personality quirks. A customer talking to your AI should feel like they're texting your office manager, not a robot.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It flags the conversation and hands it to you with full context. You reply with one message, and the AI learns for next time. Your first few days will have more handoffs than month three.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It replaces the part of your receptionist's job that wastes time—fielding repeat questions, asking qualifying questions, hunting for calendar availability. They can focus on relationship work instead.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. Leads at midnight get instant responses. Leads at 6 AM get instant responses. Your team sleeps. Your business doesn't.

How long does setup take?

Two weeks from discovery call to go-live. Most of that is us building it. Your time investment is one conversation and a few rounds of feedback.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

It's transparent about what it is. But in practice, most customers are fine with instant answers and booked appointments. They care about getting their windows replaced faster than the alternative.
