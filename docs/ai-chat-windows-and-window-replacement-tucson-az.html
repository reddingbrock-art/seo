FAQ

Questions About Website Chat for Windows & Window Replacement

Will the chat widget actually sound like my business?

Yes. We interview you about your voice, your process, and how you talk to customers. The chat uses that tone. It won't sound corporate or robotic—it'll sound like someone from your team.

What happens when the chat can't answer something?

It says so. If a visitor asks something outside the chat's knowledge base, it offers to connect them with your team via text or lets them book a call. The conversation never dies.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It handles after-hours chats and visitors who'd never call anyway. Your team still gets booked calls, qualified leads, and customer data. It just means fewer tire-kicker calls.

What hours does the chat widget work?

24/7. It's answering visitors at midnight on Sunday and on Christmas. Outside your business hours, it still qualifies leads and books appointments for your next available slot.

How long does setup take?

10-14 days from start to live. We need about an hour from you upfront for discovery. After that, our team handles the rest.

What if a customer wants to call instead of chat?

The chat can offer your phone number. But most visitors who come through chat will stay in chat—they chose it over calling for a reason. Your team sees who they are before they ever dial.
