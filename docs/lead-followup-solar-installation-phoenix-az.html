FAQ

Lead Follow-Up Automation for Solar Installers in Phoenix — Frequently Asked Questions

How many follow-ups does it send?

That depends on your sales cycle and what works best for solar leads in Phoenix. We typically set up 3–5 touches across calls, texts, and email over 5–7 days, but we customize it to match your closing timeline.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. You approve all messaging before we go live. Every text, email, and call script reflects your brand and your process—not a generic template.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can opt out at any time, and the system respects that immediately. Compliance is built in—we follow all regulations for multi-channel communication.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Typically within 1–3 minutes of the lead coming in, depending on the time of day and lead source. You can set rules for off-hours delays if you prefer.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in your CRM and get logged as unresponsive after the sequence completes. You can run re-engagement campaigns later or hand them off for nurturing—they don't disappear.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Yes. We integrate with your current lead sources and CRM so the system complements what you're already doing, not replaces it. Your team keeps their workflow.
