FAQ

Automation for Solar Installers: Common Questions

What exactly gets automated?

Inbound call routing, lead capture from web forms, instant acknowledgment to leads, follow-up reminders when calls go unanswered, and automated review requests after job completion. Everything that repeats every day gets handled by the system instead of your team.

Do I need technical skills to run this?

No. You answer calls and close jobs the way you always do. The system runs in the background. If you need to change when review requests go out or adjust a callback time, we show you how—but most owners never need to touch it.

What if my team resists new tools?

Your team doesn't learn software. They just get better notifications and fewer dropped leads. They still answer phones, they still dispatch crews. The system makes their job easier, not harder. Resistance usually disappears after the first week of fewer callbacks they missed.

How is this different from buying software myself?

You'd spend weeks picking a platform, months learning it, and still do most of the setup yourself. We do the setup, integration, and testing. You flip a switch and go live. You're paying for done-for-you, not a tool license.

What does the setup process look like?

One discovery call, one week of building, then go-live. We handle integrations with your existing tools if you have them. You never touch code. By day 10, calls are routing and forms are logging.

What happens if something breaks after launch?

We monitor it for the first two weeks. If something's off, we fix it. You're not on your own. After that, you've got our support line if you need changes, and the system runs on its own.
