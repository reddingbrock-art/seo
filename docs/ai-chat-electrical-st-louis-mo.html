FAQ

Common Questions About AI Chat for St. Louis Electrical Contractors

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on your actual job descriptions, terminology, and how you talk to customers. When someone asks about a panel upgrade, it answers like you would—specific about labor time, code compliance, and next steps.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It says so and captures the customer's info, then flags the conversation for you. You still own the complex questions—the AI just makes sure they don't get ignored while you're in the field.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. If you have one, the AI handles the volume spike and after-hours calls so your receptionist focuses on keeping crews organized, scheduling follow-ups, and relationship work. If you don't have one yet, you don't need to hire one.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. Calls at midnight, 6 AM, or Saturday afternoon all get answered and qualified. You control business hours if you want callbacks only during certain windows, but most electrical shops run it around the clock because winter emergencies and summer overloads don't follow a schedule.

How long does setup take?

10-14 days from kickoff call to live. We handle the integration with your calendar and texting system. You don't install anything yourself.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

Most don't ask, especially if they got through and got booked. If they do ask, the AI is honest about it and emphasizes that you'll call them back right away to confirm details. Transparency builds trust better than pretending.
