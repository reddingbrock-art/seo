FAQ

Review Automation for Waterproofing Companies in San Antonio—Answers to Your Questions

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests only to customers whose jobs are actually complete. Google's system filters out anything suspicious. You're just asking the people you actually worked for.

What if a customer is unhappy?

They won't get a request. The request goes out only after job completion. If there's a problem during the work, your team handles it before that moment arrives.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google primarily. That's where your San Antonio customers are searching, and it's where reviews move the needle on your visibility.

How does the timing work?

You define when a job is 'complete' in your system—could be end of day, next morning, or 24 hours after the last crew member leaves. The request goes out on that schedule automatically.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll replace it with something that actually runs without you thinking about it. If you have a manual system, we'll automate it and get you better compliance.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Most teams see significantly more reviews once requests are automated. The difference: asking at the right moment, consistently, without fail. Exact numbers depend on your job volume and customer base.
