Yes. We interview you and your team about how you talk to customers, what matters to your business, and your tone. The AI uses that voice in every conversation.

No. It handles initial intake and scheduling so your receptionist focuses on closing, follow-up, and customer service. If you don't have one, you don't need to hire one.
