FAQ

Google Reviews & Automation for Irrigation Companies: Questions Answered

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send review requests only to customers who actually completed a job with your crew. Google's algorithm can spot fake reviews, and we're not interested in that anyway. Real reviews from real customers is the only thing that builds sustainable ranking authority.

What if a customer is unhappy?

We're only sending a review request after job completion. If a customer has a complaint, they'll tell you—through the review, through a call, or through direct feedback. The automation doesn't suppress anything. What you do with that feedback is up to you, and Google actually values how you respond to negative reviews more than the volume.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google is the primary focus because that's where 94% of your irrigation customers are looking when they search. We can also set up requests for other platforms depending on your situation—we'll cover that on your discovery call.

How does the timing work?

The request goes out within one hour of your crew marking the job complete on their phone or tablet. That's the window when customers are most likely to remember the work and respond. No delays, no manual triggering.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll look at what you're doing now and build the automation around it. If you're already asking for reviews manually and getting some response, automation will multiply those results by removing the inconsistency. If you're not asking at all, we start from scratch.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

That depends on your job volume and current response rate. If you're doing 40 jobs a month and currently getting 2-3 reviews, automated requests typically pull 12-20 per month. The exact number shows up in your discovery call when we map your actual workflow.
