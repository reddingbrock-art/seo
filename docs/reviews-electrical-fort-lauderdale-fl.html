FAQ

Review Automation Questions for Electrical Businesses

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send review requests only to customers who've completed jobs with you. Google's algorithm and policies are designed to catch fake reviews anyway, and we're not circumventing that—we're just automating the legitimate ask.

What if a customer is unhappy?

The request still goes out to all completed jobs. If a customer leaves a negative review, that's honest feedback and it's searchable on Google regardless. Automation doesn't filter reviews—it just makes sure you're asking consistently so your real satisfaction level shows up in your ratings.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google primarily, since that's where most homeowners search for electricians. We can also send requests to other review platforms depending on your setup.

How does the timing work?

The request sends the same day the job closes or the next morning, depending on how you mark completion in your system. Timing is configurable so you're hitting the satisfaction window.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll work around it. If you're already sending reviews manually, automation replaces that step. If you want to keep some jobs manual, we can segment which jobs trigger automated requests.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

It depends on your response rate, but most businesses see significantly more reviews because the ask is never missed. If you're doing 10 jobs a week and 3-4 used to slip through the cracks, you're suddenly capturing those. The math is: job volume × response rate = your new baseline.
