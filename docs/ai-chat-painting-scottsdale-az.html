FAQ

Questions About the AI Chat Widget for Painting Contractors

Will the chat widget actually sound like my business?

Yes. We don't use generic responses. The chat is trained on how you actually talk to customers—your language, your process, your personality. It sounds like someone from your office, not a robot.

What happens when the chat can't answer something?

It flags the question and sends you a notification so you can jump in, or it books a callback and passes the full conversation to your team. You're always in the loop.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It handles the first conversation—qualifying, booking, answering FAQs—so your receptionist or crew isn't fielding unqualified inquiries. It frees your team up for the work that actually closes estimates.

What hours does the chat widget work?

24/7. It's live whenever someone's on your site, so you're never missing a lead because it's after 5 p.m. or Sunday morning. Appointments book into your calendar automatically.

How long does setup take?

We go live in 10–14 days from your discovery call. That includes configuration, customization, integration with your calendar, and training your team on the dashboard.

What if a customer wants to call instead of chat?

The chat gives them the option to call or book an appointment right there. They choose. Either way, you've got their information and their project details before they hang up or navigate away.
