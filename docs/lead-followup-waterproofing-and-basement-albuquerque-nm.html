FAQ

Lead Follow-Up Questions for Waterproofing & Basement Contractors

How many follow-ups does it send?

That's your choice. Most waterproofing shops use 3 to 5 touches over 7 to 10 days: first call within minutes, text 2 hours later, another call next day, email after that, and a final text before dropping them into a passive list. You control the sequence entirely.

Can I customize what it says?

Completely. Your company name, your phone number, your offer, your tone—it all stays yours. We're not inserting generic language; we're automating your voice.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They're automatically removed from the sequence. We comply with opt-out requests immediately. You'll see that in your dashboard so you don't accidentally follow up again.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Within 2 to 3 minutes of the lead submitting their information. If they call in directly, your team still has to answer, but form submissions and web leads get contacted before your crew finishes the current job.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They move into a passive list where they get occasional check-ins—maybe one text or email every two weeks. That way, when they're actually ready for work (next spring, after winter damage), you're still top-of-mind.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Yes. Your crew still takes calls, still schedules estimates, still runs the jobs. This just makes sure nothing gets forgotten and every lead is being worked while you're busy.
