FAQ

AI Chat for Fencing: Colorado Springs Contractors Ask

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on your estimate questions, your service area, your pricing approach, and the way you actually talk to customers. It answers as your company, not as a generic bot.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It captures the question and customer info, then hands off to your team with a note. Complex requests (custom designs, insurance claims) go straight to your scheduler or estimator.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It replaces the missed calls and the callback queue. If you have a receptionist, they get to focus on follow-up, scheduling, and higher-value work instead of fielding every incoming call.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. Calls come in at 6 a.m. on a Saturday or 11 p.m. on a Thursday, they're answered. You capture leads whenever homeowners decide to call.

How long does setup take?

Live in 10–14 days from your discovery call. We handle the build, training, and integration. No technical work on your end.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

Some will figure it out. That's fine. They still got their question answered in 10 seconds instead of waiting 3 hours for a callback. They still respect you for being responsive. The system transfers them to a human instantly if they ask.
