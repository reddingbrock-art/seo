FAQ

Common Questions About Review Automation for Roofing Companies

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send review requests only to actual customers after completed jobs. Google removes fake reviews anyway — this is designed to capture real feedback from real people who used your service.

What if a customer is unhappy?

The system asks for reviews from customers who completed their jobs with you. If someone had a bad experience, they may not respond or may leave honest feedback. That's valuable. You'll see it and can actually fix problems instead of being blind to them.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google reviews are the priority. That's where roofing customers look first and where it matters most for your ranking. We build around your current workflow.

How does the timing work?

Review requests go out the same day you mark a job complete. You decide the exact timing — immediately, after 24 hours, or whenever fits your process. We set it once and it runs automatically every time.

What if I already have a review process?

We can work with what you've got. If you're already asking and want to scale it, we automate it. If you're not asking at all, we build it from scratch. Either way, the goal is removing the manual work.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Most roofing businesses that actually ask for reviews see significantly higher response rates than those that don't. The exact number depends on your job volume and customer base, but consistency is what matters. We'll show you real numbers once we're live.
