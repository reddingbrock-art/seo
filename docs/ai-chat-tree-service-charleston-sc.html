FAQ

AI Chat for Tree Services: Common Questions

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We learn your service offerings, your neighborhoods, how you price jobs, and what disqualifies a lead. The agent uses your language and decision logic—not generic chatbot speak.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It collects the customer's info and creates a task for your team to follow up. Nothing falls through the cracks. You're not losing leads because the AI doesn't know.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It handles after-hours calls, initial qualification, and scheduling. Your team still handles estimates, customer relationships, and the actual work. You're just not wasting labor on repetitive qualification calls.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. It answers on nights, weekends, and holidays. You set when it can book estimates—during your crew's actual availability—and it never schedules outside your windows.

How long does setup take?

10-14 days from kickoff to live. We handle the technical build. You handle a discovery call and one review session. Then it's running.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

Most don't. But if they ask, the AI tells them. Transparency builds trust. A customer who knows they got a callback at midnight still appreciates the speed—and they're still booked for an estimate.
