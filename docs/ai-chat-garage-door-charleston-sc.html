FAQ

Questions About AI Chat for Garage Door Service in Charleston

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on your service descriptions, how you talk about jobs, your neighborhoods, and your tone. Customers should feel like they're talking to your team, not a generic bot.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It tells the customer it's connecting them to the team and hands off a detailed summary. Your dispatcher or office sees the context—what the customer asked, what they need—so the human conversation starts informed.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. If you have one, they shift from answering calls to closing higher-value work—scheduling follow-ups, managing invoicing, coordinating with your crew. If you don't have one, the AI eliminates the need to hire.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. Calls and form submissions come in anytime. You decide which hours trigger instant appointment booking versus 'we'll confirm in the morning' handoffs. Most garage door shops let it book during normal service hours.

How long does setup take?

10-14 days from discovery to going live. We handle the heavy lifting. You review and approve before we switch your number over.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

Transparency is built in. The system discloses it's an AI agent upfront. Most customers don't care as long as they get their appointment booked fast and talk to a human when they need to.
