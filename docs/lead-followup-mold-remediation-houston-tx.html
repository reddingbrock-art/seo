FAQ

Lead Follow-Up Questions for Houston Mold Remediation Owners

How many follow-ups does it send?

That's up to you. Most mold companies send 3-5 touches over 7-10 days: an initial text, a follow-up text 24 hours later, a phone call attempt, and 1-2 reminder messages. You define the cadence and message content.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. Every message is written in your voice and can include your specific service details, pricing mention, or branding. We provide templates; you control what goes out.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They're removed from the sequence immediately. The system respects opt-outs and keeps you compliant with messaging regulations.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

It depends on your settings. You can have it send immediately, within 15 minutes, or on a schedule you choose. Most companies doing it right send the first message within 30 minutes of lead capture.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in the system for your defined follow-up window. After your final scheduled message, they're marked inactive. You can re-engage them later if you want, or let them go.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. It sits on top of however you're already running things. If you're using a CRM or scheduling tool, we integrate with it. It handles the first several follow-ups so your team can focus on closing and serving.
