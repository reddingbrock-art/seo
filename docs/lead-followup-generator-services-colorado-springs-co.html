FAQ

Lead Follow-Up System FAQs for Colorado Springs Generator Services

How many follow-ups does it send?

Typically 3–5 touches over 10–14 days, depending on how you want it structured. More if a lead engages—the system can keep reaching until conversion or opt-out.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. Every message is written for your business and approved by you before it goes live. You control the tone, offer, and timing.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They're removed immediately. The system respects opt-outs and logs them—you're never sending to someone who doesn't want to hear from you.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Usually within 5–15 minutes of submission or inbound call, depending on your settings. Fast enough that it feels like you answered.

What happens to leads that never respond?

The system completes its sequence and stops. You can review them later or they stay in a list for manual outreach if you want to circle back.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. It runs in parallel to how you're already working. Your team doesn't change—the system just makes sure nothing falls through the cracks.
