FAQ

AI Chat for Foundation Repair: Questions Answered

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on your language, your service offerings, your neighborhoods, and how you actually talk to customers. It sounds like someone from your team, not a bot.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It tells the customer it needs to get you involved and captures their info so you can follow up. It doesn't make things up or pretend to know something it doesn't.

Does this replace my receptionist?

It replaces the part of your receptionist's job that's answering calls and booking when no one's in the office. If you have office staff, they can focus on follow-up and customer service instead of phone duty.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. It answers calls and messages at night, on weekends, and during your busy season when your team can't pick up.

How long does setup take?

Live in 10-14 days from discovery to first call answered. Most owners see it working by week two.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

It's honest about it. Customers actually prefer getting an instant answer and knowing they're talking to AI over leaving a voicemail and waiting. The transparency builds trust.
