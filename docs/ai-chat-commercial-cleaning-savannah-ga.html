FAQ

Questions About AI Chat Agents for Commercial Cleaning

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We configure it with your voice, your service offerings, and your tone. It answers questions the way you would — friendly, direct, and focused on getting the job scheduled.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It collects their contact info and flags the conversation for you with all the context. You follow up on anything outside its scope, but most questions get handled without your involvement.

Does this replace my receptionist?

It handles incoming calls and form submissions so your team isn't distracted during the job. If you have a receptionist, they can focus on customer service and scheduling instead of answering phones all day.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. Nights, weekends, holidays — it answers every inbound lead whenever they contact you. Your team responds to qualified leads during business hours.

How long does setup take?

10-14 days from start to live. We handle the configuration; you don't need technical skills. You're answering leads before the month ends.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

They usually don't notice until you mention it. But if they do, it's fine — we're upfront about it if asked. Most people just care that their question got answered and their appointment got booked.
