FAQ

AI Chat & Phone Automation for Window Replacement Businesses in Atlanta

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We write the exact words your team uses—your objection answers, your scheduling language, your tone. It doesn't sound like a robot reading a script. It sounds like someone from your office.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It captures the question, gets the caller's info, and routes it to you or your team immediately. Nothing falls through the cracks.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. If you have one, this handles the calls while they focus on admin, follow-up, and customer service. If you don't, this stops you from having to hire one.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. It's on when you're sleeping, when your team's on jobs, on Sundays, holidays—whenever a homeowner decides to call.

How long does setup take?

10–14 days from discovery call to live. You'll be taking calls through the system within two weeks.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

We don't hide it. If they ask, the AI tells them the truth and offers to route them to your team. Transparency builds trust. Most customers don't care as long as their problem gets solved and their appointment gets booked.
