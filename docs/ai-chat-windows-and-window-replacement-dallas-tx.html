FAQ

Questions About AI Chat for Windows & Window Replacement in Dallas

Will the chat widget actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on how you talk to customers—your service areas, your pricing approach, the way you handle objections. When a homeowner chats with you, they're talking to your business, not a generic bot.

What happens when the chat can't answer something?

It collects the question and their contact info, then flags the conversation for you. You get notified, and you can follow up with a call or text. No lead goes unanswered.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It replaces the phone calls you're losing to competitors and the form submissions that go nowhere. Your receptionist still handles calls—but now they're only getting serious inquiries instead of tire-kickers or wrong numbers.

What hours does the chat widget work?

24/7. Your site's always capturing conversations. You respond during business hours. Visitors see when you'll get back to them, and they get a calendar link for booking whenever it works for them.

How long does setup take?

10–14 days from discovery call to live. You're not waiting weeks. We move fast because every day your site goes silent is a lead your competitor's chat just grabbed.

What if a customer wants to call instead of chat?

The chat has your number. They can call whenever they want. But we've found most people prefer the chat—it's faster, they can do it while they're looking at their windows, and they don't feel like they're interrupting anything.
