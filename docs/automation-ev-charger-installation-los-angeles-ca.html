What exactly gets automated?

Inbound calls, text messages, and web form submissions. Each one gets answered, qualified based on your criteria, scheduled into your calendar, and sent a confirmation. Your team sees only ready-to-book leads.

Do I need technical skills to run this?

No. You get a dashboard where you can see all incoming leads, their details, and their appointment status. Everything else runs in the background. Your team operates it the way they'd use email.

What if my team resists new tools?

Your team doesn't operate the automation—they just check a dashboard and run installations. They actually have fewer interruptions, not more. Most crews prefer it immediately.

How is this different from buying software myself?

You'd buy the software, figure out the setup, train your team, manage ongoing changes, and still have to answer calls manually or hire someone. We build it, run it, and support it as a done-for-you service.

What does the setup process look like?

One discovery call with you, one week of us building the system in the background, feedback and testing, then live. You don't need to hire anyone or learn new platforms. We handle the whole thing.

What happens if something breaks after launch?

We monitor the system continuously. If a call isn't routing, a text isn't sending, or a calendar sync breaks, we catch it and fix it. You just keep running your business.
