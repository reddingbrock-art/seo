FAQ

Questions About Review Automation for Locksmiths

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send review requests only to customers you've completed work for. Google filters out fake reviews anyway. Real customers from real jobs are what matter.

What if a customer is unhappy?

The system sends a request to everyone. If someone leaves a negative review, that's real feedback—and Google values authenticity. You can respond to any review publicly.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google reviews are the priority because that's where customers search for locksmiths in Phoenix. We focus there.

How does the timing work?

The request goes out automatically within hours of the job completing. You can set the exact timing—same day, next morning, whatever fits your workflow.

What if I already have a review process?

We map it out on the discovery call. Most owners send requests manually or ask their team to remember. We just automate what you're already trying to do.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

It depends on your job volume. Most owners see significantly more reviews once the ask is automatic and happens at the right moment. The system removes friction—that's the difference.
