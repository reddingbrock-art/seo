FAQ

Lead Follow-Up System Questions for Roofing Owners

How many follow-ups does it send?

We design a sequence based on your process. Most roofing companies use 2-3 touches over 5-7 days, but it's completely customizable to match how you sell.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. We write it first based on what you tell us, you approve it, and we can adjust anytime. It stays on-brand and on-message.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can opt out anytime. The system respects that immediately. You won't hear from them again through the automation, but you can always reach out manually if you choose.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Within minutes of them calling or submitting a form. You set the timing—some owners want it instant, others prefer a 15-minute delay to check it first.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in your system but stop getting contacted after the sequence ends. You can see the full list and decide whether to call them yourself or move on.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. It doesn't replace your team—it supports them. Your guys still take calls, still close deals. This just makes sure no lead falls through the cracks while they're busy.
