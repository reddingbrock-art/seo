FAQ

Lead Follow-Up Questions for Henderson Window & Replacement Contractors

How many follow-ups does it send?

Usually 2 to 3 over the course of a week. First one within minutes, second one a few days later if they didn't respond. The exact sequence is built around how your business moves.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. We write the messages so they sound like you and speak to what your leads actually care about—price, timeline, warranty, energy savings. You review and approve everything before it goes live.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They're removed immediately. We respect that. Most leads don't ask because the messages are helpful, not pushy, but it's their choice and we honor it.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Within minutes of the lead submitting an inquiry. Most of your competitors are checking their phone hours later. You're already in the conversation.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in your system for your team to follow up with manually if you want, or they drop off after the automated sequence. You control what happens next.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. It's built to fit how you already work. Your estimator, your scheduler, your sales process—nothing changes except that leads are contacted faster and pre-qualified before they reach your team.
