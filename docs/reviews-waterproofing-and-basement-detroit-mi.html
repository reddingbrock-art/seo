FAQ

Common Questions About Review Automation for Waterproofing & Basement Contractors

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We only send requests to customers after you've actually completed work for them. Google sees real customers reviewing real jobs. Fake reviews violate Google's terms and we don't do that.

What if a customer is unhappy?

They won't post. An unhappy customer won't leave a review just because you asked. The system sends the request to everyone—only satisfied customers follow through. If there's a real problem, it's better you know about it before they post negatively.

Which platforms does this work on?

We focus on Google Reviews because that's where homeowners look first when they need a waterproofing contractor. Facebook reviews are included as a secondary channel.

How does the timing work?

The request goes out the same day the job is marked complete in your system. Customers have 48 hours to respond. Most who are going to review do it within the first 12 hours while the work is fresh in their mind.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll work around it. If you're already collecting reviews manually, we can either replace that with automation or layer in additional requests for jobs that slip through the cracks. No conflict.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

It depends on your current review rate and how many jobs you close per month. Most contractors see a significant jump in review volume within 30 days because we're capturing customers at the right moment instead of hoping they remember to post later.
