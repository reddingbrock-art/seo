FAQ

Landscaping Review Automation FAQs for Houston Contractors

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We only request reviews from customers who've actually worked with you. Google's detection systems are sophisticated, and fake reviews hurt your credibility and rankings. We only send requests to real people tied to your jobs.

What if a customer is unhappy?

That's the point. If someone's upset, we route that feedback to you privately first so you can fix it before it becomes a public review. You get the chance to make it right.

Which platforms does this work on?

We focus on Google primarily, since that's where your Houston customers are searching. We can also integrate with other review sites depending on your goals.

How does the timing work?

We send requests a day or two after the job's completed, when the work is fresh in their mind but they've had time to see the full result. You set the preferences; we automate the rest.
