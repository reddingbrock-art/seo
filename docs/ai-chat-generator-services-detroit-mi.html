FAQ

Generator Services AI Chat Agent Questions

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on your actual language—your service area names, how you describe packages, your tone. It sounds like someone from your team who knows the job, not a bot script.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It escalates to your dispatcher or a team member with full context captured. No customer transferred to a dead line. The handoff is warm and the human's got everything they need.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It replaces voicemail and callback delays. If you have a receptionist, they handle follow-up tasks, complex calls, and relationship work. If you don't, the AI handles intake so your dispatcher has room to breathe.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. Nights, weekends, holidays—it's answering and taking details. You can set business hours routing, but the system's always live if you want it to be.

How long does setup take?

10–14 days from discovery call to live. We handle the integration. You don't touch the backend. Takes about 2 hours of your time across the whole process.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

Most don't notice. But if they ask, the AI tells the truth—'I'm an automated system that can help get your info in the queue.' Then it keeps solving their problem. Transparency builds trust.
