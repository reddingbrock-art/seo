FAQ

Automation for Electrical Contractors: Common Questions

What exactly gets automated?

Incoming phone calls, web form submissions, text inquiries, and chat messages all get instant responses. The system qualifies the job, confirms the appointment, and logs everything into your dispatch board—no manual entry needed.

Do I need technical skills to run this?

No. Your team logs in, sees qualified leads, and books jobs. We handle all the backend logic and integrations. If you can use email, you can use this.

What if my team resists new tools?

We build it to make their job easier, not harder. Less admin, clearer job details, fewer callbacks—they'll feel the relief in the first week. We also provide live training and stick around during the launch.

How is this different from buying software myself?

We don't sell you software and wish you luck. We build the entire system for you, connect it to your existing tools, tune it to how you actually run jobs, and maintain it. You get done-for-you automation, not a platform login.

What does the setup process look like?

One discovery call, about two weeks of build time while we set everything up, then a go-live week where we're live with your team. Full launch to handling real leads takes about three weeks from start to finish.

What happens if something breaks after launch?

We support you. If a connection drops, a workflow needs tweaking, or you want to add a new process, you reach out and we fix it. Maintenance and updates are part of what we do.
