FAQ

Lead Follow-Up Automation: Questions from Miami Waterproofing & Basement Contractors

How many follow-ups does it send?

It depends on your sequence, but typically 3–5 touches over 7–10 days: text or email on day 0, day 1, day 3, and day 7. You control the exact sequence and timing.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. We write the initial templates based on your brand and process, and you can edit them anytime. Every message reflects your voice.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They click unsubscribe or tell you to stop, and they're removed immediately. The system respects their preference. Compliance is built in.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Within 5 minutes of the lead coming in. No waiting for your crew to see it or remember to call back.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in the system and cycle through your follow-up sequence. After the final touch, they move to a nurture list you can check on later. No lead falls off the map.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. It feeds into whatever CRM or dispatch system you're already using. It's an add-on that scales your response capacity, not a replacement for your team.
