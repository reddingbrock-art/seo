FAQ

Lead Follow-Up Questions for Houston Plumbing Businesses

How many follow-ups does it send?

We customize the sequence to your business, but typically 3–5 touches across SMS, email, and phone over 7–10 days. Once a lead books or opts out, it stops.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. We write your initial scripts during setup and align everything with your brand voice, service areas (Woodlands, Pearland, Spring Branch, etc.), and pricing. You approve before it goes live.

What if a lead asks to stop?

One click and they're removed from the sequence. We handle compliance automatically—no legal risk, no manual work on your end.

Does this work with my current CRM?

If you're using basic tools, we integrate or migrate you to GoHighLevel. If you already have a system, we can sync the two. Either way, it's handled during onboarding.
