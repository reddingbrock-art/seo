FAQ

Lead Follow-Up Questions for Houston Plumbing Owners

How many follow-ups does it send?

Typically 3–5 touches over 7 days, depending on how you want it configured. Each one is timed to catch leads at the right moment without feeling pushy.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. We write the initial messaging based on your business, and you can revise it before go-live. Your voice, your terms, your brand.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can unsubscribe with one click, and the system respects it immediately. You stay compliant with texting regulations automatically.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Typically within 3–5 minutes of the lead coming in. Fast enough to catch them while they're still shopping.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in your system. You can see the full history—who opened messages, who replied but went quiet, who never engaged. Helps you spot patterns and retarget later.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Absolutely. It integrates with how you're already taking leads. No switching systems or retraining your team.
