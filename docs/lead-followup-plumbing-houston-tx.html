FAQ

Common Questions About Lead Follow-Up Automation for Plumbing

How many follow-ups does it send?

You control it. Typically we set up 2-3 touches over 48 hours—an immediate text, an email the next morning, and one more text or email if they haven't engaged. After that, they move to a slower nurture sequence. You can customize the count and timing for your business.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. Every message—text, email, AI response—is written by you or our team based on your actual voice and what your leads need to hear. We're not using some generic plumbing script. It sounds like *your* business.

What if a lead asks to stop?

They can opt out with one reply, and they're unsubscribed immediately. We respect that. The system logs it, and you never message them again about that service type. It's built into the setup from day one.

Does this work with my current CRM?

If you're using GoHighLevel, yes—it integrates directly. If you're on something else, we can either pull data in or rebuild your follow-up inside GoHighLevel. Either way, your leads stay where they are, and the automation wraps around your existing workflow.
