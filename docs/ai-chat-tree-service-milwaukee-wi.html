FAQ

FAQ: AI Chat Agents for Milwaukee Tree Service Businesses

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on your tone, your service details, and how you actually talk to customers. It won't sound robotic or overly formal — it'll sound like someone from your team answering a basic question.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It flags the conversation and hands it to you with full context. You jump in and take over the chat, so the customer never realizes they were talking to an AI. You also get to see what it couldn't handle, so we improve it over time.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It replaces the repetitive part of their job — answering the same questions 50 times a day. Your team now focuses on closing leads and managing customers instead of fielding basic inquiries.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. Customers can get answers at 10 p.m. on a Sunday when you're not in the office. During your business hours, you're jumping into conversations that need your judgment.

How long does setup take?

Discovery to live is typically 10–14 days. Some of that is us asking questions and training the system. The rest is testing and making sure it sounds right before it touches your customers.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

Some will figure it out, and that's fine. The AI doesn't pretend to be a person — it's transparent about what it is. Most customers just care that they got an answer fast. And if they want to talk to you, they ask, and the conversation moves to a human immediately.
