FAQ

Septic & Drain Review Automation: Questions Answered

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests only to actual customers you've served. They choose whether to reply. The reviews that land are real feedback from real jobs.

What if a customer is unhappy?

Unhappy customers won't leave reviews through this system—they're just as unlikely to leave one manually. If someone has a problem, they'll tell you directly, and you handle it before a review request goes out.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google Reviews is the priority. That's where homeowners search and make decisions. Other platforms can be layered in based on your needs.

How does the timing work?

A review request goes out automatically the same day or next business day after you mark a job complete. Customers usually reply within 24–48 hours if they're going to reply at all.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll work around it. If you're asking for reviews manually and want to stop, we take over. If you want to keep your process, we won't duplicate it.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Most shops see a significant uptick because requests are actually getting sent. If you're running 10 jobs a week and previously asked for reviews on 2, expect to see a real difference within the first month.
