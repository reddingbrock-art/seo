FAQ

Common Questions About Roofing Review Automation in Baltimore

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send review requests only to customers you've completed work for. Google will reject fake reviews anyway. We're only asking real customers who actually hired you.

What if a customer is unhappy?

The system sends requests to completed jobs. If someone's unhappy, they won't leave a review—or they'll leave an honest one. That's fine. One bad review among 40 good ones won't hurt you. And it keeps your profile authentic.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google primarily. That's where roofing customers search. We can configure it for other platforms, but Google's where your visibility problem lives.

How does the timing work?

The request goes out the day you mark the job complete. Customers get one request via email or text—your choice. If they don't respond in 10 days, it stops. No spam.

What if I already have a review process?

This replaces it. You stop doing the manual ask, and the system does it for you. If you've got a process that's working, we talk about that first—but most roofing crews tell us they're not getting consistent responses anyway.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Most of your completed jobs already have customers willing to review. You're probably getting 20-30% of jobs as reviews right now if you're asking manually. Automation usually gets you to 50-60%. The number depends on your job volume and how many you're currently missing.
