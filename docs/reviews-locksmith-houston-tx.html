FAQ

Google Review Automation FAQs for Locksmiths

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We only send requests to customers with a completed job in your system. Google's algorithms catch fake review patterns—we're building your reputation the way that actually sticks.

What if a customer is unhappy?

The system asks for feedback after job completion, not before. If a customer had a bad experience, they won't be pressured—and honestly, a legitimate negative review is better for credibility than radio silence. You can respond to it professionally.

Which platforms does this work on?

We focus on Google reviews because that's where Houston customers go first. We can also configure it for other platforms depending on your business goals.

How does the timing work?

Requests go out immediately or within hours of job completion, depending on your preference. The customer gets the request when the experience is fresh—that's when response rates are highest.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll either improve what you've got or replace it entirely. If you're doing reviews manually, we automate it. If you have another system, we can often integrate or run parallel to it.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

It depends on your job volume and current ask rate. Most shops see significantly more reviews once requests are systematic—but the real win is consistency. You'll get them every month, not sporadically.
