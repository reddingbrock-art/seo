FAQ

Common Questions About Review Automation for Locksmiths

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests only to customers after actual job completion. Google detects fake reviews and penalizes profiles that use them. We're building real proof from real work.

What if a customer is unhappy?

You'll see the request like any other. If they leave a negative review, you can respond professionally and address the issue. Real feedback helps you improve and shows future customers you stand behind your work.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google Business Profile is the priority—that's where your customers search. The system integrates with your existing tools so reviews flow into your inbox automatically.

How does the timing work?

The request goes out automatically when a job is marked complete in your system. You decide the timing window—immediately after, next morning, whatever fits your workflow. Customers respond faster when the work is fresh in their mind.

What if I already have a review process?

If you're manually texting or emailing customers now, automation just replaces that work. If you're not asking at all, it jumpstarts the whole process. Either way, you're removing the step that gets forgotten.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

It depends on your job volume and how many customers you're currently asking. Most shops see a significant increase in review velocity because the ask becomes consistent and automatic instead of sporadic or forgotten.
