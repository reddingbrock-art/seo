FAQ

AI Chat Questions for Memphis Fencing Companies

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on recordings of your calls and your real estimate process. It uses your terminology, your pricing approach, and your tone. Customers shouldn't feel the difference.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It tells them the truth—that you'll follow up with specifics—and books a time for you to call them back. It never makes up answers or oversells. That keeps trust intact.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It replaces the voicemail and callback delays. If you have a receptionist, she now focuses on prep work, scheduling, and follow-up instead of fielding 40 estimate calls a day.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. Calls and texts come in at midnight, 6 AM, Saturday afternoon—it answers them all. You decide when you want to call customers back.

How long does setup take?

10–14 days from discovery call to live. We need recordings and details from you, then we build and test. Most owners are live before their next big seasonal push.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

It's straightforward about it. Transparency builds trust more than pretending. And by then, the lead is already qualified and booked. The conversation moves forward naturally.
