FAQ

Common Questions About Review Automation for Fire & Water Restoration

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. The system requests reviews from actual customers who completed jobs with you. Real people, real work, real feedback.

What if a customer is unhappy?

We're only sending requests to customers after work is finished. If they're unhappy, that's a conversation you should have directly—the system doesn't suppress or filter negative experiences. It just asks for honest feedback from people who used your service.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google, Yelp, and the major review sites your customers actually use when searching for fire and water restoration. We focus on the ones that move leads.

How does the timing work?

The request goes out automatically after the job is marked complete—usually within 24 hours. That window is when people are most likely to remember the experience and actually write something.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll talk through what you're doing and either integrate with it or replace it if the system is cleaner and faster. Most owners find they're not being consistent, which is where automation wins.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Most owners see a significant increase once the system runs. The exact number depends on your job volume and how many customers were willing to review but never got asked. That's the conversation we have on the discovery call.
