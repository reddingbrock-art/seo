FAQ

AI Chat for Garage Door Companies: Common Questions

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We listen to how you talk to customers—the language you use, what you prioritize, how you qualify jobs. The AI mimics that. Someone calling in doesn't feel like they're talking to a bot. They feel like they're talking to your front desk.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It flags the call or chat for your team. If a customer asks something outside its knowledge base, the system knows to hand it off immediately instead of guessing. You control what it can handle and what requires a human.

Does this replace my receptionist?

Not necessarily. It replaces the parts of the job that break your business—missed calls, forms nobody looks at, callbacks that happen too late. If you have a receptionist, this makes them more effective. If you don't, this is way cheaper than hiring one.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. It answers calls at 2 AM on a Sunday just as well as it answers them at 9 AM on Tuesday. Your team only gets notified during the hours you set, so your inbox stays sane.

How long does setup take?

10-14 days from your kickoff call to the system being live and taking real calls. That includes discovery, configuration, integration with your calendar, and testing. You're not waiting months.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

It doesn't matter. They got their question answered, their job got scheduled, and they're moving forward. We've found that customers care way more about getting a fast response than they do about whether it came from a person or a system.
