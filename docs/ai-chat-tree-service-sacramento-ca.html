FAQ

AI Chat and Call Handling for Tree Service Businesses

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on your brand voice, service areas, and the way you actually talk to customers. It's not generic—it knows your crew's capabilities, seasonal windows, and your pricing approach.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It gathers the information and flags it for you. The caller leaves a message, gets a confirmation of next steps, and your team handles the follow-up. Nothing falls through the cracks.

Does this replace my receptionist?

It replaces the constant phone interruptions and missed calls. If you have a receptionist, they stop fielding repetitive questions and focus on admin work or dispatch. If you don't, you've just hired someone who never takes a break.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. Calls at 6 a.m., 11 p.m., or 2 a.m. get answered. You decide when it's available to book estimates—you can set business hours or stay open round-the-clock during storm season.

How long does setup take?

Discovery to live is 10–14 days. Most of that's us building and training. Your involvement is minimal—mostly just reviewing the script and voice before we launch.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

We're transparent about it from the start. The AI introduces itself clearly and professionally. Most customers don't care as long as they get their question answered and an estimate booked fast. That's what they called for anyway.
