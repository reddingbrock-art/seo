Garage Door Automation Questions Answered

What exactly gets automated?

Lead capture from your website, text, and calls; instant response with qualification questions; automatic appointment booking into your calendar; review requests after job completion. Anything repetitive that your team does manually gets handed to the system.

Do I need technical skills to run this?

No. You log in, see your leads, approve dispatch assignments, and run your business. If your team can use a phone and calendar, they can use this. We handle the backend.

What if my team resists new tools?

We train everyone during setup, and the system works alongside their current workflow—not against it. Most teams see the benefit immediately when leads stop being missed and callbacks get easier. Buy-in happens fast when the friction disappears.

How is this different from buying software myself?

You could buy tools individually and wire them together yourself, but you'll spend weeks integrating and troubleshooting. We handle the entire build, testing, and integration. You get a working system in 10–14 days, not months.

What does the setup process look like?

One call to understand your process, then we build and test with your team. Setup takes 1–2 weeks from discovery to launch. You're not waiting months or dealing with IT projects.

What happens if something breaks after launch?

We monitor it and fix it. You've got support built in. If a lead doesn't capture or a booking doesn't sync, you call us and it's fixed that day—not a ticket queue you chase for a week.
