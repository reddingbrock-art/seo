FAQ

Lead Follow-Up Questions for Paving & Asphalt Owners

How many follow-ups does it send?

Typically 3–5 touches over 7–10 days (text, email, or both depending on what you have). We customize the sequence based on your preferences and what your leads respond to.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. We write the first version based on your business voice, and you approve every message before it goes live. After that, you can tweak or swap messaging anytime.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They're removed from the sequence immediately. We handle the unsubscribe request and keep you compliant—no manual work on your end.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Within 1–3 minutes of the lead submitting a request or calling in. The faster you respond, the higher your conversion rate.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They drop out of the sequence after the final touch. You can still see them in your history, but we don't keep hammering unresponsive contacts.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Yes. We integrate with your scheduling software, CRM, or however you track jobs today. The system hands off booked leads to your team—nothing changes about how you operate.
