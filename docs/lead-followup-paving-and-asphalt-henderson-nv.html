FAQ

Lead Follow-Up Automation: Questions from Paving Contractors

How many follow-ups does it send?

Usually 3–5 touches over 2–3 weeks. You set the number and timing. Most jobs get an estimate before the sequence is done.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. You write the message once, we deploy it for every lead. It sounds like you, not a template.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can unsubscribe. One click, they're off the list. You stay compliant.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Within 5 minutes of the lead coming in, if you want it that fast. You control the timing.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in your queue marked as 'no response.' You can reach out manually later, or let them age out. Your choice.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Yes. It plugs into your current phone number, email, and CRM. No replacement required.
