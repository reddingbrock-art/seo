FAQ

Common Questions About AI Chat for Generator Service Businesses

Will the chat widget actually sound like my business?

Yes. We write the voice and personality based on how you talk to customers. It answers questions about your specific service areas, equipment brands you work on, and your booking process—not generic chatbot nonsense.

What happens when the chat can't answer something?

It escalates gracefully. If a question is outside its scope, it captures the customer's info and lets them know someone from your team will follow up within X hours. No dead ends.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It replaces the moment you lose a lead because nobody's answering the website. Your team still handles calls and in-depth conversations. This just qualifies them first.

What hours does the chat widget work?

24/7. It works nights, weekends, holidays—whenever a visitor lands on your site. You can set it to offer callback scheduling during your team's actual availability.

How long does setup take?

Ten to fourteen days from our first call. We handle the writing, integration, and testing. You review once. Then it goes live.

What if a customer wants to call instead of chat?

They can. The chat offers both options—continue chatting or request a callback. Whoever comes in through either path, you've already captured their info and their problem.
