FAQ

AI Chat for Plumbing Companies: Common Questions

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on your language, your service areas, your pricing, and how your team actually talks to customers. It's your voice, not a generic bot.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It's honest about it and transfers the call to you or offers to take their information and have you call them back. No frustrated customers arguing with a chatbot.

Does this replace my receptionist?

It replaces the phone-answering part. Your team stops managing hold queues and can focus on sales, job quality, and running the business. If you have a receptionist, they do higher-value work.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. Nights, weekends, holidays—whenever a customer calls or fills out your form, it's answered and routed.

How long does setup take?

Live and answering calls within 10-14 days of your initial call. Most of that is us building and you reviewing. The actual switch-over is quick.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

They get a smooth transfer to you, or they're already booked and moving forward. The goal is to capture the appointment, not hide the technology. Most customers just care that their problem got solved and their call was answered fast.
