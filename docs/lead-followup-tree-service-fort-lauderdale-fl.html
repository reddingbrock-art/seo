FAQ

Lead Follow-Up System FAQs for Tree Service Owners

How many follow-ups does it send?

It depends on your business. We typically set up an initial response, then 1 to 3 additional touches if the lead doesn't respond — spread out over a few days. You decide the sequence.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. We write it in your voice using your service details, guarantees, and personality. It's your system from day one.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can opt out, and we respect that immediately. They're removed from follow-up sequences. No harassment, no compliance risk.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Within minutes of the lead coming in — whether it's a form submission, call, or chat. Most leads see a response before you finish the current job.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in your system and show up in your dashboard. You can review them, reach out personally if you want, or let them age out. It's your call.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Absolutely. It plugs into however you take leads now — your phone, your website, your forms. Your crew's process doesn't change. The automation just fills the gaps.
