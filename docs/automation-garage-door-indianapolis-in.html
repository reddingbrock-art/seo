FAQ

Automation for Garage Door Service: Questions Answered

What exactly gets automated?

Lead intake from all sources (website forms, text, phone), instant assignment to available techs, confirmation texts to customers, and review requests after job completion. Your dispatcher's routine becomes: jobs arrive assigned, tech confirms, job closes. That's the loop.

Do I need technical skills to run this?

No. You don't touch the backend. Your team logs in, sees their jobs, updates status, closes the ticket. It runs itself. We handle all the integration and any changes you need down the road.

What if my team resists new tools?

They won't resist something that makes their day easier. Once they see jobs showing up already assigned and customers confirming before they arrive, adoption is fast. We also handle onboarding calls so there's no guessing.

How is this different from buying software myself?

You'd buy a platform, spend weeks setting it up, train your team, troubleshoot when it breaks, and manage upgrades. We do all of that. You get a working system in 10 days, not 10 weeks, and you don't hire someone to babysit it.

What does the setup process look like?

Discovery call to map your operation. We connect your existing tools (website, phone, scheduling if you have it). We test on a few live leads. You give feedback. We go live. Total time: about two weeks from first call to full operation.

What happens if something breaks after launch?

You reach out and we fix it. Issues are rare because we test before going live, but if something does slip through, it's our job to tighten it. You're not troubleshooting on your own.
