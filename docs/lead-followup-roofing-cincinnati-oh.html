FAQ

Common Questions About Lead Follow-Up for Roofing

How many follow-ups does it send?

We build the sequence based on your business. Most roofing companies use 3–5 touches over 2–3 days. You control how many and when.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. We write the initial copy with you, and you can adjust messaging anytime. It should sound like your company, not a robot.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They're removed immediately. The system respects opt-outs the same way you would if you were calling by hand.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Within 1–2 minutes of the lead coming in, depending on how you set it. Most roofing companies set it to 3–5 minutes to feel personal without losing the speed advantage.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They get the full follow-up sequence you set up. After that, they stay in your system so you can review them later or follow up manually if you choose.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. The system feeds leads to your team, your CRM, or wherever you're already working them. It replaces the chaos part, not your process.
