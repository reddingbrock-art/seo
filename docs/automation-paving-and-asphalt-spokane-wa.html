What exactly gets automated?

Inbound calls and web form submissions. The system answers, qualifies leads, books appointments, and sends you confirmations. It doesn't touch your job scheduling, invoicing, or crew coordination—just the front door.

Do I need technical skills to run this?

No. You receive scheduled appointments in your calendar and phone notifications. Your office staff can update availability or add notes, but nothing technical is required to operate it.

The system is invisible to your crew. They see booked jobs in their usual schedule. Your office team gets a single dashboard with incoming leads and appointments—most contractors find it simpler than their old process.

How is this different from buying software myself?

We configure it, train your team, and monitor it for you. You're not managing another vendor or learning another platform. We own the setup and ongoing support so you stay focused on paving.

What does the setup process look like?

One discovery call, then 10–14 days of configuration and training. We connect your phone lines, set up form handlers, and test with your team before go-live.

What happens if something breaks after launch?

We monitor the system and handle fixes. If a call line drops or a form stops routing, we know about it and fix it before you do. You've got a dedicated point of contact for support.
