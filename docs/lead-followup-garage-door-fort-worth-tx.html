FAQ

Lead Follow-Up Questions for Garage Door Companies

How many follow-ups does it send?

Typically 3–5 touches over 7–10 days. We customize the sequence based on your process and how quickly you want to close.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. Every message is written in your voice, not a generic template. You approve the wording before it goes live.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can opt out immediately and they're removed from the sequence. Compliance is built in.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Within minutes of them submitting a form or call coming in. Not hours.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in your system tagged as unresponsive, so you can see the full picture of lead quality. No data gets thrown away.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. It plugs into whatever system you're already using—your CRM, your call center, your forms. No rip-and-replace needed.
