FAQ

Google Review Automation FAQs for Miami Waterproofing & Basement Companies

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests only to customers whose jobs you've actually completed. Google's system filters out anything suspicious, and real reviews from real customers are the only ones that stick.

What if a customer is unhappy?

The system doesn't force anyone to leave a review. It sends a request after job completion. If a customer's dissatisfied, they won't respond, and you never get stuck with a bad review you didn't earn.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google Reviews is the core. We also support requests to other platforms like Yelp and industry-specific review sites depending on your needs. Most waterproofing owners focus on Google first since that's where homeowners search.

How does the timing work?

The request goes out automatically after a job is marked complete in your system. Timing is usually within 24–48 hours, when satisfaction is highest and the work is fresh in the customer's mind.

What if I already have a review process?

If your current process is inconsistent or manual, this replaces it with automation so nothing falls through the cracks. If you're already systematized, we hook into your workflow so it works alongside what you're doing, not against it.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Most waterproofing companies see a significant increase because requests are consistent and timely. The exact number depends on your job volume and response rate, but most owners report 3–4x growth in the first few months compared to manual follow-ups.
