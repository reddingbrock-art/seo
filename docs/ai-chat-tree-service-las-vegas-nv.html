FAQ

Common Questions About AI Chat for Tree Service in Las Vegas

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on your service offerings, how you describe what you do, and your service area. A caller won't know they're talking to AI unless they ask—and by then, they've already given you the job details you need.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It escalates to your team immediately. Complex questions, custom quotes, or anything outside its scope goes straight to a crew lead or your dispatcher as a priority ticket.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. If you have one, they're now handling callbacks, followups, and customer relationships instead of answering the same five questions 30 times a day. If you don't have one, you gain the intake capacity without the hire.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. Calls that come in after hours or on weekends get captured, logged, and wait for your team to route in the morning. You're never losing a late-night storm damage call again.

How long does setup take?

10–14 days from discovery call to live. We handle the integration with your calendar and dispatch system. Your only job is reviewing the AI's responses and telling us what to adjust.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

It doesn't hurt you. They've already given you the information you need. And honestly, most customers care more about getting a fast response and a clear next step than about whether a machine took their initial details.
