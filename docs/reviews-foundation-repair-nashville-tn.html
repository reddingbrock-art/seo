FAQ

Common Questions About Review Automation for Foundation Repair

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests to real customers after real jobs are completed. The reviews that come back are from people who actually worked with you. It's not padding—it's capturing what you've already earned.

What if a customer is unhappy?

The requests go to customers after job completion, so most will be satisfied. If someone isn't happy, they can leave that feedback—which is legitimate data. Our system doesn't filter or suppress responses. It asks the question and documents the answer.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google primarily, which is where most homeowners look for foundation repair companies. We focus on the platform that moves the needle for your business.

How does the timing work?

Requests go out automatically when a job is marked complete in your system. You control the timing settings—whether that's same day or a few hours later. It's built around your workflow, not against it.

What if I already have a review process?

Most teams have something informal—texts, verbal asks, maybe a follow-up email here and there. We make it consistent and automatic, so nothing falls through the cracks. If you have a formal process, we integrate with that or replace it with something more reliable.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

It depends on your job volume and how many reviews you're currently capturing. Most owners see 10–20 new reviews per month once the system's running. You'll know your number within the first month.
