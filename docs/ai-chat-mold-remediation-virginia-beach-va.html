FAQ

AI Chat for Mold Remediation: Common Questions

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on your actual process, your terminology, your service area boundaries, and how you typically respond to questions. It doesn't sound robotic—it sounds like your dispatcher or office manager with perfect availability.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It flags the conversation and sends it to you with context. You step in, answer the question, and that answer gets added to the training for next time. It learns as you go.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It replaces the missed calls and unanswered messages. If you have a receptionist, they now focus on follow-ups and customer relationships instead of fielding basic intake questions. If you don't, you've got one now—except it never takes a lunch break.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. Calls come in at 2 AM on a Sunday? Your AI responds. Most mold issues are discovered at night or on weekends when homeowners are home—so you capture those leads instantly.

How long does setup take?

10-14 days from our first call to the agent going live. We handle everything—configuration, integration with your calendar, testing. You just give us access to your process and your systems.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

Most don't. But if they ask, it's honest: 'Yes, this is our AI intake system designed to get your information to our team quickly.' By then, they've already shared the details, property address, and damage description. The next human touch is your inspection specialist with full context.
