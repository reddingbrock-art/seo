Yes. We train it on your actual service descriptions, your tone, and the way you talk to customers. It answers using your neighborhood names, your service packages, and your booking process—not generic responses.

It says so honestly and asks for their contact info so you can follow up. It never makes something up or sends a customer to a competitor.

No. It handles the volume you can't handle yourself—nights, weekends, and back-to-back calls during your crew work. If you have a receptionist, they focus on scheduling and logistics instead of answering the same questions all day.

24/7. Customers can reach you Sunday at midnight or Tuesday at 6 AM. You only get notified during hours you set.

10-14 days from discovery call to live. You're up and running faster than hiring and training a new person.
