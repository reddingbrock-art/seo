FAQ

Lead Follow-Up Questions for Window & Window Replacement Companies in Los Angeles

How many follow-ups does it send?

That depends on your preference. Most window shops do 2-3 contacts in the first 48 hours—first reach is immediate, then a text and/or email 4-6 hours later if they don't respond. We configure it to match your process.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. We write the initial messaging for you, but you approve every word. It stays on-brand and sounds like you. If you want it adjusted after launch, we update it.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They're unsubscribed immediately. The system respects opt-outs the same day. You never contact them again—it's built in.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Within 5 minutes of the lead coming in. Whether they fill a form, call, or text, they hear back before most competitors even see the notification.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in your CRM tagged as contacted. You decide whether to attempt one final reach after a week or two. The system doesn't spam—it follows the sequence you set and stops.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. If you've got a sales team or a manager handling callbacks, this feeds leads to them faster. It doesn't replace your team—it makes them more effective by eliminating the timing lag.
