FAQ

Chat Widget Questions for HVAC Companies

Will the chat widget actually sound like my business?

Yes. We learn how you talk to customers—your tone, your process, your policies—and build that into every response. It's not a corporate bot; it's your shop answering questions.

What happens when the chat can't answer something?

It flags the conversation for your team and asks the customer if they want to wait for a person or leave a message. Nothing gets lost; nothing falls through.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It handles the visitors and questions your receptionist doesn't see—the 10 PM browsers, the Saturday prospects, the people who want to ask something before calling. It frees your team from repetitive questions so they can focus on running jobs.

What hours does the chat widget work?

24/7. It's always there—nights, weekends, holidays. During business hours, it can hand off to your team. After hours, it still captures and qualifies the lead.

How long does setup take?

10–14 days from discovery call to live. We handle all the integration with your booking system and calendar so there's no manual work on your end.

What if a customer wants to call instead of chat?

The chat gives them your number anytime they ask for it. We're not hiding the phone option—we're just offering a faster alternative that works for the people who prefer it.
