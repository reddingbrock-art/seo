FAQ

Review Automation FAQs for Tucson General Contractors

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send review requests only to customers you've actually worked for, after the job is complete. Google sees real customer names and real jobs. Your profile stays clean and compliant.

What if a customer is unhappy?

They get the same request as everyone else. The difference is you'll see feedback faster so you can respond and fix it—while it's still fresh. You're not filtering unhappy customers; you're just getting honest feedback before they leave it elsewhere.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google reviews are the primary focus. That's where Tucson customers are looking and where search results matter most for general contracting. We can discuss other platforms during your discovery call.

How does the timing work?

You set the window—usually 2-5 days after job completion when the customer is most likely to remember the work and want to share. The system sends at that moment automatically.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll look at what you're doing and either replace it with automation or add to it. If you're already getting results, we'll just make it frictionless and consistent. If it's not working, we'll fix it.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Most of your past customers would've left a review if asking had been easy and timely. You'll see a significant uptick within the first 60 days as you request from completed jobs. The exact number depends on your job volume.
