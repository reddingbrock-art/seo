FAQ

Common Questions About Review Automation for Septic & Drain Businesses

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests only to customers whose jobs you've actually completed. The reviews that come back are from real people who hired you and got service. That's it.

What if a customer is unhappy?

Unhappy customers usually won't submit a review. If they do, that's real feedback—the kind that helps you fix something. We're not filtering or suppressing anything. We're just asking people who used your service to share their experience.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google is where septic customers look first in Fort Worth. That's what this focuses on. Other review platforms work differently and matter less for field service.

How does the timing work?

You close a job in your system. The request goes out the same day or the next day, while the experience is fresh. No waiting weeks, no manual reminder to send it.

What if I already have a review process?

If it's working, great. If it's not, it's probably because it requires someone to remember and manually do it. Automation removes that friction. Most owners find they pick up 5-7 more reviews per month just by making the request consistent and automatic.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Depends on your current volume and how many jobs you're closing. We've seen businesses pick up significantly more reviews per month just by asking reliably. The exact number depends on your operation. A discovery call will give you a clearer picture.
