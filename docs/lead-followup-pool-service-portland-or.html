FAQ

Lead Follow-Up Questions for Portland Pool Service Owners

How many follow-ups does it send?

You decide. Most pool service owners run three to five touches over 7-10 days. We set the timing and sequence based on what works best for your market and your capacity.

Can I customize what it says?

Completely. We write it based on your voice and your service offerings. You can adjust messaging, timing, and which leads get which follow-up. Changes happen on your timeline.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can opt out with a single click or reply. We handle that automatically and remove them from future sequences. You stay compliant and they stay happy.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Minutes. As soon as a lead comes in, the first message goes out while they're still thinking about their pool problem. You're competing on response time, not just quality.

What happens to leads that never respond?

The system keeps working. After your final follow-up, they stay in your system so you can re-engage them when seasonal demand shifts or they're actually ready to book. Nothing gets lost.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Yes. If you've got a CRM, a scheduling system, or a specific workflow, we integrate with it. This layer on top of what you're already doing—it doesn't replace your team or your process.
