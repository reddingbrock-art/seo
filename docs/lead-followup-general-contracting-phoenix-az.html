FAQ

General Contracting Lead Follow-Up FAQs

How many follow-ups does it send?

Typically 4-6 touches over 7-10 days—call, text, email mix—depending on what you want. You control the sequence and messaging.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. We write it based on your business and tone, then you approve it before it goes live. It's your voice, not ours.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can opt out at any time. The system respects that immediately and removes them from the sequence.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Within minutes of the lead coming in. Calls go out immediately; texts follow within seconds if that's the first touch.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They finish the sequence but don't get pestered after. They stay in your system and can be contacted again later if they reach back out.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. It automates the front-end chase so your sales team can focus on closing. It works with your CRM, your phone system, and your schedule.
