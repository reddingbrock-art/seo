FAQ

Lead Follow-Up Questions for Miami Landscaping Owners

How many follow-ups does it send?

Typically 3–5 touchpoints over 7 days, depending on how you want to configure it. Each one is timed to catch them at different points in their decision window.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. We set up your messaging during the build. You control the tone, offer details, and call-to-action. Changes can be made anytime.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can unsubscribe with one click. The system logs that and removes them automatically. You stay compliant.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Typically within 15 minutes of the lead coming in. Faster response = higher answer rates and booking rates.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in the system but move to a separate list. You can review them, re-engage them later, or let them age out. The data's always there.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. It plugs into your current lead sources—forms, calls, texts, whatever you're using. Your team still does estimates and closings; the system just makes sure no lead falls through the cracks.
