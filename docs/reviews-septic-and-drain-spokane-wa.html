FAQ

Review Automation for Septic Companies: Questions Answered

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We only send requests to customers you've actually served. The reviews they leave are their own. We don't generate, filter, or manipulate them.

What if a customer is unhappy?

That's on you to handle directly. We send the request after the job closes. If there's a problem, you catch it and fix it before asking for feedback. Our job is to get requests in front of customers who had good experiences.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google, Yelp, Facebook, and industry-specific review sites. We configure it based on where your customers already leave feedback and where you want to build authority.

How does the timing work?

Requests go out the same day the job is marked complete in your system. Customers have up to 72 hours to respond. Most reply within 24 hours.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll map around it. If you're already collecting reviews manually on certain jobs, we focus automation on the ones that slip through. No duplication, no extra noise.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Most businesses see significantly more reviews per month than before. The exact number depends on your job volume and customer base. We'll show you specifics during your discovery call.
