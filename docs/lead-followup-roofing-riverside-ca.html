FAQ

Lead Follow-Up System FAQs for Riverside Roofing Companies

How many follow-ups does it send?

Typically 3–4 touches over 7 days: first contact same day, then spaced follow-ups if they don't respond. We customize the cadence based on your process.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. We build the messaging with you so it matches your voice and process. Every message can be tailored to your brand and approach.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can opt out at any time. The system respects their request immediately and removes them from future follow-ups.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Same day, usually within minutes of the lead coming in. Timing is one of the core reasons this works.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in the system through the full sequence so they get multiple touchpoints. After the sequence ends, they're logged as contacted. You can follow up manually later if needed.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. It plugs into what you're already doing—your website, your forms, your CRM. It doesn't replace anything; it fills the gaps.
