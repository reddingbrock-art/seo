Will the chat widget actually sound like my business?

Yes. We configure it to match your voice, your service areas, and your pricing structure. It sounds like you—not like a bot reading a script.

What happens when the chat can't answer something?

It routes the conversation to you with full context. You see exactly what they asked and what the chat already told them, so you're not starting from zero.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It replaces the missed calls and dropped inquiries. If you have a receptionist, they'll love it because they spend less time on inbound calls and more time on follow-up and scheduling.

What hours does the chat widget work?

24/7. It captures and qualifies leads even when you're sleeping or on a job. No more missing an inquiry because it came in after-hours.

10-14 days from discovery to live. Most of that is us building. Your time commitment is minimal—mainly just answering questions about your service areas and pricing.

What if a customer wants to call instead of chat?

They can. The chat offers a phone number as an option, but most people who land on your site and see a chat widget prefer to use it. You still get both paths to reach you.
