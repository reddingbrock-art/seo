FAQ

Review Automation for Garage Door Repair: Frequently Asked Questions

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. Requests go only to customers whose jobs you actually completed. They're real people making real decisions about whether to leave feedback. We're not generating fake profiles or incentivizing false reviews.

What if a customer is unhappy?

They get the same request as everyone else. If they don't respond, nothing happens. If they leave a negative review, that's feedback worth reading. Most unhappy customers who don't review simply don't engage—the request doesn't change that.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google reviews primarily. Google is where Charlotte homeowners search for garage door repair and make decisions based on ratings. That's where the traction is.

How does the timing work?

Requests go out after you mark a job complete in your system. The timing is automatic—no waiting for someone to remember to send it. The sooner after the work is done, the better the response rate.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll integrate with what you're doing. If you're asking manually and inconsistently, this replaces that—every job, every time, zero exceptions. If you have something more structured already, we can work around it.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

It depends on your job volume and current response rate, but most shops see significantly more reviews because you're reaching every customer consistently instead of some of them randomly. Many owners are shocked at how many customers were willing to leave reviews—they just needed to be asked.
