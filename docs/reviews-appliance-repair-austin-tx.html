FAQ

Review Automation Questions for Appliance Repair Owners

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We're only asking actual customers who completed jobs with you. Real work, real reviews. Google's system catches fake ones anyway—this keeps you clean.

What if a customer is unhappy?

The request still goes out, but they're not required to respond. If someone's upset, they'll tell you directly or skip the review. We're not filtering or suppressing anyone—we're just asking.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google is the main play. That's where customers search for appliance repair in Austin and make their decision. We focus there because that's where it matters most.

How does the timing work?

The request goes out the day after the job closes in your system. If a job completes on a Friday, the review request lands Saturday morning. Timing drives response.

What if I already have a review process?

Tell us what you're doing. If it's working, great. If it's manual and spotty, this replaces it and you'll see the difference in consistency.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Depends on your job volume and what you're doing now. If you're asking zero customers, most owners see a significant increase in 30 days. If you're already asking some, the automation makes it faster and more consistent.
