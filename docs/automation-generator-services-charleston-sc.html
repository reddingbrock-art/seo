FAQ

Automation for Generator Services: Common Questions

What exactly gets automated?

Incoming calls get answered and qualified. The system collects job details, checks your availability, and books appointments into your calendar. After jobs complete, it sends review requests automatically. Your team still handles the actual work and can answer calls when they want to—the system just handles what would normally sit in voicemail or take 10 minutes to book.

Do I need technical skills to run this?

No. You don't manage the system day-to-day. We build it, train your team on what they need to know, and handle updates. If something changes in your business—new service types, seasonal hours—you tell us, we adjust it.

What if my team resists new tools?

They usually don't, because it makes their job easier. They're not learning software—they're just checking their calendar and showing up to jobs that are already booked. Most resistance disappears after the first week when they realize they're not answering the same intake questions anymore.

How is this different from buying software myself?

You'd have to set it up, train your team, troubleshoot problems, and keep it running. We do all that. You get the operational result—calls answered, jobs booked—without the IT overhead. That's the whole point.

What does the setup process look like?

Discovery call, then 5–7 days of build work while you keep running your business normally. We test everything on our end, do a walkthrough with your team, and you go live. Total time commitment from you is probably 2 hours. The rest is on us.

What happens if something breaks after launch?

We monitor the system and fix it before you notice. If something isn't working the way you expected, you call us, we adjust it. You've got support included—not just during the first week, but ongoing.
