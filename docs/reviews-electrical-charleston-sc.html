FAQ

Review Automation Questions for Charleston Electrical Contractors

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests only to customers you've actually completed work for. Real jobs, real feedback. Google flags artificial activity, so we don't play games.

What if a customer is unhappy?

They get the same request as everyone else. If they leave a lower rating, that's real feedback you can learn from. We never filter or suppress unhappy customers—that's how Google's system works.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google reviews are the priority because that's where customers search and trust the most. We focus there first.

How does the timing work?

Requests go out automatically within a few hours of job completion, while the experience is fresh. You set the cadence; the system handles the delivery.

What if I already have a review process?

Most contractors do it manually or inconsistently. We're automating what you're already trying to do, so it actually happens every single time.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

It depends on your job volume and current ask rate. Contractors who go from zero requests to systematic ones typically see significantly more feedback within the first 60 days. We'll show you what your baseline looks like.
