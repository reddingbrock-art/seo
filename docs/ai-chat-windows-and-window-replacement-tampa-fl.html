FAQ

Questions About AI Chat Agents for Window Replacement in Tampa

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on your terminology, pricing, service areas, warranty language, and how you talk to customers. It answers as your business, not as a generic chatbot.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It's honest. If a question is outside its knowledge, it collects contact info and schedules a callback with you. No fake answers, no pretending.

Does this replace my receptionist?

It replaces the phone-answering part of the job. Your team handles the selling, the measuring, the follow-up. The AI handles incoming calls, qualification, and booking.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. Calls at midnight, Saturday mornings, holidays—all answered. You set business hours for scheduling, but the AI's always listening.

How long does setup take?

10-14 days from our first call to go-live. We need your service details, pricing, and CRM access. Most of the time is us building and training, not you.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

It's transparent from the start: "Hi, this is [Your Company]'s AI assistant." Customers appreciate fast, honest service. They're calling to book an estimate, not to chat with a human—they just want an answer now.
