FAQ

Common Questions About Review Automation for EV Charger Installers

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests only to customers you've actually completed work for. The reviews that come back are real because they're from real people who just had an install done.

What if a customer is unhappy?

They're free to leave a negative review, just like they are now. We're not filtering or suppressing anything—we're just asking. Some unhappy customers won't respond at all, and that's fine.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google is the primary platform. That's where EV charger customers are looking and where it matters most for local search.

How does the timing work?

You mark a job complete in your system. The request goes out automatically within 1–2 hours, while the experience is fresh.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll integrate with what you've got. If you're already doing something, we'll either replace it, layer on top of it, or pause our automation so there's no double-messaging.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

It depends on your volume and how many customers actually read and respond to the request. Most crews see significantly more reviews within 60 days because they're asking consistently instead of sporadically.
