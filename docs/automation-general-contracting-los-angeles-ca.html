FAQ

Automation FAQs for General Contracting Businesses

What exactly gets automated?

Lead capture from all sources (web forms, texts, calls, chat), immediate response and qualification, appointment scheduling, follow-up sequences, and review requests after job completion. Everything except the actual sales conversation and job execution stays in the system.

Do I need technical skills to run this?

No. You don't touch the backend. Your job is checking your inbox, talking to qualified leads, and closing work. The system handles everything else automatically.

What if my team resists new tools?

They won't be using a new tool. They'll just see better leads showing up in their inbox, pre-qualified and ready to book. It makes their job easier, not harder.

How is this different from buying software myself?

You'd buy the software, spend weeks learning it, figure out how to connect it to your other systems, and still have to build all the logic and messaging yourself. We do all that. You just show up and close leads.

What does the setup process look like?

One discovery call with us. Then our team handles the build—integration, automation sequences, messaging—and tests everything. You review and approve. Takes 10–14 days total.

What happens if something breaks after launch?

We monitor the system and you have direct access to support. If something stops working, we fix it. You're not troubleshooting on your own.
