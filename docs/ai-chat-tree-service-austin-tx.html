FAQ

AI Chat for Tree Service: Questions Answered

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We record how you and your team actually talk to customers—the terms you use, the jokes you make, the way you explain a job. The AI mirrors that voice, not some generic corporate tone.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It escalates. If a customer asks something outside the scope—like a specialized insurance question—the AI transfers them to you or books a callback. It knows its limits.

Does this replace my receptionist?

It depends on what your receptionist actually does. If they're answering the phone and booking appointments, yes—this handles that. If they're managing your crew comms or running the business, they're still essential.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. Calls at midnight, 6 AM, Sunday afternoon—doesn't matter. It's always on. You can set quiet hours if you want, but most owners don't.

How long does setup take?

Discovery to live is typically 10-14 days. The actual integration is fast. The training—making sure the AI sounds right—takes the most time, and that's because we get it right.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

They usually don't. But if they ask directly, the AI tells them. Transparency builds trust. Most customers care more about getting scheduled than who answered the phone.
