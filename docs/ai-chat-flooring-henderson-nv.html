FAQ

Common Questions About AI Chat for Henderson Flooring Businesses

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on your tone, your service offerings, and the way you answer customer calls. It's not a generic bot—it talks like someone from your team.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It collects the customer's question and info, then flags it for you to follow up on. You still own the relationship—the AI just makes sure no inquiry falls through the cracks.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It handles incoming messages 24/7 so your receptionist (or you) can focus on estimates, callbacks, and scheduling instead of answering the same questions fifty times a day.

What hours does the AI work?

All of them. It answers texts and chats at 3 AM on a Sunday just as reliably as it does at 9 AM on a Tuesday. You set the business hours it displays, but the actual response never stops.

How long does setup take?

Live in 10–14 days. We do the heavy lifting on training and integration. You spend a couple of hours in discovery and review, then it goes live.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

Most don't notice because it talks like your business. If they ask directly, it's honest about being an automated system—but it's already answered their questions and gotten them in the queue for a callback. They're not frustrated; they're impressed you responded at midnight.
