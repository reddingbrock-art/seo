FAQ

Common Questions About Automated Review Requests for Commercial Cleaning

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests only to customers whose jobs actually completed. Google's system flags fake activity. We're asking real customers to share real experiences.

What if a customer is unhappy?

They get the request like anyone else. If they leave a negative review, that's feedback about your service—not a system problem. Most unhappy customers don't review at all; the system doesn't change that. Your job is to make sure crews do the work right.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google is the priority for commercial cleaning—that's where facility managers and property companies look first. We can set up requests for other platforms too, but Google is where your visibility matters most.

How does the timing work?

The request goes out the same day the job is marked complete in your system. Customers respond over the next few days. No manual work on your end.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll map out what you're doing now and either integrate with it or replace it with something that actually runs on its own. If your current process requires crew follow-up and isn't generating consistent results, this is built to do it without them.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

It depends on your job volume and how many customers actually respond. Most businesses see a significant increase because you're asking systematically instead of randomly. The real win is consistency—new reviews showing up every week, which keeps your profile fresh and relevant.
