FAQ

Common Questions About Review Automation for Waterproofing & Basement Contractors

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We only request reviews from customers whose jobs you've actually completed. The system sends a request—what they do with it is up to them. You get real reviews from real customers, or you get nothing.

What if a customer is unhappy?

Unhappy customers are less likely to respond to the request anyway. If someone does post a negative review, that's real feedback about your work—not a platform failure. You handle it the same way you'd handle any review.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google is the priority because that's where Charlotte homeowners are looking. We focus there because that's where it matters most for your business.

How does the timing work?

The request goes out the same day or the day after job completion—whenever your crew marks the job as done. That's when customers are most likely to respond.

What if I already have a review process?

If you're asking manually and getting results, great. Most owners find they're inconsistent about it—some jobs get asked, others don't. This removes the guesswork and ensures every completed job gets a request.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

It depends on your job completion rate and how many customers you're currently asking. Most owners see a significant lift because they're now asking consistently instead of sporadically. You're capturing reviews you're already earning.
