What exactly gets automated?

Inbound calls are answered and routed to your team or captured with customer details. Web form submissions trigger immediate acknowledgments and alert your dispatcher. Do I need technical skills to run this?

No. Your dispatcher sees a clean dashboard and gets notifications like they always have. Your technicians get job cards like they always have. The difference is the information's already there and it arrived instantly instead of three hours later.

We build this to fit how you already work, not the other way around. Your team will actually like this because it means fewer dropped calls, fewer callbacks asking for the same information twice, and less phone time overall. It makes their job easier.

How is this different from buying software myself?

You could buy a phone system, a CRM, a form tool, and integration software separately—and spend weeks configuring them, training your team, and fixing things that break. We handle the entire build, integration, and support. You get a working system in 10-14 days.

What does the setup process look like?

What happens if something breaks after launch?
