FAQ

Review Automation Questions for HVAC Contractors

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests only to customers you actually served. They choose whether to leave a review or not. Real customers, real reviews.

What if a customer is unhappy?

They won't leave a review if they don't want to. Our system requests reviews from real customers after completion—nothing more. You're not filtering or suppressing feedback. If someone had a bad experience, they can leave an honest review, and you'll see it immediately so you can respond.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google Business Profile and Facebook. Those are where customers look and where search engines notice. That's where we focus.

How does the timing work?

The request goes out automatically after job completion—within a few hours. That's when customers are most satisfied and most likely to act. No delays, no manual scheduling.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll talk through what you're doing now and where the gaps are. If you're asking in person or via text, automation often captures more because it's consistent and doesn't rely on your team remembering. We'll show you the difference.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Most shops we work with see significantly more reviews in the first 90 days because they're asking consistently for the first time. The exact number depends on your volume and current baseline, but you'll feel the difference fast. Book a call and we'll show you what you're missing.
