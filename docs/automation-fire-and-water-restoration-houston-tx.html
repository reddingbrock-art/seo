FAQ

Common Questions About Automation for Houston Fire & Water Restoration

What exactly gets automated?

Incoming calls and web forms get answered immediately with a qualification conversation. Property details, damage type, and customer info get captured and organized. Unqualified or low-priority leads are filtered out. Callbacks and follow-ups get triggered automatically based on job status. Your team sees a clean, prioritized lead list instead of raw inquiries.

Do I need technical skills to run this?

No. Your team uses it like any other tool—they log in, see their leads, and respond. We handle all the backend configuration and updates. If something needs to change, you tell us and we adjust it.

What if my team resists new tools?

The system actually makes their job easier—fewer phone calls to answer, better organized information, less scrambling to find lead details. Training takes about an hour. After a week, they'll wonder how they ever worked without it.

How is this different from buying software myself?

You'd be buying a platform and figuring out integration, configuration, and ongoing management on your own. We build the entire system for you—it's configured, connected, and live. We maintain it, update it, and support it. You just use it.

What does the setup process look like?

Discovery call to understand your workflow. We connect your phone system and web forms to the platform. We set up qualification rules and define what a good lead looks like for your business. We test everything with your team, make adjustments, then go live. Total timeline is 10-14 days.

What happens if something breaks after launch?

We monitor the system daily for the first two weeks and fix anything that's not working right. After that, you've got direct support—if something goes wrong, you let us know and we fix it. System updates and maintenance are included.
