FAQ

Painting Lead Follow-Up Questions Answered

How many follow-ups does it send?

We customize the sequence based on your process, but typically 2-4 touchpoints over 5-7 days if the lead doesn't respond initially. You control the cadence.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. We write and refine the messages so they sound like your company. You approve everything before it goes live.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can opt out with a single text reply, and the system honors that immediately. No compliance issues.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Within minutes of the lead reaching out. No wait, no manual work.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in your system and show up in your follow-up list. You can decide if you want to reach out manually or let them age out.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. It fills the gap when you're busy or off-site. Your team stays in control of actual job conversations and closing.
