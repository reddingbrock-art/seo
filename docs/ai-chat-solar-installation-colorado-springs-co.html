AI Chat Automation for Solar Installers: Questions Answered

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on your language, your process, and the way your team actually talks to customers. It'll answer with your company's personality and answer the questions your crew gets asked every day.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It tells the customer the truth—that it needs to pass them to your team—and captures all the details so your team knows exactly what was asked. Nothing gets lost.

Does this replace my receptionist?

It handles the intake layer—answering phones, capturing info, qualifying leads. Your team still does the real work: callbacks, site visits, and closes. You're not cutting anyone; you're freeing them up for higher-value work.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. It answers at midnight the same way it answers at noon. Your leads get a response whenever they reach out—even if you're sleeping.

How long does setup take?

Discovery and build takes about a week. You're live and answering calls within 10–14 days of signing on.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

It doesn't hide what it is. It introduces itself as your automated assistant, asks what it can help with, and does its job—which builds trust instead of breaking it. Customers respect transparency.
