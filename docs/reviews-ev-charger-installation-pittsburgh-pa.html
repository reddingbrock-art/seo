FAQ

Questions About Review Automation for Pittsburgh EV Charger Installation

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests to actual customers after actual job completion. Real people, real work, real reviews—or no review. That's it.

What if a customer is unhappy?

They don't leave a review. We're just asking for feedback from people who hired you. If someone's upset, they won't respond, and that's the right outcome.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google reviews are the priority. That's where homeowners and contractors look first. Other platforms depend on your specific goals—we talk through it during setup.

How does the timing work?

Requests go out after your job is completed and marked finished in your system. We time it to hit when customers are most likely to respond, usually within 24-48 hours of the install.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll work around it. If you're manually asking some customers, we make sure we don't duplicate requests. We're filling the gap where your process has cracks, not replacing what's working.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Most installers we work with see significantly more reviews because requests that would've been forgotten now happen automatically. The exact number depends on your install volume and how many jobs you're closing now.
