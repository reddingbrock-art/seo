FAQ

Lead Follow-Up Questions for Flooring Companies

How many follow-ups does it send?

Depends on your business. We typically build a sequence of 3-5 touches spread over 5-7 days — enough to convert a serious prospect without annoying someone who's not interested. You decide the exact sequence.

Can I customize what it says?

Completely. We write in your voice using details about your flooring work — whether you're selling hardwood refinishing, tile installation, or vinyl planks. You review and approve every message.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can opt out anytime. The system respects that immediately. You want people who want to hear from you.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Within 5-15 minutes of them submitting a form or calling in. That's the window where they're still thinking about flooring.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in your system. If they don't engage in the first sequence, you can set up a re-engagement message weeks later to catch them when they're ready. You're not throwing anyone away.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Yes. It doesn't replace your crew or your current workflow — it just makes sure nothing falls through the cracks while you're out on jobs. Your team still handles the estimate and close.
