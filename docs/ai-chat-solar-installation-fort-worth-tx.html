FAQ

AI Chat for Solar Installation Websites: Common Questions

Will the chat widget actually sound like my business?

Yes. We write it in your voice, using your language, your process, and your real install timeline. It sounds like someone on your team answering questions—not a robot.

What happens when the chat can't answer something?

It tells the prospect the truth and offers to connect them with you via message or phone. Your team gets a summary so context is already there.

Does this replace my receptionist?

It handles the visitors your receptionist never meets—the ones who visit your website at night or don't want to call. It frees your team up for estimates and closings, not basic questions.

What hours does the chat widget work?

24/7. Prospects get answers on Sunday morning, Tuesday at 6 AM, or whenever they're researching. You decide if you want to book estimates only during business hours.

How long does setup take?

10-14 days from start to live. We handle all of it. No coding, no complexity on your end.

What if a customer wants to call instead of chat?

The chat gives them your phone number anytime they ask. They can always switch to a call. Some do. Most finish the conversation via chat and book.
