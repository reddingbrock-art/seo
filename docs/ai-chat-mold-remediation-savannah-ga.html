FAQ

AI Chat Agent FAQs for Savannah Mold Remediation Companies

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on your actual scripts, brand voice, and how you talk to customers. You hear it in action before it goes live, and we adjust until it matches your style.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It tells the customer exactly that—'I need to get you in touch with one of our team members'—and either offers a callback time or connects them to someone live if you have that option set up.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It replaces the waiting, the voicemail loops, and the callback delays. Your team can focus on selling, not being glued to the phone during jobs.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. Nights, weekends, holidays—whenever a lead calls or submits a form, it answers. You decide what information you want captured after hours versus during business hours.

How long does setup take?

You're live and taking calls in 10-14 days from the discovery call. Most of that is us training the system on your voice and process—not waiting on you.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

They almost never do because it's designed to sound like a real person on your team. But if they ask directly, it's honest about it immediately and focuses on getting them scheduled or connected to someone who can help.
