FAQ

Water Treatment Review Automation: Common Questions

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests only to customers you've actually served. Real people, real jobs. Google catches fake reviews and penalizes you—we don't operate that way.

What if a customer is unhappy?

They can leave an honest review, just like anyone else. We're not filtering or suppressing anything. We're just asking for feedback at the right moment. Unhappy customers were always going to post—now at least you know who they are instead of being blindsided.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google, primarily. That's where your customers are looking for water treatment services in Austin. We focus there because it's where the calls come from.

How does the timing work?

Your crew marks the job complete in your system. Within a few hours, the customer gets a review request. They click a link, leave a review if they want to, and it posts directly to Google. You see it in real time.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll work around it. If you've been manually sending requests or asking customers on the spot, this just makes that automatic. No conflicts, same end result—more reviews, less effort.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

That depends on your volume and your customer response rate. Most companies see significantly more reviews once asking becomes automatic. Some jobs won't get reviewed no matter what, but you'll capture far more than you do now because you're asking at the right time instead of never at all.
