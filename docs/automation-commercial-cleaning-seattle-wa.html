FAQ

Automation for Commercial Cleaning: Common Questions

What exactly gets automated?

Call answering and qualification, appointment scheduling, job routing to your team, and review requests after completion. Everything else—sales conversations, custom quotes, contract discussions—stays in your hands.

Do I need technical skills to run this?

No. You log in, see your leads, approve jobs, and manage your crew like you do now. The system does the heavy lifting behind the scenes.

What if my team resists new tools?

Your crew gets simpler work: show up, do the job, see it on their phone. No more hunting through emails or calling the office for details. Most teams embrace it within a week.

How is this different from buying software myself?

You could buy software, hire someone to configure it, integrate it with your calendar, train your team, and troubleshoot when it breaks. We do all that—and we own the outcome. You just use it.

What does the setup process look like?

One discovery call to map your workflow, about 5–7 days of build and testing, then go-live. You're never more than a phone call away from us during the process.

What happens if something breaks after launch?

You have direct support. Call or email, and we fix it. We maintain the system, handle updates, and make sure it keeps running. You're not managing infrastructure—we are.
