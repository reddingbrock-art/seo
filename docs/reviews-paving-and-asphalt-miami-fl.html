FAQ

Google Review Automation for Paving & Asphalt: Common Questions

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. The system sends requests only to customers from completed jobs in your records. Reviews come from real people who actually used your service.

What if a customer is unhappy?

The system sends the same request to everyone. If a customer leaves a negative review, you handle it the way you normally would—by responding professionally on Google. Some companies use the response to turn the conversation around.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google is where most property managers and facilities teams look first. That's the priority. We also support other platforms depending on your business model—ask during the discovery call.

How does the timing work?

The request goes out the day the job is marked complete in your system. Timing matters because that's when the work is fresh in their mind and they're most likely to take 90 seconds to leave feedback.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll look at what you're doing now and either replace it or layer on top of it. If you're manually requesting reviews sometimes, automation fills in the gaps from jobs you forget about.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

It depends on your current follow-up rate. Most businesses capture 2–3x more reviews once requests stop falling through the cracks. Some customers won't respond no matter what—that's normal. But most will if you ask at the right moment.
