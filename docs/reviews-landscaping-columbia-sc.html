FAQ

Google Review Automation for Landscapers in Columbia: Common Questions

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send review requests to real customers you've actually worked for. The reviews that come back are genuine—they're based on real work you did. We don't generate, buy, or filter reviews.

What if a customer is unhappy?

The system sends the request to everyone. If someone's unhappy, they may not respond. That's fine—it's honest. You're not manipulating outcomes; you're just making sure happy customers have a chance to say so.

Which platforms does this work on?

We focus on Google reviews because that's where most customers are looking. Your Google rating is what shows up in search results and on the map. That's where the leverage is.

How does the timing work?

You mark a job complete in your system. The review request goes out automatically that same day or the next morning. We handle the timing so it hits when the work is fresh in their mind.

What if I already have a review process?

We can build around what you're doing, or we can replace it entirely. If you're already asking manually, automation will get you faster responses and consistency. If you're not asking, we start from scratch.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

That depends on how many jobs you complete each week and your current review request rate. Most owners see a meaningful increase within the first month because they're asking consistently instead of sporadically. We can show you the math during a call.
