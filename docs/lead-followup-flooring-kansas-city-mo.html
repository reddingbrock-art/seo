FAQ

Lead Follow-Up System Questions for Kansas City Flooring Companies

How many follow-ups does it send?

It depends on your preference and the lead's response. Most flooring companies use 3-5 touches over 7-10 days: an immediate call, a text if they don't pick up, an email, and a second call or message. We customize the sequence to match your sales cycle.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. You tell us what you want to say—your name, your value, your call-to-action—and we build the messages around your voice. It sounds like your team, not a robot.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can opt out with one text or email reply. The system stops contacting them immediately, and you're notified. Full compliance, no hassle.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Within 2-3 minutes of the lead coming in. If it's a phone call, we try to reach them right away. If no answer, texts and emails follow minutes later.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in your system. You can see the full history of every touch we sent. Some leads need to see you 3-4 times before they're ready to talk. The data stays there so you or your team can circle back later if needed.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. If you've got estimates, job scheduling, or crew dispatch software running, this plugs in around it. Your team's workflow doesn't change—the system just makes sure nothing falls through the cracks.
