FAQ

Lead Follow-Up System FAQs for Pool Service Owners

How many follow-ups does it send?

You decide. Typical setup is 2-4 touches across a 7-10 day window, but it's customized to your process.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. We write the initial templates in your voice, and you approve every message before it goes live.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can opt out at any time, and they're removed immediately. Compliance is built in.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Usually within 2-5 minutes of the lead coming in, depending on the source.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They get all scheduled touches, then the sequence ends. No infinite spam loops. You can manually follow up if you want to, but the system doesn't.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. It integrates with how you already take leads. No ripping and replacing your current workflow.
