FAQ

Review Automation Questions for Generator Service Owners

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. The system sends review requests only to customers you've actually served. Real customers. Real jobs. Google will see authentic behavior—requests going out right after completion, honest reviews coming in over time.

What if a customer is unhappy?

We're only sending the request after a job's marked complete—that's on you. If you want to screen out dissatisfied customers before the request goes out, we can help you build that into the workflow. But we're not filtering or suppressing anything.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google reviews, which is where generator service searches happen. That's where your customers look. That's where we focus.

How does the timing work?

Review request goes out automatically the same day the job's completed, or the next morning if you prefer. That's when customer satisfaction is highest and they're most likely to respond.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll integrate with what you've got. If you're already asking manually, the system takes over and handles volume without replacing your existing touchpoints. We're adding consistency, not creating friction.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Most generator service owners see significantly more reviews once the request is automatic and timed right. The exact number depends on your job volume and completion rate. A discovery call will give you a real picture for your crew size.
