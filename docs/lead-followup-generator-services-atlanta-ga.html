FAQ

Lead Follow-Up Automation for Generator Services: Common Questions

How many follow-ups does it send?

That's customized to your process. Most generator services run 2–4 touches over 5–7 days: initial response, qualification message, appointment reminder, and post-no-show follow-up. We build it to match your timeline.

Can I customize what it says?

Completely. We write your first draft based on how you talk to customers, then you approve or revise every message before it goes out. It sounds like your business, not like automation.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can opt out, and the system respects that immediately. No more messages go to them. We handle compliance so you don't have to worry about it.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Within 5 minutes of the lead inquiry coming in. Most of the time, it's the first contact they receive from any company.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They cycle through your follow-up sequence as planned. If they don't book after the final message, they're tagged as 'no response' so your team can decide whether to try a different approach later or move on.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Yes. It integrates with your dispatch, your CRM, and your booking system. It automates the repetitive outreach part so your team can focus on closing and service quality.
