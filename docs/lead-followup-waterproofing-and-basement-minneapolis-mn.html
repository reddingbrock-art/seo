FAQ

Lead Follow-Up System FAQs for Waterproofing & Basement Contractors

How many follow-ups does it send?

Typically 3–5 touchpoints per lead over 7–10 days, depending on what you want. A text first, then email, then another text. You decide the cadence and message.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. We write the templates based on your voice and what works in your market. You approve the wording before it goes live.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can reply STOP and they're off the list immediately. The system respects opt-outs right away.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Within minutes of the lead coming in — whether it's a form submission, phone call transfer, or text. Not hours later when you remember to call.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in your CRM tagged and organized. You can see at a glance which leads went cold and which ones are still warm. Nothing gets lost.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. It fills the gaps in your current follow-up. If you take a call directly, the system knows it. If a lead books through your website, the automation stops because they're already yours.
