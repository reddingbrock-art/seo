FAQ

AI Chat & Phone Automation Questions for Fort Worth Plumbers

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We write the agent's voice using your scripts, your terminology, and how your best tech actually talks to customers. It's trained on your business, not a generic template.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It escalates to your phone or text queue immediately. You get an alert that a live person is needed. The customer never experiences a dead end—they just talk to you instead.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It replaces the voicemail and handles the repetitive qualification work so your team can focus on jobs and follow-up. If you have a receptionist, they get their time back.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. Midnight calls, holiday weekends, emergencies at 3 a.m.—it's answering. Your customers reach you when they panic, not during business hours.

How long does setup take?

10–14 days from kickoff to live. We need one discovery call and a few hours to refine the voice. It's live and answering calls before the month ends.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

Most don't. But if they ask, it's honest about it and transfers them to a person. Transparency builds trust. Some customers even prefer it because they get booked instantly instead of waiting for a callback.
