Yes. We train it on how you talk to customers, what you emphasize, and your service area specifics. A lead can't tell the difference between the AI and someone in your office.

It gathers their info and tells them you'll follow up with a real person within a few hours. It never makes something up. Trust stays intact.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It replaces the hours they'd spend answering phones and taking messages. They can focus on scheduling, follow-ups, and customer care that actually moves the needle.

24/7. Weekends, nights, holidays. Whenever a lead tries to reach you, they get a response. You can set it to only book calls during your operating hours.
