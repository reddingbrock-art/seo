FAQ

AI Chat Agent for Locksmith Services—Questions Answered

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We build it from your voice, your tone, and how you actually talk to customers. It answers questions like you would—direct, professional, no corporate robot voice. Customers don't feel like they're talking to a machine.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It tells the customer the truth and offers to have you call them back immediately. It doesn't make stuff up. It captures their number and details so you can follow up with a real answer.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. If you have a receptionist, this handles the calls they can't—nights, weekends, overflow during peak season. If you don't have one, you finally have coverage you couldn't afford to hire for.

What hours does the AI work?

24 hours a day, every day. It answers whether it's 3 AM on Sunday or noon on Wednesday. You control which hours it actively books appointments versus which hours it just takes messages.

How long does setup take?

10–14 days from discovery call to live. That includes building the agent, integrating it with your phone and calendar, and testing it with your team.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

Some will figure it out. That's fine. The AI is transparent about being automated, and customers respect that you're available instantly instead of making them wait. Speed and availability matter more than pretending it's human.
