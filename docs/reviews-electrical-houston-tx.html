The Problem

Why Houston Electrical Contractors Struggle With Online Reviews

You're busy running jobs, managing crews, dealing with permits, and trying to actually grow your business. The last thing you want to do is sit down at night and craft responses to customer reviews or spend your Sunday figuring out how to ask satisfied customers for feedback. The Fix

We Install Your Review System. You Focus on Electrical Work.

Here's what we do: we set up a done-for-you review collection and management system powered by GoHighLevel and AI. Every time you finish a job, your customer gets asked for a review through their preferred channel—text, email, or both. We monitor Google, Yelp, Facebook, and HomeAdvisor for incoming reviews. When a review comes in, our AI drafts a professional response that sounds like you actually wrote it. You review and approve it in seconds. Then we post it.

We don't filter who gets asked for reviews. Every customer gets the request. If someone leaves a negative review, you'll see it immediately, and we'll help you respond thoughtfully—whether that's addressing a legitimate concern or clarifying what actually happened on that job.

The whole thing is installed and running within 10-14 days. You're not configuring software or learning new platforms. You just do your job, and we make sure your reputation keeps up.
