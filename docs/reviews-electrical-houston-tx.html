FAQ

Review Automation FAQs for Houston Electrical Companies

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We only request reviews from actual customers whose service was completed and logged in your system. Google flags fake reviews anyway—we're building authentic reputation.

What if a customer is unhappy?

Our system detects sentiment before the review hits Google. If someone's frustrated, you get the feedback first so you can address it privately and potentially earn a better review.

Which platforms does this work on?

We focus on Google first because it's where prospects in Houston look you up. We also integrate with other review platforms as part of your overall strategy.

How does the timing work?

Requests go out 24–48 hours after service completion, when the job's fresh but you're not nagging. Timing's adjustable based on your call patterns and service type.
