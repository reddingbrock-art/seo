FAQ

Common Questions About Review Automation for Garage Door Repair

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We only send requests to actual customers after completed jobs. Real work, real people, real reviews. We're not buying reviews or manipulating the system—we're just making sure you ask at the right moment instead of never asking at all.

What if a customer is unhappy?

They see the request like everyone else. Some will leave an honest negative review, some won't respond. That's normal and actually helps your credibility—Google penalizes profiles with no negative reviews because they look fake. You manage any reviews the same way you do now.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google, Facebook, and the major review sites your customers actually use. We focus on the platforms that drive local search visibility and customer trust, not every obscure review site that doesn't send you work.

How does the timing work?

You set when the request goes out—same day the job closes, next day, or a few days later. We send it based on your job completion date, not manual intervention. You control the timing, the system handles the sending.

What if I already have a review process?

We integrate with whatever you're already doing or replace it entirely if it's not working. If your crew's already asking and it's working great, we just automate the backup requests for jobs where they forgot. Most companies find they get significantly more reviews when it's fully automated.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Depends on your volume and response rates, but most companies see their annual reviews increase substantially once requests go out consistently. Instead of hoping customers remember to leave a review, you're asking everyone. More asks means more reviews.
