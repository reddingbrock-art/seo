FAQ

AI Chat Automation for Waterproofing Companies — Common Questions

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on your past conversations, your crew's language, and your actual closing process. A homeowner in Carmel won't hear corporate speak—they'll hear someone who knows waterproofing because you taught the system what that sounds like.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It tells the customer the truth—'I'll have the owner call you back about that'—and flags the conversation for you immediately. You get the context, the customer gets a real person, and the lead doesn't die because the AI ran out of answers.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. If you have one, it frees them up to handle callbacks on your terms, process estimates, and manage the day-to-day. If you don't have one, this means you're not losing leads to missed calls anymore. Either way, your staff focuses on work that actually moves the needle.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. A lead can call or message at 11 p.m. on a Friday and get a response immediately. You can set availability windows if you want (e.g., schedule calls between 8 a.m. and 5 p.m.), but the AI answers every message as it comes in.

How long does setup take?

You'll be live and taking calls in 10-14 days. We handle the training, the integration, and the testing. Your job is just a discovery call and approving how your AI sounds before it goes live.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

Some will. That's okay. We build honesty into the greeting: 'Hi, I'm an AI assistant for [your company]. I can answer questions and book your inspection. If you'd rather talk to someone directly, I'll get the owner on the phone.' Most customers don't care as long as their problem gets solved.
