FAQ

Questions About Review Automation for Electrical Contractors

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We're only sending requests to customers you've actually worked for. Google's system validates that the reviewer had a genuine interaction with your business. The reviews you get are real—they're just from people who would've left one anyway if they'd been asked at the right time.

What if a customer is unhappy?

They don't have to leave a review. The request is an invitation, not pressure. If someone has a complaint, they're not more likely to leave a negative review because we asked politely via email. The worst outcome is they ignore it.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google Reviews is where Riverside customers search for electricians, so that's where we focus. Your reviews land on Google, and they show up across Google Maps, local search, and your Business Profile.

How does the timing work?

Requests go out the same day the job is marked complete in your system. That's when the experience is fresh. You set the frequency—some contractors send immediately, others wait 24 hours so the invoice is already processed.

What if I already have a review process?

If it's manual and inconsistent, this replaces it with something automatic. If you're already asking and getting some reviews, we'll increase the volume significantly by making sure every completed job gets a request.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Most contractors see 3-5 new reviews per week once the system is live, assuming you're completing that volume of jobs. It depends entirely on how many customers you're working for. The bigger your operation, the faster your profile builds.
