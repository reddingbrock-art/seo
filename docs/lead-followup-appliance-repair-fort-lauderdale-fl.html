FAQ

Lead Follow-Up Automation Questions for Appliance Repair Owners

How many follow-ups does it send?

Typically 3–5 touchpoints over 7 days, depending on how you want it set up. We customize it to match your sales cycle. More urgent jobs might get more touches; low-urgency inquiries get fewer.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. We write the initial messages based on your brand and service area, then you approve them. If you want to tweak the tone or add something specific about your warranty or service area, that's built in.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can unsubscribe with one click. The system respects that and stops all follow-up to that contact. Compliance is automatic.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Within 2–5 minutes of the lead coming in. The exact timing depends on the channel—a form submission, a call, a text—but it's all within that window.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in the system for the full sequence, then go quiet. You can see them in your lead log if you want to reach out manually later, or we can set up a separate nurture for cold leads. Your call.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. It runs parallel to whatever you're doing now—your team still takes calls, your dispatch still works the same way. This just makes sure nothing falls through the cracks between the lead and the first callback.
