FAQ

Lead Follow-Up Questions Garage Door Companies Ask

How many follow-ups does it send?

That depends on your preference. Most garage door companies use a 3 to 5-touch sequence over 48 hours. We customize it based on your service area and typical lead behavior.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. You control the message, the timing, and the channels—text, email, or both. It sounds like your company because it's written by you.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can opt out anytime with a single reply. The system respects that immediately. You stay compliant and build goodwill by honoring their preference.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Usually within 5 minutes of the lead coming in. No delays, no lag. Speed is why this works.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in your CRM tagged and segmented. You can circle back with seasonal campaigns or regional promotions when it makes sense. No lead gets forgotten.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Absolutely. It sits on top of what you're already doing. Your team still takes calls, closes jobs the same way—this just makes sure nobody slips through the cracks while you're busy.
