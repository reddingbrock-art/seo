FAQ

Review Automation Questions for Salt Lake City Contractors

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests to real customers who actually completed jobs with you. The reviews that come back are genuine — they're either five stars from satisfied customers or nothing at all. We don't manufacture anything.

What if a customer is unhappy?

We only send requests after job completion, so unhappy customers usually address problems before the request goes out. If someone's upset, they don't leave a review. Happy customers do. That's the whole point.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google is where most homeowners search for contractors and read reviews. That's where we focus first. We can discuss other platforms during your discovery call.

How does the timing work?

Requests go out the same day you mark a job complete in your system. Customers get the request while the work is still fresh in their mind. Most responses come back within 48–72 hours.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll map what you're doing now and either enhance it or replace it entirely depending on what's working. If you're getting results, we'll build on that. If you're not, we'll fix it.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

It depends on your job volume and current review rate, but most contractors see significantly more reviews within 60 days because the requests are going out consistently and on time. We'll give you a real number during discovery based on your actual numbers.
