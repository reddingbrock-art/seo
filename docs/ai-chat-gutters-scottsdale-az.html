Common Questions About AI Chat for Gutters Companies

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on how you talk to customers, your service offerings, and your tone. It answers like someone who works for you, not like a robot.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It says so and collects the customer's info so you can follow up. It's not trying to be perfect—it's trying to keep the conversation moving and get the appointment booked.

Does this replace my receptionist?

It handles inbound calls and texts when your team's busy or after hours. If you have a receptionist, they can focus on other tasks. If you don't, this is way cheaper than hiring one.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. Calls come in at midnight, it answers. Customers text on Sunday, it responds. You can set business hours if you want, but most owners let it run all the time.

How long does setup take?

10-14 days from discovery call to going live. You're handling calls before the next busy season hits.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

Most don't notice. Some do, and they're fine with it because they got their answer or booked their appointment. It's faster than waiting on hold.
