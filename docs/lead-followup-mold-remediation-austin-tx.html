FAQ

Common Questions About Lead Follow-Up Automation for Mold Remediation

How many follow-ups does it send?

Typically 5 over the course of a week, spaced so you're not annoying the lead but you're not disappearing either. We customize the sequence based on your timeline for closing estimates.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. We write it in your voice and match the way you actually talk to customers. You review it, tweak it, approve it before it goes live.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can opt out with one click, and the system stops. We handle compliance so you don't have to worry about it.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Usually within 2–3 minutes of the lead coming in. Phone leads, form submissions, texts—all the same speed.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in the queue for the full follow-up sequence. Some people need three touches before they're ready to commit. After the sequence runs, if they don't respond, they're flagged so you know where you stand.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. We integrate with whatever CRM or system you're already using. Your team doesn't change how they work—the automation just handles the early follow-up so they can focus on closing and scheduling.
