FAQ

Common Questions About Automation for Water Treatment Businesses

What exactly gets automated?

Lead capture from all sources (phone, web forms, text), instant acknowledgment to the customer, routing to your dispatcher, appointment reminders, and post-job review requests. Your team still talks to customers and sells—the system just makes sure nothing falls through the cracks.

Do I need technical skills to run this?

No. We set it up end-to-end. Your team just logs in and uses it like they're already doing—taking calls, texting back, booking appointments. There's no coding or system maintenance on your side.

What if my team resists new tools?

We build it so it feels like a natural extension of how you already work. Your dispatcher doesn't have to learn anything new—they just see the same calls and texts organized better. Most teams see the time savings in the first week and stop resisting.

How is this different from buying software myself?

If you buy software, you're responsible for configuring it, integrating it with your phone system, training your team, and troubleshooting problems. We do all of that. You get a working system, not a tool that sits half-implemented.

What does the setup process look like?

We run one discovery call with you, then build the system offsite while you keep working. We do a brief training session with your dispatcher, flip the switch, and monitor it closely for the first week. Total time investment from you is about two hours.

What happens if something breaks after launch?

We provide ongoing support. If something's not working the way it should, you reach out and we fix it. You're not on your own after go-live.
