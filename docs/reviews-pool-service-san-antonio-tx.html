FAQ

Review Automation for Pool Service: Common Questions

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests only to customers you've actually serviced. Google's system filters out anything suspicious anyway. You're just asking people who already used you to share their real experience.

What if a customer is unhappy?

The system requests reviews from everyone. If someone had a bad experience, that's feedback you need to see and fix. Suppressing unhappy customers doesn't help your business—understanding why they're unhappy does.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google Reviews is where pool service customers look first. That's where we focus. Some businesses use Yelp or Facebook, but Google is the engine that matters for local search.

How does the timing work?

You tell us when a job is complete—usually through your existing scheduling system or with a quick manual trigger. The request goes out within hours. No delays, no human bottleneck.

What if I already have a review process?

Most pool shops have good intentions but inconsistent execution. This replaces the manual "remember to ask" part with automation. If you're already getting 5+ new reviews a week consistently, you're the exception.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Depends on your current volume and how many customers are actually willing to leave one. Most owners see significant uptick in the first 60 days once the system is handling the ask. Some customers never respond—that's normal. But you're capturing the ones who will if you just ask.
