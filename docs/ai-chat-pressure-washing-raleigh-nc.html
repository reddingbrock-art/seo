FAQ

AI Chat for Pressure Washing: Common Questions

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We learn how you talk to customers, what neighborhoods you service, and your pricing model. The AI answers in your voice and tone, not some generic bot script.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It flags the inquiry for you with context about what the customer asked. You get notified in real time and can jump in or follow up manually. Nothing gets lost.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It replaces the phone ringing unanswered while you're on jobs and the three-hour callback lag. If you have a receptionist, they focus on higher-value work instead of fielding repetitive questions.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. Midnight inquiry on a Sunday? It answers. Morning lead before you're awake? Booked. You pick which hours are active if you want to pause at certain times.

How long does setup take?

Discovery and handoff is 30 minutes. Build and integration takes 10-14 days. Then you're live and adjusting as needed. Fast enough that you're capturing this week's leads with it.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

It's transparent from the start. Customers know they're interacting with an automated system, and most prefer instant answers to waiting. The goal is speed and booking, not deception. They still get a human for complex questions or final confirmation.
