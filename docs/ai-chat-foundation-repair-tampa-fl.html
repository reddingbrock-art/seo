FAQ

AI Chat Widget Questions for Foundation Repair Owners

Will the chat widget actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on your language, your service descriptions, your process—even your company tone. A visitor talking to your chat should feel like they're talking to someone on your team.

What happens when the chat can't answer something?

It tells the visitor exactly that, captures their information and question, and routes it to you with full context. You follow up with the details already in hand.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. If you have one, it frees them up from answering the same foundation questions over and over. If you don't, it means you're not missing leads anymore because no one's available to answer.

What hours does the chat widget work?

24/7. It's live whenever your website is, even at 2 a.m. A homeowner worried about a crack can get answers and book a time without waiting for business hours.

How long does setup take?

10-14 days from discovery to live. Most of that is us building and testing. You're not bogged down in technical work.

What if a customer wants to call instead of chat?

The chat can direct them to your phone number anytime. But most won't ask—they got what they needed through chat and already have an appointment booked.
