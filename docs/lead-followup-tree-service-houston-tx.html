FAQ

Lead Follow-Up Automation FAQs for Houston Tree Service Owners

How many follow-ups does it send?

Typically 3–5 messages over 7–10 days, depending on your preference. We customize the sequence so it fits your brand and doesn't feel like spam.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. You tell us your messaging, and we write and set up the follow-up sequence. It sounds like you, not like a bot.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can reply 'STOP' or opt out, and they're removed immediately. We handle compliance so you don't have to worry about it.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Within 3–5 minutes of the lead coming in. The longer you wait, the more likely they call someone else.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in your system and you can see them in your dashboard. You can reach out manually later, or let them fall off after the full sequence runs.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. It plugs into what you're already doing. If you use a CRM or dispatch software, we integrate with it. If you don't, we build the whole thing from scratch.
