What exactly gets automated?

Inbound call routing and qualification, form capture, appointment booking into your calendar, customer confirmation texts, and reminder sequences. Everything that happens between a lead arriving and your team getting a ready-to-serve appointment.

Do I need technical skills to run this?

No. We set it up and manage it. You log in to see your leads and appointments. Your team receives jobs in their normal workflow. You don't touch the automation—it works for you.

What if my team resists new tools?

Your team actually sees less work, not more. They get pre-qualified leads instead of voicemail, and appointments book without them answering calls. Most teams embrace it within a week because it makes their job easier.

How is this different from buying software myself?

You'd buy it, set it up, integrate it, troubleshoot it, and manage it—or hire someone to. We do all of that. You pay for a working system, not a tool you have to figure out.

What does the setup process look like?

One discovery call to understand your business. We build and configure for 7–10 days. You review. We go live and support you for the first week while your team adjusts. Total time from first call to live: about 2 weeks.

What happens if something breaks after launch?

We're monitoring the system. If there's an issue, we fix it. We also provide support for adjustments—adding service areas, updating hours, changing pricing tiers. It's part of the service, not an extra charge.
