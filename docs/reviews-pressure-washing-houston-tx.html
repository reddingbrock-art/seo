FAQ

Common Questions About Google Review Automation for Pressure Washers

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests only to customers you've actually serviced. Google flags fake review patterns and kills accounts. We don't do that.

What if a customer is unhappy?

The system sends review requests after job completion. If someone's upset, they won't leave a review—and that's fine. You wouldn't want a bad review anyway. This targets people who already had a good experience.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google primarily. That's where pressure washing customers search and trust reviews most. Other platforms aren't worth your time.

How does the timing work?

The request goes out automatically after you mark a job complete in your system. We can customize the delay—immediately, next day, or a few hours later—depending on what feels natural for your business.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll audit what you're doing and either replace it or integrate with it. If you're already getting reviews consistently, we'll make sure we don't duplicate effort. Most crews aren't asking at all though.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

It depends on your job volume and customer base. A crew doing 10-15 jobs a week will see significantly more reviews after 30 days than they did before. Even a 15-20% response rate compounds fast.
