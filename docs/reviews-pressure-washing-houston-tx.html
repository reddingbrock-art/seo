FAQ

Google Review Automation for Pressure Washing: Answers to Your Questions

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests only to actual customers after real jobs. Google can tell the difference between authentic reviews and inflated ones. We're asking your real customers for real feedback.

What if a customer is unhappy?

They're still welcome to leave an honest review. We're not filtering or suppressing anything. We're just making sure the customers who had a great experience actually get asked. If someone's unhappy, that's valuable feedback—and you'll see it.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google primarily. That's where pressure washing prospects are looking. We can also route requests to other platforms, but Google is where it matters most for your visibility.

How does the timing work?

Requests go out the day after job completion, when the experience is fresh but your crew's already moved on to the next job. No manual timing required from you.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll audit it and either enhance it or replace it with something that requires zero effort from your team. Most owners realize their current process isn't actually happening consistently.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

It depends on your job volume. Most pressure washing companies see significantly more reviews within the first 60 days simply because they're actually asking. More jobs = more requests = more reviews. We'll show you exactly what to expect based on your numbers.
