FAQ

Lead Follow-Up Questions for Home Security Companies

How many follow-ups does it send?

Typically 3-5 touches over 10-14 days, depending on how you set it up. We customize the sequence based on your sales cycle and what's worked in the past.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. We write the initial messaging based on your brand and your typical sales conversation, then you approve it before it goes live.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can opt out at any time, and the system stops sending immediately. We handle compliance so you don't have to think about it.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Within minutes of the lead coming in. No queue, no delay, no human step required.

What happens to leads that never respond?

The system stops after the final follow-up in your sequence. You'll see in the dashboard that they didn't engage, so you know not to waste time on them.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. It plugs into where leads are currently landing—your website, phone system, whatever you're using now. Your team keeps doing what they do; this handles the initial nurturing.
