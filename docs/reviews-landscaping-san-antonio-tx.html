FAQ

Review Automation Questions for San Antonio Landscapers

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests only to real customers after they've completed a job with you. Google's system filters out artificial reviews anyway, and we never incentivize anyone. You get genuine feedback from people who actually hired you.

What if a customer is unhappy?

That's on you to handle directly—a negative review isn't the system's problem, it's information you need. We send the request; what they write is theirs. The goal is honest reviews, not perfect ones. Real feedback is more credible than spotless profiles.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google is the priority—that's where homeowners search. We can configure requests for other platforms, but Google is where your visibility matters most in San Antonio.

How does the timing work?

The request goes out automatically after the job is marked complete in your system—usually the same day or within 24 hours. That's when customers are most likely to respond, before they forget about the experience.

What if I already have a review process?

We replace it with a hands-off version. If you're already asking but it's manual or sporadic, we automate it and increase consistency. If you're not asking at all, we start from scratch.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Most owners see a significant increase in review volume within the first month because the request goes out every time, not just when someone remembers. The exact number depends on your job volume and how many customers already use Google, but you'll notice the difference fast.
