FAQ

AI Chat for Pool Service: Questions Answered

Will the chat widget actually sound like my business?

Yes. We write it in your voice—how you talk to customers about pools, pricing, scheduling. It sounds like your team answering, because we build it that way.

What happens when the chat can't answer something?

It tells the customer a tech will follow up within a specific timeframe and takes their contact info. Nothing gets dropped—it just becomes a callback you actually make.

Does this replace my receptionist?

It replaces the phone tag and missed calls. If you have a receptionist, they stop fielding repetitive questions and focus on more complex customer issues. If you don't, you gain one without paying salary.

What hours does the chat widget work?

24/7. Even at 11 p.m. when someone remembers their pool needs opening, the chat captures them. You reply during business hours.

How long does setup take?

10-14 days from discovery call to live. We handle the heavy lifting—writing, training, integration. You just review and approve.

What if a customer wants to call instead of chat?

The chat offers your phone number as an option. Some people will call—that's fine. But most of the ones who would've hung up and called a competitor will book through chat instead.
