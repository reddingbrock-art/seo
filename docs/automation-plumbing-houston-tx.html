FAQ

Plumbing Automation FAQs for Houston Owners

What exactly gets automated?

Lead capture and initial response (phone, text, form), customer qualification, appointment booking, schedule confirmation, and review requests after job completion. Your team handles the service and the upsell—the system handles the scheduling and lead routing.

Do I need technical skills to run this?

No. You won't be building or coding anything. Your team gets a simple dashboard to manage availability, view booked jobs, and see which leads came in. If you can use a calendar and a text app, you can run this.

What if my team resists new tools?

This removes work from their plate, not adds to it. They spend less time on callbacks and scheduling, more time on actual jobs. Most teams see the relief within the first week and won't go back.

How is this different from buying software myself?

You could buy 3–4 separate tools and spend 60 hours integrating them, or we do it for you in 10 days, keep it running, and own the connection between your phone, your forms, your texts, and your dispatch. You get done-for-you, not DIY.

What does the setup process look like?

We handle it. You give us access to your existing tools (phone system, website, dispatch software, text if you use it), we build the connections, test it with your team, and launch it. You attend a brief training—that's it.

What happens if something breaks after launch?

You have a dedicated point of contact. If a booking doesn't sync, a response doesn't go out, or something gets misconfigured, we fix it on our end. You don't troubleshoot it yourself.
