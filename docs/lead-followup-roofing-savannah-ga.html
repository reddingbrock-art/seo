FAQ

Lead Follow-Up Automation: Common Questions for Roofing Owners

How many follow-ups does it send?

Typically 3-5 touches over 7-10 days—text, email, call—depending on how you want it set up. You decide the sequence and timing.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. Every message is yours. You write it, approve it, and the system sends it. No templates, no generic language.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can opt out with one reply. The system respects their request and stops contacting them. You'll still see them in your database.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Immediately. The moment a lead comes in from your website, Google, or another source, they get contacted. No waiting for your office.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They cycle through your follow-up sequence and then sit in your system tagged as "inactive." You can review them later or circle back manually if you want.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Absolutely. If your sales team is already calling leads, this backs them up and fills the gaps. It doesn't replace your team—it makes them more effective.
