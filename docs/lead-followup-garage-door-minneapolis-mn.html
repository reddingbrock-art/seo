FAQ

Questions About Lead Follow-Up for Garage Door Businesses

How many follow-ups does it send?

We configure your sequence—usually 2–4 touches over 48–72 hours depending on how your leads convert. You decide the cadence.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. We write your messaging based on your business voice and what closes leads fastest. You approve everything before it runs.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They're removed immediately. The system respects opt-outs and you stay compliant.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Within minutes of the lead coming in—same day, same hour. No batch delays.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in your system and can be re-engaged later or moved to longer nurture sequences. You don't lose them.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. It plugs into how you already take calls and book jobs. Your team doesn't change their workflow.
