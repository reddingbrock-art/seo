FAQ

Automation for Tree Service: Common Questions

What exactly gets automated?

Lead capture from your website and forms, instant responses to customer inquiries, job qualification questions, appointment booking, follow-up to customers who viewed but didn't book, and review requests after completion. Phone calls aren't automated—your team still takes those. We just make sure none of them are missed.

Do I need technical skills to run this?

No. If you can check email and update a calendar, you can run this. We handle setup and integration. Your job is to review leads and close estimates—the part that actually makes money.

What if my team resists new tools?

Most crews get onboard once they realize it means fewer interruptions and clearer job confirmations. We train everyone during launch week. Resistance usually fades after a few days of actually seeing fewer lost leads.

How is this different from buying software myself?

You could spend months piecing together tools, building workflows, and figuring out what works. We've already done that work a hundred times. You get a built system, ongoing support, and someone who understands how tree service actually operates. That's the difference between a tool and a solution.

What does the setup process look like?

Discovery call to understand your operation, 5–7 days of configuration and testing, then launch with a week of hand-holding. Total time from call to live: about 10–14 days. We handle the heavy lifting.

What happens if something breaks after launch?

You've got support. If a lead isn't flowing through correctly, a response template isn't working, or something needs tweaking—you reach out and we fix it. We stay live with you.
