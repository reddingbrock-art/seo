FAQ

Lead Follow-Up Questions for General Contractors

How many follow-ups does it send?

That depends on your business. We typically set it up so leads get 3-5 touches over 7-10 days—enough to catch them when they're ready, not so many that you annoy them.

Can I customize what it says?

Completely. Every message is written by you or us, and it reflects your voice and your process. If you want it to mention financing options or your warranty, it does.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can unsubscribe at any time, and the system respects that. You stay compliant and they stay happy.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Usually within 5-15 minutes of them filling out your form or calling in. The goal is to reach them before they've started talking to someone else.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in your pipeline and get your full sequence. After that, they're marked as cold, and you decide if you want to reach out again later or move on.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Yes. It integrates with how you already work—your phone, your email, your calendar. We're not replacing your system; we're plugging the gaps in it.
