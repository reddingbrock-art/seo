FAQ

AI Chat for Irrigation & Sprinklers: Questions Answered

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on your language, how you describe services, your pricing model, and how you handle common objections. It responds the way you would if you had time to pick up every call.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It's smart enough to know its limits. If a question falls outside its training, it flags the lead as high-priority and sends it directly to you with context. You get the handoff, not a dead conversation.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It handles the inbound volume so your team can focus on dispatch, billing, and customer follow-up. If you have office staff, they're now managing qualified leads instead of screening spam calls.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. Calls and texts come in at 2 a.m.? The AI answers. Your customer gets service details collected and next-day appointments booked before you even wake up.

How long does setup take?

10-14 days from discovery call to live. We handle the integration with your booking system and phone lines. You don't touch any backend—we do it for you.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

It's fine. Some customers ask, and honesty builds trust. The AI tells them straight up, and most customers don't care as long as they got their answer fast and booked their appointment. Speed matters more than illusion.
