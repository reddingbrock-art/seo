FAQ

Common Questions About Lead Follow-Up for Henderson Restoration Companies

How many follow-ups does it send?

It depends on your process. We typically start with an immediate response, then follow up at 2 hours, 4 hours, next day, and 3 days out. You decide the sequence.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. We write the initial templates based on how your team actually talks, then you can adjust them anytime. It should sound like your company, not a bot.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can opt out with one click. The system respects that immediately and stops all follow-ups for that prospect.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Immediately. The moment a lead comes in—form, call, text—they get contacted within minutes while they're still thinking about water damage or fire damage.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in your system. The sequence continues until they opt out or you manually remove them. Some leads take a few days to respond.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Absolutely. It integrates with how you already work. Your team still books, closes, and handles jobs. The system just handles the repetitive follow-up part.
