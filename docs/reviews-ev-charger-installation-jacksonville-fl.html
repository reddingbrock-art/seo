FAQ

Common Questions About Review Automation for EV Charger Installation

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests only to customers you've completed work for. Google's system detects and removes reviews that aren't authentic—we're not gaming that. You'll get real reviews from real installations.

What if a customer is unhappy?

The request still goes out. What they do with it is up to them. This system doesn't filter or suppress unhappy customers—it just makes sure happy ones have an easy way to leave feedback. If there's a real problem, you'd want to know and fix it anyway.

Which platforms does this work on?

We primarily focus on Google reviews because that's where most EV charger buyers are looking. We can also send requests for other platforms depending on your workflow.

How does the timing work?

Requests go out the day a job's marked complete in your system. Customers get a text or email with a direct link to leave a review. Most respond within 24-48 hours.

What if I already have a review process?

We can integrate with what you're doing or replace it entirely. Either way, the goal is removing the manual step so reviews actually happen consistently.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Most installers see a significant increase because they're capturing reviews they weren't getting before. The exact number depends on your current volume and response rate, but we'll show you projections during the discovery call.
