Yes. We train it on your messaging, your service style, and the way you actually talk to customers. It represents your business, not a generic template.

It tells the customer you'll follow up personally and logs the question. Nothing falls through—you get the lead and the context.

It handles the calls and forms your team can't answer when you're in the field. If you don't have a dedicated receptionist, you're suddenly covering 24/7 availability. If you do, they're freed up for other work.

All of them. It answers at 2 a.m. the same way it answers at 2 p.m. Your availability is no longer a limit on your business.
