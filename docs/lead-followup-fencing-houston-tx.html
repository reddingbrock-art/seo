FAQ

Questions About Lead Follow-Up Systems for Houston Fencing Companies

How many follow-ups does it send?

We build a custom sequence based on your business. Most fencing contractors use 3-5 touches over a week—mix of text, email, or voicemail depending on what works best for your leads.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. We write the messaging to match your voice, and you review and approve everything before it goes live. It should sound like you're reaching out, not an automated system.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can opt out anytime, and the system respects that instantly. We handle compliance for you so you stay clean.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Usually within 15-30 minutes of the lead coming in. That's the window where speed matters most.

What happens to leads that never respond?

The system keeps them in your database. Some take weeks to get serious about a fence project. You can re-engage them with seasonal campaigns or follow-up later without cold-calling.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. We plug into your current lead sources and hand off warm leads to your team when they're ready. Your estimators and crew see the same data you do.
