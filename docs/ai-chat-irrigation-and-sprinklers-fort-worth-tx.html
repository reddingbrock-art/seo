FAQ

AI Chat for Irrigation Companies in Fort Worth—Common Questions

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We listen to how your team talks on sales calls, learn your terminology, your service areas, your pricing structure. The AI sounds like someone who's worked in your business. Customers don't know they're talking to an AI unless you tell them.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It says so. If a customer asks something outside the AI's knowledge, it takes their info, tells them a real person will follow up, and hands the lead to you immediately. No guessing. No bad answers.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It replaces the part of your receptionist's job that's spent answering the phone during busy season or after hours. Your team stops doing phone tag and starts doing the work that actually makes money.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. It answers calls at midnight, Saturday morning, during lunch—whenever. You get every call, every time. Most of our clients turn it on during their peak season and keep it running year-round.

How long does setup take?

10-14 days from discovery call to live. We handle everything—training, testing, integration with your calendar, your phone number routing. You don't touch the backend.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

Most don't. But if they do, the AI has a natural response. Something like: "You're talking to our automated booking system. If you'd prefer to speak to someone, I can have our team call you back in the next hour." Most customers are fine with it once they've booked their appointment.
