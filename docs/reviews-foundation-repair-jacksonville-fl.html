FAQ

Review Automation Questions for Foundation Repair Owners

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We only request reviews from actual customers who completed jobs with you. Real work, real request, real review. Google catches fake patterns instantly anyway — this is the opposite of that.

What if a customer is unhappy?

They don't have to leave a review. The request is a reminder, not a demand. If someone had a bad experience, they'll tell you directly or they won't respond. This doesn't change that dynamic.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google primarily. That's where foundation repair customers are looking and where Google's local algorithm weights reviews most heavily for service businesses in Jacksonville.

How does the timing work?

The system sends the request shortly after you mark a job complete in your workflow. We'll dial in the exact window during setup so it lands when the experience is fresh but the customer isn't immediately overwhelmed.

What if I already have a review process?

We usually replace it because most manual processes get inconsistent or forgotten. If yours is working, we'll audit it first. Most owners find automation lifts their volume significantly because it's never skipped.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Depends on your current request rate and your job volume. Most owners see their review velocity increase significantly because consistent, timely requests pull in responses that never came from sporadic manual follow-ups. We'll show you benchmarks during the demo.
