No. We handle the setup and integration. Your team uses a simple dispatch interface—if they can take a phone call, they can use the system.

Discovery call to understand your flow, our team builds the system and integrates with your current phone, website, and email, then we launch and train your crew. Takes 10-14 days start to finish.
