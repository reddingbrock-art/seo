FAQ

Common Questions About AI Chat for Flooring Companies

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We interview you about how you talk to customers, what you emphasize (warranty, speed, craftsmanship), and how you qualify jobs. The AI's trained on your actual intake process, not a generic template.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It tells the customer you'll follow up with them directly. It captures their question, stores it in your dashboard, and flags it so you see it immediately. Nothing falls through the cracks.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It handles the phones when your team's busy or after hours. If you have a receptionist, they're now doing higher-value work—following up on proposals, managing customer relationships, handling exceptions the AI flags.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. It answers at midnight, 6 a.m., and Sunday at 3 p.m. You set your business hours in the system, and it books appointments only into slots you mark as available.

How long does setup take?

10–14 days from discovery call to live. We handle all the integration with your calendar and phone system. You're not doing the technical work.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

They almost never do in the first message. But if they ask, it tells them honestly—then qualifies the job and books them in anyway. Most customers don't care as long as their appointment is confirmed.
