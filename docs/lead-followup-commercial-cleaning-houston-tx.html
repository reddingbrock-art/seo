FAQ

Lead Follow-Up System FAQs for Houston Commercial Cleaning

How many follow-ups does it send?

Typically 3–5 touches over 7–10 days, spread across text and email. We customize the sequence based on your preference and what works for your market.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. We write the initial outreach and follow-up messaging to match your voice and positioning. You review it before it goes live.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They're removed immediately. The system respects unsubscribe requests and compliance requirements.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Within 5–15 minutes of form submission. The faster you engage, the higher your odds of catching them before they shop competitors.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They cycle through the full sequence. If they don't convert after the final touch, they're logged so your team can decide if a personal call makes sense later.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. It sits on top of your current workflow. Leads still come to you; the system just makes sure nothing falls through the cracks while you're in the field.
