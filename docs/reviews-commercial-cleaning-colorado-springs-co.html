FAQ

Review Automation Questions for Commercial Cleaning in Colorado Springs

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. The system sends requests to actual customers after actual jobs. The reviews that come back are real feedback from people you served.

What if a customer is unhappy?

They can leave an honest review—good or bad. This system doesn't filter or suppress feedback. It just makes sure happy customers actually get asked.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google is the priority. That's where your prospects are looking for commercial cleaners in Colorado Springs and where the algorithm favors businesses with recent, consistent reviews.

How does the timing work?

Requests go out automatically after a job is marked complete in your system. Timing hits that 24–48 hour window when the experience is fresh but the customer's already moved on to the next thing.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll use what's working and fix what isn't. If you're getting inconsistent results, the system brings that consistency. If you're not getting consistent requests out, automation will change that immediately.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Most owners see a significant lift because a consistent ask reaches customers who were willing to leave feedback but never got prompted. It's not magic—it's the reviews you were already earning, just captured systematically.
