FAQ

Review Automation FAQs for Fort Lauderdale Painters

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests only to customers who actually completed jobs with you. Google detects fake reviews—the system protects you by only asking real customers.

What if a customer is unhappy?

That's on you to handle before the job closes. The system just sends requests to completed jobs. If a customer is unhappy, don't mark the job complete until it's resolved.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google Local is the priority—that's where most leads come from. Yelp, Facebook, and other platforms can be integrated depending on your needs.

How does the timing work?

Requests send automatically when you mark a job as complete in your system. You control the delay if you want to wait a few days after completion before asking.

What if I already have a review process?

We work alongside it. If you're already asking manually, the system just removes the manual work—you keep your message and approach, we automate the delivery.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Most contractors see 60–80% of customers respond when asked at the right time with a simple request. The number depends on how many jobs you close per month, not on luck.
