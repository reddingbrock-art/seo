FAQ

Common Questions About Review Automation for Paving Contractors

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests only to customers you've actually worked for, after their job is complete. Real work, real customers, real reviews.

What if a customer is unhappy?

The system asks for feedback, but it doesn't filter or suppress negative reviews. If someone's upset, they can leave a bad review—and that's honest. Most unhappy customers never review at all; this just gives them the option. You'll also see negative sentiment early enough to respond.

Which platforms does this work on?

Primarily Google, which is where most customers look first. We can also configure requests for other review platforms depending on your needs.

How does the timing work?

You choose when requests go out—immediately after job completion, next morning, or a few days later. The system sends them automatically based on your schedule.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll work with what you've got. If you're already asking for reviews manually and want to automate it, we set up the system to replace that process. If you're doing nothing, we start from scratch.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Most contractors see a significant increase because they're actually asking. If you're completing 10 jobs a month and getting no reviews, automating requests typically results in 3-5 new reviews monthly. The longer you run it, the more momentum builds.
