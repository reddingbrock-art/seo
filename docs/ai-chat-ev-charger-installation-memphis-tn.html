FAQ

AI Chat for EV Charger Installation: Questions & Answers

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on how you talk to customers—your tone, your service area, your specific process. Callers won't detect they're talking to an AI. They'll get a professional response that matches your brand.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It's honest about it and collects the customer's information so you can follow up. We also give you a dashboard showing every question it couldn't answer, so you can train it better over time.

Does this replace my receptionist?

It handles intake and qualification—the work that kills your receptionist's day. If you have someone on staff, they move into follow-up and relationship work instead of answering the same questions 50 times.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. Calls at 11 PM get answered. Form submissions at 3 AM get qualified. You decide what time window you want callbacks prioritized during business hours.

How long does setup take?

10–14 days from discovery to live. You're not waiting months. One call to kick off, a couple of reviews along the way, and then it's running.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

It's fine. The AI is upfront if asked. But most customers don't care—they just want someone to listen and get them scheduled. A fast, accurate answer beats silence every time.
