FAQ

AI Chat Agent FAQs for Commercial Cleaning Businesses

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on your scripts, how you talk about jobs, your service types, and your process. When a prospect calls, they hear your brand voice and the way you answer questions—not generic corporate robot.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It says so directly. If a prospect asks about something outside your typical scope, the agent tells them it needs to connect with your team and transfers or schedules a callback. No guessing, no making things up.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It handles the first touchpoint—answering, qualifying, scheduling. If you have admin staff, they focus on prep work, scheduling, and followup. If you don't, you're finally getting one.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. Calls at 11 PM get captured. Texts on Sunday morning get answered. Your phone stops ringing at 2 AM—the system handles it and your team sees it in the morning.

How long does setup take?

10-14 days from discovery to live. Most of that is us building and you reviewing. You're not waiting months or learning new software.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

The agent is honest about it if asked. Most prospects don't care—they care that someone answered and got them scheduled. If they want to talk to you directly, the system can transfer them or schedule it. Transparency builds trust.
