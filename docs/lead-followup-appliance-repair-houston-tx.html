FAQ

Questions About Lead Follow-Up Automation for Appliance Repair

How many follow-ups does it send?

It depends on your preference. Most appliance repair companies use 3–4 touches over 5–7 days. We customize it based on what converts for your market and your bandwidth.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. We write it based on how you talk to customers. You can change messaging anytime, and we'll update the sequence for you.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can opt out anytime. The system removes them immediately and respects their preference. You stay compliant.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Usually within 2–5 minutes of form submission or lead capture. It's automatic—no waiting for a team member to see the notification.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in the system and get your full sequence. If they don't convert after that, they're marked as unresponsive but stay in your CRM so you can reach out manually later if you want.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. It sits on top of what you're already doing. Your dispatcher still controls the schedule. Your team still does the work. This just handles the part that's slipping through the cracks.
