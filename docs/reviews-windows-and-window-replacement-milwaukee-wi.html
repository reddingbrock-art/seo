FAQ

Automated Review Requests for Windows & Window Replacement: Common Questions

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests only to customers in your system who completed a job. Google filters out anything that looks inauthentic. You're asking real customers to leave real reviews.

What if a customer is unhappy?

The system requests reviews from everyone. If a customer had a bad experience, they can leave that feedback, and you'll see it. That's actually useful—it tells you where to improve. We're not filtering or suppressing anything; we're just automating the ask.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google reviews is the priority. That's where Milwaukee homeowners search for window companies. We focus there because that's where the phone calls come from.

How does the timing work?

Requests go out the day after job completion. That's when customers remember the experience most clearly but before they've forgotten about you. Timing matters.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll talk through what you're doing now. If you're asking verbally or sending manual emails, this replaces that and makes it consistent. If you're doing nothing, we're building the system from scratch.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

It depends on your job volume and past response rate. Most businesses see significantly more reviews within 30-60 days because the requests are actually getting sent. Some customers who wanted to leave a review but forgot now have the prompt.
