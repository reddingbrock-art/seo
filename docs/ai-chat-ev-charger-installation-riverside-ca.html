Yes. We train it on your company name, service areas, pricing, and how your team talks to customers. It'll mention Riverside, Corona, Moreno Valley—whatever your coverage area is—and explain your installation process in your voice.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It escalates to your team immediately with a transcript of the conversation. You're never out of the loop. Complex questions about financing, HOA approvals, or electrical upgrades go straight to a human.

No. It replaces the intake and scheduling work your receptionist does so they can focus on closing, follow-up, and customer service. Most teams end up more efficient, not downsized.

24/7. It answers calls and chats at 6 AM on Saturday or midnight on Tuesday. You set business hours for booking and send after-hours inquiries to your team the next morning, or let the AI capture them and you respond during business hours.

10–14 days from discovery call to live. We handle all integration and training. Your team just needs to answer one discovery call and review the AI once before launch.
