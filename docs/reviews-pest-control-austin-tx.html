FAQ

Common Questions About Review Automation for Pest Control

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We only send requests to customers whose jobs you actually completed. The reviews that come back are from real people you've already serviced.

What if a customer is unhappy?

The system sends review requests to everyone post-job. What they write is up to them. If someone leaves a bad review, that's actual feedback you can act on—and it's more credible to Google than no reviews at all.

Which platforms does this work on?

Primarily Google. That's where your Austin customers are looking for pest control, and that's where review count matters most for visibility.

How does the timing work?

Requests go out automatically when you mark a job complete in your system. You can set a delay if you want—like 2 hours after completion—so the customer's had time to experience the full service.

What if I already have a review process?

We can work around it or replace it depending on what you're doing now. Most companies we talk to don't have a consistent process—this just makes it systematic and hands-free.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

It depends on your close rate and how many jobs you're running. Most companies see significantly more reviews once requests become automatic. You're capturing reviews you were already earning—you just weren't asking for them.
