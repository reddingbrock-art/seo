FAQ

Review Automation for Fire & Water Restoration: Questions Answered

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests only to customers with completed jobs in your system. Real work, real customers, real reviews.

What if a customer is unhappy?

They can leave whatever review they want. We're asking for feedback after work's done—that's on you to deliver quality. If you do, reviews follow.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google, primarily. We integrate with your job data and send requests that direct customers to your Google Business Profile.

How does the timing work?

You set it. Most owners want requests going out same-day or next-day after job completion. You control the delay.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll take over or work alongside it. If you're asking manually now, automation just removes that task and makes sure no job slips through.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Most jobs have a customer who can review. If you're finishing 8 jobs a week and asking 6 of them, expect 1–2 reviews weekly if your work quality is solid. Over a year, that's 50–100 new reviews.
