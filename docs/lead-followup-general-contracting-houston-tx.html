FAQ

Lead Follow-Up Questions for Houston General Contractors

How many follow-ups does it send?

Typically 3-5 touches over 7-10 days, depending on how your business works. We customize the sequence to match your sales cycle, not the other way around.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. You tell us your messaging, tone, and what you want to ask. We build it from there. It sounds like you, not a template service.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can unsubscribe with one click, and they're out. We handle compliance. No exceptions, no manual work on your end.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Within minutes of the lead coming in. Before they've even left your website, sometimes before they close the browser.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in the sequence until the last touch goes out. After that, they're marked dormant. You can re-engage them later with seasonal campaigns if you want.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. It plugs into where your leads come from—your website, phone system, whatever you're using now. Your team's process doesn't change; the system just handles the repetitive part.
