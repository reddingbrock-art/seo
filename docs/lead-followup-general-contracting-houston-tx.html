FAQ

Lead Follow-Up Questions for Houston General Contractors

How many follow-ups does it send?

That's up to you. Most GC offices run 5–7 touches over 14–21 days: initial text, follow-up email, reminder call, secondary text, and a final 'this expires' message. You set the cadence and the message. We execute it.

Can I customize what it says?

Completely. You write the templates or we write them with you. The system personalizes each one with the lead's project details, your quote amount, and their preferred contact window. All while staying on-brand.

What if a lead asks to stop?

One click and they're out. Full compliance with Do Not Contact requests. Your team controls the opt-out list, and the system respects every preference. You stay compliant; you stay professional.

Does this work with my current CRM?

Yes. We integrate with the major platforms—Buildr, Jobber, HubSpot, Zoho—or pull directly from your estimating software. If your leads live in a spreadsheet, we can work with that too. Your data moves automatically, no manual entry.
