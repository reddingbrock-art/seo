FAQ

Common Questions About Lead Follow-Up Automation for Houston Contractors

How many follow-ups does it send?

You decide. Most contractors use 3-5 follow-ups spaced over 7-10 days. If a lead responds or books, it stops. If they ignore all attempts, it moves them out of the active queue.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. You write the message template once—your words, your tone, your call-to-action. We build it into the system. Every lead gets the same message, so nothing falls through cracks based on who's responding that day.

What if a lead asks to stop?

They can reply STOP or click an unsubscribe link in any message. They're removed immediately and never contacted again. Full compliance, no exceptions.

Does this work with my current CRM?

We build it in GoHighLevel, which integrates with most CRMs and job management platforms. If you're on something custom, we can connect it. During onboarding, we map out exactly how your data flows.
