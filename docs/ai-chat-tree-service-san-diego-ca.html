FAQ

AI Chat for Tree Service: Questions & Answers

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on your language, your service offerings, and how you actually talk to customers. It doesn't sound robotic or corporate. It sounds like someone who knows tree work.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It routes the customer to you with full context—the conversation history, their address, their request. You take it from there. No information is lost.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It covers when your receptionist (or you) can't pick up. It qualifies leads and books appointments. Your team still closes the sale and does the work. It's a filter, not a replacement.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. Calls and form submissions come in at any hour. Your chat is responding instantly, every time, whether it's 2 a.m. on Sunday or Tuesday at noon.

How long does setup take?

Discovery to live deployment is 10 to 14 days. Most of that is us building and refining. You're not waiting months. You're taking calls in two weeks.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

It's not hidden. The chat makes it clear it's an automated assistant helping to get information and schedule. Customers don't mind. They care about being heard and getting a callback. The AI does both.
