FAQ

AI Chat Agent FAQs for Flooring Businesses in Orlando

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We configure it with your name, your service descriptions, your pricing, and even your communication style. Callers hear a natural voice that matches how your team talks.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It's designed to handle the core flow — qualifying the job, collecting contact info, and booking the estimate. If a caller asks something outside that, it collects their message and routes it to you immediately.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It replaces the voicemail, the callback delays, and the missed leads. If you have a receptionist, they now focus on managing jobs and customers instead of fielding intake calls.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. Nights, weekends, holidays — it's always on. You can set specific hours if you prefer to close certain times, but most owners let it run around the clock.

How long does setup take?

Discovery and configuration take about a week. You're live and answering calls in 10-14 days from your first call with us.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

Most don't notice. The voice is natural and the conversation flows like talking to a real person. If they do ask, it's fine — you're transparent that you're offering 24/7 availability so they never get voicemail.
