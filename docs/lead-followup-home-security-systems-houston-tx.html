FAQ

Lead Follow-Up System: Questions From Houston Security Companies

How many follow-ups does it send?

We typically run 2–3 touches over 48 hours. You can adjust the sequence based on your preference and what works for your customers. We'll optimize it after the first 2–3 weeks once we see what converts.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. We write it to match your brand and your process. You review every message before it goes live, and you can update them anytime if your offer or messaging changes.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can opt out at any time. The system respects that immediately and logs it so your team knows the contact doesn't want to be reached again.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Within minutes of the lead coming in. No queue, no waiting for business hours. If it's 11 p.m., they still get touched before morning.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in your system. You can see the full history—all touches sent, all responses received. Your team can follow up manually if you want, or re-engage them during peak season later.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. It feeds into your current workflow. Prospects respond, conversations sync to your system, and your dispatcher schedules from there. Nothing changes about how you close deals—just what happens before they land in your hands.
