FAQ

Review Automation FAQs for Scottsdale Generator Services

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests only to customers whose jobs are documented in your system. Real jobs, real customers, real reviews.

What if a customer is unhappy?

The system doesn't suppress or filter unhappy customers. It sends to everyone who had work completed. If someone's genuinely upset, that's a service issue to fix separately—not a review problem to hide.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google, Yelp, Facebook, and industry-specific review sites. The system pushes requests to whichever platforms matter most for your business.

How does the timing work?

Requests go out automatically when your crew marks a job complete. If you close it at 5 PM Tuesday, the customer gets the request that evening or the next morning. Timing's always fresh.

What if I already have a review process?

We integrate with what you've got or replace it entirely. Either way, we eliminate the manual steps and make sure nothing falls through the cracks.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

That depends on your job volume and customer responsiveness. Most operators see significantly more reviews simply because requests now go out automatically instead of getting delayed or forgotten.
