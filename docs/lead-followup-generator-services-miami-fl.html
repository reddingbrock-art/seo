FAQ

Generator Services Lead Follow-Up: Common Questions

How many follow-ups does it send?

We typically set up 3-5 touches over 7-10 days, depending on your process. You decide the exact sequence and timing during setup.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. Every message is written by you or customized during our setup call. It should sound like your team, not a template.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They're immediately removed from the follow-up sequence. Compliance is built in.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

The first contact goes out within minutes of the lead coming in. We capture them while they're hot.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in your system so you can see the full history. You can review them, follow up manually if you want, or let them age out. You have complete visibility.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Yes. We build it to fit how you already work. If you have a CRM or dispatch software, we integrate with it. If you don't, we work with what you have.
