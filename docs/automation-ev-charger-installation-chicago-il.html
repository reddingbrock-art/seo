What exactly gets automated?

Inbound calls and form submissions. The system answers, qualifies the job, checks your availability, books the appointment, and sends confirmations. If a call needs a human, it rings your team. Everything else runs 24/7.

Do I need technical skills to run this?

No. We build and manage it for you. You manage jobs the same way you do now. The system works in the background.

What if my team resists new tools?

They won't be using a new tool. Your crew gets cleaner job details and fewer interruptions. Your office team gets fewer repetitive calls to handle. It makes their day easier, not harder.

How is this different from buying software myself?

You'd be shopping, learning, integrating, troubleshooting, and maintaining it solo. We do all that. We connect it to your specific workflow, train your team, and keep it running. You pay once and it works.

What does the setup process look like?

One discovery call with us. We handle the rest—plugging in your phone, connecting your calendar, testing every scenario. You review it once. We launch it. That's it.

What happens if something breaks after launch?

We monitor it and fix it. If a call doesn't route right or a booking doesn't sync, we catch it and repair it on our end. You don't get stuck with broken automation.
