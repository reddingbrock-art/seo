FAQ

Lead Follow-Up Questions for Jacksonville Mold Remediation Companies

How many follow-ups does it send?

Typically 3-5 touches spread across 48-72 hours, depending on how you want to set it up. We customize the sequence so it matches your style and doesn't feel spammy.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. We write it to sound like you, not like a bot. You approve every message before it goes live.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can opt out with one click. The system respects that immediately. You stay compliant, and nobody gets annoyed.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Within 60 seconds of the lead coming in. If someone submits a form at 9:47 a.m., they get a response before 9:48.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in the system and get your full sequence. After that, they're marked in your CRM so you know they didn't bite. Your team can circle back manually if you want, but the automation has done its job.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. The system integrates with whatever you're already using—your forms, your CRM, your dispatch board. It supplements your operation, doesn't replace it.
