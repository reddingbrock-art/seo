FAQ

Lead Follow-Up Questions for Mold Remediation Companies

How many follow-ups does it send?

Typically three to five touches over 7-10 days—a combination of calls and texts. You set the sequence based on what works for your market.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. Every message comes from you, written by you. We don't send generic templated copy—it sounds like your company.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They're removed immediately. Compliance is built in. No exceptions.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Within minutes of the lead coming in. Same day, while they're still thinking about calling three other companies.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in your system as cold leads. You can re-engage them seasonally or manually if you choose. Nothing gets deleted without your say-so.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. It's a layer on top of what you're already doing—estimating, scheduling, crew management. Doesn't touch any of that.
