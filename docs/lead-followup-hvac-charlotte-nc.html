FAQ

Lead Follow-Up System FAQs for HVAC Contractors

How many follow-ups does it send?

Usually 3: text immediately, email if no reply within 30 minutes, second text within 2 hours. You can adjust the timing and number of touches based on what works for your business.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. We work with you to write the exact messaging so it sounds like your business, not a template. You approve every word before it goes live.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can reply with STOP or unsubscribe, and they're removed from that sequence immediately. No violations, no compliance headaches. You stay clean.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Text goes out within 2-3 minutes of form submission. If they submit at 2 p.m., they're hearing from you at 2:03 p.m.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in your system. You can run them through a different nurture sequence later, or let them sit. No lead gets deleted—you decide what happens next.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. It plugs into whatever you're already using—Google, Facebook, your website, your CRM. Nothing changes about how you operate. This just fills the gap.
