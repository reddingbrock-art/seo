FAQ

Lead Follow-Up System FAQs for Generator Services

How many follow-ups does it send?

Typically 3-5 touches over 7-10 days—initial contact, reminder, final check-in. We customize based on what works for your business and what compliance requires.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. We write the messaging to match your voice and service area. You approve every message before it goes out.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can opt out with a single reply. The system stops all future messages to that contact immediately. We handle compliance automatically.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Typically within 5-15 minutes of the lead landing. Timing can be adjusted based on when your team's most likely to be available to close.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They fall off the sequence after the final touch. The system logs them so you can see patterns. Some businesses circle back to cold leads quarterly—we can set that up too.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. If you're already using a scheduling tool or dispatch software, we integrate with it. This layer sits on top of what you're doing and catches the leads you'd otherwise lose.
