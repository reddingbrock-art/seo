No. You don't interact with the technical side. We build it, test it, launch it. You log in to see booked appointments and review captured leads. That's it.

Your team doesn't have to learn anything new. The system handles calls and forms—your crew still shows up to jobs exactly like they do now. Actually, they'll push back less because they're not answering phones anymore.

What happens if something breaks after launch?
