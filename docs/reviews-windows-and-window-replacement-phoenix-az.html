FAQ

Google Review Automation FAQs for Window Replacement Companies

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests only to actual customers whose jobs you've completed. Google filters out fake reviews anyway, and risking your profile isn't worth it. We're asking real customers to share real experiences.

What if a customer is unhappy?

You have the option to screen unhappy customers before the request goes out—or let it go out and respond professionally in your reply. Either way, the system doesn't suppress honest feedback; it just makes sure requests are going out consistently.

Which platforms does this work on?

We integrate with most job management systems and CRM platforms. During your discovery call, we confirm compatibility with whatever you're using now.

How does the timing work?

The request goes out automatically when you mark a job as complete. You can set a time delay if you prefer—for example, 24 hours after completion—to give the customer a moment before asking.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll work with whatever you're doing now. If you're manually asking and want to cut that work in half, we can handle the automation while you keep your personal touch where it matters.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Most owners see a significant lift in review volume within the first 30 days because requests are consistent and well-timed. The exact number depends on your job volume and customer base, which we'll estimate during your call.
