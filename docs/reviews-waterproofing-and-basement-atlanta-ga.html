FAQ

Google Review Automation: Questions from Atlanta Waterproofing Contractors

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests only to customers whose jobs you've actually completed. Google will block anything that looks inauthentic. This is real feedback from real customers.

What if a customer is unhappy?

The system doesn't suppress or filter negative feedback. If someone's not satisfied, they're not going to leave a review anyway. We send the request; what they do with it is up to them. Most contractors find their satisfaction is high enough that this isn't a problem.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google is the priority because that's where customers look first. Other platforms can be included depending on your setup. We focus on Google because that's where the local search visibility happens.

How does the timing work?

The request goes out 24-48 hours after you mark the job complete. That window is when the experience is fresh, but you're not interrupting them right at the end of the workday.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll work around it. If you're already sending manual requests, we can take over the routine ones so you focus only on the edge cases. Most contractors just turn their old process off and let the system handle it.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

It depends on your volume and current review rate. If you're doing 15 jobs a month and getting three reviews, the system typically converts significantly more of those customers. Expect to see the difference within 60 days.
