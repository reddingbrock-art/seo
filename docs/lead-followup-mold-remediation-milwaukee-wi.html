FAQ

Questions About Lead Follow-Up for Mold Remediation in Milwaukee

How many follow-ups does it send?

We typically send 2-3 follow-ups over 5-7 days if a lead doesn't respond to the first message. You control the timing and messaging.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. We write your initial message and follow-ups based on how you talk to customers. You review and approve everything before it goes live.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can opt out anytime. The system respects that immediately and removes them from future sends.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Within 1-2 minutes of the lead submitting a form or your team tagging them in the system. Phone leads get a callback message just as fast.

What happens to leads that never respond?

After the follow-up sequence ends, they stay in your system as a past lead. You can review them anytime or we can re-engage them seasonally if you want.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. It plugs into how you already take leads and work with customers. If you're using another tool, we integrate with it.
