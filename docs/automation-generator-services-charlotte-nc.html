Generator Service Automation: Questions Answered

What exactly gets automated?

Lead capture from phone, web forms, and chat; qualification questions that dig into job type and urgency; routing to the right dispatcher or tech; and scheduling confirmations. Your team still handles the actual service calls and estimates—we just make sure nothing falls through the cracks.

Do I need technical skills to run this?

No. We set it up end-to-end. Your dispatcher or office manager just logs in, sees the leads, and acts on them. It's built to work the way your team already thinks.

What if my team resists new tools?

Most resistance comes from unclear training or a tool that doesn't fit how they actually work. We build it around your process, not the other way around. We also do the training, so your team isn't left guessing.

How is this different from buying software myself?

If you buy generic software, you're responsible for connecting everything, training your team, and fixing it when something breaks. We do all of that. We also know generator service workflows specifically—we're not selling you a generic tool.

What does the setup process look like?

Discovery call, 5–7 days of configuration and testing, training session with your team, then live launch. You're up and running in 10–14 days, not months.

What happens if something breaks after launch?

You've got us. We monitor the system, handle updates, and fix issues. If a call isn't routing right or a form stops working, you contact us and we fix it—usually within a few hours.
