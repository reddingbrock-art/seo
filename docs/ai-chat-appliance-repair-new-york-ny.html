Common Questions About AI Chat for Appliance Repair

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on your actual calls, your tone, how you talk about jobs, and your specific service approach. It's not generic—it represents your business, not some corporate template.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It hands the customer off to your team immediately with context. Your dispatcher or office manager takes over the call with full job details already captured. No dropped calls, no confused customers.

Does this replace my receptionist?

It replaces what a receptionist does on the phone—answer calls, collect information, and book appointments. Your team stays focused on dispatch, job scheduling, and customer relationships. You might reduce admin hours, or you might have that person doing higher-value work.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. Calls and texts come in at midnight, 6 a.m., or Sunday—the AI picks them up instantly. You decide what information gets captured after hours and whether appointments can be booked before your first technician rolls out.

How long does setup take?

You're live and taking calls within 10-14 days. We handle the integration with your phone system and booking calendar. There's minimal work on your end after the initial setup call.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

The AI's designed to be transparent if the customer asks directly. Most don't—because the conversation feels natural. If they prefer a human, the AI transfers them immediately. Transparency builds trust; hiding it doesn't.
