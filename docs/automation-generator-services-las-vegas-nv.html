FAQ

Generator Service Automation Questions & Answers

What exactly gets automated?

Phone calls, text inquiries, and web form submissions get answered instantly with service options. Customers confirm appointments, which auto-populate your calendar and route to the right tech. Follow-ups, reminders, and review requests happen on their own schedule.

Do I need technical skills to run this?

No. You don't touch the backend. Your team gets a simple dispatch interface—they see jobs, confirm arrival, mark complete. That's it. Everything else is handled by the automation.

What if my team resists new tools?

The new tool removes work from their plate instead of adding to it. They get fewer phone interruptions, clearer job assignments, and fewer back-and-forth texts. Resistance usually flips once they see the first week running smoother.

How is this different from buying software myself?

You can buy software, but you still have to build the workflows, integrate your phone line, train your team, and troubleshoot when something breaks. We do all of that. You get a working system, not a tool you have to figure out.

What does the setup process look like?

We handle onboarding. You share your phone number, calendar access, and team member contact info. We build the automation, test it, and walk your team through the dispatch interface before going live. Most owners are hands-off after the discovery call.

What happens if something breaks after launch?

You've got dedicated support. If a call doesn't route, a text doesn't send, or a calendar sync hiccups, we fix it. You're not troubleshooting on your own—we keep the system running.
