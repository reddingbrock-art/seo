FAQ

Common Questions About AI Chat Agents for Home Security Businesses

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on your company voice, service language, and how your team talks to customers. It'll reference your specific neighborhoods, your guarantee, and your process—not generic security industry jargon.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It knows its limits. If a prospect asks something outside its knowledge, it collects their info and flags the conversation for you. No made-up answers. No frustration.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It replaces the phone tag and missed calls. If you have a receptionist, they now handle complex calls, scheduling conflicts, and special requests instead of answering 100 routine questions a day.

What hours does the AI work?

All of them. It answers at 2 AM when someone's worried about a break-in attempt, books appointments during business hours, and sends follow-ups whenever you need.

How long does setup take?

10–14 days from your discovery call. We handle the training, testing, and integration. You just review and approve before we flip the switch.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

Most don't notice. But if they ask, it's honest and transparent. It offers to transfer them to a human or answer what it can. You're using it to answer fast—not to trick anyone.
