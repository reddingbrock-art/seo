FAQ

Common Questions About Google Review Automation for Foundation Repair

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send review requests only to customers you've actually worked for. Google's algorithm filters out fake reviews anyway, and your reputation depends on authentic customer feedback. We're just making sure the real customers actually leave it.

What if a customer is unhappy?

Unhappy customers won't leave a review just because you asked. Our system requests feedback after job completion—if the work went well, they're more likely to respond. If there's a problem, it surfaces before the review request goes out, not after.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google is the priority because that's where homeowners search for foundation repair. We can also push to other platforms your customers use. We discuss your target platforms during the discovery call.

How does the timing work?

The review request goes out automatically 24–48 hours after you mark a job complete. That's when the work is fresh in their mind and they're most likely to respond. You set the timing rules that work for your business.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll audit what you're doing now and show you where reviews are falling through the cracks. Most crews find that replacing manual requests with automation surfaces 30–50% more reviews without extra effort.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

It depends on your current volume and how many customers you're already losing. Most foundation repair companies see a significant increase within the first 60 days because they're finally asking at the right moment. We'll give you a more specific estimate during your discovery call.
