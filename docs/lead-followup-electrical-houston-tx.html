FAQ

Lead Follow-Up System FAQs for Houston Electrical Contractors

How many follow-ups does it send?

You control the sequence. Most electrical shops use 3–7 touches over 14–30 days (text on day 1, email day 3, call day 7, etc.). You set it once and it runs automatically for every new lead.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. You write the template once, and it personalizes with the lead's name, service type, and call reason. You also decide which channel goes out when and how many times before it stops.

What if a lead asks to stop?

They can reply STOP to any text, or you can manually pause them in the dashboard. The system respects opt-outs automatically—no compliance risk, no angry callbacks.

Does this work with my current CRM?

If you're using GoHighLevel, it's native. If you're in Jobber, ServiceTitan, or another platform, we integrate it and sync your lead data so nothing falls through the cracks.

How long does it take to set up?

10–14 days from kickoff. We handle the integration, build your follow-up sequence, connect it to your lead sources, and train your team. You don't touch the technical side.

Will this actually convert more leads?

Consistency converts leads. Most shops see faster callbacks and more booked jobs within the first 30 days because leads actually hear from you when they're still thinking about the job. Your numbers will show it.
