FAQ

Review Automation Questions for Miami Generator Services

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests only to customers you've actually worked for. Google flags fake review patterns—we're just making sure you ask the customers who know you.

What if a customer is unhappy?

They can leave any rating they want. Our job is to ask fairly. A three-star from a real customer is better than silence, and it signals authenticity to Google.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google Business Profile and Facebook. Those are where Miami customers look and where search algorithms pay attention.

How does the timing work?

The request goes out the day after job completion, or on a schedule you set. We control the frequency so you're not spamming customers.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll work around it. If you're getting reviews inconsistently, we layer automation on top. If nothing's happening, we replace the process entirely.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

That depends on your current request rate. Most owners see significantly more submissions once asking becomes automatic and consistent. You'll notice the difference within a month.
