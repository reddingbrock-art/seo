FAQ

Common Questions About AI Chat Agents for Fire & Water Restoration

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We write the agent's voice using your team's actual call language, how you talk about pricing, service areas, and what makes your company different. It'll sound professional and local, not robotic.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It flags the call or text and alerts you immediately with the full transcript. Your team jumps in. The customer never realizes they talked to AI first—they just got a fast response.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It handles the calls your team can't pick up—nights, weekends, while you're on jobs. If you have a receptionist, they focus on scheduling, follow-ups, and customer service instead of fielding repetitive qualification calls.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. It answers at 2 a.m. just as well as it does at 10 a.m. You can set it to business hours only if you prefer, but most restoration owners leave it live all night.

How long does setup take?

10–14 days from your discovery call to live. We need your phone number, your scheduling system access, and 20 minutes to map how you talk about jobs. Then we build and train.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

Some will. We don't hide it—the agent can confirm it's an AI assistant if asked, then transfers or schedules a call with your team. Most customers just care that someone answered and can help them.
