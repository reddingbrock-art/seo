FAQ

Common Questions About Review Automation for Irrigation Contractors

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send review requests only to customers whose jobs are marked complete in your system. Google can verify these are real customers. Fake reviews violate Google's terms and we don't do that.

What if a customer is unhappy?

If they don't leave a review, nothing happens. If they do, it's honest feedback. That's the point—you're asking actual customers, not filtering who gets to respond. Use negative feedback to improve.

Which platforms does this work on?

We focus on Google reviews because that's where customers search and where search ranking matters most. If you use other review sites, we can discuss those separately.

How does the timing work?

The review request goes out the same day the job is marked complete, or the next morning—whatever timing you choose. That's when customers remember the work and are most likely to respond.

What if I already have a review process?

If your crews are already asking consistently and collecting reviews, you might not need this. If it's inconsistent or sporadic, this fills the gap and multiplies what you're already doing.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Most irrigation shops find that 15–30% of customers will leave a review when asked at the right moment. The exact number depends on your job volume and customer base, but you'll see results within the first month.
