Yes. We customize it with your service areas, your material options, your pricing structure, and your tone. It answers questions the way you would if you picked up the phone—not with corporate script language.

If you have one, it replaces the parts that kill her day—data entry, answering the same questions about service areas and timelines, scheduling estimates. You keep the human relationship parts. If you don't have one, you've just hired 24/7 coverage without a salary.

What hours does the AI work?

All hours. It answers at 2 AM just like noon. You get notifications and can jump in anytime, but leads are never waiting for business hours.
