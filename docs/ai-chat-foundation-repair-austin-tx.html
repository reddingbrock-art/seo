FAQ

AI Chat for Foundation Repair: Common Questions

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We write the responses based on how you talk to customers and what you actually say during estimates. It sounds like someone from your team, not a generic bot.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It flags the conversation and routes it to your team with full context, so whoever picks it up knows exactly what the customer's asking. No restart, no frustration.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It replaces the time your crew spends answering the same questions repeatedly. If you have a receptionist, they'll spend their time on higher-value work. If you don't, you're finally not dropping leads because no one's picking up.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. It works nights, weekends, and holidays. You set your availability—it can take messages after hours or qualify leads in real time whenever you prefer.

How long does setup take?

10-14 days from discovery call to live. Most of that is us building and you reviewing. You're not waiting months for a complex rollout.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

They'll know within the first message or two that it's not a human. That's fine. Most customers prefer getting an instant answer about their foundation to waiting on hold. The AI clearly identifies itself and hands off to a real person when it's time to schedule.
