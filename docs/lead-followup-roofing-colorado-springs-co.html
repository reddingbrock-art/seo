FAQ

Lead Follow-Up Automation FAQ for Colorado Springs Roofing

How many follow-ups does it send?

We typically configure 3-5 touchpoints over 7-10 days using a mix of text and email. The exact sequence depends on your sales cycle and what you want to test.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. We write the initial templates based on how you talk to leads, then you approve them before they go live. You can update messaging anytime.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

The system respects opt-out requests immediately. If they reply with a stop command or tell you directly, they come off the follow-up sequence.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Within 2-5 minutes of the lead submitting a form. The faster you reach out, the better your odds of getting a response.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They finish the sequence and go dormant. You can pull a list of cold leads anytime and decide whether to reach out manually or let them rest.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. If you're already using another system, we integrate with it. The automation doesn't replace your team—it handles the first 48 hours so your crew can focus on estimates and jobs.
