FAQ

Lead Follow-Up Questions from Austin Electrical Contractors

How many follow-ups does it send?

Typically 3–5 touches over 7 days depending on what your prospect does. If they respond or book, the sequence stops. If they go silent, you still have them tagged for manual outreach or nurturing.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. We write copy that matches your brand and business, but you can adjust it. If you want to emphasize emergency service or specific expertise, that goes into the messages.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They're removed from future sequences immediately. Compliance is built in. No exceptions.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Within minutes of the lead submitting your form or reaching your number. No waiting for business hours.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in your system tagged by status—warm, cold, or unresponsive. You can manually reach out, add them to a slower nurture track, or re-engage them seasonally.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. Whether you're using a current CRM, dispatch software, or spreadsheets, we integrate it. The system complements your workflow; it doesn't replace it.
