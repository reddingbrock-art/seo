FAQ

Review Automation FAQs for Virginia Beach Irrigation & Sprinkler Contractors

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests only to customers whose jobs you actually completed. Google can tell the difference between authentic reviews from real customers and fake ones. We're not cutting corners.

What if a customer is unhappy?

They won't leave a review you don't want. An unhappy customer typically won't respond to a review request at all. The system is sending requests—not forcing anyone to leave anything. If there's a real problem, you fix it before the request goes out.

Which platforms does this work on?

We focus on Google Reviews because that's where your customers are searching. We can also set up requests for other platforms depending on your needs. We'll discuss your priorities on the call.

How does the timing work?

The request goes out the day after an invoice closes. That's when the customer is still satisfied and the work is fresh in their mind. It's the highest-converting window.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll audit what you're doing and either build on it or replace it with something faster and more reliable. Most contractors are asking for reviews sporadically—we make it consistent and automatic.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

It depends on your monthly job volume and your current ask rate. A contractor closing 20 jobs a month who's asking for zero reviews now will see significantly more. Most see a steady stream within the first month.
