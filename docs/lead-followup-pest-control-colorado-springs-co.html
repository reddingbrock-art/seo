FAQ

Lead Follow-Up Questions for Colorado Springs Pest Control Owners

How many follow-ups does it send?

That depends on your preference. Most pest control owners use a 3-touch sequence: immediate text, email after 4 hours, phone call after 24 hours. You decide the cadence based on what your office can handle.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. We write the first draft based on your service area and typical lead type, but you can edit every message. Your voice stays in the copy, not ours.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can reply STOP to any text, and they're out of the sequence immediately. Compliance is built in.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Usually within 60–90 seconds of a lead submitting a form or your team logging the call. Phone calls go out the same day or next morning, depending on when the lead contacted you.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in your CRM and get marked as cold after the final touch. Your office can still call them manually if they want, but you'll know which leads are worth the effort based on engagement.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Yes. If you're using a phone system, scheduling software, or dispatch app already, the follow-up system sits on top of it and feeds leads directly to your workflow. No rip-and-replace required.
