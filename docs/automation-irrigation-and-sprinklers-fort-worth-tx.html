No. Your team answers calls and texts normally. The system works behind the scenes. If something needs adjusting, we handle it—you don't log into a dashboard and configure anything.

Most resistance fades when people see they're doing less manual work, not more. Your team still uses their phones and email. They're just freed up from repetitive tasks like logging leads and typing callbacks.
