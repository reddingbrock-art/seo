Will this get me fake reviews?

No. Requests go only to customers whose jobs you actually completed. Google's system detects fake reviews and penalizes accounts. We're building your reputation the way that lasts.

What if a customer is unhappy?

Review requests go to all customers. If someone's unsatisfied, they can leave a review—and you get a chance to respond and make it right. That transparency builds trust faster than hiding from feedback.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google reviews are our focus because that's where customers search for you. We can also send requests to other platforms, but Google is the priority for local appliance repair businesses.

How does the timing work?

Requests go out within minutes of a job being marked complete. The faster you ask, the higher the response rate. That's the whole point.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll map it out on your discovery call and build the automation around what's already working. If nothing's working, we'll design one from scratch.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?
