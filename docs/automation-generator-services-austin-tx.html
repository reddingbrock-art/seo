FAQ

Automation for Generator Services: Common Questions from Austin Owners

What exactly gets automated?

Incoming calls and form submissions get answered and qualified automatically. The system asks for job location, equipment type, and preferred appointment times, then books appointments directly into your calendar and notifies your team.

Do I need technical skills to run this?

No. You don't manage or maintain the system—we do that. You log in, see booked jobs, and dispatch them like normal. It feels like you hired a really organized dispatcher who never sleeps.

What if my team resists new tools?

Your team isn't using a new tool—they're just receiving cleaner data and fewer interruptions. Appointments land in your dispatch board already qualified and scheduled. Most teams embrace it because it reduces noise, not increases it.

How is this different from buying software myself?

You'd buy software, train your team, integrate it yourself, and maintain it when things break. We handle all of that. You get a turnkey system deployed and monitored by us, not a platform you have to figure out.

What does the setup process look like?

One discovery call with us, a few days of configuration on our end, a brief training session with your team, and then it goes live. The whole process takes about two weeks from your first call to first automated booking.

What happens if something breaks after launch?

We monitor the system continuously and fix issues before you notice them. If a lead doesn't get captured or an appointment doesn't sync, we catch it and correct it. You have direct support during and after launch.
