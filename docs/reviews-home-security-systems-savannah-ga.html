FAQ

Review Automation Questions for Savannah Home Security Companies

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. The system asks for reviews from customers whose jobs you've actually completed. Real jobs, real customers, real feedback.

What if a customer is unhappy?

The request goes out the same way, and they're free to leave honest feedback. Most unhappy customers won't leave a review without being asked, so you find out what happened and can respond. It's better than silence.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google, Yelp, and other major review platforms where your customers are searching for you. The system handles the routing.

How does the timing work?

The request goes out within a day or two after your tech completes the job—when the experience is fresh and the customer is most likely to respond.

What if I already have a review process?

This replaces it with something you don't have to manage. If you're already asking manually and getting reviews, automation will remove the labor and likely get you more.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Most field service businesses see significantly more reviews once requests are automated and timed right. The exact number depends on your install volume and customer base, which we'll discuss on your discovery call.
