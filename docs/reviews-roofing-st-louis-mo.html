FAQ

FAQs About Review Automation for Roofing

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests to actual customers after their jobs are complete. The reviews that come back are genuine—they're either satisfied or they're not. We're just making sure the satisfied ones actually say so.

What if a customer is unhappy?

They don't have to leave a review. The request is an invitation, not a requirement. Unhappy customers typically won't respond. If they do complain, that's actually useful feedback before it becomes a public problem.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google, primarily—that's where roofing customers are searching and checking ratings. We build it to fit your existing systems and how you already mark jobs complete.

How does the timing work?

Requests send automatically the same day a job is marked finished in your system. You define what "complete" means—final walkthrough, invoice sent, whatever milestone makes sense for your workflow.

What if I already have a review process?

We integrate with what you've got. If you're manually asking sometimes, we automate that so nobody falls through. If you're not asking at all, we build the process from scratch.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

It depends on your job volume and your customer satisfaction. Most roofing companies see significantly more reviews once they ask systematically. Some customers will ignore the request. Most won't. That difference compounds quickly.
