FAQ

Common Questions About Review Automation for Pressure Washing

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests only to actual customers you've already serviced. Google's system verifies the customer and the service address. Everything is legitimate.

What if a customer is unhappy?

They're not obligated to leave a review. The request is just a prompt. If they don't want to review, nothing happens. You're not filtering or suppressing feedback—you're simply asking satisfied customers to share their experience.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google reviews, primarily. That's where Fort Lauderdale customers look first. We can also integrate with other platforms depending on your setup.

How does the timing work?

The request goes out the same day the job is marked complete in your system. Usually within a few hours. That's when the experience is fresh and they're most likely to respond.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll audit what you're doing and integrate with it. If you're already asking manually, we automate it so you stop relying on memory. If you have nothing, we build it from scratch.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

It depends on your job volume and response rate. Most teams see significantly more reviews within 60 days because the ask is consistent and timely. We'll show you what typical looks like for your crew size during your discovery call.
