FAQ

Common Questions About Automation for Generator Services

What exactly gets automated?

Inbound calls get answered with a customized greeting, routed to your dispatcher or to a scheduling system where customers confirm their own appointment. Text confirmations and follow-ups happen automatically. Your dispatcher logs everything and routes confirmed jobs to the right tech.

Do I need technical skills to run this?

No. You won't touch code or interfaces. Your team logs in, sees their schedule and customer details, and focuses on running the business. We handle everything else.

What if my team resists new tools?

We set it up so they use what they're already familiar with—their phone, their calendar, text. There's no new app to learn. It just works in the background. Most teams prefer it within a week because it saves them work.

How is this different from buying software myself?

You'd be installing and configuring tools, training your team, troubleshooting when something breaks, and integrating pieces yourself. We do all of that. You get a done-for-you system that's built specifically for how generator services operate, not a generic platform.

What does the setup process look like?

We schedule a 30-minute discovery call, ask about your current process, then build and test the system with you over 5–7 days. Once you approve, we go live. Training and support happen on launch day.

What happens if something breaks after launch?

You've got direct support from our team. If a call isn't routing right or a text isn't sending, we fix it immediately. You're not on your own troubleshooting—we monitor and maintain the system for you.
