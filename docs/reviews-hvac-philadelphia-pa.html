FAQ

HVAC Review Automation Questions—Answered

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests to actual customers after they've paid and the job is finished. The reviews that come back are real—because they're from people who actually used your service.

What if a customer is unhappy?

They can still leave feedback. Our goal is to capture honest reviews at the moment when satisfaction is highest—right after a successful job. If someone had a bad experience, that's on the service itself, not the timing.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google is the priority because it's where customers search and where visibility matters most. We focus there first and can expand to other platforms based on your business.

How does the timing work?

The request goes out automatically the moment a job is marked complete in your system. Customers get it when they're still thinking about the experience, not weeks later when they've forgotten.

What if I already have a review process?

We can build on what's working or replace it entirely. If you're doing it manually and it's inconsistent, automation fixes that. If it's working, we'll talk through how to improve it.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

It depends on your job volume and current response rate. Most businesses see a significant increase once requests are consistent and timed right. More jobs completed means more requests sent. We'll show you what's possible during your call.
