FAQ

Lead Follow-Up System FAQs for Riverside Electrical Contractors

How many follow-ups does it send?

We typically run 3–5 touchpoints over 7–10 days per lead—calls, texts, and emails depending on what the lead responds to. You can adjust the sequence based on your preferences.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. We write initial messaging based on your business, but you own all the language. If you want to change how we introduce your company or what questions we ask, you tell us.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can unsubscribe, and we remove them immediately. No pushback, no reminder sequences. One request, they're out.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Usually within 15 minutes of the lead coming in. If they call, you can get a response in your first minute. If they submit a form, they're contacted before end of business the same day.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in the system on a longer cycle so they come back into rotation if they search for you again or you want to re-engage them later. You're not throwing away future business.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. If you've got a CRM or dispatch system, we integrate with it. If you don't, we set everything up so your team sees new leads in one place and books from there.
