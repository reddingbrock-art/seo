FAQ

Review Automation for Foundation Repair — Common Questions

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. The system sends requests only to customers who actually completed jobs with you. We're not generating fake reviews or asking for them—just making sure the people you've already worked for see the request when they're most likely to respond.

What if a customer is unhappy?

The system requests reviews from everyone, satisfied or not. That's actually good—authentic profiles with a mix of feedback rank better than pristine ones. And unhappy customers who leave honest reviews give you the chance to respond publicly and show how you handle problems.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google is the priority—that's where estimates actually happen. We can configure requests for other platforms, but Google Reviews is the ranking factor that moves the needle for foundation repair in Tucson.

How does the timing work?

The request goes out the day your job closes, based on your workflow. If you mark an estimate as complete in your system, the customer gets the request that same afternoon or the next morning. You control when it fires.

What if I already have a review process?

We integrate with what you're doing. If you're manually requesting, the system automates that step so nothing falls through. If you're not asking at all, this fills that gap. Either way, you're not creating extra work.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Most foundation repair companies see significantly more reviews once requests go out automatically—you're capturing customers who'd otherwise never think to leave one. The exact number depends on your volume and how many jobs you close per month.
