FAQ

Questions About AI Chat for Septic & Drain Businesses

Will the chat widget actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on how you talk to customers, the problems you actually solve, and your service areas. It sounds like your business because it is your business talking to prospects.

What happens when the chat can't answer something?

It tells the visitor you'll follow up with them directly and captures their info. You get the conversation and contact details so you can pick up where the chat left off.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It replaces the part of your receptionist's job that's answering "do you serve my area?" and "when can you come out?" The human conversations still happen—they just happen with qualified leads who've already given you the baseline information.

What hours does the chat widget work?

All hours. It runs 24/7 on your website. You decide how to handle off-hours conversations—the chat can collect information and let prospects know when you'll respond, or you can set it to book directly into available time slots.

How long does setup take?

Discovery and building takes a week. Deployment is instant. You're live and capturing leads in 10-14 days from your first call with us.

What if a customer wants to call instead of chat?

The chat shows your phone number. They can call whenever they want. But most won't—they'll chat, get their answer, and book a time. That's the point.
