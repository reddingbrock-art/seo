FAQ

Google Review Automation: Common Questions from Houston Garage Door Shops

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests only to actual customers who completed jobs in your system. You can't control whether they leave a review or what they say—that's up to them. We're just making sure they know you're asking.

What if a customer is unhappy?

Unhappy customers are your feedback, not a problem to hide. If someone had a bad experience, that's data you need. Our system doesn't filter or suppress anyone—it just automates the ask. You handle the service; Google reflects the truth.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google, Yelp, Facebook, and your own website. Google is the priority for local search, but we route requests strategically across platforms depending on customer demographics and service type.

How does the timing work?

Requests go out 1–3 days after job completion, when the customer has paid and experienced your service but hasn't forgotten about it yet. We manage the cadence so you're not spamming the same customer twice.
