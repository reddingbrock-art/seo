FAQ

AI Chat Widget for Irrigation Companies—Common Questions

Will the chat widget actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on your language, your service areas, your pricing model, and the way you explain systems to customers. It sounds like someone from your company, not a bot.

What happens when the chat can't answer something?

It tells the visitor that and offers to book a callback or send a message to your team. Nothing gets lost—every unanswered question gets flagged for you to follow up on.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It handles after-hours and weekend visitors, and it qualifies leads faster than a phone call would. If you have a receptionist, they now focus on other work instead of fielding basic questions about service areas and scheduling.

What hours does the chat widget work?

24/7. Weekends, holidays, 3 AM—it's always on your site, always available. You can set it to hand off to a live person during your business hours if you want.

How long does setup take?

10-14 days from discovery call to go-live. Most of that is us building and training. Your part is one conversation and a quick review before we flip it on.

What if a customer wants to call instead of chat?

The chat offers your phone number as an option. Some customers will call. Most of the ones who won't call will chat—and that's where you're winning appointments you'd otherwise lose.
