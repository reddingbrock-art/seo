FAQ

Lead Follow-Up System FAQs for Generator Services

How many follow-ups does it send?

We build the sequence based on what works for generator services in Salt Lake City. Most sequences include 3-5 touchpoints over 7-10 days, but we customize this during your discovery call.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. We write the initial messaging and give you full control to adjust tone, timing, and content. It should sound like your business, not like automation.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can opt out at any time, and the system respects that immediately. We handle all compliance so you don't.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

It goes out within minutes of the lead coming in. No waiting for someone to manually process it.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in the sequence until they opt out or book. You'll also get a report showing which leads engaged and which didn't, so you can follow up manually if you want.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. We integrate with your current intake, scheduling, and dispatch tools. It adds capacity without replacing your team.
