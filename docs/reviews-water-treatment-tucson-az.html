FAQ

Google Review Automation for Water Treatment: Common Questions

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests only to customers you've actually served—people on your completed job list. Real work, real customers, real reviews.

What if a customer is unhappy?

They don't have to leave a review. The request is an invitation, not a demand. And if someone's unhappy, you'd rather hear about it privately than read it on Google—so reach out directly before the request goes out if you know there's an issue.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google primarily. That's where water treatment customers search and make decisions. Other review sites can be added depending on your setup.

How does the timing work?

The request goes out automatically when a job is marked complete in your system—usually within a few hours. The customer gets it when the experience is fresh.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll build around what you're doing now. If you're already asking for reviews manually and want to stop, we replace that. If you want to keep your current process and add automation on top, we can do that too.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Most water treatment businesses see significantly more reviews once the timing shifts to right after job completion. Exact numbers depend on your job volume and customer base, but the pattern is consistent: ask when they remember, and more say yes.
