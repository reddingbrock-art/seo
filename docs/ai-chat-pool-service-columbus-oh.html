FAQ

AI Chat for Pool Service: Questions Answered

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on your voice, the way you talk to customers, and your specific service offerings. Callers won't hear a generic chatbot—they'll hear someone who knows pool service.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It either collects the customer's info and the question routes to you, or it recognizes it's beyond scope and transfers the call. No guessing. No bad information.

Does this replace my receptionist?

It replaces the voicemail and the callback lag. If you have a full-time receptionist, they move to higher-level work. If you don't, you stop losing calls to the phone system.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. Calls and texts come in at midnight, 6 a.m., or Saturday evening—and they still get answered. You can set business hours for callbacks if you prefer, but inbound qualification happens around the clock.

How long does setup take?

10-14 days from discovery to live. Most of that is our team building and testing. Your actual time involvement is minimal.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

Honesty is part of the system. If they ask, the AI tells them. What matters is that their question got answered instantly and their appointment got scheduled—no waiting, no runaround.
