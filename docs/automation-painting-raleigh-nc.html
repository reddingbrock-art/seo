FAQ

Painting Automation FAQs for Raleigh Business Owners

What exactly gets automated?

Phone calls get answered and routed, estimate requests from your website get scheduled automatically, job confirmations and reminders send to customers, and review requests go out after completion. Basically everything that doesn't require you to swing a brush.

Do I need technical skills to run this?

No. You manage it the same way you manage your calendar—log in, check your scheduled appointments and customer conversations. If you can use a phone, you can run this.

What if my team resists new tools?

This reduces their workload, not adds to it. Your office staff stops answering the same questions over and over. Your crews get clearer job information upfront. The resistance usually disappears when they see what stops getting handed to them.

How is this different from buying software myself?

You'd buy a platform, figure out which features to use, integrate your phone and email, write the messages, test it, troubleshoot it. We do all that. You get a working system ready to go, not a tool you have to become an expert in.

What does the setup process look like?

One call to understand your workflow. We handle integration and customization. You review the automated messages and approve. System is live in 10-14 days. Minimal time on your end.

What happens if something breaks after launch?

We support it. If a call isn't routing right, a message isn't sending, or something changes with your business, you contact us and we fix it. This is our responsibility, not yours.
