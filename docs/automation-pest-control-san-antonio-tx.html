FAQ

Automation for Pest Control: Common Questions from San Antonio Operators

What exactly gets automated?

Lead capture and qualification from all inbound channels—calls, text messages, website forms, Google inquiries. The system asks what pest problem they have, where they're located, and what time works for an appointment. It books qualified jobs directly into your calendar and sends the homeowner a confirmation with technician details.

Do I need technical skills to run this?

No. One person on your team manages the dashboard—that's it. You can see all incoming leads, upcoming appointments, and job status without touching code or networks. We handle the backend.

What if my team resists new tools?

Resistance usually drops fast once they see fewer phone calls interrupting their day. Dispatchers especially appreciate not being the bottleneck. We train them once during setup and stay available if questions pop up.

How is this different from buying software myself?

We don't sell you software. We build a system that integrates with your existing tools—your calendar, your dispatch software, your phone system. We configure it, maintain it, and adjust it. You own the operation; we own the infrastructure.

What does the setup process look like?

Discovery call, system configuration (typically 3-5 days), live testing with you, training call, and launch. Ten to fourteen days from start to live. You're involved at every checkpoint.

What happens if something breaks after launch?

You have direct support. Any issues—integrations, missed connections, system glitches—you reach out and we fix them. That's part of our model. You're not troubleshooting alone.
