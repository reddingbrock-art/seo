FAQ

FAQs About AI Chat for Foundation Repair in Baltimore

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We feed it your language, your service areas, your process, and your tone. It doesn't sound like a robot—it sounds like your office answering questions the way you would.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It collects the information, lets the customer know you'll follow up, and flags it for you. You see it in the dashboard and call them back with a real answer.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It replaces the times when nobody's there to pick up—nights, weekends, during jobs. If you have a receptionist, the AI handles overflow and after-hours. If you don't, you stop losing leads because nobody answered.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. It works while you sleep, while you're on a job site, while you're driving to the next appointment.

How long does setup take?

10-14 days from discovery call to live. Most of that is us configuring and training. Your part takes a couple of conversations.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

Transparency is built in. The agent can tell them upfront it's an AI, or it can handle things smoothly and they never ask. Either way, the lead gets booked and qualified. That's what matters.
