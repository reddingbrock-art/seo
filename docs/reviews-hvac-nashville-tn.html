FAQ

Common Questions About HVAC Review Automation in Nashville

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests only to real customers with completed jobs on your account. They choose to write a review or not — we just make sure they get asked.

What if a customer is unhappy?

You'll see unhappy feedback just like happy feedback. The system doesn't filter or hide anything. It just makes sure requests go out consistently so you get a true picture of how customers actually feel.

Which platforms does this work on?

We handle Google reviews. That's where most HVAC customers search and leave feedback.

How does the timing work?

The request goes out automatically when a job's marked complete. Customer gets a text or email with a link to your review page. No delay, no manual step.

What if I already have a review process?

Most shops do it manually and inconsistently. We replace that with automation so nothing falls through the cracks. If you're already getting solid reviews every month, we'll match or beat that pace.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

It depends on your current close rate and how many jobs you complete each month. Most shops see a significant increase because requests are going out on time and consistently, instead of whenever someone remembers.
