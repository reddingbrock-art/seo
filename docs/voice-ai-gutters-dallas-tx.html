Common Questions About Voice Automation for Gutter Businesses

Do I have to learn new software?

No. The system answers calls and populates your calendar. You manage jobs the same way you do now. If you use a calendar or basic CRM, you're already set.

How fast will I see results?

You'll see calls being answered and jobs booked within the first few days of going live. Full impact—better scheduling, fewer missed leads—shows up within the first month of peak season.

What does setup look like?

We handle everything. You give us access to your phone line and calendar, we configure the system, test it with you, and flip it on. Total time investment from you: under an hour spread across a few days.

What happens after we go live?

We monitor the system, listen to calls, and make adjustments if needed. If something isn't working or a script needs tweaking, we fix it. You're not on your own.

Yes, but it's designed around your business, not to lock you in. We'll discuss terms on your discovery call.

What if something breaks?

Calls still ring your phone as a backup. The system doesn't replace your line; it adds to it. If anything goes wrong, we're alerted and we fix it before you even notice.
