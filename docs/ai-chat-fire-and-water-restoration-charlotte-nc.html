FAQ

Chat Widget Questions for Fire & Water Restoration Owners

Will the chat widget actually sound like my business?

Yes. We build it using your tone, your service areas, your specific job types, and the language you use with customers. It sounds like someone who knows restoration, not a robot reading a script.

What happens when the chat can't answer something?

It hands the conversation to your team with full context. The prospect knows exactly what they've already told us, so your team picks up without the customer repeating themselves.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It replaces the dead time when no one's there to answer—nights, weekends, during jobs. Your receptionist stays focused on dispatch, clients, and work that needs a human. The chat handles intake.

What hours does the chat widget work?

24/7. It's always there answering, qualifying, and booking. After-hours visits become booked appointments instead of lost leads.

How long does setup take?

About 10 to 14 days from your first call to live on your site. We handle all the integration with your calendar and booking system.

What if a customer wants to call instead of chat?

They can. The widget lets them request a callback or grab your phone number. But most won't need to—the chat qualifies them and books the job right there.
