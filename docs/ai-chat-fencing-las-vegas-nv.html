FAQ

AI Chat for Fencing: Questions & Answers

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on your actual estimates, past conversations, and how you talk to customers. It'll reference your service areas, handle fence-specific questions, and match your tone—whether you're formal or conversational.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It tells the customer it'll pass the question to you and sends you the details immediately. No transfer or hold time—the customer knows you're getting it and when to expect a callback.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It replaces the need to hire one. If you already have someone answering phones, it frees them up for follow-up, scheduling, and admin work that actually makes you money instead of just sitting on hold.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. You pick which hours to auto-answer and which hours to route to voicemail or your cell. Most fencing crews set it to answer live during business hours and collect leads overnight.

How long does setup take?

We go live in 10-14 days from your discovery call. Most of that time is us training the agent and getting your approval. Your actual downtime is minimal.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

Some will figure it out. The AI tells them upfront it's an automated system and confirms they're cool with it. Most customers are fine with it—they just want their question answered. And honestly, getting an instant answer is better than leaving a voicemail and hoping.
