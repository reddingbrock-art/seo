FAQ

Common Questions About Lead Follow-Up Automation for Plumbers

How many follow-ups does it send?

Typically 3–5 touches over 7–10 days, spread across calls, texts, and email. We customize the sequence based on your preferences and what your leads respond to.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. We write the messages for you to match your voice and offer. You review them before anything goes out, and you can adjust anytime.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can unsubscribe, and they're removed immediately. The system respects opt-outs automatically.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Within 5 minutes of the lead coming in. You're contacting them while they're actively thinking about the problem.

What happens to leads that never respond?

The sequence completes and they stay in your system. You can see who didn't convert so you can follow up manually later if you want, or we can set up re-engagement campaigns.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. It integrates with your current lead sources and CRM. Your team keeps doing what they're doing—the system just handles the initial follow-up automatically.
