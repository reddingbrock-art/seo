FAQ

Common Questions About AI Chat Agents for Commercial Cleaning in Tucson

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We record a brief voice sample or use a voice that matches your brand. We train it on your service menu, pricing, and how your team actually talks to customers. It's built for your operation, not generic.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It says so and gets the caller's details. We send you their info, and your team follows up. No fake answers, no pretending to know something it doesn't.

Does this replace my receptionist?

It replaces voicemail and the back-and-forth of tag-you're-it. If you have a receptionist, they focus on callbacks and closing instead of answering the 47th 'do you service my area' call.

What hours does the AI work?

All of them. Nights, weekends, holidays. Your phone works when you're not in the office.

How long does setup take?

Discovery to live is 10–14 days. Most of that is us building and you reviewing. The flip to production is same-day once you sign off.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

Most don't, because the conversation is natural and quick. But if they ask, the AI tells them the truth and offers to transfer to a human or take a message. Honesty builds trust faster than pretending.
