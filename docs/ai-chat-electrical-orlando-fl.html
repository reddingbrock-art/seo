FAQ

AI Chat Widget FAQs for Electrical Contractors in Orlando

Will the chat widget actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on your actual language, your service descriptions, your policies, and your tone. It sounds like someone from your team, not a robot.

What happens when the chat can't answer something?

It tells the customer you'll get back to them and hands the conversation off to your team with full context. Nothing falls through the cracks.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It handles the questions and bookings that happen outside your office hours and when your team's busy. Your receptionist still owns phone relationships and can focus on more complex conversations.

What hours does the chat widget work?

24/7. It's there at midnight, 6 a.m., on weekends—whenever a potential customer lands on your site. You decide when it books appointments (you can set blackout hours for emergencies or days off).

How long does setup take?

10-14 days from discovery call to go-live. We handle all the configuration and training. You just need 30 minutes upfront to tell us how your business works.

What if a customer wants to call instead of chat?

The widget has a button to call your number. If they prefer phone, they can get there in one tap. But most will chat if it's faster and easier than waiting on hold.
