FAQ

Review Automation Questions for Solar Installation Owners

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests only to customers you've actually worked for, using their real contact information from your job records. The reviews that come back are genuine customer feedback.

What if a customer is unhappy?

The system requests reviews from real customers. If they're unhappy, that's a service issue, not an automation issue—and you'd want to know that before they tell everyone else. Automation doesn't suppress negative feedback; it just makes sure you ask everyone equally.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google primarily, since that's where solar customers search. We can configure requests to other platforms based on your workflow.

How does the timing work?

Requests go out automatically when a job is marked complete in your system—usually within hours. You control the timing window (same day, next day, etc.).

What if I already have a review process?

We'll integrate with what you've got. If you're getting reviews manually, the automation just removes that manual step and does it consistently for every job.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

It depends on your job volume and how many customers actually see and respond to requests. Most owners see significantly more reviews within 60 days because they're asking everyone, not just the ones their crew remembers.
