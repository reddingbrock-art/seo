Common Questions About Appliance Repair Automation in Milwaukee

What exactly gets automated?

Inbound call answering and routing, text message responses to inquiries, appointment scheduling and confirmation, job reminders to customers, and review request delivery after completion. Everything that happens before and after the job—not the repair itself.

Do I need technical skills to run this?

No. We set up the system and your team learns how to monitor it and make adjustments. It's designed for field service owners, not IT people. If you can use your phone and email, you can manage it.

What if my team resists new tools?

The system actually removes work from their day—no more manual callbacks, no data entry, no tracking down appointment details. Once they see it reduce their workload, resistance usually disappears fast.

How is this different from buying software myself?

You'd buy software, set it up yourself, troubleshoot issues, integrate it with your dispatch system, and maintain it. We do all of that. You just use it. That difference means you stay focused on running the business instead of learning a new platform.

What does the setup process look like?

First call is discovery—we learn your workflow. Then we build and integrate with your existing systems. You review and test. Then we go live and train your team. Whole process takes 10-14 days from start to finish.

What happens if something breaks after launch?

We monitor your system and you have direct access to our team for support. If something's wrong, we fix it. You're not on your own figuring out what went sideways.
