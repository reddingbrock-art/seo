FAQ

Questions About AI Chat for Pressure Washing Companies

Will the chat widget actually sound like my business?

Yes. We write it in your voice, with your service details and your tone. It doesn't sound robotic or generic—it sounds like someone from your crew answering questions.

What happens when the chat can't answer something?

It tells the visitor it doesn't have an answer and asks for their info so you can follow up. Nothing falls through the cracks.

Does this replace my receptionist?

It replaces the part of your receptionist's job that's answering "do you serve my zip code?" and "how much does pressure washing cost?" You still have to close estimates and build relationships—that's where the real work is.

What hours does the chat widget work?

24/7. Customers can chat at midnight or 6 AM. You respond to booked appointments during your normal business hours.

How long does setup take?

Discovery and training take about a week. Live and booking within 10-14 days.

What if a customer wants to call instead of chat?

The chat offers your phone number as an option. Some people prefer to call, and that's fine. The widget just makes sure you're not losing people who prefer to text or chat.
