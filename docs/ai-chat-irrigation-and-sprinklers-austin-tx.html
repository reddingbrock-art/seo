FAQ

AI Chat for Irrigation & Sprinklers: Your Questions Answered

Will the chat widget actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on your language, service areas, and how you actually talk to customers. It sounds like someone who knows irrigation, not a robot reading a script.

What happens when the chat can't answer something?

It tells the visitor you'll get back to them and routes the conversation to your team. Nothing falls through the cracks. You see every chat, answered or not.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It does the work your receptionist shouldn't have to do—answering the same questions at midnight and qualifying tire-kickers. Your team focuses on jobs that matter.

What hours does the chat widget work?

24/7. Your website never sleeps. Visitors get answers at 2 a.m. if they're shopping. You respond to qualified leads during business hours.

How long does setup take?

10-14 days from discovery call to going live. We handle everything. You just point us to your website.

What if a customer wants to call instead of chat?

They can. The widget gives them your number. We're not forcing chat on anyone. We're just giving visitors who prefer typing a real option.
