FAQ

Review Automation for Flooring: Common Questions

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send review requests only to customers whose jobs are actually in your system. We're asking for honest feedback from people you've already served.

What if a customer is unhappy?

They can leave an honest negative review. That's actually fine—it's real. Most flooring shops that ask all customers see a natural distribution of 5s and 4s, not 100% perfection. That authenticity builds trust.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google is the primary channel. We focus there because that's where your customers and potential customers are already looking. Other platforms can be added depending on your market.

How does the timing work?

Requests go out 24 hours after job completion. Early enough for the experience to be fresh, late enough for the customer to actually see the finished floor. Timing matters for response rates.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll integrate with what you have or replace it entirely if it's not working. Either way, the goal is consistency and zero manual follow-up on your end.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Depends on your current ask rate and your customer response rate. Most flooring shops see significantly more reviews once requests are going out every single week instead of sporadically. The difference is usually visible within 60 days.
