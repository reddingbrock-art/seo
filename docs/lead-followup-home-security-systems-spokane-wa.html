FAQ

Lead Follow-Up System Questions for Spokane Home Security Companies

How many follow-ups does it send?

We set up a sequence—typically 3–5 touches over 5–7 days via text and email. You control how many and how far apart. If a lead responds, the sequence stops.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. We write the initial templates based on your selling process, and you can edit them anytime. Your voice, your offer, your timing.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can unsubscribe or reply STOP, and they're removed immediately. Compliance is built in.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Within 2–5 minutes of lead submission. The system doesn't wait for office hours or a person to manually trigger it.

What happens to leads that never respond?

The sequence runs its course and they sit in your system as a non-respondent. You can always reach back out manually later or wait for them to re-engage.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. It plugs into your current lead sources and sits on top of what you're already doing. Your team still handles the conversations once a lead responds.
