FAQ

Foundation Repair Lead Follow-Up Questions

How many follow-ups does it send?

That depends on your process. Most foundation repair companies use 3-5 touchpoints over 7-10 days. We customize the sequence to match how your team actually closes deals.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. We write the initial sequences, but you can change the exact message, tone, and timing. It should sound like your business, not a template.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can opt out with one click. The system respects that immediately and logs it. You stay compliant without any manual work.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Within 2-5 minutes of the lead submitting a form or texting in. If you want it instant, we can do that too.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in the sequence for the full cycle you set. After the last follow-up, they're marked as unresponsive and can be exported or re-engaged later with a different message.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. It integrates with your current CRM, texting platform, or email system. It doesn't replace what you're doing—it just stops the gaps where leads slip through.
