FAQ

Chat Automation for General Contractors—Questions Answered

Will the chat widget actually sound like my business?

Yes. We write the conversation flows based on how you actually talk to leads—your process, your objections, your closing language. It doesn't sound like a bot. It sounds like someone from your office.

What happens when the chat can't answer something?

It hands off to you with all the context it's already gathered. The lead's name, scope, address, and timeline are already captured. You're picking up a warm conversation, not starting from zero.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It handles visitors your receptionist never would've gotten a chance to talk to—the ones chatting at night, on weekends, or from their phone while you're busy. It frees up your team to focus on closing and managing jobs.

What hours does the chat widget work?

24/7. It qualifies and books while you sleep. During your office hours, you can hop in and chat with leads directly if you want. Off-hours, it's handling everything.

How long does setup take?

10-14 days from kickoff to live. We need one conversation with you about your process, then we build and test. No long implementation. No waiting months.

What if a customer wants to call instead of chat?

The chat can give them your number. That's their choice. But most of the time, once they're chatting, they finish the conversation in chat. It's faster and they have a record of what was discussed.
