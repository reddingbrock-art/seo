AI Chat Widget FAQs for EV Charger Installation in Tucson

Will the chat widget actually sound like my business?

Yes. We write every response based on your tone, pricing structure, service area, and the way you actually talk to customers. It's trained on your business, not a generic template.

What happens when the chat can't answer something?

It recognizes when it's out of bounds and hands the conversation to you or offers to schedule a call with you directly. No frustration, no dead ends.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It replaces the moment before your receptionist gets involved—it captures and qualifies visitors who haven't picked up the phone yet. Your team still closes deals and manages relationships.

What hours does the chat widget work?

It runs 24/7 on your website. It books appointments, answers questions, and re-engages prospects while you're sleeping or on the job site.

How long does setup take?

10–14 days from your discovery call to going live. That includes writing, training, testing, and your approval before anything touches your website.

What if a customer wants to call instead of chat?

The chat can offer a callback or give them your direct number. Some prospects prefer chat. Some want to talk. Your widget handles both without making the prospect choose wrong.
