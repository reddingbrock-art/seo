FAQ

Review Automation Questions for Home Security Systems Owners

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests only to customers who actually completed a job with you. They choose whether to leave a review. Authentic reviews only.

What if a customer is unhappy?

They can leave whatever review they want. We're not filtering or suppressing anything. You get the real feedback—both good and bad—which is way more valuable than silence.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google, primarily. That's where your customers search for security companies in Buffalo. We can discuss other platforms during your call.

How does the timing work?

The request goes out within hours of job completion. That's when the customer's most likely to have time and motivation to leave feedback. Waiting days or weeks kills the response rate.

What if I already have a review process?

We can build around it or replace it. Most existing processes are manual or inconsistent. Ours is automatic. We'll figure out what makes sense for your workflow.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Depends on your job volume. Most owners see a significant increase within the first 60 days because they're actually asking. If you're closing 8-10 jobs a week, expect at least 6-8 new reviews monthly just by closing that loop.
