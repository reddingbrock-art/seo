FAQ

Common Questions About AI Chat for Houston Flooring Businesses

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We interview you and your team about how you talk to customers—your tone, your terminology, your process. The AI is trained on your actual intake questions and closes like your best salesperson, not a robot.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It asks clarifying questions to understand the situation better, then either gathers the information for you to follow up on, or connects the customer to your team if it's urgent. Nothing gets dropped.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It replaces the part of your receptionist's job that wastes time—holding calls, taking messages, repeating the same questions. Your team stays focused on closing and delivery. If you have a receptionist, they handle follow-ups and admin faster because inbound is already qualified.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. Calls at 11 p.m. Sunday still get answered and qualified. Your team reviews them during business hours and calls back the hot leads.

How long does setup take?

Live in 10-14 days from your discovery call. We handle all the technical integration with your site, phone, and calendar. You don't need IT experience or technical setup on your end.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

They're not upset about it. They got their question answered immediately without waiting. If they prefer to talk to a human, the AI can transfer them to your voicemail or connect them to your team directly. Most customers don't care—they just care that someone answered.
