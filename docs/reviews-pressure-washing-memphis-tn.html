FAQ

Review Automation Questions for Pressure Washing Owners

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests only to customers who actually booked and paid you. You're asking real people for honest feedback on work they've already seen.

What if a customer is unhappy?

The system sends the request, and they can choose to respond or not. You see what they wrote. If there's a problem, you handle it directly—that's still your job.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google, Facebook, and any review platform that matters to your market. We handle the technical routing so you don't have to think about it.

How does the timing work?

You set it. Most owners send the request a few hours after job completion—when the customer has had time to see the work but while they're still thinking about it.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll look at what you're doing now. If it's working, we can build on it. If it's not, we replace it with one that actually gets responses.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

That depends on your job volume and your current ask rate. Most owners see a significant increase in the first 30 days once the system's running. Some see 3x more reviews than they were getting before.
