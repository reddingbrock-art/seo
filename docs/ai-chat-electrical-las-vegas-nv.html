FAQ

Common Questions About AI Chat for Las Vegas Electrical Contractors

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We build it to match how you talk to customers—your pricing thresholds, your service area, your warranty language. It answers the same questions you do, in the same way.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It escalates to you or your team with all the context captured. The customer never feels abandoned—the agent tells them a real person will call back within a timeframe you set.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It replaces the parts of reception that kill you: answering after-hours calls, handling initial intake, booking appointments. Your team handles the real customer conversations and dispatch decisions.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. It runs around the clock, even on weekends and holidays. You choose whether certain queries escalate to your team during off-hours or if the AI handles everything independently.

How long does setup take?

10-14 days from discovery to live. We need your input on how you qualify jobs and what questions you ask, but the agent is built, tested, and deployed fast.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

Most don't ask. But if they do, the agent's honest about it and stays helpful. Customers care about fast answers and getting their job scheduled—not whether they're talking to a person or automation.
