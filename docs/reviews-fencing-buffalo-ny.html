FAQ

Common Questions About Review Automation for Fencing Companies

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests only to customers from your actual job list. Real people, real jobs. Whether they leave a review or not is up to them.

What if a customer is unhappy?

The system still sends a request—that's their opportunity to tell you what went wrong directly, not through a one-star review to strangers. You get the feedback, you can fix it, and often you can turn it into a positive outcome.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google primarily. That's where prospects are looking first when they search for a fencing company in Buffalo. We can discuss other platforms on your call.

How does the timing work?

The request goes out the day after you mark the job complete in your system. Early morning, so it's fresh. Customers get one touch—we don't spam them with multiple reminders.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll talk through what you're doing now and where it's slipping. Most owners are asking inconsistently or too late. We tighten the timing and make sure every job gets a request.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Depends on your job volume and current review rate. Most owners see 15–40% more reviews within the first 90 days just from better timing. Book a call and we'll give you a real number based on your numbers.
