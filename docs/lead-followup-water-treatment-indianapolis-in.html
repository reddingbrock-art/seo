FAQ

Lead Follow-Up Questions for Water Treatment Owners

How many follow-ups does it send?

We customize it to your process. Most water treatment companies use 2-3 touches over 24-48 hours, but you decide the sequence and timing.

Can I customize what it says?

Completely. We write the initial scripts based on how you talk to leads, then you approve everything before it goes live. It's your voice.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They get removed from the sequence immediately. We don't fight compliance or send people messages they don't want.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Usually within 2-5 minutes of the lead coming in, depending on the time of day. Fast enough that they remember they just submitted something.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in your CRM and get tagged so you can review them later or try a different approach. Nothing disappears.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Yes. This handles the automatic first contact and follow-ups. Your team still closes the deal and does all the actual work—we're just making sure nothing falls through the cracks.
