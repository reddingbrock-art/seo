FAQ

Review Automation Questions for EV Charger Installation Owners

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send review requests only to customers who've actually completed an installation with you. Google filters out anything that looks artificial. Real customers, real work, real reviews.

What if a customer is unhappy?

They receive the request like anyone else. How they respond is up to them. Our job is to make sure satisfied customers actually leave a review instead of just thinking about it. You get more honest feedback across the board.

Which platforms does this work on?

We focus on Google reviews, which is where 90% of your leads are looking. That's where the conversion power is for your business.

How does the timing work?

The request goes out automatically after you mark a job complete in your system. The timing is configurable—same day, next day, or a few days after, depending on what works best for your customers.

What if I already have a review process?

We replace the manual parts. If you're asking manually now, the system takes over that job. If you're doing nothing, the system creates the process for you. Either way, you get consistency and scale.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Most installation shops see a significant jump—typically 2-3x their previous volume once the system runs for a full month. The exact number depends on your job volume and customer base, which is why we talk through your numbers on a call.
