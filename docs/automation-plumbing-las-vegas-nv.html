Automation Questions for Las Vegas Plumbing Owners

What exactly gets automated?

Lead capture from any source, initial qualification, appointment scheduling, customer notifications, callback reminders, and review requests after job completion. Your team stays focused on diagnosis and repair.

Do I need technical skills to run this?

No. You'll get trained on how to use it and modify simple things like service area or pricing, but day-to-day operation doesn't require tech knowledge. We handle the backend.

What if my team resists new tools?

We've built this to fit how your team already works, not force them into a new workflow. Most crews see the benefit immediately because their job gets easier—fewer callbacks, clearer schedules, better notes. Adoption is usually natural.

How is this different from buying software myself?

You could buy five standalone tools and spend two months learning integrations that may or may not work. We design the entire system for plumbing, plug everything together, train your team, and manage it ongoing. You get a working business system, not a collection of apps.

What does the setup process look like?

One discovery call to understand your current ops, then 1-2 weeks of our team building and integrating. You'll see test runs before anything goes live. Launch happens once everything's proven to work in your environment.

What happens if something breaks after launch?

We don't disappear after go-live. You have ongoing support and we monitor the system. If something needs adjustment or an integration fails, you contact us and we fix it. This is your system to keep running.
