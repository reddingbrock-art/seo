FAQ

Lead Follow-Up Questions for Fort Lauderdale Restoration Contractors

How many follow-ups does it send?

It depends on your process. Most Fire & Water teams use 3–5 touchpoints over 7–10 days. We customize the sequence so it matches what actually closes deals for your business.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. Every message reflects your voice and your offer. You set the copy, the timing, and when to escalate to a phone call or hand-off to a crew member.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can opt out with one click, just like any compliant messaging system. Respect their request, flag them in your CRM, and move on without friction.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Immediately—within 2–5 minutes of form submission or phone inquiry capture. Your prospect sees you respond before they've even finished their call to the next contractor.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in your system on a longer cadence. Some convert weeks later when they're ready or after another storm hits. You don't abandon them—you just stop wasting time on active follow-up if they're unresponsive.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. It integrates with your estimating software, job tracking, and team communication. It handles the repetitive follow-up so your crew can focus on what they do best—closing jobs.
