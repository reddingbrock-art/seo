FAQ

AI Chat for Plumbing: Common Questions

Will the chat widget actually sound like my business?

Yes. We interview you on the discovery call, learn how you talk to customers, and build the responses around your voice and policies. It sounds like you because we write it to match how you work.

What happens when the chat can't answer something?

It collects the customer's info and question, sends it to you, and lets them know you'll follow up. Nothing falls through the cracks — every chat thread is logged.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. If you have a receptionist, this handles after-hours and high-volume periods so they focus on actual phone conversations. If you don't have one, this is your always-on front desk.

What hours does the chat widget work?

24/7. It's live whenever someone lands on your site — nights, weekends, holidays. You control the business hours message if you want to set expectations on when you'll respond to complex questions.

How long does setup take?

About 10-14 days from discovery call to live. Most of that is our configuration work. You're involved for the initial interview and one review. Then it's running.

What if a customer wants to call instead of chat?

The chat provides your phone number and encourages them to call if they prefer. Some people will. That's fine. The chat exists for the ones who won't call but will chat.
