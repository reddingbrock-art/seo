FAQ

AI Chat for Plumbing: Common Questions About 24/7 Lead Capture in Fort Lauderdale

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on your language, your service areas, your policies, and how your team talks to customers. It's built to represent you, not some generic bot.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It collects the customer's info and sends you their question in real time with full context. You stay in control—the AI just handles the stuff that shouldn't wait for business hours.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. If you have a receptionist, this handles overflow and after-hours. If you don't, it stops you from missing calls. Either way, humans still close jobs.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. Weekends, holidays, 2 AM—whenever your phone rings, it answers.

How long does setup take?

Go-live in 10-14 days from your discovery call. Most of that is configuration on our end. You don't need to learn software or train your team differently.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

It happens rarely, and when it does, there's no deception—the AI can acknowledge what it is while assuring them they're getting routed to a real person or that their info is recorded and you'll call back within a set time.
