FAQ

AI Chat for HVAC: Tampa Owner Questions Answered

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We record you or work from your actual call recordings so the voice, tone, and the way you ask questions stays yours. A customer calling you hears you—not some generic bot.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It listens, confirms the information, and tells the customer you'll follow up. Then it routes everything to you or your team with all the details captured. You always have the final word.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. If you have one, they handle follow-up, scheduling confirmation, and high-touch customer work. The AI is the always-on first responder. If you don't have a receptionist yet, you just bought yourself one—24/7.

What hours does the AI work?

All of them. It answers calls and texts at 2 AM on a Saturday just like it does at 9 AM on a Tuesday. You choose how quickly leads get routed to you and what the after-hours message says.

How long does setup take?

10-14 days from discovery call to live. We need a little info from you—your questions, your service area, how you like to qualify leads—and then we build and test it before flipping it on.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

They don't get upset or hang up. We train the system to be direct about what it is—'This is an automated system for [Your Business Name]'—and customers appreciate getting answered fast. It's honest and professional, and they're already talking to you instead of dialing your competitor.
