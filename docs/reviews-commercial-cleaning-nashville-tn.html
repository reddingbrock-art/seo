FAQ

Common Questions About Review Automation for Commercial Cleaning

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. The system sends requests to actual customers who've completed jobs with you. We're asking real people to share real experiences. Google catches fake reviews and penalizes businesses for them—we don't do that.

What if a customer is unhappy?

They won't be asked to leave a public review. We send requests after job completion, and unhappy customers typically reach out to you directly before that moment. If they do leave a negative review, that's actionable feedback—and responding professionally to it actually builds trust with prospects reading your profile.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google, primarily. That's where commercial facility managers search for cleaning companies and where Google Maps rankings matter most for local contract work. We can configure other platforms too—just discuss that on your call.

How does the timing work?

Your crew marks a job complete in your system. The request goes out within a few hours—when the customer's experience is fresh. They click a link, leave a review in seconds. No delay, no forgotten requests.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll work with it. If you're manually asking and getting inconsistent results, automation fills the gaps—every job gets a request, no missed contracts. If your current process is solid, we'll match it or improve timing.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

That depends on your job volume and customer base. A company running 3-5 trucks typically sees 15-30 new reviews in the first 90 days, then 8-12 per month ongoing. The system just makes asking happen every time—conversion depends on your service quality.
