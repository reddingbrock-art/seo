FAQ

Review Automation for Generator Services: Your Questions Answered

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests only to customers who actually completed a job with you. We never generate reviews or use fake profiles. Real service, real customers, real feedback.

What if a customer is unhappy?

We send the request—that's it. What the customer writes is entirely up to them. If someone leaves negative feedback, that's valuable information you can actually respond to and improve. That's how review systems are supposed to work.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google, Yelp, and industry-specific review sites where your customers are searching. We focus on platforms that matter for generator services and service businesses in your area.

How does the timing work?

The system automatically sends a review request the same day your job is marked complete. You set the time window—morning, evening, whenever makes sense for your customers—and the automation handles the rest.

What if I already have a review process?

We can build around what you're doing or replace it entirely. If you're getting manual reviews now, we'll shift that workflow to automation and free up your team's time. If you're doing nothing, we start from zero and build the process.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

It depends on your current volume and customer base. Most generator services companies see significantly more reviews once requests happen consistently after every job. The jump comes from automation making the ask consistent, not occasional.
