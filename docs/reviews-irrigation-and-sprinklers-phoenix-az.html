FAQ

Google Review Automation FAQs for Irrigation & Sprinkler Contractors

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests to actual customers who've paid you. Google can verify they're real. Real work = real reviews.

What if a customer is unhappy?

They'll leave an honest review—good or bad. But most unhappy customers won't volunteer that feedback. Automation just increases your honest review volume from satisfied customers.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google primarily. That's where 85% of your searches happen. We focus the energy where it counts most.

How does the timing work?

The request goes out after invoice payment, usually within a few hours. Customers see it when they've just paid and the work is fresh. Timing matters.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll look at it during discovery. Often we can integrate with what you have or replace manual steps with automation—same outcome, less work.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Depends on your job volume and current collection rate. Most contractors see 15–30 additional reviews monthly once it's running. Some see more. You'll know after 30 days.
