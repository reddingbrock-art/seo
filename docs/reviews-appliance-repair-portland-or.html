FAQ

Common Questions About Review Automation for Appliance Repair in Portland

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests to real customers after real jobs. What they leave is their honest experience. If they don't want to review, they don't have to.

What if a customer is unhappy?

They'll leave an honest review, and you'll see it. That's the point—to know where the problem is so you can fix it. We're not filtering or hiding anything.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google and Yelp. Those are where appliance repair customers actually look. That's where the volume matters for your local ranking.

How does the timing work?

Requests go out automatically after a job is marked complete in your system. No manual triggers needed. If the completion updates in real-time, so do the requests.

What if I already have a review process?

If you're asking manually and getting low response rates, this replaces that. If you're already getting reviews consistently, you'll get more. Either way, your team stops doing the work.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

It depends on your job volume. A shop doing 40-50 repairs a month typically sees 8-15 new reviews a month, where they were getting 1-2 before. The system asks everyone consistently.
