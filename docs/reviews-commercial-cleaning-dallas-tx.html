FAQ

Google Review Automation FAQs for Dallas Commercial Cleaning

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests only to customers you've actually serviced. They choose whether to leave a review. Google's system detects fake reviews and kills your credibility—we're not doing that.

What if a customer is unhappy?

They won't leave a review if they don't want to. A review request isn't a threat; it's an ask. If there's a real problem, you'll hear about it directly from the customer, not through Google. Address it then.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google primarily—that's where commercial cleaning customers search and decide. We integrate with however your team marks jobs complete right now.

How does the timing work?

The request goes out within 24 hours of job completion. That window is crucial—the customer still remembers the work. Too late and it feels random; too fast and they haven't had time to reflect.

What if I already have a review process?

Most owners have good intentions and weak execution. If you're manually sending requests and it's working, keep doing it. If you're not getting enough reviews, this replaces the guessing.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

It depends on your current volume and how many customers actually get requests. Most owners see their monthly review count double within 60 days. Some triple it.
