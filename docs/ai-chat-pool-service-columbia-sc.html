FAQ

AI Chat Agent FAQ for Pool Service Companies

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on how you talk to customers—your tone, your guarantees, the way you describe services. Callers hear your business, not a generic bot. It's indistinguishable from a real team member who's trained but not your lead tech.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It transfers the call to you or a team member instantly. You set the rules—some calls go straight through, others get logged with notes so you can follow up. The agent knows its limits and hands off seamlessly.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. If you have a receptionist, this frees them up for deeper work—follow-ups, upsells, customer service. If you don't, it fills that gap 24/7. Either way, your team has more time to sell and service.

What hours does the AI work?

All of them. It answers calls at midnight, 6 a.m., during lunch, on holidays—whenever your phone rings. You set office hours for callbacks if you want, or have the AI book appointments for the next available slot.

How long does setup take?

10–14 days from discovery call to live. We handle everything—integration, training, testing. You don't touch the backend. We activate it, monitor the first week, and you're done.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

Most don't. But if they ask, the AI tells the truth and offers to transfer them to a human. Transparency builds trust. And honestly—most callers care less about talking to AI and more about getting a time slot that works. The agent solves that problem.
