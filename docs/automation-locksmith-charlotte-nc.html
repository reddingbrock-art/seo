Automation for Locksmiths in Charlotte: Common Questions

What exactly gets automated?

Call routing, text message responses, chat on your website, calendar booking, job assignments to your team, and review requests after completion. Anything repetitive that's eating your time or costing you leads gets handled by the system.

Do I need technical skills to run this?

No. We set it up and your team uses it the same way you'd use a phone or dispatch app. If you can send a text or check your calendar, you can operate this.

What if my team resists new tools?

We train everyone before go-live and stay available if questions come up. Most teams see the benefit within the first week because jobs are clearer and callbacks stop piling up.

How is this different from buying software myself?

You'd spend weeks finding the right tools, connecting them, training your team, and troubleshooting. We do all of that for you. You just show up and your system works.

What does the setup process look like?

One 30-minute discovery call, then we spend a week integrating and configuring. You stay involved but don't have to learn the backend. We go live in 10 to 14 days.

What happens if something breaks after launch?

We're your support. If a call doesn't route correctly or a booking fails, you reach out and we fix it. You're not on your own figuring out why something stopped working.
