FAQ

Lead Follow-Up System FAQ for Nashville Tree Service

How many follow-ups does it send?

Typically 3–5 touches over 7–10 days depending on how your business operates. We customize the sequence so it matches your sales cycle.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. We write the initial sequence based on your style and your business. You can edit it anytime. Most owners keep it simple—just enough to confirm interest and book the estimate.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They get unsubscribed immediately. The system respects opt-outs. No exceptions.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Usually within 5–15 minutes of the lead coming in, depending on whether they call, text, or fill out a form. The sooner the better.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in your system and get the full follow-up sequence. If they still don't respond after the sequence runs, they're marked inactive. You can reach back out manually later if you want.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. It fits around your crew, your estimators, and however you're running things now. It's built to save your team time, not replace what's already working.
