FAQ

Common Questions About Review Automation for EV Charger Installers

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests only after real jobs you've completed. The customer is the one writing the review—we're just making sure they see the request when they're most likely to respond.

What if a customer is unhappy?

They're not obligated to leave a review. But if they do write something negative, that's real feedback you can address. Most unhappy customers who do review give you a chance to make it right—and that conversation often turns into a positive review.

Which platforms does this work on?

We focus on Google Business Profile, which is where most customers and property managers search for EV charger installers in Fort Worth. That's the review platform that actually moves the needle.

How does the timing work?

The request goes out as soon as you mark the job complete. You set it up once, and it runs automatically every time. No manual triggers, no forgetting to send it.

What if I already have a review process?

Most installers have a process that works sometimes—when someone remembers to ask or when a customer volunteers. Automation makes sure it happens every single time, which is why you'll see significantly more responses.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Most installation businesses see 3-5 new reviews per month once the system is live, depending on your job volume. The exact number depends on how many jobs you're completing and your customer response rate—we'll give you a realistic forecast during discovery.
