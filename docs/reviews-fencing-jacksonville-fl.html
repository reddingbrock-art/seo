FAQ

Reviews and Automation FAQs for Jacksonville Fencing Contractors

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests only to customers you've actually completed work for. They choose whether to leave a review or not. Google will flag fake reviews, and that tanks your credibility faster than anything else.

What if a customer is unhappy?

They don't have to leave a review. The system just sends a request—they control what happens next. If someone's upset, you probably want to know that before it becomes a public review anyway.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google primarily. That's where people search for local services and where your fence customers are looking. It's the review platform that matters most for your visibility.

How does the timing work?

Requests go out automatically after job completion—usually within a day or two. Customers see it when they're still thinking about the work. No manual scheduling on your end.

What if I already have a review process?

If it's working and reviews are flowing in consistently, you're in good shape. If it's spotty or manual, this fills those gaps and makes it automatic. We can talk through what you're doing now on a call.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Depends on your job volume and how many customers you're currently asking. Most contractors see significantly more reviews once the request system's running, because they're asking consistently instead of sporadically.
