FAQ

AI Chat Agent FAQs for Solar Installation Businesses

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on your service area, your typical customer questions, and the way your team actually talks about solar installations. It knows whether you're serving Federal Hill or Dundalk, and it answers like someone who knows your market.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It flags the conversation and passes it to your team with full context. You get a notification, jump in, and the customer never experiences a handoff delay—the conversation just continues.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It replaces the void. If you have a receptionist, they focus on closing and strategy instead of answering "Do you serve my zip code?" forty times a day. If you don't, you suddenly have one handling your inquiries 24/7.

What hours does the AI work?

All of them. Midnight, Sunday, Thanksgiving—it's always on, always answering. Your team works normal hours; the AI works around the clock.

How long does setup take?

Discovery and training take about a week. Go-live is 10–14 days from kickoff. You're answering customer inquiries automatically within two weeks.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

They still get their answer, their question answered, and their appointment booked. Being transparent about automation doesn't cost you the lead—being slow does.
