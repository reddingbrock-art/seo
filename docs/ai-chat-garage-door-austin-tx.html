FAQ

Common Questions About AI Chat for Garage Door Services in Austin

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on your language, your service areas, your pricing, and how you actually talk to customers. A customer calling you should feel like they're talking to your business, not a generic chatbot.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It escalates to your team or collects the customer's info and hands them off as a warm lead with context. It's not trying to be a technician—it's qualifying and booking.

Does this replace my receptionist?

It replaces the phone-answering and form-response part of that role. Your team shifts focus to confirming appointments, managing the schedule, and supporting crews. You get more productivity from the people you already have.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. Calls come in at midnight, 3 AM, 6 AM—your AI answers every time. It books appointments within your actual service hours.

How long does setup take?

10–14 days from discovery call to go-live. Most of that's us building and training. Your involvement is light—mostly answering questions about how you operate.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

They won't assume it's fake—it sounds and acts human. If they ask directly, we keep it honest: 'You're connected to our automated system, which is booking your appointment right now.' Transparency builds trust, not skepticism.
