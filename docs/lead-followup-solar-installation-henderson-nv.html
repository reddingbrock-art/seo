FAQ

Lead Follow-Up Questions for Solar Installation Companies

How many follow-ups does it send?

We build a sequence customized to your process—typically 3–5 touchpoints over 7–10 days. Your first message goes out within minutes; the rest are timed to keep momentum without being pushy.

Can I customize what it says?

Completely. We write the initial messaging together based on your positioning, and you can tweak language anytime. It should sound like your company, not ours.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can opt out anytime, and they're removed from the sequence immediately. We handle that compliance for you automatically.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Within minutes of the lead converting. The faster you're in their inbox or on their phone, the better your chances of being their first call.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They complete the full sequence you've designed. If they never engage, they're marked as such in your system, and you can decide whether to manually follow up or move on.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Yes. It plugs into your current lead sources and CRM. Your team keeps doing what they do best; the system handles the timing part that usually gets missed.
