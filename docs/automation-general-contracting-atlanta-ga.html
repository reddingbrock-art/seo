FAQ

Automation Questions for Atlanta General Contractors

What exactly gets automated?

Lead capture from all sources (phone, text, web forms, chat), qualification based on your requirements, assignment to the right crew or estimator, and follow-up reminders if a lead goes quiet. You control what gets auto-assigned and what still needs your approval.

Do I need technical skills to run this?

No. Your office manager or dispatcher uses a simple dashboard. If they can check email, they can run this. We handle all the backend setup.

What if my team resists new tools?

Most resistance disappears once they see it saves them 10-15 hours a week of admin work. We train your team and stay available for questions. The system makes their job easier, not harder.

How is this different from buying software myself?

You'd still have to build the integrations, train your team, decide what automates and what doesn't, and handle breakdowns on your own. We do all that. You just get the benefit.

What does the setup process look like?

One discovery call with you, 7-10 days of us configuring the system to your workflow, a full training session with your team, and a soft launch with support alongside you for the first week.

What happens if something breaks after launch?

You've got us. We monitor the system and fix issues before they impact your leads. If your phone provider changes or a form needs tweaking, we handle it.
