FAQ

Common Questions About Foundation Repair Review Automation in Charlotte

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send review requests only to customers you've actually served and invoiced. Google flags and removes fake reviews quickly—we don't do that. You get real feedback from real jobs.

What if a customer is unhappy?

The system sends to everyone, yes. But unhappy customers are rare after a completed job, and when they do leave a review, it gives you a chance to respond professionally and show how you handle problems. That builds credibility.

Which platforms does this work on?

We focus on Google, where foundation repair leads search most. That's where your local visibility matters most in Charlotte.

How does the timing work?

You mark a job complete in your system. The request goes out the next day, when the experience is fresh and the homeowner's most likely to respond. No manual timing needed.

What if I already have a review process?

If it's working, great—but most contractors we talk to have a process they forget to follow. We make it impossible to skip. If you want to keep your current message and just automate it, we can do that too.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Most contractors see a significant increase in review volume within 30 days because they're actually asking. The exact number depends on your job volume, but consistency matters more than any single number.
