FAQ

Lead Follow-Up System FAQs for Charlotte Waterproofing & Basement Contractors

How many follow-ups does it send?

It depends on your process, but typically 4–6 touches over 10–14 days across text, email, and chat. We customize this based on your sales cycle.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. We write messages based on your business, your service area, and the type of leads you get. You approve everything before it goes out.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They get removed from that sequence immediately. Compliance is built in.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Within 60 minutes of the lead coming in. No waiting for your team to see the notification.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in the system and can be re-engaged later, or they get tagged so your team knows they went silent. Nothing's wasted.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. It's built to fit into whatever you're already doing—no ripping out your current tools or changing your workflow.
