FAQ

Foundation Repair Lead Follow-Up Questions

How many follow-ups does it send?

That depends on your operation. Most foundation repair companies use 3 to 5 touches across text, call, and email over a 7 to 10 day window. We customize the sequence to match your close timeline.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. We write it with you, and you approve every message before it goes live. It sounds like your company, not like automation.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can opt out, and they'll be removed from future follow-ups. We handle the compliance side so you don't have to worry about it.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Within minutes of the lead arriving in your system. Most companies set their first touch for under 5 minutes, while the lead's still on the phone or browsing.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in the sequence until it completes. If they don't convert after all touches, they sit dormant. You can always reach back out manually if you want to, but the system doesn't keep pestering them forever.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. If your estimators already have a routine, we integrate around it. The system fills gaps and keeps leads warm while your team does what they do best.
