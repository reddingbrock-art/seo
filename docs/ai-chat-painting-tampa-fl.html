FAQ

AI Chat Widget for Painting Contractors: Questions Answered

Will the chat widget actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on your language, service descriptions, and how you talk to customers. It sounds like your team, not a generic bot.

What happens when the chat can't answer something?

It tells the visitor you'll follow up and captures their info. You get a notification, and the lead stays in your queue. Nothing gets dropped.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It handles initial qualification and scheduling so your receptionist or office team can focus on other tasks. If you don't have a receptionist, it fills that role entirely.

What hours does the chat widget work?

24/7. It answers questions and books appointments even when you're sleeping or on a job site. You decide which time slots are available for scheduling.

How long does setup take?

About 10–14 days from kickoff to launch. We handle everything—you just answer a few questions about your business and review the final version.

What if a customer wants to call instead of chat?

Your phone number is in the widget. They can always call if they prefer. Most don't, which is the point—you capture people who were never going to pick up the phone in the first place.
