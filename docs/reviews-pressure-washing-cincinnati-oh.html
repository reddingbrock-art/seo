FAQ

Common Questions About Automated Review Requests for Pressure Washing

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We're sending requests to actual customers you've already served. You only get real reviews from people who experienced your work.

What if a customer is unhappy?

They're not forced to leave a review. The system sends a request, but only satisfied customers are likely to respond. Unhappy customers usually reach out directly to you anyway.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google is the primary focus—that's where most of your customers search and read reviews. We can discuss other platforms during your discovery call.

How does the timing work?

The request goes out shortly after you mark a job complete. The timing is designed to hit when the experience is still fresh in your customer's mind.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll integrate with what you're already doing. If you're manually asking, we automate that step so nothing falls through the cracks.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

It depends on your job volume and response rates, but most owners see significantly more reviews per month once automation is running consistently.
