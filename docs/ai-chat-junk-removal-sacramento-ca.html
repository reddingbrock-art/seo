FAQ

AI Chat Agent FAQs for Junk Removal

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We build it around your voice, your service area, your typical customer questions, and how your team actually talks to customers. It's trained on your specific operation, not a generic template.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It tells the customer it's connecting them to you or scheduling a time for you to call back. It never pretends to know something it doesn't. You still own the relationship.

Does this replace my receptionist?

It replaces the part of your operation that misses calls and voicemails—the answering and qualifying step. If you have a receptionist, they can focus on dispatch, customer follow-up, or other work. If you don't, you're no longer bleeding leads.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. Calls come in at 2 a.m.? It answers. Texts roll in Sunday night? It responds. You control which hours it books appointments for (so customers don't get a Tuesday 3 a.m. slot), but it's always listening.

How long does setup take?

We go live in 10-14 days. Discovery call, build phase, testing with you, then launch. You'll be fielding AI-answered calls within two weeks.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

Most don't. But if they ask or prefer to talk to you, the AI tells them straight and can transfer or schedule a callback. Honesty builds trust. Customers care that they got answered fast and booked without hassle.
