FAQ

Common Questions About AI Chat for Painting Businesses

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We customize its voice, terminology, and tone during setup. It references your neighborhoods, services, and process—not generic painting small talk.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It escalates the conversation to you with full context. You jump in, answer the question, and the customer never knows there was a handoff.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It replaces the part of your receptionist's job that's pure repetition—answering when you're on a call or out at a job. Your team stays focused on selling and serving.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. Leads at midnight or 6 AM get responses just like leads during business hours. You decide if certain times should escalate to you.

How long does setup take?

10–14 days from your discovery call to go-live. Most of that is our build time, not yours. You're involved in 2–3 short review sessions.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

We recommend being upfront about it. Something like: 'Hi, I'm an AI assistant for [Your Company]. I'll get you the fastest answer to your question.' Honesty builds trust. And if they'd rather talk to a human, they can say so and you take over immediately.
