FAQ

Common Questions About Review Automation for Contractors

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We request reviews only from customers you've actually completed work for. Real jobs, real customers, real reviews. Google flags artificial patterns, and we don't create them.

What if a customer is unhappy?

You handle it the same way you always do—directly with them. We're just automating the ask to satisfied customers who already told you the job went well. Unhappy customers are a business problem, not a system problem.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google primarily, since that's where homeowners search for contractors. You can also pull reviews onto your website. We focus on platforms that actually drive calls.

How does the timing work?

Requests go out automatically after a job is marked complete in your system. You control the timing—could be same day, could be a few days later if you want the dust to settle. It's your call.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll work around it. If you're already asking for reviews manually, we can fill gaps or take it over entirely. The goal is consistency, not replacement for replacement's sake.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

It depends on your job volume and customer base. Most contractors see a significant increase because they're asking systematically instead of sporadically. Some customers always ignore requests—that's normal. But you'll see more reviews than you get now.
