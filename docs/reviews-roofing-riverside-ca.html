FAQ

Common Questions About Google Review Automation for Roofers

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests only to customers who actually had work completed. Google detects and removes fake reviews anyway—we're not taking that risk. Your reviews come from real jobs.

What if a customer is unhappy?

We only send review requests to completed jobs—not refunds, disputes, or ongoing work. If there's a problem, you handle it directly with the customer before they ever see a request. That's between you two.

Which platforms does this work on?

We focus on Google Reviews because that's where homeowners search for roofing contractors and where it matters most for your visibility. Other platforms follow once you've got momentum there.

How does the timing work?

The request goes out the day after job completion, when the work's fresh in the customer's mind but they've had time to see the final result. You choose what time of day makes sense for your market.

What if I already have a review process?

Tell us what you're doing now. If it's working, we make sure the automation complements it instead of creating duplicate requests. If it's not working, we replace it with something that actually runs.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Most contractors see a significant increase once requests go out automatically at the right time. The exact number depends on your job volume and customer response rates—we'll know more after the first 30 days of data.
