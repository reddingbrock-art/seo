Automation for Home Security: Questions Answered

What exactly gets automated?

Lead capture from your website, initial qualification questions, callback scheduling, lead routing to the right tech or manager, and follow-up reminders. Basically everything between 'someone fills out a form' and 'someone picks up the phone.'

Do I need technical skills to run this?

No. Your office manager or whoever's running scheduling will use a simple dashboard. We handle the backend. You just manage jobs and customers like normal.

What if my team resists new tools?

We've seen it. The resistance usually stops when they realize the system removes busywork, not jobs. Less phone tag, fewer missed callbacks, more actual selling. Your team will like it once they see it working for a week.

How is this different from buying software myself?

You could buy a CRM and spend two months trying to get it working with your website and your workflow. We handle all that. You get a system that's already built for field service, already connected to what you're using, already live. Done-for-you means we own the setup and the outcome.

What does the setup process look like?

Discovery call to understand your flow. Our team builds and tests everything. You sign off. Goes live 10-14 days after we start. Your only job is a 60-minute team training on day one.

What happens if something breaks after launch?

We support it. Bugs, integrations, workflow changes—that's on us. You're not scrambling to figure out why a lead didn't route. We keep it running and adjust it based on what your team learns.
