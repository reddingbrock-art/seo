FAQ

Google Review Automation FAQs for Fort Worth Foundation Repair

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send review requests only to customers who actually completed a job with you. What they write is entirely up to them. You're asking real customers for real feedback.

What if a customer is unhappy?

They can leave that feedback, and you'll see it. Honest reviews—positive and critical—matter more than a high count. Most customers won't leave a bad review unless something was genuinely wrong, and in that case, you want to know about it early so you can respond.

Which platforms does this work on?

We focus on Google Business Profile because that's where foundation repair customers in Fort Worth are searching and making decisions. You can also manually share positive reviews on your website or Facebook if you want.

How does the timing work?

The request goes out 24 hours after you mark a job complete in your system. That's the sweet spot—late enough that the work is done and fresh in their mind, early enough that they remember the experience clearly.

What if I already have a review process?

We can complement it or replace it. If you're already asking for reviews manually and getting a few, automation will accelerate that. If you're not doing anything, this is the starting point.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

It depends on your job volume and response rate, but most foundation repair businesses see 2-3x their previous monthly review count within 60 days. If you're doing 8-10 jobs a month and currently getting 1-2 reviews, you should expect 6-10 per month once the system settles in.
