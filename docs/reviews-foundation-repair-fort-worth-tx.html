Google Review Automation for Foundation Repair: Common Questions

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests only to customers you've completed work for. Google validates that the reviewer actually had a service interaction with your business. Real jobs create real reviews.

What if a customer is unhappy?

We send the request to everyone equally. If someone had a bad experience, they'll let you know in the review or elsewhere. You'll see it in real time and can respond professionally. That honesty is better than silence.

Which platforms does this work on?

We focus on Google reviews because that's where Fort Worth homeowners are searching for foundation repair contractors. That's where the visibility and trust actually matter.

How does the timing work?

The system watches for job completion in your operation. Once marked done, the review request goes out automatically. You control when that trigger fires based on your schedule.

What if I already have a review process?

We can fit around whatever you're doing now. Most owners are asking manually or not at all. If you've got a process that's working, we can integrate the automation into it without disrupting anything.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Most foundation repair owners see significantly more reviews once the ask is automated and timed right. The exact number depends on your job volume, but you'll notice the difference within 30 days of going live.
