FAQ

Common Questions About Lead Follow-Up Automation for Water Treatment

How many follow-ups does it send?

That's your call. Most water treatment owners set it to 2–3 touches over 24–48 hours (first contact, second text or call, final email). We'll recommend a sequence based on your typical sales cycle.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. You write the messaging—or we can draft it based on your intake questions and service type. Every message goes out under your name and voice.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can reply STOP or unsubscribe, and the system respects it immediately. They'll be removed from follow-ups.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Instantly. Form submission or inbound call triggers the sequence right away—no delay.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They complete the follow-up sequence and then drop off. You'll have a record of when they were contacted and whether they engaged with any message. You can review them later or let them go.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. If you're already using a CRM or scheduling tool, the system integrates alongside it. It doesn't replace your dispatch or service flow—it just handles the messy part in the middle.
