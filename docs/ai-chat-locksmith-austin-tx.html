AI Chat Agent for Locksmith Services—Common Questions

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We customize it with your name, your service area, your pricing, and the tone you want—professional, friendly, whatever matches your brand. It's not a generic bot voice; it sounds like your team.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It knows to ask clarifying questions or escalate. If it's something outside its scope—custom requests, pricing exceptions—it notes it and sends the full context to you so you can follow up smart.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It replaces the gaps—nights, lunch breaks, job time, peak call surges. If you have a receptionist, they work together. If you don't, the AI does the heavy lifting while you stay focused on the field.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. Nights, weekends, holidays—it's on. You set your response times and service windows, and the AI qualifies leads whenever they call.

How long does setup take?

You're live in 10-14 days. One discovery call, we build it, and it starts answering your calls. No technical work on your end.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

Most don't notice until they're already scheduled. But if they ask, it's transparent—you're using AI to make sure they get a fast answer and a locked-in appointment. Customers respect efficiency.
