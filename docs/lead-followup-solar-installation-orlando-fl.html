FAQ

Lead Follow-Up System Questions for Solar Installers

How many follow-ups does it send?

Typically three to five touches over 7-10 days—call, text, email in a sequence that keeps the lead engaged without feeling spammy. We customize it to match your sales cycle.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. We work with you to write every message so it sounds like your business, not a robot. Your crew leads review it before we deploy.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can opt out anytime. We honor that request immediately and mark them in the system so no further contact goes out.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Within 15-30 minutes of form submission, depending on the time of day and your preferences. You can also set it to wait until business hours if you want more human control.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in your system and receive the full sequence. After that, they move to a nurture track that touches base periodically. Most either respond or fade—we don't delete them.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. It integrates with whatever CRM or form tool you're using now. Your crew leads still close the deals—we just make sure no one falls through the cracks.
