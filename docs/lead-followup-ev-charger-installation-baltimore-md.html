FAQ

Lead Follow-Up System FAQs for EV Charger Installation

How many follow-ups does it send?

That depends on your business. Most companies run 3–5 touches over 7–10 days. You decide the sequence and timing before we go live.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. You write the messages or we draft them for you based on how you talk to prospects. It reflects your voice, not a template.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can opt out with one click or reply. They're removed immediately. No spam, no pushback.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Within minutes of form submission. If it's after hours, it goes out the first thing the next business day based on your settings.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in your CRM so your team can circle back manually if needed. The system doesn't delete anyone—you keep the data.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. It plugs into your current forms, scheduling, and CRM. No replacement. Just automation on top of what you're already doing.
