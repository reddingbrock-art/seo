Common Questions About AI Chat for Water Treatment Websites

Will the chat widget actually sound like my business?

Yes. We write it based on how your team talks to customers—your terminology, your service areas, your personality. It's trained on your business, not a generic template.

What happens when the chat can't answer something?

It tells the visitor it's connecting them with your team and captures their info so someone can follow up. No dead ends, no frustrated customers.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It replaces the moment your receptionist isn't available. It handles the greeting and basic qualification so your people spend their time on actual work, not intake calls.

What hours does the chat widget work?

24/7. It's live whenever someone lands on your site, even at 2 a.m. or on Sunday. You can set business hours if you prefer to disable it after-hours.

How long does setup take?

10–14 days from discovery to live. We handle the technical work. You just give us access to your website and answer a few questions about how you work.

What if a customer wants to call instead of chat?

The widget offers both. They can call your main line or continue chatting. We don't force anyone into a channel they don't want.
