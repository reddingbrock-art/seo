FAQ

Lead Follow-Up Questions for Appliance Repair Owners

How many follow-ups does it send?

That depends on your strategy, but most appliance repair shops start with 3–5 touches over 7 days—text, email, maybe another text if they opened the first message. You control the cadence.

Can I customize what it says?

Completely. You set the tone and message for every follow-up. We build the automation; you own the voice. If a lead mentions a specific appliance in their intake form, you can reference it in the follow-up.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can unsubscribe, and they're removed immediately. It's built in. You stay compliant and respect their preference.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Within minutes of the lead submitting a form or calling in—depending on how you want it configured. Most owners set it for instant, so leads know someone's paying attention.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in your system. You can re-engage them later with a different message, move them to a slower nurture sequence, or export the list for seasonal campaigns. Cold leads don't just disappear.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Yes. It integrates with where your leads come from now—your website, phone line, Facebook, whatever. It doesn't replace your team; it fills the gaps while they're on service calls.
