FAQ

Google Review Automation for Plumbing: Answers to Common Questions

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests only to customers whose jobs actually closed in your system. Google's algorithm flags fake review patterns instantly, and we don't touch those guardrails. Real requests, real customers, real reviews.

What if a customer is unhappy?

They still get the request, same as anyone else. Some will leave honest negative feedback, and that's data you need. We're not filtering or suppressing—we're asking everyone. Your job is to earn the rating you deserve.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google is the priority because that's where customers search for plumbers in Indianapolis. The system focuses on capturing reviews there first.

How does the timing work?

The request goes out automatically when a job invoice closes in your system. You set the delivery time—morning, afternoon, or evening—and it goes out on schedule without you lifting a finger.

What if I already have a review process?

We replace it with one that's actually automatic. If your current process relies on a crew member asking or a manager following up, you're not capturing most reviews. This one runs itself.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Most plumbing shops capture fewer than 5% of the reviews they're eligible for. Moving that to 15–25% is common once the system's running. Your actual number depends on your job volume and how many customers you're reaching right now.
