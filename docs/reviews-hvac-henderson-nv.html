FAQ

Review Automation Questions for HVAC Companies

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send the request to real customers after they actually get service. Whether they leave a review is up to them. Fake reviews are a liability and we don't operate that way.

What if a customer is unhappy?

The automation works regardless. What you do with unhappy feedback is up to you—many owners use it as a signal to follow up directly and fix the problem before it becomes a public issue.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google primarily, since that's where new customers find you. Other review sites can be included depending on your goals—we'll discuss during discovery.

How does the timing work?

The request goes out automatically the same day or next morning after a job closes in your system. The timing is designed to catch customers while they're still satisfied and thinking about the work.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll build around what you're doing. If you're getting solid volume already, we optimize timing and response rate. If you're not, we replace the manual process entirely.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Most HVAC companies see response rates jump from 3–5% to 20–30% in the first month. If you're doing 20 jobs a week, that's 3 or 4 reviews a month becoming 4 to 6 a week. Growth compounds from there.
