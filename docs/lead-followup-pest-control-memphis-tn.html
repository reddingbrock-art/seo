FAQ

Questions About Lead Follow-Up Automation for Pest Control

How many follow-ups does it send?

It depends on your lead type and preferences. We typically set up 3-5 touches over the first week—text, call, or both. We customize it based on what works for your service area.

Can I customize what it says?

Absolutely. We write the first draft based on your brand and service model. You approve it, we tweak it, then it runs. All communication sounds like your business, not like automation.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can opt out anytime. We handle compliance. Unsubscribe requests are honored immediately, and we track them so you're never contacting someone who's asked to be left alone.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Within 5-10 minutes of the lead coming in. That's during business hours. After hours, it queues for first thing the next morning, depending on your preference.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They still get all the follow-ups on schedule. After the sequence runs, they stay in your system. You can reach out manually whenever you want, or they'll re-enter the flow if they contact you again.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Yes. Your dispatcher still manages callbacks, estimates, and jobs. This system just handles the initial follow-up so no lead goes untouched. It makes your current process faster.
