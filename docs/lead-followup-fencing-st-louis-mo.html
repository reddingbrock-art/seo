FAQ

Questions About Lead Follow-Up Automation for Fencing

How many follow-ups does it send?

Typically 3–5 touches over 7–10 days depending on how you configure it. Initial response goes out immediately, then spaced follow-ups if they don't reply. You control the sequence.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. We write templates based on your company voice, but you can edit them. Every message should sound like your team, not a robot.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can opt out and the system stops. We handle compliance so you don't have legal headaches. It's built in from day one.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Within a few minutes of form submission or lead capture. Fast enough that you're usually the first company they hear from.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in the system on a longer follow-up track, or they move to a nurture sequence if you want to stay in front of them. You decide what happens next.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. It integrates with your calendar, phone system, and existing tools. It handles the repetitive touches so your team can focus on closing.
