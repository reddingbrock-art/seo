FAQ

AI Chat for Locksmiths in Indianapolis: Questions & Answers

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We build it using your scripts, your language, your pricing, and the way you actually answer phones. It doesn't sound corporate or robotic—it sounds like someone from your team picking up.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It logs the question and flags it for you, or transfers the customer to your phone if it's live. Nothing falls through the cracks. We also use those gaps to refine what the AI learns.

Does this replace my receptionist?

It replaces the *waiting*. If you have a receptionist, they're now handling callbacks and customer service instead of fielding repeat questions and scheduling calls. If you don't have one, this is your always-on first responder.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. Nights, weekends, holidays—it's answering. You decide what it does during your operating hours versus after-hours (like offering emergency rates or promising a callback).

How long does setup take?

10–14 days from kickoff to live. We gather your info, build the AI, integrate it with your systems, and train you on how to monitor and adjust it.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

Most don't. But if they do, it's honestly fine—they're getting their job booked while you're busy, and they appreciate the immediate response. Transparency builds trust.
