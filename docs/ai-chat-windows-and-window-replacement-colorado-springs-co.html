FAQ

AI Chat for Windows & Window Replacement: Questions Answered

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on your messaging, service language, and how your team talks to customers. It speaks like someone from your company who knows windows, neighborhoods in the Springs, and your pricing structure.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It escalates to you or your team with full context. You get the conversation history, the customer's name and number, and the exact question. You take it from there.

Does this replace my receptionist?

It handles the initial engagement—answering questions, qualifying, booking estimates. If you have a receptionist, they move from answering phones all day to handling complex conversations and managing your schedule. It's a promotion, not a replacement.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. It answers at 3 a.m. the same way it answers at 10 a.m. Most of your leads probably come in after hours anyway when people are home researching window replacement.

How long does setup take?

10-14 days from discovery call to go-live. You'll be answering leads and booking estimates before the month ends.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

It doesn't hide. It's transparent about being an automated system, but it's so knowledgeable and responsive that most customers don't care—they just appreciate getting an answer immediately instead of waiting.
