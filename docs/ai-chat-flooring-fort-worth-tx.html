FAQ

AI Chat for Flooring: Common Questions Answered

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on your language, your service offerings, and your brand tone before it goes live. Customers will hear someone who knows flooring, knows your service area, and represents your business—not a generic bot.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It tells the customer you'll call them back and logs the question. It never makes something up or transfers to a dead number. You get the full context and call them directly during business hours.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It replaces the voicemail box and the missed-call problem. If you have a receptionist, they stay focused on operations instead of returning 20 callbacks. If you don't, you finally have someone answering the phone.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. Calls at midnight, Sunday morning, Christmas Eve—they all get answered and logged. You respond during your business hours with leads already qualified.

How long does setup take?

10–14 days from your discovery call to live. Most of that is us building and you reviewing. Once you approve, we flip the switch the same day.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

They usually don't. But if they ask directly, they get an honest answer and the option to leave a callback. Either way, their information is captured and you follow up. No lost leads.
