FAQ

AI Chat Agent FAQs for Foundation Repair Contractors

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. During discovery, we learn how you talk to customers—your inspection process, how you explain foundation issues, even your pricing structure. The agent sounds like someone from your team, not a robot.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It admits it doesn't know and offers to send the question to you directly or suggest scheduling an inspection call with you. Never makes up an answer. Never promises something you don't deliver.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. If you have a receptionist, this takes the after-hours and overflow load off them. They focus on phone calls and in-office tasks. If you don't have one, the AI handles inbound entirely.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. Every day. It answers at midnight, 3 AM, Christmas, and every moment you can't. Appointments still respect your actual business hours—it won't book a 6 AM inspection if you don't start until 8 AM.

How long does setup take?

10–14 days from discovery call to live. We handle all the backend work—calendar integration, CRM connection, knowledge training. You don't touch the technical side.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

Transparency is built in. If they ask, the agent tells them. Most customers don't care—they got their answer, booked their appointment, and moved on. It's not deceptive; it's just efficient.
