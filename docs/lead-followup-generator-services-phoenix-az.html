FAQ

Generator Services Lead Follow-Up: Common Questions

How many follow-ups does it send?

That depends on your sales cycle. We typically build a 3-5 touch sequence over 7-10 days. You control when it stops—some owners pause after a callback, others let it run to completion. You decide.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. We write the initial templates around your brand voice, and you review every message before it goes live. You can update copy anytime if you want to test new angles.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can opt out with one click or reply, and they're removed from the sequence immediately. Compliance built in, no manual work.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Within minutes of form submission or lead intake. The faster you touch them, the higher your conversion. That's the whole point.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in your CRM with full history. Some convert later—maybe they shopped and came back, or got another quote and decided to call you. You can re-engage them manually or let them sit. Your choice.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. We integrate with your CRM and dispatch software so your team's workflow doesn't change. This just fills the gap between lead intake and when your phone gets picked up.
