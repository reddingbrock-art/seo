FAQ

Lead Follow-Up FAQs for Mold Remediation Businesses

How many follow-ups does it send?

Usually 3-5 touches over 7-10 days: an initial call/text within minutes, a follow-up the next day, and a couple more if they don't respond. We customize the sequence based on how your team actually closes deals.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. We write all messaging to sound like you, reference your specific services, and match how your team talks to customers. You review and approve everything before it goes live.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

The system honors unsubscribe requests immediately. They're removed from future contact and tracked in your dashboard so your team knows not to call them manually.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Within minutes of the lead calling or filling out a form, depending on the time of day and whether it's a call or text. Most initial contacts happen before you'd even check voicemail.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in your dashboard marked as "no response." Your team can still call them manually if you want, or the system stops after the final touch depending on your preferences. Nothing disappears.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. If you already use a CRM or scheduling software, the system integrates with it or feeds leads to you in a way that fits your current workflow. We build it to work with you, not replace you.
