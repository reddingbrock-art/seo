FAQ

Generator Services Review Automation: Frequently Asked Questions

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send review requests only to customers whose jobs are marked complete in your system. Real customers, real work, real feedback. What you do after that is up to them.

What if a customer is unhappy?

That's their right—and honest feedback is better than silence. A five-star review next to a three-star review tells potential customers that you're real, not manufactured. If unhappy customers do leave reviews, you respond and fix it. That's how reputation actually works.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google is the primary focus because that's where most generator service searches happen in Houston. Other platforms can be included depending on your needs—we discuss that during setup.

How does the timing work?

The request goes out when your job status changes to 'complete.' We can adjust the delay—same day, next morning, 24 hours later—based on what feels natural for your business and your customer experience.

What if I already have a review process?

Most generator service owners have asked for reviews manually, or sporadically. This replaces that burden. If you've got a formal process in place, we integrate this so nothing duplicates. More often, this just becomes your process.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

That depends on your job volume and customer response rate. Most generator service businesses see significant more reviews within the first 60 days compared to their historical rate. We'll give you a baseline after the first month.
