FAQ

AI Chat Agent FAQs for Water Treatment Businesses

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on how you talk to customers—your terminology, your tone, your approach to explaining water treatment. A prospect talking to your AI should feel like they're talking to someone who knows your business.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It recognizes the limit and hands the conversation to your team with full context—the customer's name, address, system details, and the exact question that stumped it. No back-and-forth needed.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It replaces the part of your receptionist's job that's just answering the same questions over and over. Your team handles complex calls, complaints, and relationship-building. The AI handles intake and qualification.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. Nights, weekends, holidays. It never stops answering. You set business hours if you want, but most of our water treatment clients run it round-the-clock because emergencies don't wait.

How long does setup take?

10–14 days from discovery call to live. We handle the heavy lifting. You just need to answer questions about your business and review the training.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

It's fine. You can have it disclose upfront, or it stays transparent if asked. Most customers don't care as long as their problem gets solved and they can book an appointment. Speed beats the illusion every time.
