FAQ

Google Review Automation Questions for Septic & Drain Owners

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send review requests only to customers whose jobs are marked complete in your system. Google can verify they're real customers. Fake reviews get you penalized and delisted.

What if a customer is unhappy?

The system requests reviews from completed jobs. If a customer had a bad experience, you'd usually know before they leave. We don't force anyone to review—we ask, and they choose.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google is the priority because that's where your customers search for septic and drain work. We focus the system there.

How does the timing work?

The request goes out the same day you mark the job complete—when the customer is still thinking about the work they just had done. That's when response rates are highest.

What if I already have a review process?

We replace the manual process with an automated one. If you're already asking but forgetting some jobs, we catch all of them. If you're not asking at all, you'll see the difference immediately.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Most field service shops see 3-8 new reviews per month once the system is live, depending on your job volume. The number climbs as you capture requests you were already losing.
