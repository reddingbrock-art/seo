FAQ

AI Chat Widget for Junk Removal: Common Questions

Will the chat widget actually sound like my business?

Yes. We write the conversation using your language, your pricing structure, and the questions your team actually asks. It doesn't sound robotic or generic.

What happens when the chat can't answer something?

It collects the customer's info and lets them know someone from your team will follow up. You get notified and can call or message them back.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It handles the initial qualification and booking. Your team still manages the job, the customer relationship, and the work. It just fills the gaps when you're not available.

What hours does the chat widget work?

24/7. It books appointments and answers frequently asked questions anytime, day or night.

How long does setup take?

We go live in 10–14 days from your discovery call. Most of that is us building and you reviewing. It's not a long process.

What if a customer wants to call instead of chat?

They can. The widget doesn't force chat. If someone wants your phone number, we include it. Chat is just another way for people to reach you.
