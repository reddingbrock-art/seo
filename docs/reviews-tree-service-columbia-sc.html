FAQ

Google Review Automation FAQs for Tree Service Owners

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests only to customers you've actually worked for. They choose whether to review—we're not manufacturing opinions, just making sure the ask reaches people who already know your work.

What if a customer is unhappy?

They won't leave a five-star review. That's fine—authentic reviews are better than inflated ones. If you're consistently losing unhappy customers to reviews, that's an operational problem separate from the request system.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google primarily. That's where tree service leads look first. If you want reviews on other platforms later, that's a different conversation.

How does the timing work?

Requests go out the day after job completion, when the experience is fresh but the customer's moved on from the stress of scheduling. We can adjust timing if your workflow is different.

What if I already have a review process?

If it's working and generating reviews consistently, keep it. If it's manual, inconsistent, or depends on you remembering to follow up, this system replaces that friction.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Most tree services see significantly more reviews once requests actually get sent. The number depends on your completion volume and customer response rate—we'll know more after your first month live.
