FAQ

Questions About AI Chat for Appliance Repair in Charlotte

Will the chat widget actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on how you talk, your service areas, your pricing approach, and your tone. It's not generic. When a customer chats, they're talking to your business—not a corporate bot.

What happens when the chat can't answer something?

It hands off gracefully. If the customer asks something outside its scope—maybe a technical question it can't resolve—it tells them you'll follow up, captures their info, and queues it for you. Nothing gets lost.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It replaces the missed calls and lost leads. Your team still handles the jobs, the scheduling, the follow-ups. This just makes sure conversations that happen on your website actually turn into appointments.

What hours does the chat widget work?

24/7. Even when you're closed or on jobs, the chat's there. It can qualify leads, book appointments into your open slots, and keep conversations going. You check them whenever you're ready.

How long does setup take?

10–14 days from discovery to live. One week to build and refine, a few days for you to review, then deployment. You're live and capturing chats within two weeks.

What if a customer wants to call instead of chat?

Great. Your number's still there. The chat doesn't remove the phone option—it just gives people another way in. Most customers use both at different times, and that's fine.
