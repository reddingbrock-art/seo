FAQ

Review Automation Questions for Painting Companies

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send the request to actual customers after they've received your work. Whether they leave a review is up to them—we're just asking at the moment they're most likely to respond.

What if a customer is unhappy?

They won't leave a review, and that's fine. This system asks everyone, but it doesn't force anyone. It's not designed to suppress negative feedback—it's designed to capture reviews from satisfied customers at the right time.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google, Yelp, Facebook, and other major review platforms. The system distributes requests across all of them so customers can leave reviews wherever they're most comfortable.

How does the timing work?

You define when a job is 'complete' in your workflow—could be same day, next day, after final walkthrough. We send the request at that trigger point. The system is flexible to match how you actually operate.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll work with what you've got. If you're sending requests manually and getting low response rates, we'll show you how automation lifts that. If you're already getting some reviews, we'll amplify it.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Most painting companies see a significant increase once they start asking consistently. The jump depends on your current ask rate—if you're barely asking now, the difference is dramatic. We'll show you specifics based on your volume.
