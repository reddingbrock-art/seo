FAQ

Google and Online Review Automation for Pool Service in Charlotte

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send review requests only to customers from completed jobs in your system. Only real customers who actually worked with your crews receive the request. What they write is entirely up to them.

What if a customer is unhappy?

A review request is just an ask. Unhappy customers won't leave positive reviews, and that's honest feedback. You won't hear from them through this system—it only goes to customers whose jobs were completed. If you want to handle concerns before they become reviews, that's a different conversation.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google, Yelp, and Facebook primarily. We can configure requests for whichever platforms matter most to your pool service business. Google is almost always the priority.

How does the timing work?

The request goes out on the same day as job completion, or the next morning if the job finishes late. The goal is to hit the customer while they're still happy about the work, before they forget.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll integrate with what you're doing or replace it entirely. If you're asking for reviews manually, we automate it. If you're not asking at all, we build the system from scratch.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Most pool service businesses see significantly more reviews within 30 days because you're asking at the right moment. The exact number depends on your job volume, but most owners go from sporadic reviews to steady monthly growth.
