FAQ

AI Chat Agent Questions for Indianapolis Water Treatment Owners

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train the agent on your service language, your neighborhoods, your seasonal patterns, and your process. When a Carmel customer calls about water softener salt delivery, the agent sounds like someone who knows your business.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It's honest about the limitation and transfers the call or schedules a callback with a specific time. No fake answers, no frustration.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. If you have a receptionist, they move to higher-value work like scheduling, follow-ups, or customer relationship tasks. If you don't have one, you gain one.

What hours does the AI work?

You decide. Most water treatment shops run 24/7 answering for emergencies, so the agent can too. You can also set it to business hours only if you prefer.

How long does setup take?

We go live in 10-14 days from your discovery call. You'll be answering calls before your next seasonal spike.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

We don't hide it. The agent is transparent about what it is and what it can do. Most customers don't care—they're just glad someone answered and took their information. It saves them 30 minutes of callback waiting.
