FAQ

Common Questions About Lead Follow-Up for Landscapers

How many follow-ups does it send?

Depends on your setup. Most landscapers do 3-5 touches over a week—initial response, a couple check-ins, then a final reminder. We build it based on what works for your closing timeline.

Can I customize what it says?

Completely. We write the messages with you so they sound like your business, use your terminology, and ask the questions that matter to your estimates.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can opt out anytime. The system respects that and removes them from future sequences.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Instantly. The moment a lead comes in via form, phone, or text, your first response is already on its way.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in your system. You can reach back out later in the season, or we can set up a final check-in after the sequence ends.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Yes. It plugs into how you already take leads and just handles the follow-up part that's usually broken. Your estimators and crews see qualified leads in their inbox, nothing changes about how you close.
