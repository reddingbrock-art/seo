FAQ

Lead Follow-Up FAQs for Scottsdale Fire & Water Restoration

How many follow-ups does it send?

It depends on your process. Most restoration shops use 3–5 touches over 7 days: first response within minutes, then outreach at 2 hours, 1 day, and 3 days. You decide the sequence.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. We write templates that sound like your business—not corporate. You review them, approve them, and tweak anything you want before it goes live.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can opt out with one click or reply. The system logs it, stops sending, and marks them as unsubscribed. We handle compliance so you don't have to think about it.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Within 2–5 minutes of the lead coming in. Phone calls ring your team. Text and email follow within seconds.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in your pipeline until you manually close them or they reach the end of your sequence. You always have visibility into which leads are still warm and which ones are cold.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Yes. It plugs into your current setup—your CRM, your scheduling system, your phone line. It handles the repetitive follow-up work so your team focuses on closing and service delivery.
