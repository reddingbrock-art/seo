FAQ

Reviews and Automation FAQs for Foundation Repair Owners

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. The system asks real customers to leave real reviews after you've completed their job. If they don't want to leave one, they don't. You're just making sure the ask actually happens instead of falling through the cracks.

What if a customer is unhappy?

The request goes out after job completion when they've had time to assess the work. If there's a real problem, they usually tell you directly before that point. This isn't a trap—it's a way to capture satisfied customers before the moment passes.

Which platforms does this work on?

Reviews land on Google, your company website, and other major platforms depending on your setup. Google is where most of your prospects look first, and that's where the impact shows up fastest.

How does the timing work?

The request typically goes out within a few hours of job completion—when the customer is most likely to remember the experience clearly. You control when the request sends based on your workflow.

What if I already have a review process?

If you're asking customers manually and they're actually leaving reviews, great. Most foundation repair shops aren't consistent enough to make it stick. We just make sure it happens automatically so nothing gets skipped.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Most satisfied customers will leave a review if asked. The actual number depends on your job volume and how many customers say yes. You'll see the difference immediately because requests stop disappearing into the void.
