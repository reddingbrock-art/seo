FAQ

Review Automation Questions for Savannah Generator Services

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. The system sends requests only to customers with completed jobs in your system. We're asking real customers who actually worked with you—nothing synthetic or paid.

What if a customer is unhappy?

We send review requests to everyone post-job. If someone had a bad experience, they're free to leave that feedback. That's honest. The goal is volume and timing—not suppressing negative opinions. You want to know about problems anyway.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google Business Profile is the primary platform. That's where most of your prospects are looking, and it's where local rankings matter most for generator services. Other platforms can be added depending on your workflow.

How does the timing work?

The request sends automatically when a job is marked complete in your system—usually within hours of the technician closing the work order. The timing is configurable based on your preference.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll audit what you're doing now and fill the gaps. Most shops send reviews manually and inconsistently. We make it automatic so nothing gets missed. If you've got something custom, we integrate with it.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Most shops see significantly more reviews once they're asking consistently. If you're doing 25–30 jobs monthly and getting 3–4 reviews, automation typically moves that to 10–15 monthly. Response rates depend on how you're currently capturing customer contact info, but the gap is almost always the ask itself, not the willingness.
