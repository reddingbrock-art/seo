FAQ

Questions About Lead Follow-Up for Fire & Water Restoration

How many follow-ups does it send?

Typically 3-5 touchpoints over 7-10 days, depending on how you configure it. Fewer if a lead books or responds sooner. We adjust based on what you're seeing.

Can I customize what it says?

Completely. The messages come from you—your voice, your phone number, your brand. We help you dial in what resonates with your market.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They get removed from the sequence immediately. We honor opt-outs and build compliance into the system from day one.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Within 5-10 minutes of a lead coming in. The faster you move, the better your odds of being the first call they remember.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They complete the full sequence and sit in your database. You can pull them back in later with seasonal campaigns or targeted outreach based on their original inquiry.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Yes. If you've got a dispatcher, a CRM, or a phone system already running, we integrate around it. The system fills the gaps, doesn't replace what's working.
