FAQ

AI Chat for Septic & Drain: Common Questions

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on your service descriptions, pricing, neighborhoods you serve, and the way you talk to customers. It'll mention local streets, seasonal windows, and details that make it sound like someone from your team picked up.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It notes the question and transfers the customer's info to you with context about what they asked. You follow up with the customer directly—but the lead's already qualified and the appointment framework is built.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. If you have a receptionist, it handles overflow and after-hours calls so they can focus on scheduling, invoicing, and customer service. If you don't have one, this fills that gap without the cost.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. Calls and chats get answered at 2 a.m. on a Sunday just like they do at 10 a.m. on a Monday. You set which hours count as emergencies if you want different handling.

How long does setup take?

Configuration and training takes about a week. You're live and taking calls within 10–14 days of your discovery call.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

Most don't notice—it's natural and conversational. If they ask, the AI is honest about it and quickly transfers them to you or confirms their appointment is booked. Transparency builds trust.
