Do I need technical skills to run this?

No. Your team uses it the same way they'd use a phone or email. We handle all the backend wiring and updates. If something's confusing, we show you how to use it—that's part of the deal.

You'd buy a tool, try to wire it to your other tools, get stuck, and either hire someone to fix it or abandon it half-built. We do the wiring. You own the system, we own the setup and the problems that come with it.

What happens if something breaks after launch?
