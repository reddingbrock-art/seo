FAQ

Lead Follow-Up Questions for Junk Removal Companies

How many follow-ups does it send?

Typically three to five touches over a seven-day window—initial confirmation, availability request, reminder, then a final check-in. You can adjust the sequence to match your sales cycle.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. Every message is customized to your voice and your business. It doesn't sound generic or robotic.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can opt out with one reply. The system respects that immediately and removes them from future sequences.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Within minutes of the lead coming in. No waiting for office hours or for someone to check email.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in your system and you can see them in the dashboard. Some businesses re-engage them three months later when those folks actually need junk removal. You decide whether to reach back out.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. It plugs into your current intake—phone calls, forms, Facebook messages, text requests. Automation doesn't replace your team. It backs them up so they can focus on closing jobs.
