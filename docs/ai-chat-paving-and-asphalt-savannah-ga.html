Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on your actual language, how you talk to customers, your pricing structure, and your service areas. It sounds like someone from your crew, not a bot reading a script.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It tells the customer the truth—'Let me have the owner call you back about that'—and captures their info so you can follow up. No fake answers, no frustration.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It handles the calls that would otherwise go to voicemail, freeing your receptionist (if you have one) from phone duty during peak hours. It's a filter, not a replacement.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. Calls at midnight, 6 a.m., Sunday—they all get answered. You decide when you want to call leads back.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

Most don't notice. But if they ask, the AI is honest about it—then connects them with you immediately if they want to talk to an owner. Transparency builds trust.
