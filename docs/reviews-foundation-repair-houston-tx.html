FAQ

Review Automation Questions for Foundation Repair Owners

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests only to customers you've actually completed work for. Real jobs, real customers, real reviews. Google's algorithm catches fake reviews—we don't go there.

What if a customer is unhappy?

They're not obligated to leave a review. The request doesn't force anything. If someone's dissatisfied, they don't review—they just don't respond. There's no filtering or redirecting. The system works only for customers who complete the job and get billed.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google is the priority for foundation repair because that's where homeowners search. We also integrate with other review platforms your market cares about. We'll show you exactly which ones matter most in your area during the discovery call.

How does the timing work?

The request goes out automatically after your job invoice closes. That's typically 1–3 days after final payment. Your customer is still thinking about the work, still happy it's done, still fresh on the experience. Perfect timing.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll audit what you're doing now—whether it's manual requests, text reminders, or sporadic asks—and either improve it or replace it entirely with automation that doesn't break your workflow. No friction, no double-touch.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

That depends on your closure rate and how many customers currently review you without prompting. Most foundation repair companies see their review count climb significantly once automated requests go live. You'll see the difference in the first 30–60 days.
