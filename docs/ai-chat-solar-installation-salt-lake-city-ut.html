FAQ

AI Chat FAQs for Solar Installation Business Owners

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on your actual language, your crew's voice, your pricing philosophy, and the way you explain timelines. Conversations feel like they're coming from your team, not a corporate template.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It escalates to your team with full context. The prospect gets a message like 'That's a great question—I'm looping in our install manager who has the exact answer.' Your person jumps in and looks like a hero.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It replaces the *problem* your receptionist has when she's on two calls and three prospects are waiting in your form queue. Your team handles conversations earlier and with better info.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. It answers at 11 PM, 3 AM, during lunch, on Saturdays. Every conversation gets logged so your team follows up during business hours with complete context.

How long does setup take?

You're live and answering leads within 10-14 days. We handle the platform, the training, and the integration with your forms. You approve it, and we flip the switch.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

They're told upfront 'I'm an AI assistant here to help qualify your project.' Transparency builds trust. Most appreciate getting an instant answer instead of waiting for a callback.
