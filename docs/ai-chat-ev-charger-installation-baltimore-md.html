FAQ

AI Chat Agent Questions for EV Charger Installation Companies

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We interview your team, listen to your actual calls, and write scripts that match your voice—local, direct, and focused on what matters to Baltimore customers. No corporate bot feel.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It transfers the conversation to a human teammate or logs the question so you can review it. Nothing gets dropped. The customer always gets a resolution path.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It replaces the voicemail. Your team still owns the relationship and the close. This just makes sure the customer isn't sitting in a queue or calling a competitor while waiting.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. Calls at midnight or 6 AM get answered the same way calls during business hours do. Your team decides what timezone to operate in and when humans join the conversation.

How long does setup take?

10 to 14 days from discovery call to live. We handle all the heavy lifting—you just tell us how you work.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

The system doesn't hide it. If a customer asks, we tell them straight. Most don't ask because they're focused on getting a quote, booking an appointment, and moving forward. Transparency builds trust faster than pretense.
