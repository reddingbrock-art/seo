Yes. We write it to match your tone and the way your team actually talks to customers. It's trained on your service menu, pricing, and neighborhoods you serve. A visitor will feel like they're talking to someone from your company.

It escalates. If a customer asks something outside the widget's scope, it triggers a notification to you and offers to send their details so you can follow up directly. Nothing falls through.

No. It handles the visitors who would never call anyway — and it qualifies them before they take up your time. Your team still talks to customers; this just means fewer wrong-fit inquiries clogging your calendar.

24/7. It's answering and qualifying leads whether it's 10 a.m. on a Tuesday or 10 p.m. on a Saturday. Your business is always responding.
