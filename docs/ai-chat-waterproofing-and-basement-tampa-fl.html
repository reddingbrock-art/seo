FAQ

AI Chat Widget Questions for Waterproofing & Basement Companies

Will the chat widget actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on your language, your neighborhoods, your process, and how you actually talk to customers. It won't sound generic or corporate.

What happens when the chat can't answer something?

It tells the visitor you'll follow up and captures their contact info. The conversation lands in your inbox so you can pick it up where the chat left off.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It handles the questions that come in outside business hours and qualifies inbound traffic so your team isn't wasting time on bad leads. It works for you, not instead of you.

What hours does the chat widget work?

24/7. It answers questions at 2 AM on Saturday when your phone isn't ringing. That's when most homeowners are researching water damage anyway.

How long does setup take?

We deploy it in 10-14 days from your discovery call. That includes training the chat, testing it, and going live.

What if a customer wants to call instead of chat?

The chat can offer your phone number as an option. You're not forcing anyone into chat. You're just giving them a choice—and most will choose chat when it's available.
