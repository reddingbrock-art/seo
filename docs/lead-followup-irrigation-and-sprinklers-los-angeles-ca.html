FAQ

Lead Follow-Up System FAQs for Irrigation & Sprinklers Owners

How many follow-ups does it send?

Typically 3 to 5 touches over 7 days—initial message, reminder, re-engagement attempt. You control the exact sequence and timing.

Can I customize what it says?

Completely. You write the messages or we write them for you. Every word reflects your business and your process.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can opt out anytime, and they're removed immediately. No exceptions, no workarounds.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Within 15 minutes of form submission. Faster than your office staff can see the email.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in the system and get the full follow-up sequence. After that, they're logged as inactive and kept for seasonal re-engagement campaigns.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Yes. It integrates with your calendar, your website forms, and your team's workflow. It handles the initial follow-up so your team can focus on closing.
