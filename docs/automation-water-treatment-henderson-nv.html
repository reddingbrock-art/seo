FAQ

Automation Setup & Operations — Water Treatment FAQs

What exactly gets automated?

Inbound calls and form submissions are answered and qualified instantly. Appointment scheduling, reschedule requests, and follow-ups all run without manual entry. We automate the repetitive questions and data capture — not the actual service calls.

Do I need technical skills to run this?

No. You don't manage the system — it runs in the background. Your team receives qualified leads ready to schedule, and they focus on service delivery. We handle monitoring and adjustments.

What if my team resists new tools?

Your team won't see a new tool — they'll see fewer interruptions and more organized leads. We set it up so it integrates with your existing dispatch software. No new login, no learning curve, just fewer ringing phones.

How is this different from buying software myself?

You'd buy software, configure it, integrate it, train your team, troubleshoot when it breaks, and optimize as you go. We do all of that. You get results in 10–14 days instead of months of trial and error.

What does the setup process look like?

We have a discovery call, build the system to match your job types and service areas, connect it to your existing tools, and launch it live. Most of the work happens on our end — you just approve the flow and we turn it on.

What happens if something breaks after launch?

We monitor the system continuously and handle any fixes or adjustments. If a lead isn't qualifying correctly or a booking issue pops up, we find it and fix it before it costs you a job.
