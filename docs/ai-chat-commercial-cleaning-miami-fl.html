FAQ

AI Chat Agent FAQ for Commercial Cleaning Businesses

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We record your voice or write scripts that match your tone—professional, friendly, whatever fits your brand. It doesn't sound robotic or corporate unless you want it to.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It flags the conversation for your team and either transfers the call to you or lets the customer leave a message with all the context attached. You get the full picture, not a blank voicemail.

Does this replace my receptionist?

Not unless you want it to. Most owners use it to handle volume during peak hours or when the team's on jobs. If you have a dedicated person answering phones, the AI handles overflow and after-hours. You decide how much work it takes on.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. Calls and web forms come in anytime—nights, weekends, holidays. The AI never sleeps. You can set it to take messages after hours or route calls to your cell if you prefer.

How long does setup take?

10-14 days from discovery call to go live. We handle the integration with your calendar, phone line, and website. You don't install anything or learn new software.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

They can ask for a person and get transferred immediately. Most don't care as long as they got answered fast and their information was recorded accurately. Speed and clarity win here—not the illusion of human contact.
