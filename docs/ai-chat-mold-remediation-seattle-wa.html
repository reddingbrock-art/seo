FAQ

AI Chat Agent FAQs for Mold Remediation Companies

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on your exact language, how you describe your process, and what your tone is on the phone. A homeowner calling won't know they're talking to AI—they'll think it's your office.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It tells the customer it will have a team member call them back with that answer, captures their contact info, and routes it to you. Nothing falls through the cracks.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. If you have a receptionist, this frees them up from call-answering so they can focus on follow-ups, scheduling, and customer service. If you don't, it covers the gap so leads never hit voicemail.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. Calls come in at midnight or 6 a.m.? The AI picks up. You can set specific hours if you prefer, but most mold teams keep it live all the time during peak season.

How long does setup take?

Discovery, training, and configuration take 10-14 days. You'll review everything before it goes live, and we handle all the technical work. No coding required on your end.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

Customers appreciate that they got an immediate response instead of voicemail. If they ask, the AI is transparent. Most don't ask because the conversation feels natural and they've already booked their appointment.
