Common Questions About Google Review Automation for Appliance Repair

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests only to customers who actually completed a job with you. They choose to leave a review or skip it. Google's system validates every review.

What if a customer is unhappy?

They receive the same request as anyone else. Most unhappy customers won't leave a review. Those who do give you a chance to respond publicly and show how you handle issues.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google reviews primarily. That's where homeowners search for appliance repair in San Antonio and where reviews matter most for your search visibility.

How does the timing work?

The request goes out automatically when you mark a job complete. The customer gets it that same day or the next morning—while they still remember the work.

What if I already have a review process?

We replace it with one that actually runs on its own. If you're already asking for reviews manually or via text, this eliminates that work entirely.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

It depends on your job volume. Most shops see significantly more reviews within 60 days because the requests go out consistently and automatically—no more asking twice then forgetting.
