FAQ

AI Chat for Fencing Companies: Your Questions Answered

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We record your voice or copy your messaging, then build the AI to match your tone. It sounds like your team answering the phone, not a robot reading a script.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It flags the message as complex and sends it to you immediately with context. You reply once, and the AI learns. Over time, it handles more without escalation.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It replaces the worst part of the job—fielding calls at 2 AM or taking form submissions and booking calendar invites. Your team stops doing admin and focuses on estimates and installations.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. Inquiries come in at midnight, 6 AM, or Sunday. The AI responds instantly. You check your calendar in the morning and see who booked while you were sleeping.

How long does setup take?

10–14 days from discovery call to go-live. We handle everything: building the agent, integrating it with your website and calendar, and testing real conversations.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

Most don't ask. The AI qualifies and books seamlessly. If they do ask, it says so and offers to transfer to your team immediately. Transparency builds trust.
