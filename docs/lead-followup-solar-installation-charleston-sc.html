FAQ

Lead Follow-Up System FAQs for Solar Installation Companies

How many follow-ups does it send?

Usually 3-5 touchpoints over 5-7 days, depending on response. If a prospect engages early, the sequence stops escalating. We customize this for your process during setup.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. You control the message. We draft templates based on what actually works for solar installers, but every word is yours to modify before it goes live.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can opt out at any time, and the system respects that immediately. You stay compliant, and you don't burn bridges with someone who might come back in 6 months.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Within 3-5 minutes of form submission or missed call. The system doesn't sleep, so it's moving even if your whole crew is on-site.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in your system and show up on your dashboard. You can decide whether to follow up manually or let them age. Nothing disappears—you're always aware of who's in the pipeline.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. It fills the gap while your team's busy. Once a lead's engaged and an appointment's scheduled, your sales process takes over from there.
