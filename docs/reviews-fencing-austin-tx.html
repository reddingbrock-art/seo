FAQ

Common Questions About Review Automation for Fencing Companies

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests only to customers you've actually completed work for. The reviews are real—they come from real people who hired you. We just make sure you ask.

What if a customer is unhappy?

We send review requests after completion, when the work is done. If there's a problem, address it first. The customer's opinion of the finished fence is what matters. An unhappy customer won't leave a positive review regardless—and they shouldn't.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google, Facebook, and any other platform your customers use. We send review requests to wherever your best customers actually spend time looking for recommendations.

How does the timing work?

You mark a job complete in your system. The request goes out immediately or at the time window you choose. Most contractors see the best response when the request lands the next morning or the day after completion.

What if I already have a review process?

Tell us what it is. If your current process is working, we don't need to reinvent it. If it's not—if your team keeps forgetting, if your response rate is low, or if you're not asking consistently—we can replace it with one that actually runs without you.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Depends on your volume and how many jobs you're currently asking for reviews on. If you're not asking at all, most contractors see 3–5 new reviews per month right away. If you're asking inconsistently, automation typically doubles your monthly count within 60 days.
