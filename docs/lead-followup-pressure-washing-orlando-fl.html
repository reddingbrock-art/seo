FAQ

Questions About Lead Follow-Up Automation for Pressure Washing

How many follow-ups does it send?

That's up to you. We typically recommend a three-touch sequence—usually within the first 24 hours—but you decide how many times to reach out and on what timeline.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. We write your follow-up messages to sound like your business. You review and approve everything before it goes live.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can unsubscribe from any message, and they're automatically removed from future follow-ups. You stay compliant.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Within minutes of the lead coming in. That's the whole point—speed matters when you're competing with other shops for the same lead.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in your system and get your follow-up sequence. After the sequence runs, you can pull a report of who's still interested versus who's gone cold.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. If you're already using a scheduling system or taking calls a certain way, the automation plugs in around it. It doesn't replace your crew—it gets more leads to them.
