FAQ

Review Automation for EV Charger Installers: Common Questions

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests only to customers tied to your completed jobs. The reviews that come back are from real people who actually installed with you.

What if a customer is unhappy?

They're not forced to review. We send the request—they choose whether to respond. The goal is to make it easy for satisfied customers to share feedback, not to solicit complaints.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google primarily. That's where your Miami prospects are looking first. Other review sites can be addressed separately—let's talk about your priorities during the discovery call.

How does the timing work?

Requests go out within hours of job completion. You're hitting customers while the work is fresh and satisfaction is high. No delays, no manual delays.

What if I already have a review process?

We can replace it, or we can build on top of what's working. If you're already getting some reviews, we'll show you where the gaps are and how to fill them without doubling your effort.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

It depends on your current volume and completion rate. Most owners see significantly more reviews within 90 days because the ask stops falling through. We'll give you honest numbers during setup based on your actual job flow.
