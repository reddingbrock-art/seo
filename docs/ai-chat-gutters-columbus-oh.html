FAQ

AI Chat for Gutters: Common Questions from Columbus Owners

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on your actual voice, terminology, service offerings, and how you talk to customers. It doesn't sound robotic or generic—it sounds like someone who works for you.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It notes the question, collects the customer's information, and flags it for you to follow up on. Nothing gets lost or left hanging—it just escalates intelligently.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It replaces the phone ringing unanswered and the admin work of logging basic lead info. If you have a receptionist, the AI handles overflow and after-hours calls so they can focus on more complex tasks.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. Calls come in at 11 PM, weekend mornings, or during your crew's lunch—the AI's there. You can set business hours if you only want to book estimates during certain windows.

How long does setup take?

Discovery to live deployment is 10 to 14 days. Most of that is training and refinement on your end. Once we integrate it with your phone and booking system, it's running.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

They'll figure it out eventually, but by then they've already booked an estimate and left their details. The transparency actually builds trust—you're being efficient, not deceptive. Most customers don't care as long as they get a response and a scheduled appointment.
