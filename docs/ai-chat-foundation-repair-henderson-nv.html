FAQ

AI Chat Widget for Foundation Repair: Common Questions

Will the chat widget actually sound like my business?

Yes. We write the personality into it based on what you tell us about how you talk to customers. It's your voice, not generic corporate bot speak.

What happens when the chat can't answer something?

It collects their question, contact info, and reason for asking. You get the conversation in your inbox and can follow up directly—or we flag it so you can improve the script next time.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It replaces the missed calls and unanswered inquiries. It captures leads 24/7 while your team handles estimates, repairs, and customers. Your receptionist can focus on callbacks and coordination.

What hours does the chat widget work?

Around the clock. It answers foundation repair questions at 2 AM the same way it does at 2 PM. You decide if you want it to mention your office hours or adjust messaging for nights/weekends.

How long does setup take?

10–14 days from your discovery call to live on your site. No code changes needed on your end. We handle the install.

What if a customer wants to call instead of chat?

Your phone number is still there. Chat is another way to reach you. Some people will call, some will chat. Both lead to the same inbox and your follow-up process.
