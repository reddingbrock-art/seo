FAQ

Questions About AI Chat for Septic & Drain Businesses

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We feed it your company name, service area, pricing, typical job flow, and your tone. When a customer talks to it, they're getting your voice—not a generic bot voice.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

If the job is outside your service area, too specialized, or a question it hasn't been trained on, it gathers the customer's details and flags it for you to follow up. Nothing falls through the cracks.

Does this replace my receptionist?

It replaces the parts of your receptionist's job that waste time—answering the same questions, confirming availability, scheduling. It frees them up for follow-ups, customer service, and work that needs a human touch.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. Calls come in on nights, weekends, holidays. Your AI answers them all. Customers get instant service. Jobs get booked when you sleep.

How long does setup take?

10-14 days from the time we kick off. You'll have the discovery call, we'll build and integrate, and you'll go live with a fully trained agent.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

Most don't realize it. But if they ask or mention it, the AI tells them the truth—it's there to get them booked fast so a human can help them with the details. Most customers don't care as long as the appointment is confirmed.
