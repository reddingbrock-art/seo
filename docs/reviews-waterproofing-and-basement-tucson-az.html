FAQ

Common Questions About Review Automation for Waterproofing Contractors

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We're sending review requests only to customers who actually completed work with you. The system just automates the ask—the reviews are real feedback from real jobs.

What if a customer is unhappy?

The request goes out regardless. That's your opportunity to catch dissatisfaction early and fix it before it lands on Google. Most unhappy customers won't leave a review if you reach out and solve the problem.

Which platforms does this work on?

Primarily Google. That's where homeowners search for waterproofing contractors in Tucson, and that's where your reviews matter most for local visibility.

How does the timing work?

The request triggers automatically when a job status changes to complete in your system. You set the timing—immediate, next day, or a few days out—whatever fits your follow-up rhythm.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll audit it and integrate with what's working. If you're already getting some reviews manually, this automation amplifies that and fills the gaps so nothing slips through.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

It depends on your job volume and customer response rates. Most waterproofing contractors see significantly more reviews within the first 60 days because they're asking at the right time, consistently, without fail.
