FAQ

Questions About Automation for EV Charger Installation Companies

What exactly gets automated?

Incoming calls get answered and logged. Leads get qualified based on what you tell us matters (service area, budget, timeline, etc.). Qualified prospects get scheduled into your calendar. Follow-ups happen automatically if someone doesn't pick up. Review requests go out after job completion.

Do I need technical skills to run this?

No. You don't touch it. We handle all the setup, monitoring, and optimization. You log in once a week to see your numbers and approve any scheduling questions. That's it.

What if my team resists new tools?

They won't see a new tool. They'll just see their calendar fuller and fewer voicemails. The system does the heavy lifting behind the scenes. Your team gets better leads delivered to them, not another app to learn.

How is this different from buying software myself?

Software sits on your shelf until someone learns it, configures it, fixes it, and babysits it. We own the whole thing—setup, training, ongoing support, adjustments. You get the outcome without the headaches.

What does the setup process look like?

We talk to you once about how you work now. We build the system in the background (7–10 days). Your team tests it. We go live. We monitor the first two weeks and tweak based on real results. Total involvement from you: under an hour.

What happens if something breaks after launch?

We support it. That's on us, not on you. If a call doesn't log, a lead doesn't schedule, or something feels off, you tell us and we fix it the same day.
