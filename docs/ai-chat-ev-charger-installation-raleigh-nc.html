FAQ

AI Chat Questions for EV Charger Installation Businesses

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We record a short conversation with you about your process and style, then train the agent to match your tone—friendly but professional, specific to EV installations. It uses your company name, your neighborhoods, your timeline language.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It tells the customer the truth—'That's a great question; let me have my team get you the exact answer'—and captures the question. You get a note so you can follow up. No made-up answers.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. If you have one, she can focus on follow-ups, scheduling confirmations, and customer service instead of just answering phones. If you don't, you save the cost of hiring one.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. It answers at midnight on a Sunday just like it does at 9 a.m. Tuesday. You control which times it books appointments versus just capturing info for you to call back.

How long does setup take?

10–14 days from your first call to full deployment. Most of that is us building and testing, not you filling out forms.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

They know, and they don't care. They got an answer instantly and an appointment booked. You can tell them upfront—'Our AI will answer your first question and book your install'—and they'll still prefer you to a competitor with no answer at all.
