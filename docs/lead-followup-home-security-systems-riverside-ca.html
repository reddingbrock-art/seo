FAQ

Lead Follow-Up Automation: Common Questions for Riverside Home Security Companies

How many follow-ups does it send?

Depends on your process. We typically set up an immediate first contact, then 2-3 follow-ups over 48-72 hours if there's no response. You control the sequence and timing.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. We write the messaging based on your brand voice and sales process. You approve everything before it goes live. You can also update messages anytime without technical help.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can opt out with a single reply, and the system respects that immediately. We handle compliance so you don't have to worry about it.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Within minutes of the lead coming in—typically 2-5 minutes. Faster than any dispatcher can manually respond.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They complete the sequence and then sit in your CRM tagged as 'no response.' You can reach back out manually later, or they stay in a nurture queue if you want.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. It doesn't replace your dispatcher or sales team—it handles the first contact so they can focus on closing conversations that are already warm.
