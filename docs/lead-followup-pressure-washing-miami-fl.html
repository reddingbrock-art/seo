FAQ

Lead Follow-Up Questions for Miami Pressure Washing Owners

How many follow-ups does it send?

Typically 2–3 over 5 days. First message goes out in 15 minutes. Second goes 2 days later if they don't reply. You control the sequence and timing.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. We write the initial messages based on your voice, and you can adjust them anytime. Every follow-up has your branding and your words.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can unsubscribe, and the system respects that immediately. You also see a flag so you know they opted out and can honor it if they call.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Within 15 minutes of form submission or lead arrival in your system. No delay, no waiting for someone to check their email.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in your CRM tagged as contacted. You can export the list or let the system send them a final check-in after 7 days. You're not losing them; you're just not chasing dead weight daily.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. It plugs into your current forms, calendar, and CRM. Your crew keeps doing what they're doing; the follow-ups just happen automatically in the background.
