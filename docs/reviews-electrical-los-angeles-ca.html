FAQ

Google Review Automation for Electrical Contractors: Common Questions

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. The system only requests reviews from customers you've actually completed electrical work for. Google's filters catch inauthentic activity anyway—fake reviews hurt your ranking more than they help it.

What if a customer is unhappy?

The system requests reviews from completed jobs. If there's a problem, it's usually caught during the job, not after. You handle it then. Most unhappy customers don't leave reviews at all—happy ones do, when asked.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google is the primary platform. That's where your prospects are searching for electricians in Los Angeles. We can also configure requests for Yelp or other local listings depending on your market.

How does the timing work?

The request goes out the day after the job is marked complete in your system. Early enough that the experience is fresh, late enough that the invoice and payment are processed.

What if I already have a review process?

We can work around whatever you're doing now, or replace it entirely. If your current process isn't generating reviews consistently, automation will multiply what you're already getting.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Most contractors see significantly more reviews because they're asking every customer instead of waiting for the rare one who thinks to do it unprompted. Your actual number depends on your job volume and customer base.
