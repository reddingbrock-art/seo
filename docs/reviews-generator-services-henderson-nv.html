FAQ

Review Automation Questions for Generator Services Owners

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests only to customers who actually received an invoice from you. Real jobs, real customers, real reviews. That's how it stays compliant and stays effective.

What if a customer is unhappy?

We send the request, but if they don't respond positively, that's on them. We never suppress negative reviews or manipulate the process. The system works because it's honest.

Which platforms does this work on?

We focus on Google primarily, since that's where Henderson customers are searching for generator services. Other platforms can be included depending on your setup.

How does the timing work?

The request goes out 24 hours after invoice. Customers have a week to respond. If they don't, the system doesn't nag—one ask, then done. Clean and professional.

What if I already have a review process?

We can integrate with what you're doing or replace it entirely. Either way, automation removes the manual labor. You decide how many requests per week feels right for your business.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Most shops see 3-5 new reviews per month within the first 60 days once the system's running. It depends on your job volume and customer quality. We track it and show you the numbers.
