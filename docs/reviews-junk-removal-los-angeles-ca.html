FAQ

Common Questions About Review Automation for Junk Removal Companies

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests only to customers who actually completed jobs with you. We don't create, filter, or suppress reviews—we just ask real customers at the right time.

What if a customer is unhappy?

That's on you to handle before the job ends. Our system requests reviews after completion—it doesn't screen or block unhappy customers. If you know there's a problem, fix it before the request goes out.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google is the primary focus—that's where junk removal customers search and where reviews move the needle most. We integrate with your existing systems to make it happen.

How does the timing work?

The request goes out the day your job is marked complete in your system. That's when customers are most engaged and most likely to leave feedback.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll look at what you're doing and why it's not delivering. Most manual processes die because the ask comes too late or gets forgotten. This one doesn't.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Depends on your job volume and current request rate. If you're doing five jobs a week and asking for reviews manually, you're probably getting one or two. Automation typically surfaces significantly more because the timing is consistent and never missed.
