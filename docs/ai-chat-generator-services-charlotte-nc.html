FAQ

Questions About AI Chat for Generator Services in Charlotte

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on your language, your service offerings, your neighborhoods, and how your team actually talks to customers. It answers like someone who knows your business.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It tells the customer the truth and collects their info so your team can follow up. It never makes up an answer or pretends to know something it doesn't.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. If you have one, they move from answering phones to managing the qualified leads the AI books and handling anything that needs a human touch. If you don't have one, the AI does the intake so your team can focus on the field.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. Calls come in at midnight on Sunday? It answers. You set your available appointment windows, and customers can only book during times you actually work.

How long does setup take?

Discovery to live takes 10–14 days. Most of that is us building and testing. Your time investment is minimal—mainly one call upfront and reviewing the finished agent.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

Most don't care as long as they get their answer and can book an appointment. You're not trying to trick anyone—you're offering a faster way to get help. If they ask to talk to a person, the AI connects them to your team or notes their preference for a callback.
