FAQ

Lead Follow-Up Questions for Columbia Electrical Companies

How many follow-ups does it send?

Typically 3–5 touches over 7–10 days depending on how you set it up. Phone first, then text, then email. You control the sequence and timing.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. We build it to sound like your business. You write the messages or we help you refine them so they match your tone and your offer.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can opt out anytime. The system respects that immediately. No arguing, no extra steps—it stops.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Within minutes of intake. Most of the time it's under 5 minutes. That's the whole point—you're calling while they're still thinking about you.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in the sequence until it's done. After that, they drop off unless you pull them back manually. You see a list of all non-responders so you can decide if a re-engagement campaign makes sense.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Yes. It fits into whatever you're already doing—whether you use a scheduler, a CRM, or pen and paper. We integrate with the tools you have.
