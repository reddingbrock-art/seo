FAQ

Review Automation Questions for Appliance Repair Owners

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We're asking real customers who actually completed a job with you. Google can verify that. Fake reviews violate their terms and destroy credibility—we're doing the opposite.

What if a customer is unhappy?

They don't have to leave a review. The request is an ask, not a requirement. But if someone's genuinely dissatisfied, it's better you know about it before they post a complaint on their own timeline. Most shops find out about real problems faster this way.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google Business Profile is where most customers search and where appliance repair shops win or lose jobs. That's where we focus the automation. Other review sites work differently and require separate strategies.

How does the timing work?

The request goes out a few hours after you mark the job complete in your system. That's when customer satisfaction is highest and their willingness to take two minutes is strongest.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll work with what you're doing and fill in the gaps—usually the jobs you're forgetting to ask about, or customers you're reaching too late. No need to scrap what's working.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Most shops are asking for reviews on 20-40% of jobs today, if at all. Automation captures closer to 60-70% of your job volume, which means significantly more reviews. The exact number depends on your current volume and how many jobs you're already asking about.
