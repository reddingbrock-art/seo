FAQ

Automation Questions for Seattle Solar Installation Owners

What exactly gets automated?

Lead capture from calls, texts, web forms, and landing pages. Follow-up routing to your team. Review requests sent after job completion. Appointment reminders and callback scheduling. No busy work, no manual data entry.

Do I need technical skills to run this?

No. We build it, deploy it, and train your team on how to use it. You log in, see your leads, and work them. That's it.

What if my team resists new tools?

Most teams resist more work. This removes work—it stops forcing your dispatcher to manually track leads and your salespeople to remember follow-ups. Once they see leads organized and waiting, resistance drops fast.

How is this different from buying software myself?

You could buy a platform and spend weeks configuring it, integrating your calendar, setting up automations, and troubleshooting. We do that for you in 10–14 days. You get a working system, not a DIY project.

What does the setup process look like?

One discovery call with us. Then our team builds and connects everything—your lead sources, your calendar, your crew schedule, your messaging. We test it, train your team, then hand off the keys. No involvement on your end except the initial conversation.

What happens if something breaks after launch?

We provide support. If a routing rule misfires, a text doesn't send, or something shifts after an update, you reach out and we fix it. You're not managing it alone.
