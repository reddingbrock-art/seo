FAQ

Lead Follow-Up System FAQs for Minneapolis Tree Services

How many follow-ups does it send?

You decide. Most services use 3–5 touches over 48–72 hours: initial greeting with booking link, reminder 24 hours later, final attempt. You control the cadence and message.

Can I customize what it says?

Completely. You write the message or we help you write it. It should sound like you, address your most common questions, and make booking easy.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can opt out anytime with a simple reply. The system respects that immediately—no more messages go to that contact.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Minutes. If a form comes in at 10:47 AM, the first message is in their inbox by 10:49 AM.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in your system for your scheduler to call manually if you want. Or you can mark them cold and move on. You decide when to stop.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Yes. It's built to slot into what you're already doing—existing forms, schedulers, crew schedules. No rip-and-replace required.
