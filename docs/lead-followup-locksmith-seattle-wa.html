FAQ

Lead Follow-Up Questions for Seattle Locksmiths

How many follow-ups does it send?

That depends on your business. Most locksmiths use 3–5 touchpoints spread across text and voicemail over 24–48 hours. We configure the exact sequence for you based on your callback goals and lead source.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. Every message is written and approved by you before it goes live. It's your voice, your offer, your emergency language. We make sure it reflects how you actually talk to customers.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can opt out anytime. The system logs their preference and stops sending them anything. You stay compliant and they don't get annoyed.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Typically within 2–3 minutes of the lead coming in. Fast enough that you're still top-of-mind when they're actively looking for a locksmith.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in the system logs so you can see your actual follow-up rate and conversion numbers. Some sequences are designed to reach out again after a few days if there's no response.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Yes. It plugs into your current CRM, dispatch system, or whatever you're already using. It automates the timing piece so your team can focus on closing and executing jobs.
