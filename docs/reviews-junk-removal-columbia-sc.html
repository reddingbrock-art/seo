FAQ

Google Review Automation FAQs for Junk Removal Owners

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. The system asks real customers who completed a real job to leave their honest experience. You're not generating false reviews—you're just making sure the customers who had a great experience actually share it.

What if a customer is unhappy?

The request asks satisfied customers to review. If a job went sideways, you handle that directly before the request ever goes out. This keeps the reviews you collect genuine and protects your reputation.

Which platforms does this work on?

Requests link to Google, Yelp, Facebook, and any other review site you want to prioritize. You control where the requests point.

How does the timing work?

The request goes out automatically after a job is marked complete in your system. You set the delay (same day, next morning, etc.). Most operators find next-morning requests convert highest.

What if I already have a review process?

If you're manually sending requests or using email, we replace that with automation that's consistent and faster. If you have nothing, we build it from scratch. Either way, you eliminate the operational burden.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Most owners see 40-60% more reviews within 60 days because you're capturing requests that never happened before. The actual number depends on your job volume, but most 3-5 truck operations jump from 2-4 reviews per month to 8-12.
