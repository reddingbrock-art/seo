FAQ

AI Chat System FAQs for Sacramento Contractors

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We write the scripts using your language, your pricing, and the way your team actually talks to customers. It's trained on your service areas and common questions. When a prospect talks to it, they hear professionalism—not a bot script.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It flags the question and routes the customer directly to you with context. No dropped calls, no 'I don't know.' The customer's issue stays with you, and you have the information you need before you pick up.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It replaces the voicemail and the callback delay. Your team still closes jobs, schedules appointments, and builds relationships. The AI just captures the lead and qualifies them so your time is spent on revenue, not callbacks.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. Calls at 6 AM on Sunday or 10 PM on Thursday—they all get answered. You decide your callback hours. Leads wait in the queue, and you reach out during your actual business hours.

How long does setup take?

Discovery and configuration take about 7-10 days. You're live within two weeks. No lengthy contracts or waiting lists.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

Most don't notice—the system is that smooth. If they do ask, it says so. But the point isn't to trick anyone. It's to answer them fast and capture their info so your team can take over the conversation and close the deal.
