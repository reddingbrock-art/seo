FAQ

Common Questions About Google Review Automation for Flooring Companies

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. The system sends requests only to customers with completed jobs in your CRM. They choose whether to review. All reviews are genuine customer feedback.

What if a customer is unhappy?

Unhappy customers won't leave a positive review anyway. You're simply asking satisfied ones to share their experience. The system doesn't suppress or filter responses—it just automates the request.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google is the priority—that's where most customers look and where reviews impact your visibility most. We also configure requests for other platforms you specify.

How does the timing work?

Requests go out 24-48 hours after you mark a job complete. That's when customers are most likely to respond, but you can adjust the timing during setup.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll integrate with whatever you're doing now. If you're doing manual follow-ups, we replace that. If you're using something else, we sync with it or transition you smoothly.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Most flooring shops see significantly more reviews within 30 days because they're asking consistently instead of sporadically. The exact number depends on your job volume, but the system captures reviews you're already earning—you're just not losing them anymore.
