FAQ

Review Automation FAQs for Chicago General Contractors

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We're sending requests only to customers you've already completed work for. Real customers, real jobs, real reviews.

What if a customer is unhappy?

The request still goes out. How they respond is up to them. You'll see honest feedback—both positive and constructive. That's how reviews work, and it's actually better than suppressing complaints and ignoring problems.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google is the priority for most contractors. That's where customers are searching and comparing. We can discuss other platforms during your call.

How does the timing work?

You mark a job complete in your system. The automation detects that and sends the review request—same day or next day, depending on your preference. It's tied to your actual workflow, not a calendar.

What if I already have a review process?

We work with what you've got. If you're asking manually right now, this replaces that work and runs it consistently. If you have nothing in place, we build it from scratch.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Most contractors see a significant jump in monthly review volume once requests are automated and timed correctly. The exact number depends on your job volume and customer response rates. We'll discuss what realistic looks like for your operation during your call.
