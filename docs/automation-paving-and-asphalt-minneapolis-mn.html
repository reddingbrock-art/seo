FAQ

Common Questions About Automation for Paving & Asphalt Companies

What exactly gets automated?

Inbound calls are answered and screened. Text and web estimate requests get immediate responses with qualifying questions. Follow-up sequences run on their own timeline—texts at days 3 and 7, for example. Review requests go out when jobs are marked complete. Your team still controls pricing, scope decisions, and which leads get routed where.

Do I need technical skills to run this?

No. You'll log in, see a queue of leads with notes and scores, and mark them as scheduled, estimating, or closed. If you can use email, you can use this. We handle all the backend configuration and troubleshooting.

What if my team resists new tools?

Most resistance melts when crews see that their phone's not ringing as much and their lead queue is already pre-screened. Resistance usually comes from not understanding what's changing. We do a brief walk-through with your team so they see exactly what stops hitting their plate.

How is this different from buying software myself?

Buying software means you configure it, troubleshoot it, and maintain it. We set it up, train your team, monitor it daily, and fix anything that breaks. You own the results. We own the execution.

What does the setup process look like?

We schedule a discovery call, ask detailed questions about your current workflow, then build the system on our end over 3–5 business days. You review it, we adjust, and you go live. Total time to live is usually 10–14 days.

What happens if something breaks after launch?

You contact us and we fix it. We monitor the system continuously and catch most issues before they impact your leads. If a call doesn't route or a text doesn't send, we diagnose and resolve it same-day.
