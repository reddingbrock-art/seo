FAQ

Google Review Automation FAQs for Painting Companies

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests to actual customers after they've completed work with you. Real people, real jobs. Google catches fake reviews immediately—we're not interested in that game.

What if a customer is unhappy?

They get the request like everyone else. You handle the interaction honestly, just like you would if they called. Unhappy customers are rare when the work is solid, and the system doesn't suppress or filter anything—it just asks.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google is the primary play. That's where customers search for painters, and that's where your ranking matters most. We can integrate with other platforms depending on your setup.

How does the timing work?

Requests go out within hours of job completion. The customer gets the message while they're still happy about the work. No waiting a month, no forgotten follow-ups.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll work around it. If you're already doing manual requests, automation either replaces that or complements it. We'll build it to match your current workflow, not fight it.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Depends on your job volume and current review rate. Most painting contractors see significantly more reviews in the first 30 days because the requests are timely and consistent. You'll get a clear baseline in your discovery call.
