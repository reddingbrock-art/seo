FAQ

Lead Follow-Up Questions for Appliance Repair Owners

How many follow-ups does it send?

Typically three to five touches across text, email, and calls over 7-10 days, depending on how you want it set up. We customize the cadence to match your schedule and service area demand.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. We write it based on what you tell us about your business, and you approve every message before it goes live. You control the tone and what questions get asked.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can opt out with one click or reply, and they're removed immediately. Compliance is built in—we handle it automatically.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Usually within two to five minutes of the lead submitting their information. The system's running 24/7, so timing doesn't matter—they get contacted right away.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in your system and can be re-engaged later, or they drop off naturally after your sequence completes. You decide if you want to keep them in a separate list.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. It integrates with how you're already taking calls and booking jobs. We just automate the gap between lead arrival and your callback.
