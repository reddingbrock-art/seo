FAQ

Lead Follow-Up Questions for Waterproofing & Basement Companies

How many follow-ups does it send?

Typically 3 to 5 touchpoints over 7 days. We customize the sequence based on your process, so you're not over-messaging or under-following-up.

Can I customize what it says?

Completely. Every message is built from your words and your process. It sounds like your team, not a bot.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can unsubscribe or let you know, and they're removed from future sends. You stay compliant and respectful.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Within 3 to 5 minutes of the lead landing. Phone call, form, text—the system sees it and moves.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in your CRM marked as inactive. You can reach back out manually at any time, or run a separate campaign to re-engage them later.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Yes. It doesn't replace your team or your estimates. It just makes sure no lead goes cold while you're running your business.
