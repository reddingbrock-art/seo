FAQ

Lead Follow-Up System FAQs for Fencing Companies

How many follow-ups does it send?

Typically 3-5 touches over 7-10 days—a mix of texts, calls, and emails depending on how the lead came in. We customize the sequence for your business, so it matches your actual closing timeline.

Can I customize what it says?

Absolutely. We write the initial messaging based on your process, but you control every word. If you want it to sound casual or professional, mention your warranty or focus on timeline—that's entirely up to you.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They're removed from the follow-up sequence immediately. They can still be contacted by you directly, but the automated touches stop.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Within 2-5 minutes of the lead coming in through your website, phone, or text. That's the whole point—you're reaching them before they call anyone else.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They cycle through the full sequence, then stay in your system tagged as non-responsive. You can still reach out manually later, or run a re-engagement campaign during busy season.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Exactly. It handles the repetitive follow-up work so your team can focus on estimating and closing. It plugs into your phone system and calendar, not against it.
