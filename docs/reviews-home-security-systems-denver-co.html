FAQ

Review Automation FAQs for Home Security Companies in Denver

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send review requests to customers who actually completed jobs with you. Whether they leave a review or what they say is entirely up to them. Google's system catches fake reviews anyway—we're not touching that.

What if a customer is unhappy?

They don't have to leave a review. The request goes to them—they decide. If you want to catch problems before they hit Google, that's a separate conversation worth having with your team. We're just making sure happy customers actually leave feedback.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google reviews is the priority—that's where most customers search and trust most. We focus there because that's where your visibility actually matters.

How does the timing work?

The request goes out within a few hours of job completion. Customers get it while the work is fresh in their minds. That timing is what makes the difference between them remembering and forgetting.

What if I already have a review process?

If you're already getting reviews consistently, great. If you're not seeing results from what you're doing, this fills the gap. Either way, we set it up so you're not duplicating effort.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Most companies see significantly more reviews within the first 60 days because they're actually asking at the right moment. The exact number depends on your install volume and your current completion rate. A discovery call will give you a clearer picture.
