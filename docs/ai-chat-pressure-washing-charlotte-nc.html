FAQ

AI Chat for Pressure Washing: Common Questions

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We build it around your language, your service areas, your pricing structure, and the way you actually talk to customers. It's trained on your business, not a generic template.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It acknowledges the question, collects the customer's info, and flags it for you to follow up on. Nothing falls through the cracks.

Does this replace my receptionist?

It handles incoming inquiries, qualification, and scheduling. If you have a receptionist, they can focus on actual business work instead of answering the same questions all day. If you don't, you're no longer leaving money on the table.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. Nights, weekends, holidays. Your leads get answered whenever they contact you.

How long does setup take?

10–14 days from discovery call to live. Most of that is us building and testing on our end. Your time commitment is minimal.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

Most don't notice. But if they ask, the AI tells them straight—then immediately offers to book them with you or transfer them if they prefer. Transparency builds trust.
