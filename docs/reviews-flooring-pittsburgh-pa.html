FAQ

Common Questions About Review Automation for Flooring Companies

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests only to customers from your actual completed jobs. If they don't want to review, they ignore it. We're asking real customers—the ones who actually worked with you.

What if a customer is unhappy?

That's on you to handle directly, like always. The system doesn't filter out unhappy customers or hide negative reviews. It just sends the request. How you respond to feedback is your business.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google primarily. Google's where flooring customers search and compare, and that's where the ranking impact happens. We can discuss other platforms during your discovery call.

How does the timing work?

We set requests to go out within 24-48 hours of job completion, when the experience is still fresh. The exact timing can be customized to fit your schedule and customer communication preferences.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll look at what you're doing and either replace it, complement it, or enhance it. If you've got something working, we integrate around it instead of starting over.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

That depends on your job volume and customer responsiveness. Most flooring contractors see significantly more reviews within 60 days because we're asking systematically instead of sporadically. We'll give you real numbers based on your specific volume during your call.
