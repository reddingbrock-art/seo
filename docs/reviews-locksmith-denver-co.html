FAQ

Review Automation Questions for Denver Locksmiths

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests only to actual customers who completed a job. Google can detect fake reviews and will remove them. We're building your real reputation.

What if a customer is unhappy?

The request goes out after service is complete. If a customer had a bad experience, they won't post a review—or they'll post honestly. That's actually better than silence. We're not filtering or suppressing anything. Honest feedback helps you improve.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google is where most of your customers search and leave reviews. That's where we focus. It's also the review platform that moves the needle for local service businesses.

How does the timing work?

The request goes out the day after a job closes. That's when the experience is fresh and the customer is most likely to post. Timing matters.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll audit what you're doing now. If you're getting results, we work around it. Most shops we talk to are asking for reviews inconsistently or not at all—this fills that gap and scales it.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

It depends on your job volume and customer base. Most shops we work with see reviews increase significantly once the system is running consistently. Expect to see movement within the first 30 days.
