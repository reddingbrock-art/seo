FAQ

Common Questions About Pest Control Automation in Charleston

What exactly gets automated?

Incoming calls are answered with a greeting and caller info captured. Web forms and texts route to your dispatcher in real time. Follow-up calls go out on a schedule you set. After a job's marked complete, review requests send automatically. Everything else—pricing, scheduling details, closing the deal—your team handles with customers directly.

Do I need technical skills to run this?

No. Your dispatcher sees a dashboard that works like texting or email—click, assign, done. We handle all the backend wiring. If you can manage a calendar, you can manage this.

What if my team resists new tools?

Most teams resist because new tools make their job harder. This one makes it simpler—calls and texts show up in one place instead of scattered across voicemail, email, and text threads. We train your team during setup and stay available if questions come up.

How is this different from buying software myself?

You could buy a phone system, a form builder, a CRM, and hire someone to wire them together. Or you can call us, tell us what you need, and we do all of that. We build it, configure it, test it, and train your team. You don't manage vendors or deal with integration headaches.

What does the setup process look like?

You take a discovery call with us. We ask questions about your business, your lead sources, and your team's workflow. Then we configure the system on our end—usually 1–2 weeks. You get a walkthrough, your team gets trained, and we flip it live. Done.

What happens if something breaks after launch?

We support it. If a call isn't routing right or a form stops working, you reach out and we fix it. You're not paying a vendor support fee per incident—it's part of the service. We want it working as much as you do.
