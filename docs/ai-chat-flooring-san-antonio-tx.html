FAQ

AI Chat Agent Questions for San Antonio Flooring Businesses

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on your language, your service process, and your areas. When a lead calls about water damage on their kitchen tile, the AI responds like someone who knows your work—not like a robot reading a script.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It says so. If a customer asks something outside your scope, the AI admits it and offers to have your team follow up. No making things up, no frustration.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It replaces the part of their job that's answering routine calls and qualifying leads. They focus on relationship-building, scheduling, and keeping your pipeline moving.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. Calls at midnight, 6 a.m., during holidays—it answers every time. You decide if it books appointments around the clock or just during your service hours.

How long does setup take?

Discovery to live is 10–14 days. We need your phone number, service areas, pricing, and a conversation about how you like leads screened. That's it.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

They know it's automated, and they're fine with it—most actually prefer it to waiting on hold. If they want a human, the AI hands them off to you immediately.
