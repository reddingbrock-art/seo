FAQ

Review Automation for Home Security Installers in Nashville—Answered

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send review requests to customers who actually completed the job. They either write a real review or they don't. We're asking real customers to share their real experience.

What if a customer is unhappy?

Unhappy customers usually won't submit a review at all. Your quality control happens before install—this system just makes sure satisfied customers leave proof. If there's a problem with the job, your team handles that directly before the request goes out.

Which platforms does this work on?

Reviews show up where your customers are looking: Google, Facebook, and other major review sites. The system sends the request and the reviews populate automatically.

How does the timing work?

The request goes out automatically within 24 hours of job completion. That's the window when customers remember the experience best and are most likely to respond quickly.

What if I already have a review process?

We integrate with what you're already doing. If you're manually asking now, we automate it so it actually gets done every time. If you're not doing it at all, we build the entire flow.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Most companies see significantly more reviews once requests are sent at the right time. The difference is timing and consistency—the same customers who ignore a delayed request will respond to one sent within hours of the job.
