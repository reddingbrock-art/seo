FAQ

Lead Follow-Up Questions for Pressure Washing Companies in Atlanta

How many follow-ups does it send?

That depends on your preference. Most owners prefer 3–5 touches over 7–10 days. We'll set it up based on what works for your business.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. We write the messages, you review and approve them before anything goes live. You keep full control over your voice and what gets sent.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can reply STOP and we remove them. Your system respects their request immediately.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Within minutes of the lead coming in—while you're still on the job site or within the first few hours. That's the whole point.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in your sequence until it completes, then they're marked as no-response. You can decide whether to reach back out manually or let them go.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. We build around how you already work. Your crew keeps doing what they do—the system just handles follow-up timing and consistency.
