FAQ

Common Questions About Review Automation for Gutters

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We only send requests to customers you've actually worked for. The reviews that come back are real experiences from real homeowners.

What if a customer is unhappy?

Review requests go to all customers. If someone's upset, they won't leave a review—or they'll leave an honest negative one. That's their choice. We're not filtering or suppressing anyone.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google, and others depending on your setup. We focus on Google because that's where homeowners search for gutter companies and make hiring decisions.

How does the timing work?

Requests go out automatically after a job is marked complete in your system. We can adjust the timing—same day, next day, or a few days out—based on what works for your crew.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll integrate with what you're doing or replace it entirely. Either way, the goal is consistency and less manual work.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

It depends on your job volume and how many customers actually respond to requests. Most owners see a significant increase compared to what they were getting before—sometimes double or more within the first three months.
