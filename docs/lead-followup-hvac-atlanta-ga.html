FAQ

Lead Follow-Up System: FAQs for Atlanta HVAC Owners

How many follow-ups does it send?

Typically 3–5 touches across phone, text, and email over 7–10 days. We customize the sequence based on what's worked for your business.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. You write the messaging or we draft it for you. Every template goes through you for approval before it goes live.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can opt out with one click, and the system removes them from the sequence immediately. You stay compliant with no extra work.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Within 2–5 minutes of the lead coming in. The system doesn't wait for business hours or for someone to notice the form.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in your CRM with a note showing they were contacted. You can revisit them later or let them age out. Nothing gets deleted or hidden.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. It sits on top of what you're already doing. Your team still takes calls, schedules jobs, and closes deals—this just handles the wait-time touches.
