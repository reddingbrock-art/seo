FAQ

AI Chat Widget for Generator Services: Common Questions

Will the chat widget actually sound like my business?

Yes. We write and train it based on how you talk to customers—your tone, your service areas, your process. It sounds like your team responding, not a generic bot.

What happens when the chat can't answer something?

It tells the customer a real person will follow up, collects their info, and hands it to you in the dashboard. You call them back when you're ready. Conversation never dies.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It handles the 24/7 qualification and booking layer so your receptionist focuses on customer service, scheduling, and operations instead of answering "Do you service my area?" 50 times a day.

What hours does the chat widget work?

24/7. It's always on, even when you're not. Prospects can chat at midnight or 5 AM. During business hours, they get faster responses. After hours, they still get qualified and booked.

How long does setup take?

10-14 days from discovery call to live. We handle everything. You don't install code or configure backend systems. It just shows up on your site.

What if a customer wants to call instead of chat?

The widget asks for their phone number, confirms the best time to reach them, and logs it in your dashboard. You call them back. Some prefer voice—that's fine. You still capture their info before they disappear.
