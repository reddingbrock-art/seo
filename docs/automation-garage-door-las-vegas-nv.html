Do I need technical skills to run this?

No. If your team can use a phone and calendar, they can use this. We set it up so it works with how you already operate, not the other way around. Training takes one session.

What if my team resists new tools?

Most resistance dies after day two because it cuts work, not adds it. Your dispatcher gets fewer missed calls and less back-and-forth texting. Your techs see jobs clearly assigned and timed. They notice the difference immediately.

How is this different from buying software myself?

We don't sell you a platform and a manual. We build the entire system for you, connect your existing tools, configure it for garage door operations, and train your crew. You get a working operation on day one, not a login screen and a dashboard.

What does the setup process look like?

What happens if something breaks after launch?

You have direct support. If something stops working—a routing issue, a text not sending, a scheduling conflict—you reach us and we fix it. This is on us, not on your team to troubleshoot.
