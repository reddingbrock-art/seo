Yes. We customize it with your service areas, your language, your tone, and your actual FAQs. It answers like someone who works for you, not like a bot.

No. It replaces the part of your job where you're answering the same questions over and over. If you have a receptionist, they now handle callbacks and deeper conversations instead of basic inquiries.

24/7. Nights, weekends, holidays—doesn't matter. It never sleeps, so you don't have to worry about missing leads because it's after business hours.
