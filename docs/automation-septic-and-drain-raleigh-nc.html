FAQ: Septic & Drain Automation in Raleigh

What exactly gets automated?

Phone calls, web form submissions, and chat inquiries all funnel into a single intake process. The system asks qualifying questions, collects your standard job information (address, issue type, preferred date/time), and books appointments into your calendar with zero manual entry.

Do I need technical skills to run this?

No. We handle the setup, integration, and ongoing management. You log in, see your appointments, and dispatch from there. If something needs adjusting, you call us—we don't need your team to understand how it works.

What if my team resists new tools?

This makes their job easier, not harder. Your crew doesn't interact with it. Your dispatcher sees cleaner appointments. Your office person doesn't field three calls about the same job. Resistance usually fades when people see fewer messy phone handoffs.

How is this different from buying software myself?

You'd be managing platform configuration, hiring someone to monitor calls, handling integration with your calendar, and troubleshooting issues. We do all that. You own the relationship with your customers; we own the operational burden.

What does the setup process look like?

We conduct a 30-minute discovery call, build your system, test it with you, and go live within 10-14 days. You don't attend any technical meetings or do any configuration work. We handle the whole thing.

What happens if something breaks after launch?

You have a dedicated contact at Field-Built. If a call doesn't route, an appointment doesn't land in your calendar, or you need to adjust the intake script, you tell us. We fix it. There's no helpdesk ticket or waiting—we support you directly.
