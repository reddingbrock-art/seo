FAQ

Lead Follow-Up Questions for Junk Removal Owners

How many follow-ups does it send?

Typically 3–5 touches over 7–10 days depending on your preferences—call, text, email, repeat. We build it around what actually converts for junk removal, not generic sequences.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. We write your messaging so it sounds like your business, then you approve it before we launch. If you want to adjust tone or details later, you can.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

The system honors unsubscribe requests immediately and removes them from future sequences. We comply with all texting and email regulations so you stay in the clear.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Within minutes of the lead landing—phone call or text, depending on your settings. Not hours later when you finally see it in your inbox.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in your system and cycle through your sequence on schedule. Some convert on the fourth or fifth touch after they've had time to think or compare options.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. It integrates with the tools you're already using—your booking software, your CRM, your email. We layer it on top so nothing changes about how you operate, just what gets handled automatically.
