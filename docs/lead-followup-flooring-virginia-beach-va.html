FAQ

Lead Follow-Up Questions for Flooring Companies

How many follow-ups does it send?

You decide. Most flooring companies use 2–3 touches over 5–7 days: first text within minutes, follow-up email if no response, then a final text. You control the cadence.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. Every message is written by you or us, in your words. You approve it before it goes live and can change it anytime.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can opt out with one reply, and we honor it immediately. You stay compliant, and they're removed from that sequence.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Within minutes of the lead coming in—text, email, or both, depending on your setup. No waiting for someone to see it and remember to call.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in your system until you tell us otherwise. Some companies keep them in a monthly re-engagement sequence; others close them out after the final touch. Your call.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. It sits on top of what you're doing now. Leads still go to your CRM, still get called by your team—we just make sure they're contacted in the critical first hours.
