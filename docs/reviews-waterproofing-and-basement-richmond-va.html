FAQ

Review Automation FAQs for Waterproofing & Basement Contractors

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests only to customers whose jobs are actually complete. Google's algorithm catches fake reviews and penalizes accounts. We stay clean.

What if a customer is unhappy?

That's on you to handle before the request goes out. If a job's done right, ask for the review. If there's a punch list, wait until it's closed. Timing matters.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google primarily. That's where homeowners search for waterproofing contractors in Richmond. Other platforms exist, but Google is where leads come from.

How does the timing work?

The request sends the day after a job is marked complete in your system. We can adjust timing based on your crew's schedule and cleanup buffer.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll integrate with what you've got or replace it entirely. Most shops have scattered processes — we consolidate and automate the whole thing.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Most contractors see 60-70% of asked customers leave a review. You'll gain 20-30 reviews in the first six months if you're completing 35-40 jobs monthly. Real numbers, built on actual job volume.
