FAQ

Automation FAQs for Phoenix Fire & Water Restoration Companies

What exactly gets automated?

Lead capture from all sources (phone, web forms, chat), qualification questions, appointment scheduling, crew assignment, and follow-up reminders. Anything manual between a lead hitting your desk and a crew getting dispatched.

Do I need technical skills to run this?

No. Your dispatcher or admin logs into a dashboard and sees booked jobs, crew assignments, and customer details. We handle the backend. You just work the system like you'd work your current calendar.

What if my team resists new tools?

The system eliminates work for them, not creates it. Your admin stops answering the same intake questions 20 times a day. Your dispatcher stops hunting for missing info. Most teams see the time savings in week one.

How is this different from buying software myself?

We build it for you, integrate it with what you're already using, configure it to your exact workflow, and train your team. You're not shopping, learning, and troubleshooting alone. We do that work.

What does the setup process look like?

One discovery call, two weeks of configuration and testing while you review, then a short training session before go-live. You're not waiting months or dealing with months of implementation.

What happens if something breaks after launch?

You have direct support for the first 30 days after launch. We monitor the system, fix issues fast, and stay available while your team gets comfortable. If something needs adjusting, we adjust it.
