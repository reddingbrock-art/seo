Google Review Automation FAQs for Generator Services

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We're sending legitimate review requests to customers who actually completed work for you. Google's system will catch and remove fake reviews anyway. We're after real testimonials from real jobs.

What if a customer is unhappy?

That's their choice. We send the request; they decide whether to respond. Unhappy customers are going to tell people anyway—at least with a review system in place, you can respond publicly and manage the conversation.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google primarily, with the option to expand to other review sites. Google's where customers search for generator services in Atlanta, so that's where you'll see the most impact.

How does the timing work?

The system sends a review request within a few hours of job completion—the same day or the next morning. The sooner you ask, the more likely you get a response while the work is still fresh.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll replace it with something that actually runs on its own. If your crew's been asking manually, you'll be amazed at the difference consistency makes.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Most businesses see a significant increase in the first 60 days simply because they're asking consistently. The exact number depends on your job volume and completion rate. We'll track it for you.
