FAQ

Lead Follow-Up Questions for Savannah Tree Services

How many follow-ups does it send?

That's up to you. Most tree services use three touches: immediate contact, 24-hour follow-up, and a final attempt at 48 hours. You can adjust the sequence.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. We write the messages to match your voice and brand. You review and approve everything before it goes live.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can opt out anytime via text or email. The system removes them immediately, and we track that for compliance.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Immediately after they submit a form or call in, or on whatever schedule you set (e.g., within 15 minutes, next business day). You choose.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in the sequence through your final contact attempt, then move to a nurture list. You can review them anytime or we can re-engage them periodically.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. It sits on top of how you're already taking calls and forms. Your team still manages the actual sales conversation once a lead engages.
