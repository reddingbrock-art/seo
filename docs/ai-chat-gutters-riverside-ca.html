FAQ

AI Chat Agent for Gutters: Questions Answered

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We build it using your language, your pricing, your service areas, and your tone. When a customer talks to it, they're interacting with your business—not a generic chatbot.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It escalates directly to you. You get a notification with the customer's name, number, and the question. You take it from there. No customer gets stuck or ignored.

Does this replace my receptionist?

It replaces the part of your receptionist's job that's answering phones and scheduling. If you have a receptionist, they can focus on operations, follow-up, and customer service instead of being chained to the desk.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. Calls at 11 PM, texts at 6 AM, forms submitted on Sunday—all get answered immediately. You control when appointments actually get booked into your calendar.

How long does setup take?

10–14 days from kickoff to live. One discovery call, one review session from you, then we deploy. You're answering leads while other shops are still thinking about it.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

Honesty is built in. If they ask, the AI tells them they're talking to an automated system, then pivots to solving their problem or getting them to the right person. Most customers don't care as long as their issue gets handled fast.
