FAQ

Lead Follow-Up Questions for Spokane Fencing Contractors

How many follow-ups does it send?

Depends on your preference. Most fencing companies run 3-5 touches over 7-10 days. We customize the sequence to match your sales cycle.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. We write the initial templates based on your process, then you approve every message before it goes live. Your voice stays in there.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can unsubscribe at any time. The system respects their preference immediately.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Seconds. The moment a lead submits a form or request, the first touch goes out—before they've had time to call anyone else.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They cycle through the full sequence, then move to a periodic check-in if you want. No lead is forgotten, but aggressive pushes stop if they're not engaging.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. If you're already using a CRM or taking calls manually, the system works with it. It fills the gaps without replacing what's already working.
