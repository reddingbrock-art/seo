Common Questions About AI Chat for HVAC Businesses

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on your actual language, how you describe your services, your pricing structure, and your tone. Customers hear your business, not a generic bot.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It says so, collects the customer's info and question, and routes it to your team for callback. No false answers, no dropped leads.

Does this replace my receptionist?

It replaces the part of your receptionist's job that's fielding initial calls and qualifying leads. Your team stays focused on selling and scheduling, not call screening.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. It answers calls and form submissions every hour of every day, including nights and weekends when you're not staffed.

How long does setup take?

10-14 days from discovery call to live. We handle integration with your phone line and website. You don't need to change anything on your end.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

Some will. The AI's transparent about what it is when asked. Most customers don't care as long as they get answered, qualified, and scheduled fast—which they do.
