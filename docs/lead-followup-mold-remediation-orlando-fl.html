FAQ

Lead Follow-Up Automation Questions

How many follow-ups does it send?

It depends on your sequence, but typically 3-5 touches over 7-10 days if the lead doesn't respond. You control exactly how many and when.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. We write it with you so it sounds like your business, not generic. Every message can be tailored to your brand and process.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can opt out anytime. The system respects unsubscribe requests immediately—it's built in and compliant.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Within minutes of the lead coming in, depending on how you set it. Most owners run it within the first 15-30 minutes, before competitors even know they exist.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in the system for your full sequence. After that, you can archive them, or they drop off. You'll see a full history of every touch so you know where they landed.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. It runs parallel to whatever you're already doing. If your team wants to jump on a lead early, they can. The automation doesn't interfere—it just fills the gaps.
