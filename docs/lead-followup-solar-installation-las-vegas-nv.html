FAQ

Lead Follow-Up Questions for Solar Installation Companies

How many follow-ups does it send?

Depends on your process. Most solar companies use a 5-7 touch sequence over 10-14 days (text, email, call, text again). You decide how many and what happens at each step.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. We write your first sequence based on how you actually talk to leads. You can edit, swap templates, or change the timing whenever you want.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They're removed from the sequence immediately. Compliance is built in.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Within 2-5 minutes of the lead coming in. Faster than a human can manually respond.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They complete the full sequence, then stop. You can pull a list of all non-responsive leads anytime if you want to do a one-time outreach or move them to a different strategy.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. If you're already using a CRM or scheduling tool, we integrate with it. If you're using pen and paper, we plug in seamlessly. We work with what you've got.
