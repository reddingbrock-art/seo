FAQ

Google Review Automation Questions for Appliance Repair Owners

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests only to customers you actually serviced. Google's system filters out any anomalies. Authentic reviews from real jobs is all you get.

What if a customer is unhappy?

They still get the request. How they respond is up to them. Occasionally you'll get a one or two-star review, which is honest feedback. The system doesn't filter or suppress unhappy customers—they're part of your real picture.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google primarily. That's where 95% of repair searches happen in Chicago. We can also push requests to Yelp and Facebook if you want, but Google's your priority.

How does the timing work?

You define when. Most shops send requests the same evening a job completes, or the next morning. We automate the send based on your job completion timestamp in your scheduling system.

What if I already have a review process?

We take over. If you're doing manual requests, we'll eliminate that task entirely. If you're using something else, we can either replace it or run alongside it—you decide.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Depends on your job volume. Most shops see significantly more reviews within 60 days because they're asking at the right moment consistently. Some customers never click review links no matter what—but more will than before.
