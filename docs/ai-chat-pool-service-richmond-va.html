FAQ

AI Chat for Pool Service: Common Questions Answered

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on your actual service offerings, your pricing, your service area, and how you communicate with customers. It answers the same way you would—informed and specific to your operation.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It flags the conversation for you, captures the customer's information, and sends it to your team. You get the context, the customer's number, and their question—you handle the handoff from there.

Does this replace my receptionist?

It replaces the work of answering phones and booking routine appointments. If you have a receptionist, they focus on higher-value tasks. If you don't, you finally have one that doesn't cost you a salary.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. Calls at midnight, weekend texts, holiday inquiries—it all gets handled. You can set business hours if you prefer it to route certain times to voicemail, but most owners leave it on all the time.

How long does setup take?

Discovery and initial build takes about a week. You'll be live and fielding calls within 10–14 days of your first call with us.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

Most don't realize it. But if they do, the AI tells them the truth and offers to transfer them or take their information. Transparency builds trust, and most customers don't mind—they just wanted their problem solved and their appointment booked.
