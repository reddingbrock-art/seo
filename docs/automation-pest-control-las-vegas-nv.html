Automation FAQs for Pest Control Owners in Las Vegas

What exactly gets automated?

Lead capture and qualification (calls, texts, web forms), appointment scheduling directly into your calendar, after-hours customer response, reschedule management, and review request delivery post-job. Your team handles sales conversations and service delivery. The system handles everything that happens before and after.

Do I need technical skills to run this?

No. Your dispatcher and office manager see a normal calendar and a lead queue. They don't configure or manage code. We handle all backend setup and maintenance. If something needs to change—your pricing, your service categories, your hours—you tell us and we update it.

What if my team resists new tools?

Most resistance comes from fear of broken workflows. We don't replace your team's job—we remove the tedious parts. Your dispatcher spends less time on the phone and more time actually running the schedule. After the first week, they see how much easier their day got. Adoption stops being a problem.

How is this different from buying software myself?

You could buy 3–4 different tools and spend months connecting them, training your team, and troubleshooting integration problems. We do the integration for you. We monitor the system. We adjust it as your business changes. It's done-for-you, not DIY.

What does the setup process look like?

You have one kickoff call to walk us through your operation. We handle everything else behind the scenes—configuration, integration, testing. You'll see a test version, we incorporate your feedback, then we go live. Total timeline: 10–14 days from kickoff to running.

What happens if something breaks after launch?

You contact us. We have someone on it immediately. Most issues get resolved within hours. We monitor system performance continuously, so we catch problems before they affect your leads. You're not managing this alone.
