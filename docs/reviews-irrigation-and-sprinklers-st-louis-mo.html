FAQ

Google Review Automation for Irrigation Contractors: Common Questions

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests to real customers who actually did work with you. Whether they leave a review is entirely up to them. Your rating stays authentic.

What if a customer is unhappy?

Unhappy customers typically call or email you directly—they don't leave negative reviews unprompted. The automation doesn't filter or suppress anyone. It just asks everyone to share feedback after the job's done.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google reviews are what drive local search ranking in St. Louis. That's where your customers look first. We focus automation there because that's where it matters most for field service.

How does the timing work?

Requests go out automatically 24 hours after you mark a job complete. Customers are still in the frame of mind where they remember the work and your crew's professionalism. That timing converts significantly better than cold outreach weeks later.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll swap it out for the automated version so your team stops spending manual time on requests. You'll likely see more reviews because the timing and consistency improve dramatically.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Most irrigation contractors see 2-3x more reviews within the first 60 days because customers who would've said yes are actually asked. The exact number depends on your current job volume and completion consistency.
