FAQ

Google Review Automation Questions for Fire & Water Restoration Owners

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests only to customers tied to actual completed jobs in your system. Google tracks review source and flags patterns. We're asking real customers for honest feedback, not fabricating volume.

What if a customer is unhappy?

That's between you and them. The system sends requests to every completed job. If a customer leaves a negative review, that's real feedback worth understanding. We never filter or suppress unhappy customers—we just remove the friction of asking.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google primarily, since that's where restoration customers search. We can include other local review platforms depending on your goals. We'll discuss options on the discovery call.

How does the timing work?

Requests go out the day after job completion or on a schedule you specify. Most companies see best response when the request lands within 24-48 hours of finished work. We can adjust based on your job cycle and customer feedback.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll map what you're doing now and either integrate with it or replace it with automation that scales better. If you're manually tracking or reminding crews, automation will free up bandwidth and catch more customers.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Depends on your job volume and response rate, but most fire and water restoration companies see significantly more reviews when requests are automated versus manual. You're removing the only barrier: remembering to ask. The lift compounds over months as Google visibility improves.
