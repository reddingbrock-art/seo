FAQ

AI Chat for Roofing Companies in New York—Your Questions Answered

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on how you talk, what you charge, what you care about, and what your customers always ask. It sounds like someone from your crew answering the phone, not a bot.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It collects their information and tells them you'll follow up within a few hours. You get their details and a note about what it couldn't handle. You take it from there.

Does this replace my receptionist?

Not unless you want it to. It handles the 80% of calls that are qualification and booking. If you have a receptionist, they focus on actual customers instead of screening calls. If you don't, you suddenly have one.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. Calls at midnight, Sunday morning, Tuesday at 6 a.m.—it answers all of them. You decide when you want to be notified and when appointments can actually be scheduled.

How long does setup take?

Live in 10–14 days. We handle everything—no technical setup on your end, no learning a new platform, no downtime.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

Most don't. But if they ask, the AI tells them straight. A lot of customers don't care—they care that someone answered and got them scheduled. If they want to talk to you, the AI transfers them or takes a message.
