FAQ

AI Chat for Windows & Window Replacement Companies—Common Questions

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on your voice, terminology, and how you talk about your services. When a customer calls, they're talking to someone who sounds like your company—not robotic or generic.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It knows when to escalate. If a question falls outside its scope, it takes the customer's info and lets your team know so you can call them back. Nothing falls through the cracks.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It replaces the part of your receptionist's job that's answering the same questions over and over—availability, service areas, basic qualifying. Your team focuses on follow-up, building relationships, and closing jobs.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. Calls and messages get answered nights, weekends, and holidays. During your normal business hours, it works alongside your team. You decide when and how your team gets notified of inbound.

How long does setup take?

We go live in 10–14 days. Discovery and training happen fast because we've done this hundreds of times. You're capturing leads while your competitors are still figuring out their voicemail.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

Customers don't care if they got an instant answer and their information was captured. The AI is transparent about what it is if asked, and most conversations feel natural enough that it never comes up. What matters is you called them back, not how fast the intake happened.
