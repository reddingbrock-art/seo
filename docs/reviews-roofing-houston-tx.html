FAQ

Questions About Google Review Automation for Houston Roofing Companies

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send review requests to customers who actually did work with you. Google catches fake reviews quickly and penalizes the profile. We don't do that.

What if a customer is unhappy?

We send review requests after the job's done—customers who hired you and you completed the work. If someone's unhappy, that's real feedback. Responding professionally to honest reviews (even critical ones) builds trust more than pretending they don't exist.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google Business Profile, primarily. That's where homeowners search for roofers in Houston. Reviews on other platforms matter less for roofing because decision-makers start with Google.

How does the timing work?

Requests go out automatically the day your job closes. Customers have a few days to respond. You'll see reviews start landing within a week, with momentum building over 10-14 days once the system's live.

What if I already have a review process?

If it's manual or inconsistent, this replaces it and makes it bulletproof. If you're already getting reviews regularly, we can audit it and often find ways to get more without more work on your end.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Depends on your job volume and current ask rate. Most roofing companies we work with see a significant increase because they're asking systematically instead of sporadically. We'll map that out on your discovery call.
