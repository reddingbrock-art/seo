FAQ

Common Questions About Roofing Review Automation in Houston

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests only to customers who've actually completed a job with you. The reviews that come back are real, from real people. Google will catch and remove anything fake anyway.

What if a customer is unhappy?

The system sends a request after job completion. If the customer had a bad experience, they can ignore it or leave honest feedback. You can respond professionally to any negative review and turn it into a chance to make it right.

Which platforms does this work on?

We focus on Google Reviews, which is where homeowners search for roofers and make decisions. That's the one that drives your phone calls.

How does the timing work?

You mark the job complete in your system, and the review request goes out automatically shortly after. You set the timing threshold—same day, next day, or whenever makes sense for your operation.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll fold this into what you're doing. If you're manually asking for reviews now, the automation replaces that manual step. If you're not asking at all, it becomes your review engine.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Most roofing companies see a significant lift once they're actually asking at the right time. The exact number depends on how many jobs you complete monthly and your customer satisfaction baseline. A discovery call will give you a clearer picture.
