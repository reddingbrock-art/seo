FAQ

AI Chat Agent for Pool Service: Common Questions

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on how you talk to customers, your service terminology, your tone. It sounds like someone from your office, not a robot reading a script.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It collects the customer's info and sends you a note so you can follow up. It knows its limits and doesn't pretend to answer questions it shouldn't.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. If you have a receptionist, this handles overflow and after-hours calls. If you don't, this is your after-hours and during-service-call answering system.

What hours does the AI work?

All hours. It answers at 6 AM on a Saturday or midnight on a Tuesday. You decide when it books jobs or just collects info for your follow-up.

How long does setup take?

10-14 days from discovery call to live. Most of that is us doing the work. You spend a couple hours answering questions.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

It can transfer them to a person if they ask, or you can set it to disclose upfront. Most don't care as long as their problem gets solved and their job gets booked.
