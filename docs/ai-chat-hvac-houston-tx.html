Will the chat widget actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on your actual service descriptions, your policies, and how you talk to customers. It sounds like your team—professional but approachable, specific to HVAC work.

It tells the customer the truth and asks for their information so your team can follow up. No fumbling, no fake answers. Honesty builds trust.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It handles the visitors your receptionist never sees—people chatting at midnight, on weekends, or between calls. It frees your team to focus on actual service work and follow-ups.

24/7. It's always there, always responding. You control when those responses get routed to your schedule or held for your team to review.
