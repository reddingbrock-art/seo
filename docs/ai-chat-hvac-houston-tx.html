FAQ

AI Chat for HVAC: Answers to Your Questions

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on your service offerings, pricing, service area, and tone. It references your real truck count, response times, and guarantee. Customers can't tell it's AI—they think it's your dispatcher.

What happens when the AI can't answer?

It escalates to your team with full context. The customer's question, contact info, and history are right there—no repeating the story. Your tech answers a fully-qualified inquiry, not a cold handoff.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It replaces the repetitive intake work—call screening, basic questions, appointment slots. Your receptionist becomes a closer, not a gate. If you have one, she handles callbacks that matter. If you don't, you keep your sanity.

What hours does the AI chat work?

24/7. It's live on your website and text line at 2 AM or 2 PM. You set availability by day/time—so it only books appointments your team can actually service.
