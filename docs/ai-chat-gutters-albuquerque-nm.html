Common Questions About AI Chat for Gutters in Albuquerque

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We interview you, listen to how you talk to customers, and train it on your pricing, neighborhoods, and style. When someone calls, they're talking to something that sounds like your office, not a robot.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It tells the customer the truth—'Let me have the owner call you back'—and flags the conversation for you. You handle the complex stuff; the AI handles the routine stuff.

Does this replace my receptionist?

It replaces the phone-sitting part. If you have someone answering phones 9-to-5, they can focus on scheduling, follow-up, and operations. If you don't have a receptionist, you just gained one that never takes a day off.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. Calls at 11 p.m. on Sunday during storm season? It answers. Texts at 6 a.m. on Saturday? It responds. You choose what it does during off-hours—book appointments or just capture contact info for you to follow up.

How long does setup take?

10-14 days from your discovery call to live. We handle the configuration. You don't need to learn anything or install software on your end.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

It's honest about it if asked. Most customers don't care—they got their question answered and booked an appointment. If it matters to you, we can adjust the script. Either way, you're answering faster than your competition.
