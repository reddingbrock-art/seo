FAQ

HVAC Automation Questions Jacksonville Owners Are Asking

What exactly gets automated?

Lead capture from your website and forms, initial qualification based on what they're asking for, calendar booking, appointment reminders, and follow-ups to people who inquired but didn't book. Review requests go out after job completion so you're building your reputation without asking your team to remember.

Do I need technical skills to run this?

No. Your team checks emails and answers texts the same way they do now. The system handles the timing and the routing. You literally log in and see what's happening—that's it.

What if my team resists new tools?

Most HVAC teams resist what makes their job harder. This one makes it easier because it removes the clutter from their inbox and puts qualified leads straight in their lap. Your dispatcher spends less time on the phone, not more.

How is this different from buying software myself?

You'd buy software, figure out how to configure it, train your team, and troubleshoot when something's not working right. We do all that for you and keep it running. You get the results without the headache.

What does the setup process look like?

We connect to your website, your calendar, your email, and any other tools you're using. We test it with your team. Then we turn it on. Most shops are fully live within two weeks.

What happens if something breaks after launch?

We monitor it and fix it. We're not handing it off and leaving you to figure it out. If an integration stops working or a process needs tweaking, you reach out and we handle it.
