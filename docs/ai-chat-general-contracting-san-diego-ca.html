FAQ

AI Chat Agent FAQs for General Contractors

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We record a brief call with you, train the AI on your vocabulary and tone, and it responds using your business name, your service areas, and the way you actually talk to customers. It's not a generic chatbot voice.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It flags the conversation for your team and sends you a note so you can follow up. Nothing falls through the cracks—you still own the relationship.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It replaces the problem of missed calls when your receptionist isn't there. If you have one, the AI handles overflow and after-hours calls so they can focus on office work that actually needs a human.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. Calls come in at midnight, 6 AM, noon, or 9 PM—the AI picks up every time. You control what hours it books appointments for (so you're not getting commitments at 2 AM if you don't want them).

How long does setup take?

10-14 days from your discovery call to going live. We handle the heavy lifting. You don't need to learn any software.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

They get transferred to a human or an appointment gets booked anyway. Most customers don't care if it's AI or a person—they care that someone answered and got the job done. You can also set it to disclose upfront if you prefer.
