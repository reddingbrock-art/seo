FAQ

AI Chat for Garage Door Service: Common Questions

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on your actual language, your service descriptions, and your tone. It'll reference your specific service areas, your emergency protocols, and the way you actually talk to customers. It won't sound like a generic bot.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It flags the conversation and routes it to you with full context. You step in, answer the question, and we feed that back into the system so it learns. Over time, it handles more on its own.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It replaces the need for a receptionist to work nights, weekends, and lunch hours. Your staff focuses on customers with complex needs or scheduling conflicts. The AI handles volume and availability questions.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. It answers texts at midnight, 6 a.m., and during your busiest afternoons. You control what it can book immediately versus what gets held for your approval.

How long does setup take?

Full deployment in 10-14 days from discovery call to live. We handle the configuration. You don't need to learn any platform or hire a tech person.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

Most don't. But if they ask, the AI tells the truth and offers to transfer to your team. Transparency builds trust. Customers care more about getting their garage door fixed than whether a human or AI booked the appointment.
