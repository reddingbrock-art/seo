FAQ

Common Questions About Lead Follow-Up for Water Treatment in Houston

How many follow-ups does it send?

Depends on your sequence. Most water treatment companies run 3–5 touches over 7–10 days. We customize it so you're not over-contacting but you're not disappearing either.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. We write the initial sequence with you. You approve every message before it goes live. After that, you can edit at any time.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can unsubscribe from any message. The system removes them from future sends automatically and logs the preference.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Within 2–5 minutes of lead capture. Depends on your settings, but most companies choose immediate or within 15 minutes.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in the system and receive the full sequence you've set up. After the sequence ends, they can be re-engaged later or tagged for a different campaign.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. It feeds into your CRM or existing platform and sits alongside whatever intake system you've got now. Your team still qualifies and closes—the system just makes sure nothing dies in the first hour.
