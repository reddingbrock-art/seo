FAQ

Common Questions About Review Automation for Solar Installers

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests only to actual customers at the moment of job completion. The reviews you receive come from real installations you've completed. Google detects and removes fake reviews anyway—this system is the opposite.

What if a customer is unhappy?

We only send the request after completion when satisfaction is typically highest. If someone leaves a negative review, that's real feedback. Most installers find that honest reviews actually build more trust than a profile with only 5-star reviews.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google is the primary platform where your solar customers search and decide. That's where we focus the automation. If you want reviews on other platforms, we'll discuss that during your discovery call.

How does the timing work?

The request goes out the day after job completion—when the customer's system is live and they're most satisfied. The timing is automatic based on your job completion date in your system.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll look at what you're doing now during the discovery call. If you're manually sending requests, this replaces that work. If you're not asking for reviews systematically, this fills that gap. Either way, we build around your current workflow.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Most installers see a significant lift compared to whatever they're getting now, especially in the first 2-3 months when the system is new. The actual number depends on your job volume and customer base—that's why we map it out during your call.
