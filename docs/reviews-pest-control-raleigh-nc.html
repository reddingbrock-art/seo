FAQ

Common Questions About Review Automation for Pest Control in Raleigh

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. Requests go only to customers whose jobs actually closed in your system. They're real people who hired you. Whether they respond and what they write is entirely up to them.

What if a customer is unhappy?

They can leave whatever review they want—good, bad, or skip it. We're sending the request, not filtering responses. If you have unhappy customers, you'll know about it. That's actually valuable intel.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google, Facebook, and other major local review platforms. Requests can direct customers to whichever platform matters most for your business.

How does the timing work?

Requests go out on the same day the job closes in your system, or on a schedule you prefer. Morning, afternoon, or next-day delivery—whatever fits your workflow.

What if I already have a review process?

We take over from there. If you're already sending manual texts or emails, this replaces that work entirely. No competing processes, just automation doing what you've been doing manually.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Most pest control businesses see significantly more reviews once requests are consistent and automated. The exact number depends on your job volume and current request rate—we'll show you the math on a demo call.
