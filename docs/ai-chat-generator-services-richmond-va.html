FAQ

AI Chat Agent FAQs for Generator Services in Richmond

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on how you talk to customers—your terminology for generators, your service areas, your tone. It uses your name and speaks like someone on your team, not like a bot.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It escalates to you or your team via text or email with the full conversation and customer details. You can jump in immediately if needed, or respond during business hours.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It replaces the voicemail and the callback queue. If you have a receptionist, they now have time to follow up on quotes, manage dispatch, or handle other work instead of saying 'one moment please' all day.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. Calls come in at night or on weekends—the AI answers them, captures the details, and your team handles the appointment the next business day. Emergency customers don't fall through cracks.

How long does setup take?

Discovery to live deployment is 10-14 days. We handle the technical side. You just confirm your service details and test it with a few calls before we flip the switch.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

Honesty works. The AI can acknowledge it naturally and explain that it's capturing their information so the right technician can call them back. Most customers appreciate the speed.
