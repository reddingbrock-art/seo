FAQ

Automation for Water Treatment Companies in Richmond — FAQs

What exactly gets automated?

Incoming calls are answered and routed through a system that asks about the job type, location, and urgency. Appointments get booked into your calendar, confirmations go out via text, and your team sees job details pre-populated in dispatch. Follow-ups and reminders happen automatically on a schedule you set.

Do I need technical skills to run this?

No. You don't touch code or configure servers. You log in, see your jobs and customer data, and manage your team like you always have. If something needs to change, you tell us and we update the system.

What if my team resists new tools?

Your team sees less work, not more. They're not answering repetitive calls or typing appointment details. They show up to jobs with everything already confirmed and ready. Buy-in usually comes fast because the system makes their day easier.

How is this different from buying software myself?

You'd have to pick the platform, integrate it with your existing systems, hire someone to configure it, train your team, and troubleshoot when things break. We do all of that. You own the relationship and the data. We just handle the build and the ongoing support.

What does the setup process look like?

One call with us to understand your operation, about two weeks of our build time while you keep working, then a soft launch where we monitor live calls and adjust as needed. You're not disrupting your schedule or pulling your team into meetings for weeks.

What happens if something breaks after launch?

You have a support line and a dedicated contact on our team. If a call doesn't route right or a calendar sync glitches, we diagnose and fix it the same day. You're not troubleshooting alone at 6 p.m. on a Friday.
