FAQ

Google Review Automation FAQs for Painting Companies

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests only to customers you've actually worked for. Google flags fake reviews and removes them—we don't risk your profile. Every review that comes in is real.

What if a customer is unhappy?

An unhappy customer won't leave a positive review. The system asks; they choose. If they had a bad experience, you'll find out from low ratings or no response, not from being forced to collect feedback you don't want.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google is the primary focus. That's where 95% of Riverside homeowners are checking reviews before they call. We concentrate there because that's what moves the needle.

How does the timing work?

The request goes out the day after you mark a job complete. Customers are still happy and the work is fresh in their mind. That timing dramatically increases the chance they'll actually leave a review.

What if I already have a review process?

Most painting contractors ask manually or rely on crew follow-up—both are inconsistent. Our system replaces the manual ask with automation, so nothing falls through the cracks. You'll still get the reviews you were supposed to get, plus the ones you've been missing.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Most contractors are capturing 10–20% of the reviews they're eligible for. Automation brings that closer to 50–70%. In a 15-job month, you might go from 2–3 reviews to 7–10. It depends on your service area and customer base, but the increase is significant.
