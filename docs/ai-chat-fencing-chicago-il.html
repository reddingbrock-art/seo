FAQ

AI Chat for Fencing Estimates: Questions Answered

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We build it around how you talk, what you care about, and how you qualify jobs. It doesn't sound robotic or like a generic answering service. Customers feel like they're talking to someone who knows fencing.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It's designed to capture the question and route it to you with full context. If a customer asks something outside the scope—like a warranty detail or financing question—the AI gets the info and flags it so you follow up with a real answer.

Does this replace my receptionist?

Not necessarily. If you have someone answering phones, this handles calls when they're busy or after hours. If you don't have dedicated office staff, it becomes your 24/7 front desk for lead capture.

What hours does the AI work?

All of them. It answers calls at 2 AM the same way it answers them at 10 AM. You decide if it should ask for a callback appointment during off-hours or just capture info and route it to you.

How long does setup take?

Discovery to live usually takes 10-14 days. One call with us, we build it, you review it once or twice, then we activate it. Your existing phone number doesn't change.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

We design it so the experience feels natural—they get answers, their info is captured, and they move toward booking an estimate. Some customers will figure it out. Most won't care if they got what they needed. And if they ask, the AI tells the truth and hands them to you if they want to talk to a person.
