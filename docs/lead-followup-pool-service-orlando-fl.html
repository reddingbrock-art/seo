FAQ

Lead Follow-Up Automation Questions for Pool Service Owners

How many follow-ups does it send?

We build a 7-day sequence. Typically it's 3–4 touchpoints spaced out so the lead stays warm but doesn't feel pestered. We customize this based on what works for your business.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. We write the messaging during setup so it matches your voice and your service area. If something needs tweaking after go-live, that's part of the process.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

The system respects opt-outs immediately. They're removed from future sends. You stay compliant and don't waste energy on people who aren't interested.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Instantly if it's a form submission. After-hours calls get a callback message within minutes of the call ending. You're never waiting for someone to manually send that first message.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They still get the full 7-day sequence because sometimes the timing or the message just wasn't right the first time. Some of your best customers respond on day 5 or 6.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. It feeds leads into your calendar, your dispatch, whatever you're using now. It's an extra layer that catches leads you'd otherwise lose—not a replacement for how you work.
