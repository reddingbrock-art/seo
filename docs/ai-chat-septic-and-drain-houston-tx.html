Will the chat widget actually sound like my business?

Yes. We interview your team, learn your terminology, your process, and your tone. The chat sounds like someone from your office, not a generic bot.

What happens when the chat can't answer something?

It gathers the customer's info and transfers the conversation to your team or books them for a callback. Nothing falls through the cracks.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It handles the initial qualification and booking. Your team still manages the customer relationship, dispatch, and the actual service.

What hours does the chat widget work?

24/7. It captures visitors and qualifies leads anytime they land on your site, including nights and weekends.

How long does setup take?

Live in 10-14 days from our discovery call. Most of that is us building and you reviewing. No long implementation.

What if a customer wants to call instead of chat?

They can. The chat offers chat as the default, but your phone number and call option are always visible. You control the flow.
