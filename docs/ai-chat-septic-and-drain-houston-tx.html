FAQ

AI Chat for Septic & Drain Dispatching — Questions Answered

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on your typical calls, how you talk about problems, your pricing structure, and your service areas. When a customer calls, they hear someone who knows septic systems in Houston, not a generic automated voice.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It transfers the call to you or a team member in real time. If it's not sure whether a job's in your service area, or if a customer's got an unusual situation, it knows to get a human on the line instead of guessing.

Does this replace my receptionist?

Not unless you want it to. Most owners use it to handle overflow, after-hours calls, and the jobs that come in while the team's buried. If you've got a receptionist, they focus on scheduling and follow-up instead of call intake.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. It's running nights, weekends, holidays, and during your crew's busiest days. A 2 AM emergency call in Spring gets answered and logged the same way a Tuesday noon callback does.

How long does setup take?

About 10-14 days from your initial call to full deployment. We spend a few days gathering your info, another few training the system, and then it goes live and starts taking calls. You're operational within two weeks.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

Most don't realize until you tell them—but if they ask, the AI's honest about it. Then it says, 'Let me get someone on the line who can help you further,' and transfers them. Transparency builds trust.
