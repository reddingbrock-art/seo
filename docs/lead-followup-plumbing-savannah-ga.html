FAQ

Lead Follow-Up System Questions for Plumbing Companies

How many follow-ups does it send?

Typically 3–5 touchpoints over 7–10 days, depending on how you want it configured. Each one is timed to catch the lead when they're most likely to respond.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. We build the messaging around your voice and your process so it sounds like you're sending it, not some platform.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can opt out at any time, and the system respects that immediately. No arguments, no spam—just professional respect.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Within minutes of the lead coming in. Whether it's a form submission or a missed call, you're reaching them before they've moved on to the next option.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in the system for a final attempt after a week or two, then get marked as non-responsive. You can still manually follow up with them later if you want.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Absolutely. It sits on top of whatever you're doing now—your dispatch system, your phone setup, your crew workflow. Nothing changes except leads stop disappearing.
