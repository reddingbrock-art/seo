No. You check your dashboard, your team answers calls and closes jobs. That's it. We handle all the backend setup and maintenance.

How is this different from buying software myself?

We don't just hand you software. We configure it for pool service, connect your phone system, set up your workflows, and make sure everything talks to each other. You get a system that works, not a tool that needs a learning curve.
