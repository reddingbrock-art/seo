FAQ

Questions About Automation for Home Security Companies in Miami

What exactly gets automated?

Phone answering, lead qualification (system type, timeline, location), appointment scheduling into your calendar, and follow-up touchpoints if someone books a call but doesn't show. Your team still owns the sales conversation and installation. We handle the part that happens before they hear from a human.

Do I need technical skills to run this?

No. You log in, check your calendar, and call booked appointments. That's it. We manage the backend. Your team uses it the same way you'd use any scheduling app.

What if my team resists new tools?

This isn't one more thing your team has to operate. It makes their job easier—they only call prospects who are already qualified and scheduled. Resistance drops fast when their calendar fills up and they're not babysitting the phone.

How is this different from buying software myself?

We don't hand you software. We build the intake system, integrate it with your calendar, train your team, and manage it from here. You get the results without the setup, hiring, or ongoing tuning. It's done-for-you, not do-it-yourself.

What does the setup process look like?

We schedule a discovery call to understand your operation. Then we design qualification questions specific to home security (new system vs. upgrade, monitoring, budget window). We integrate with your calendar, test, and launch within 10-14 days. You don't build anything.

What happens if something breaks after launch?

We monitor it 24/7. If a call doesn't get answered or your calendar doesn't sync, we fix it immediately. You have a dedicated contact who's familiar with your setup. This isn't software support—it's a service with a real team behind it.
