FAQ

Google Review Automation FAQ for Tree Service Owners in San Antonio

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests only to customers you've actually completed jobs for. Google reviews come from real people who worked with you. The system just makes sure you ask them.

What if a customer is unhappy?

We only send review requests after job completion. If someone's unhappy, that's a conversation you'd have separately. The system doesn't filter responses—unhappy customers can leave honest feedback just like happy ones can.

Which platforms does this work on?

We focus on Google Business reviews because that's where San Antonio customers search for tree service. Google shows up first on every local search, and that's where you need proof.

How does the timing work?

You tell us when a job is complete. The system sends a request within 24 hours—after the work is fresh in their mind but before they forget about it. The timing is automatic.

What if I already have a review process?

If your crew is manually collecting reviews and it's working, that's fine. Most aren't consistent. We replace the guessing game with automation so you get reviews without relying on whether someone remembers to ask.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

That depends on your job completion rate, but most owners see significantly more reviews within the first 60 days because they're finally asking every customer systematically. You'll see the difference.
