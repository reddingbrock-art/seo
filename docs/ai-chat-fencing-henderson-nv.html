FAQ

Questions About AI Chat for Fencing Estimates in Henderson

Will the chat widget actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on how you actually talk to customers—your language, your process, your personality. It's not generic corporate speak. It sounds like someone from your crew answering questions.

What happens when the chat can't answer something?

It collects the question, tells the visitor you'll follow up, and sends you a message so you can respond directly. It knows its limits and hands off gracefully.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It replaces the phone calls and emails that never happen. Your receptionist handles callbacks, scheduling confirmations, and the human stuff. The chat just captures conversations that would've walked away otherwise.

What hours does the chat widget work?

24/7. Visitors chat whenever they visit. You respond during business hours. They see when you'll get back to them.

How long does setup take?

You're live in 10-14 days from the discovery call. Most of that time is us building and you reviewing. No waiting months for implementation.

What if a customer wants to call instead of chat?

The chat offers your phone number as an option. Some people prefer calling—that's fine. The chat still captured their info and intent before they hung up.
