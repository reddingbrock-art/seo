FAQ

AI Chat Widget Questions for Savannah Pool Service Owners

Will the chat widget actually sound like my business?

Yes. We write it based on how you talk to customers. We capture your service areas, your tone, and your policies. It reads like it's coming from your team, not a robot.

What happens when the chat can't answer something?

It asks for the customer's info and creates a ticket for your team. The lead doesn't disappear—it gets routed to you with context so you can follow up.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It handles the initial qualification and booking. Your team still manages customer relationships, scheduling, and service delivery. It just catches leads before they disappear.

What hours does the chat widget work?

24/7. It captures inquiries even when your office is closed. Visitors can start conversations and book appointments anytime.

How long does setup take?

Discovery to live in 10–14 days. We handle the writing, programming, and integration. You don't have to build or maintain anything.

What if a customer wants to call instead of chat?

The chat offers your phone number as an option. They can call if they prefer. The system doesn't force anyone into chat—it just makes it easy and available.
