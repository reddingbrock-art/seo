FAQ

Chat Widget Questions for Houston Fencing Contractors

Will the chat widget actually sound like my business?

Yes. We write it based on your voice, your service offerings, and the way you talk to customers. It asks the questions you'd ask—about the fence type, the neighborhood, the timeline. It doesn't sound corporate.

What happens when the chat can't answer something?

It tells the visitor it's escalating to you and captures their message. They get your phone number or email depending on how you want to handle it. Nothing falls through the cracks.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It replaces the part of your receptionist's job that's answering the same three questions all day and scheduling estimates. Your team still closes deals—they just have qualified leads and clean info.

What hours does the chat widget work?

24/7. It captures visitors when you're sleeping, on the road, or booked solid. Daytime chats can connect directly to you. Night chats get logged and follow up automatically the next business day.

How long does setup take?

Full setup and training takes about two weeks. We handle the technical side. You just need to spend an hour telling us how you qualify jobs and what your typical process looks like.

What if a customer wants to call instead of chat?

They can. The widget offers a phone option. But most won't ask for it once they're already chatting. That's the whole point—you capture people who avoided the phone in the first place.
