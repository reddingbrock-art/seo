FAQ

Foundation Repair Review Automation: Common Questions

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests only to customers with whom you've completed work. Google can tell the difference between real reviews from actual customers and fake ones. This system just makes sure real customers actually leave them.

What if a customer is unhappy?

They don't have to leave a review. The request is just an invitation. If someone had a bad experience, forcing them to review won't help—it's better to know about it and address it. Real reviews, good or bad, build trust with future customers.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google primarily. That's where foundation repair customers search and where ratings matter most for local visibility. We focus on Google because that's where the calls come from.

How does the timing work?

Requests go out automatically after you mark a job complete. Most customers see the request within hours. If they respond in the next 24–48 hours, the review posts faster. There's no delay on your end.

What if I already have a review process?

Most companies have a process in theory but don't execute it consistently. If you're already getting 20+ reviews monthly and your ratings are climbing, you might not need this. For everyone else, automation removes the guesswork.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Most foundation repair companies capture 30–50% of potential reviews with manual follow-up. Automation usually bumps that significantly higher because the ask is timely and effortless. The exact number depends on your current completion volume and customer base.
