FAQ

Google Review Automation Questions for Miami Window Replacement Owners

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. The system sends review requests to actual customers you've completed jobs for. Whether they respond or not is up to them—we're just making sure the ask reaches them at the moment they're most likely to say yes.

What if a customer is unhappy?

You won't send review requests to customers flagged as problems in your system. And if an unhappy customer does leave a negative review, that's real feedback—it's not our job to filter who can and can't speak. The goal is to ask the satisfied majority so their voices are louder.

Which platforms does this work on?

Primarily Google, where most of your local search traffic comes from. That's where window replacement customers are looking when they're ready to decide.

How does the timing work?

You define it. Some companies send the request 24 hours after job completion. Others wait a week so the install dust literally settles. We build it around your process so it feels natural, not pushy.

What if I already have a review process?

Tell us what you're doing on the discovery call. If it's working, we enhance it. If it's a manual list you send to your office manager once a week, we automate that and get faster results.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

It depends on your job volume and how many customers you're currently asking. Most shops see a significant increase in the first 60 days once requests start going out consistently—especially if you weren't asking before.
