FAQ

Automation for Pool Service: Questions Answered

What exactly gets automated?

Inbound calls and texts get answered with your custom message. Form submissions on your website auto-respond. Appointment details get captured and synced to your calendar. Review requests go out after job completion. The system also logs everything so you see exactly who reached out and when.

Do I need technical skills to run this?

No. We build and manage the entire system for you. You log in, see your leads, confirm appointments, and answer calls when needed. That's it.

What if my team resists new tools?

Your team doesn't have to learn much. Calls and texts flow to them the same way they always do—except now every lead gets a response. Appointment details are already filled in. It actually makes their job easier.

How is this different from buying software myself?

You could buy software and try to set it up yourself, but then you're managing integrations, troubleshooting bugs, and figuring out why things aren't working. We do all that. You get a system that works out of the box and stays working.

What does the setup process look like?

One discovery call to understand your business. Then we build everything on our end—typically 5-7 days. We test it with you, walk your team through how to use it, and you go live. From call to live is usually 10-14 days.

What happens if something breaks after launch?

We monitor the system constantly. If something goes wrong, we fix it without you having to do anything. You're not on your own—that's the whole point of done-for-you.
