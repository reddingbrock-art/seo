FAQ

Questions About AI Chat for Gutters Businesses

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We build it using your language, your guarantees, your service area, and your pricing. It answers the way you do, not like a generic bot.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It tells the customer it's grabbing a human, then it routes them to you or your team with the full conversation history. You pick up knowing everything they already said.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It replaces the lag time. It answers before anyone can, qualifies the lead, schedules the appointment, and your team handles the rest. If you have a receptionist, they're now working on higher-value stuff instead of taking calls all day.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. Leads don't stop coming in at 6 PM, and neither does your system. It answers at midnight, 5 AM, whenever.

How long does setup take?

10-14 days from start to live. We handle the integration, the training, the testing. You just show up to the discovery call.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

Some will figure it out. Most won't. Either way, they're already booked. By the time they know, they've committed to the appointment and the conversation already happened. It doesn't kill the deal.
