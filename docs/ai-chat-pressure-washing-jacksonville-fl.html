FAQ

Questions About AI Chat Agents for Pressure Washing

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We build it using your language, your service names, and your tone. You'll approve the voice and messaging before it goes live so it feels like part of your team.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It collects the customer's info and tells them you'll follow up. No dead ends, no frustrated customers. Everything lands in your queue so you can close the conversation.

Does this replace my receptionist?

It replaces the parts of a receptionist that cost the most time—answering after hours, handling voicemail, scheduling intake calls. If you have someone now, they shift to higher-value work. If you don't, you stop needing to hire one.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. Nights, weekends, holidays. The second someone reaches out, they get answered. You can set quiet hours if you need them, but most owners keep it running constantly.

How long does setup take?

10–14 days from discovery call to live. Most of that is us building and testing. Once you approve, it's live the next day.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

It doesn't matter. They got answered, they got heard, and they're already talking about their job. The conversation flows naturally. Transparency is fine—most customers care that their question got handled, not who handled it.
