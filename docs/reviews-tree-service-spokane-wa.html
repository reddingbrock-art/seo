FAQ

Common Questions About Google Review Automation for Tree Service

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests only to customers who actually completed jobs with you. Google flags fake or paid reviews—we don't do that. Real customers, real feedback.

What if a customer is unhappy?

They're the ones least likely to click the review link. If someone does leave a negative review, you handle it the way you always would—respond professionally. This system just makes the happy ones more likely to share their experience.

Which platforms does this work on?

We focus on Google, since that's where most customers search for tree service in Spokane. It's also where the algorithm pushes you higher when reviews accumulate.

How does the timing work?

Requests go out the day after a job invoices. Customers have a couple weeks to respond before the window closes. You'll start seeing reviews trickle in around week two or three.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll look at what you're doing and either improve it or replace it with something that actually runs on its own. Most owners have a process they forget to execute—we remove that friction.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

It depends on your job volume. If you're closing 15 jobs a month, you should expect significantly more reviews within 60 days. The exact number comes out in the discovery call when we see your actual workflow.
