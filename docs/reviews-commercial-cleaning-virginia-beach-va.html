FAQ

Common Questions About Review Automation for Commercial Cleaning

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We only send requests to customers who actually completed a job with you. Real people, real work. The reviews that come back are genuine because they're from people who experienced your service.

What if a customer is unhappy?

That's their choice. We send requests; they decide what to write. If someone had a bad experience, they'll say so—and that feedback actually helps you improve. We're not filtering or suppressing anything.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google primarily, since that's where commercial decision-makers search. We can also push to other platforms depending on your setup. Google is where the visibility and local ranking happen.

How does the timing work?

You mark a job complete in your field app. Within a few hours, the review request goes out via email or text—whatever channel you choose. The timing is automatic and consistent.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll work around it. If you're already asking manually and getting results, we can enhance that. Most owners find they were only hitting 20-30% of customers. Automation reaches significantly more.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Depends on your job volume and response rate. Most cleaning companies see their review count grow 3-5x in the first 90 days. A crew doing 15 jobs a week will pull reviews faster than one doing 3.
