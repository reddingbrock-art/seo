FAQ

Google Review Automation FAQs for Kansas City Junk Removal

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests only to customers who completed a job with you. Google detects and removes fake reviews anyway. Real customers, real work, real reviews.

What if a customer is unhappy?

That's their choice—we're just asking. If they're unhappy, they probably won't respond. If they do, you'll see honest feedback. That's better than no feedback at all.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google is the priority—that's where customers search and where reviews matter most for your local visibility. Other platforms can be added depending on your needs.

How does the timing work?

Requests go out the same day as job completion or the next morning, depending on what you prefer. That's when customers are most satisfied and most likely to respond.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll look at what you're doing now and either improve it or replace it. If it's working, we fine-tune it. If it's not, we automate it so nothing falls through the cracks.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Most owners see a significant increase—typically 3–5 reviews in the first month, then a steady flow as the system keeps running. It depends on your job volume and customer responsiveness.
