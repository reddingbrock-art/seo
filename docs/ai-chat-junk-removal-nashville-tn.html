FAQ

AI Chat for Junk Removal: Questions Answered

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on your language, your service area, your pricing structure, and even your brand personality. Customers won't hear a generic script—they'll hear someone who knows your neighborhoods and your rates.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It flags the conversation for your team with context about what the customer was asking. You get notified immediately so you can jump in if it's a complex job or unusual request. Most calls resolve without your involvement.

Does this replace my receptionist?

If you have a receptionist, this lets them focus on dispatch, customer follow-up, and actual relationship work instead of answering the same questions 50 times a day. If you don't have one, you've just hired 24/7 coverage without payroll.

What hours does the AI work?

Round the clock. Nights, weekends, holidays—whenever a customer calls or texts, it answers. You set business hours in your calendar, and it schedules appointments within your actual availability.

How long does setup take?

Typically 10-14 days from discovery call to live. We handle the technical work. You just need to spend 20 minutes on the initial call and 30 minutes for approval once it's built.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

Most don't figure it out because it's conversational and answers like a real person. If they do ask, it's honest about it. Customers care more about whether they get booked and quoted quickly—and they do.
