FAQ

Automation for Paving Companies in Charlotte: Common Questions

What exactly gets automated?

Inbound calls and form submissions. The system answers calls, asks qualifying questions, collects job details, and books appointments on your calendar. Website forms trigger instant responses and booking links. Review requests go out after job completion. Your team doesn't touch any of it unless they choose to.

Do I need technical skills to run this?

No. You don't manage the system—we do. You log in, check your calendar, and show up to booked appointments. If you need to change something (hours, questions, booking rules), you tell us. We make the update.

What if my team resists new tools?

Your team's job actually gets easier. They're not fielding calls, managing voicemail, or following up on web forms. They see qualified jobs appear in the calendar ready to estimate. Most crews prefer less phone time and more time selling estimates.

How is this different from buying software myself?

You'd spend weeks setting it up, training staff, troubleshooting integrations, and managing updates. We handle all of that. You get a working system in 10-14 days and we maintain it. You own the results, not the tech.

What does the setup process look like?

Discovery call to map your workflows, 10-14 days of configuration and testing, then launch. We handle phone number routing, website integration, and calendar connection. You don't need to do anything except show up to the appointments.

What happens if something breaks after launch?

We monitor the system continuously. If something stops working, we fix it. You're not troubleshooting—we're on it. If your business changes (new service, different hours), you tell us and we adjust the system.
