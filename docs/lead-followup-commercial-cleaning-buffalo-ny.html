FAQ

Lead Follow-Up Questions Answered for Buffalo Cleaning Companies

How many follow-ups does it send?

We typically set up a sequence of 3–5 follow-ups over 7 days. The exact sequence depends on your closing cycle and what your data shows works best for commercial cleaning contracts.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. You tell us what message converts leads—we build it into the system. You control the voice and the offer. If you need to change something later, we make the update.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can opt out anytime. The system respects their request immediately. You won't contact them again. Compliance is built in.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Within 2–5 minutes of the lead coming in. The goal is to reach them while they're still actively searching, not after they've made a decision.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in your dashboard. You can see how many inquiries went silent and follow up manually if you want. The system doesn't throw away dead leads—it just automates the first touches so you can focus on the warm ones.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Yes. If you're using a phone system, scheduling software, or anything else, this plugs into your workflow. It doesn't replace what's working—it fills the gap your current process leaves.
