FAQ

AI Chat for Septic & Drain: Questions Answered

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on your actual language, your service area, your pricing, and how your team talks to customers. It represents you accurately, not some generic chatbot voice.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It captures the question, tells the customer you'll follow up, and hands you the detail. You call them back with the real answer. No customer gets dropped.

Does this replace my receptionist?

It replaces the part of the job that's answering phones and taking basic intake information. If you have a receptionist doing other work, they stay. If you're paying someone just to answer the phone, this does that job better and faster.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. Calls and forms get answered at 2 a.m. the same way they do at 2 p.m. Customers in emergency mode can still reach you and get scheduled.

How long does setup take?

Discovery call happens immediately. Build and training takes about 10 days. You're live and handling calls by day 14.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

The AI is designed to handle that honestly. If a customer asks, it tells them straight up. Most don't ask. They're focused on getting a time slot and moving on. The goal is qualification and scheduling, not deception.
