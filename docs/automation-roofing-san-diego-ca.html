Roofing Automation FAQs: Common Questions from San Diego Contractors

What exactly gets automated?

Lead capture from calls, texts, email, and web forms. Lead qualification based on damage type, location, and urgency. Appointment scheduling and confirmations. Review requests after job completion. Your crew gets a prioritized list every morning—no manual sorting.

Do I need technical skills to run this?

No. You log in to see your leads and manage your calendar. We handle all the backend setup and maintenance. If something needs tweaking, you tell us—we make the change.

What if my team resists new tools?

We keep it simple on their end. Crews see a daily list of qualified leads and confirm appointments via text. Most teams are on board once they realize it means fewer admin calls and more time in the field.

How is this different from buying software myself?

You buy software, we build the system. That means setup, integration, ongoing management, and support are all handled by us. You get a working operation, not a license and a manual.

What does the setup process look like?

We schedule a discovery call, gather your lead sources and team info, configure everything on our end, and test with your crew. You're live in 10–14 days with zero disruption to your operation.

What happens if something breaks after launch?

We monitor the system continuously and fix issues before you notice them. If something does go wrong, you call us and we handle it. Your job is to run roofing, not troubleshoot software.
