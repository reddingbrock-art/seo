FAQ

Waterproofing Automation FAQs for Chicago Contractors

What exactly gets automated?

Lead capture from your website, text message notifications to your team, appointment scheduling, reminder sequences to prospects, and review requests after job completion. Basically everything that happens between a lead coming in and an estimate being scheduled.

Do I need technical skills to run this?

No. Your office person checks a dashboard and manages appointments. That's it. We handle the setup and the backend. You just watch leads come in and see your schedule fill.

What if my team resists new tools?

Most resistance comes from complexity. Our system is built to reduce work, not add it. Your team will see faster callbacks and fewer dropped leads. They'll adopt it because it makes their day easier.

How is this different from buying software myself?

You could buy a platform and spend weeks figuring out how to wire it together. We do that work for you and set it up so it matches your actual process. You go live in two weeks instead of two months.

What does the setup process look like?

Discovery call where we learn your business, build week where we configure your system, then launch. You're not implementing anything. We're doing it.

What happens if something breaks after launch?

We support it. You've got a point of contact who knows your system. If a lead isn't routing right or a text isn't going out, we fix it. You don't troubleshoot—we do.
