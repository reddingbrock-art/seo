FAQ

AI Chat for Fire & Water Restoration Companies: Common Questions Answered

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on your actual voice, your scripts, how you explain damage to customers, even your humor if that's part of your brand. It'll sound like someone on your team took the call—because the AI was literally trained to mirror your process.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It escalates. If a customer asks something outside of what the AI is trained to handle, it captures all the context and routes the conversation directly to your team with full notes. No lost information, no starting over.

Does this replace my receptionist?

It replaces the phone-answering part of their job, yes. Your team can focus on scheduling follow-ups, coordinating crews, and handling complex customer issues instead of sitting on hold duty. Some companies use this to let their admin staff do higher-value work.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7/365. It answers calls at midnight on a Tuesday, during your lunch break, or on holidays. You set your service hours, and the AI works them all—no nights off, no sick days.

How long does setup take?

From your first call with us to live answering: 10-14 days. We need about an hour of your time for discovery, then our team handles the build and training while you keep running your business.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

Transparency is built in. If they ask directly, the AI tells them. Most customers don't notice or care—they're just relieved someone answered. The real win is that your job got booked and your crew showed up fast.
