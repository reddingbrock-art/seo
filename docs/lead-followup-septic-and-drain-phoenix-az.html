FAQ

Common Questions About Lead Follow-Up for Septic & Drain Services

How many follow-ups does it send?

We build the sequence based on what works for your business. Typically it's 3–5 touch points over 5–7 days, but you decide. Once a lead books or opts out, it stops.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. Every message—text, email, or voicemail—is written for your business and your tone. You review and approve before anything goes out.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They're automatically removed from the follow-up sequence. No more contact. Compliance is built in.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Depends on the lead source, but typically within 5–15 minutes. The faster the better—that's where you win against competitors.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in your system and the sequence completes as planned. Some leads convert on the third or fourth touch, not the first. Nothing gets lost.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. It sits on top of what you're already doing. If dispatch is handling some leads manually, the system doesn't interfere. Both work together.
