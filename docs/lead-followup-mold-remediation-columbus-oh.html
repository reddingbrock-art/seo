FAQ

Lead Follow-Up Questions from Columbus Mold Remediation Owners

How many follow-ups does it send?

It depends on what you want. Most mold companies run 3–5 touchpoints over 7–10 days: an initial text or call within minutes, a follow-up text at 24 hours, email at 48 hours, and a final reminder at day 7. You control the sequence.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. You write the messages or we help you write them. Every text, email, and call script reflects your business, your service area, and your process.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can opt out, and the system stops contacting them. Compliance is built in. You're not spamming—you're following up on inbound leads, which is legal and expected.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Instantly if you set it up that way, or within minutes of the lead coming in. Most owners set it for immediate text, then a call within 5–10 minutes if there's no response.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in your system. You can re-engage them in 30 days with a separate campaign, or they sit there as future prospects if someone searches again. Nothing gets deleted.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. It sits on top of what you're already doing. If you use a dispatcher, a crew app, or manage jobs manually, this just adds the follow-up layer you're missing. No rip-and-replace required.
