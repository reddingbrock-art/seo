FAQ

AI Chat for Painting Companies—Common Questions

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on your specific service areas, pricing, typical customer questions, and your voice. It doesn't sound generic—it sounds like your team.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It flags the conversation and hands it off to you with full context. You pick up knowing exactly what the customer asked and what's relevant.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It handles the volume spike—nights, weekends, holidays, and call surges. Your receptionist focuses on follow-up, scheduling details, and the conversations that need a human.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. It answers at 11 PM on a Saturday the same way it does at 9 AM on Monday. You don't have to staff nights or weekends anymore.

How long does setup take?

10–14 days from discovery call to live. We handle the heavy lifting. You tell us how your business works.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

Some will figure it out. Most won't. Either way, the AI is honest—it says it's an automated system if asked—and it moves them forward fast. That speed matters more than the method.
