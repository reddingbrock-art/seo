FAQ

Common Questions About Review Automation for Pool Service Businesses

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send review requests only to customers whose jobs actually closed in your system. We're asking real customers who paid you to share their real experience.

What if a customer is unhappy?

The system sends a review request to everyone—that's the point. If they leave a negative review, you see it early and can respond. You're not filtering out unhappy customers. You're getting feedback faster so you can fix issues before they compound.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google, Yelp, and Facebook. Those are where pool service customers look and where they leave reviews.

How does the timing work?

The request goes out the same day or the next day after the job closes—whenever you want. You choose the window. We automate the send.

What if I already have a review process?

We replace the broken one. If you're currently emailing requests manually or relying on your team to ask, this is faster and more consistent. If you've got nothing, we build the whole system.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

It depends on your job volume and your current ask rate. Most businesses see significantly more reviews once requests land automatically and on time. Some go from 1-2 per month to 5-8. It compounds.
