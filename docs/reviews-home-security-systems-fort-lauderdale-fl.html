No. We send requests only to customers whose jobs you actually completed. You don't get to manufacture reviews—you just capture the ones you've actually earned but weren't asking for.

What if a customer is unhappy?

The request still goes out, and if they leave a critical review, you get the feedback directly. You address it in your response. That's more valuable than never hearing the complaint at all.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google primarily, since that's where Fort Lauderdale home security customers search first. We can discuss other platforms on your discovery call.

How does the timing work?

The request is automated and sent shortly after job completion—usually the same day or next day. You don't have to remember or manage the timing.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll replace it. If you're already asking customers manually and getting a few reviews, automation will multiply that without adding any effort on your end.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?
