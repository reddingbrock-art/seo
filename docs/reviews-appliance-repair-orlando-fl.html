FAQ

Common Questions About Review Automation for Appliance Repair

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We request reviews only from customers whose jobs are actually completed in your system. Google verifies the customer, and you get genuine feedback from real work you've done.

What if a customer is unhappy?

They can leave honest feedback. We don't filter or suppress negative reviews—we just ask the ones who hired you. If work quality improves, so do the reviews you get.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google primarily, since that's where customers search for appliance repair. The system integrates with most job management platforms and dispatch software.

How does the timing work?

The request goes out the same day or within 24 hours of job completion, depending on how your team closes jobs. Timing is configurable so it hits when your brand experience is freshest.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll look at what you're doing. If it's working, we leave it alone. If it's manual or inconsistent, we replace it with something that actually executes every time.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

It depends on your job volume. Most shops see a significant increase once the request becomes automatic—customers who wanted to leave a review now actually do, because you asked at the right moment.
