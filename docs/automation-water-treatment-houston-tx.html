What exactly gets automated?

Job completion triggers customer data capture, review requests send automatically after service, maintenance reminders go out on schedule, and your team gets alerts when someone's due for their next appointment. Basically, every touch-point between job close and the next booking.

Do I need technical skills to run this?

No. Your team logs into a simple dashboard to see what's happening, but the automation itself runs on its own. You don't need IT people or coding knowledge.

What if my team resists new tools?

Most teams resist because they don't understand what they're getting. Once they see that automation is killing the manual work they hate—not adding to it—the resistance disappears. We train everyone before go-live.

How is this different from buying software myself?

You could spend weeks hunting for the right platform, integrating it with your existing tools, building out workflows, and hoping your team uses it right. We do all that for you, deliver a working system, and maintain it. You go live in less than two weeks instead of two months.

What does the setup process look like?

One discovery call to understand your business, then we build and configure everything off-site. We bring it back to you for review, make adjustments, train your team, and flip the switch. Total time: 10-14 days.

What happens if something breaks after launch?
