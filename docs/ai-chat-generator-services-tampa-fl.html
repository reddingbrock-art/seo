FAQ

Questions About AI Chat for Generator Services in Tampa

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on your actual call scripts, terminology, and tone. It answers with your name, your service areas, and your policies. Callers don't think they're talking to a robot—they think they're talking to your team.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It transfers the call to your dispatch team or escalates to a live person if you want. It knows when it's out of depth and hands off cleanly, with all the context already captured.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It replaces the job of answering 50 repetitive calls a day. Your receptionist (if you have one) now handles relationship work, scheduling, and complex issues. If you don't have one, you're not hiring one now.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. It answers calls at 3 AM on a Sunday just like it answers them at 9 AM on Monday. You set which hours route to live staff and which get handled by the AI alone.

How long does setup take?

Discovery to live is 10-14 days. That includes us learning your business, building the AI, testing with you, and deploying. You're not waiting weeks.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

Most don't. But if they ask, the AI is transparent: 'You're talking with our automated scheduling system. If you need to speak with someone directly, I can transfer you now.' Transparency builds trust faster than deception anyway.
