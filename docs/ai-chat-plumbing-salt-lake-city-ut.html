FAQ

AI Chat and Call Agent Questions for Plumbing Companies

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We script it with your name, your service area, your typical response times, and the way your team talks about jobs. Customers won't hear a corporate robot—they'll hear someone who works for your company.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It listens to the question, takes the customer's number, and immediately transfers to a real person on your team (or queues it if everyone's busy). Your technician gets a note with the full context, so they can pick up mid-conversation.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It replaces the part of their day spent on voicemail, form follow-ups, and scheduling callbacks. They focus on dispatch, customer follow-up, and the work that actually moves the business forward. You might not need to hire a second person.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. It's on at midnight, on weekends, on holidays. You set the availability windows your team can actually service, and the AI only books those slots. Emergency calls always get answered.

How long does setup take?

About two weeks from discovery to live. Most of that is us building and testing on the backend. Your job is one call to brief us, plus maybe 30 minutes of reviewing scripts before we launch.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

We don't hide it. The AI will say 'I'm an automated scheduling assistant for [Your Company]' upfront. Customers don't mind—they're impressed you answered in 10 seconds. If they want a real person, they ask for one and get transferred immediately.
