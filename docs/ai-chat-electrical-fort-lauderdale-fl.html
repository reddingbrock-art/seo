FAQ

AI Chat Questions Electrical Contractors Ask

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on your actual job process, the neighborhoods you serve, how you talk about emergency calls versus scheduled work, and your pricing structure. Customers hear your voice, not a generic bot.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It knows its limits. If a customer asks something outside your service area or too complex, it offers to transfer them to your team or schedule a callback. You stay in control of edge cases.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It replaces the part of your receptionist's job that's answering phones and taking basic information. Your team still handles complex questions, builds relationships, and closes bigger jobs.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. Customers can schedule or ask questions anytime. You set your available appointment windows—if someone books outside them, they get a waitlist option or a callback time you choose.

How long does setup take?

10–14 days from kickoff to live. We need your service menu, service areas, pricing, and about an hour of your time to train the AI on how you talk to customers.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

Most don't. But if they ask, the AI is honest about it and explains it's there to get them scheduled fast so your team can help. Customers care more about getting an appointment than whether they talked to a person.
