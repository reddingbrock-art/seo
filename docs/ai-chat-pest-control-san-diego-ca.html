FAQ

AI Chat Questions for Pest Control Owners

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on your language, your service names, your coverage areas, and how your team talks about pest problems. A customer can't tell it apart from your office staff—unless you tell them.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It identifies the question, captures all the context, and routes the conversation to your team with full details attached. Your technician or office manager follows up with complete information already in hand.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It handles the volume your receptionist can't—calls after hours, weekend inquiries, form submissions during service. Your team stays focused on operations and high-touch customer conversations.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7/365. It's answering at 2 a.m. on a Sunday just as fast as it answers at 8 a.m. on a Monday. Every lead, every time.

How long does setup take?

Full deployment to live is 10 business days. You'll spend about 30 minutes on the discovery call and 3–5 days reviewing and tweaking before we flip the switch.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

They still get an answer. The agent is transparent if asked directly. But most customers don't care—they care that their question was answered instantly and their appointment was booked. Transparency builds trust faster than pretending.
