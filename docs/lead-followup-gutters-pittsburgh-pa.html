FAQ

Lead Follow-Up Automation Questions for Pittsburgh Gutters Businesses

How many follow-ups does it send?

You decide the sequence. Most gutter companies use 2–4 touchpoints over 5–7 days: initial confirmation, reminder, and sometimes a final offer or review request after completion. You own the cadence.

Can I customize what it says?

Completely. We write the initial copy based on your voice and process, you review and approve everything, and then it runs. You can update messages anytime without touching the system.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can unsubscribe from any touchpoint and the system respects it immediately. You stay compliant and don't waste energy on disengaged prospects.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Within 2–5 minutes of a lead coming in. The goal is to be the first call or text they see, before they've called your competitors.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in your pipeline and you see them flagged. Some get a final re-engagement message after 7–10 days, or they move to a longer-term nurture list if you want to stay in touch seasonally.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Yes. It plugs into whatever system you're using now—your CRM, your phone, your intake form. We don't replace anything; we automate the follow-up layer on top of it.
