FAQ

Review Automation FAQs for Fort Worth Irrigation Companies

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests only to customers you've actually completed work for. Google's system catches fake reviews anyway, and we're not interested in inflating numbers. We're automating legitimate asks to customers who should be reviewing you.

What if a customer is unhappy?

The request still goes out. An unhappy customer won't leave a review—they'll ignore it. A happy one will. You get accurate feedback, not curated feedback. That's the point.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google primarily. That's where homeowners search for irrigation services and where reviews move the needle. We focus there because that's where your customers are looking.

How does the timing work?

Requests go out the day after you mark a job complete. Customers get a text or email depending on what we have on file. Most respond within 48 hours if they're going to respond at all.

What if I already have a review process?

We replace it. If you're manually sending review requests or relying on your team to remember, our system is faster and more consistent. If you have a different automation running, we can talk about whether migrating makes sense.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Most owners see a significant increase in the first 60 days because we're capturing reviews that were never being asked for. Long-term, expect one to three more per week depending on your job volume. That adds up to 50-150 per year on top of what you're getting now.
