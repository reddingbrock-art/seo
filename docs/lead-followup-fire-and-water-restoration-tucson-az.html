FAQ

Lead Follow-Up System FAQs for Tucson Fire & Water Restoration

How many follow-ups does it send?

That's up to you. Most restoration owners use 3–5 touches over 7–10 days. We set the schedule based on what works for your business and market.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. Every message comes from you, in your voice. You write or approve the wording before anything goes out. We don't use generic templates.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can unsubscribe, and the system respects that request immediately. You'll also see which leads opt out so you know who doesn't want contact.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Within the first few minutes of the lead coming in—whether that's midnight, 6 AM, or during business hours. You set the exact timing.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They keep getting your scheduled follow-ups until they reach out, ask to stop, or hit the end of your sequence. You can also see inactive leads and decide whether to pick up the phone yourself.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. The system integrates with how you already take jobs—your CRM, your scheduling, your estimates. It fills the gap between lead contact and callback, nothing more.
