FAQ

Lead Follow-Up System FAQs for Pest Control Owners

How many follow-ups does it send?

We typically run 4–6 touchpoints over 7 days depending on your preference: initial call/text, then secondary contacts spaced to catch people at different times. You decide the sequence.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. Every message, call script, and tone gets tailored to your business and neighborhood. You approve all copy before it goes live.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can opt out anytime, and we honor that immediately. They're removed from the sequence.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Within 2–5 minutes of the lead coming in, depending on whether it's a phone call, text, or form submission.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They cycle through your sequence and then move to a nurture list for periodic check-ins. You still have their information if they become active later.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. It plugs into your intake channels and hands off to your team once a lead is warm. Your crew's job is closing, not chasing cold leads.
