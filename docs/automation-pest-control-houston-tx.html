FAQ

Automation Questions for Houston Pest Control Owners

What exactly gets automated?

Lead capture from web forms, text, and calls. Qualification questions that filter serious customers. Appointment booking and confirmation. Callback scheduling and job status updates to customers. Your dispatcher focuses on routing and logistics—the system handles everything before that.

Do I need technical skills to run this?

No. We set it up. Your team logs in and uses it the way they already work. If something needs tweaking, we handle it. You're not managing servers or learning software—you're running your business.

What if my team resists new tools?

We train them and stay available during the first week. Once they see that the system removes grunt work—no more taking messages or manually entering leads—resistance usually disappears. It makes their job easier, not harder.

How is this different from buying software myself?

You'd spend weeks evaluating platforms, integrating them, training your team, and troubleshooting when something breaks. We do that work. You get a working system, not a software subscription and a learning curve.

What does the setup process look like?

Discovery call, then 1–2 weeks of configuration and training. We connect your phone, forms, and booking tool to the system, test it with your team, and launch when you're ready.

What happens if something breaks after launch?

We support it. You're not on your own. If a lead isn't coming through or something's routing wrong, you reach out and we fix it.
