FAQ

Google Review Automation FAQs for Paving & Asphalt Contractors

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send review requests only to customers who actually completed a job with you. They choose whether to leave a review—we just make sure they're asked.

What if a customer is unhappy?

An unhappy customer won't leave a review just because they got a request. If there's a real problem, you address it before they see the request—or you don't send one at all. We're not tricking anyone into anything.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google is where your paving leads are looking. That's where we focus. Your reviews show up there, show up in local search, and show up in comparison tools your customers use.

How does the timing work?

Requests go out the day after job completion. That's when customers still remember the work, the crew, and the result. Early enough to matter.

What if I already have a review process?

If you're manually asking and getting results, keep it. If you're asking sometimes and getting sporadic reviews, automation fills the gaps and makes it consistent.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Most contractors see reviews from the customers who would've left one anyway but didn't remember to. Exact numbers depend on your completion volume and how long you've been in business. We'll show you the typical range during your call.
