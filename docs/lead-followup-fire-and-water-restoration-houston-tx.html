FAQ

Questions About Lead Follow-Up Automation for Fire & Water Restoration

How many follow-ups does it send?

That depends on your process. We typically set up an initial contact, a follow-up at 30 minutes, and another at a few hours. You decide the sequence and timing.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. We write the messages with you so they sound like your business. Any changes, we update them.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

The system respects opt-outs. If someone says stop, they stop getting contacted. You're compliant and you don't burn bridges.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Immediately. If it's a form submission or a call outside business hours, they get a response within seconds.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in your system so you can follow up manually later, or the sequence can be set to end after a set number of touches. You decide.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. This is built to plug into whatever you're already doing—new customers and old systems work together.
