FAQ

Review Automation for EV Charger Installers in Kansas City—Questions Answered

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send review requests only to customers you've actually completed work for. Google's systems flag unnatural patterns immediately. Real requests from real jobs are the only sustainable path.

What if a customer is unhappy?

They won't leave a positive review. The request just sits—or they'll provide honest feedback. This is actually good: you want real customers sharing real experiences, not artificially inflated scores that tank your credibility.

Which platforms does this work on?

We focus on Google reviews, which is where your EV charger customers look first. That's where ranking, visibility, and lead generation happen.

How does the timing work?

The request goes out within hours of job completion—when satisfaction is highest and customers are most likely to respond. You define the exact trigger in your workflow.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll look at what's working and what isn't. If you're already asking and getting good responses, automation speeds it up and removes manual work. If you're not asking consistently, this fills that gap.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

It depends on your current volume and response rates. Most installers see significantly more reviews once requests are automated and timed right—because consistency and timing are what most DIY processes lack.
