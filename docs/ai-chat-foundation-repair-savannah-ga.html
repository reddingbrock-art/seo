FAQ

AI Chat for Foundation Repair: Common Questions

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We write its personality based on how you talk to customers. It uses your neighborhood names, references your service area, and follows your process. If you say 'crawl space,' it says 'crawl space.' It doesn't sound corporate.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It transfers the call to you or your team immediately. It doesn't pretend to know. If a question is outside its scope—like a detailed structural engineering question—it captures the info and hands it off warm so you can take it from there.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. If you have one, they get freed up. If you don't, you stop losing leads to missed calls. Either way, you're not managing phones anymore—you're managing the leads that come through.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. It works nights, weekends, holidays. Whenever a customer texts or calls, they get a response. You schedule callbacks during your actual business hours.

How long does setup take?

Discovery to live is 10–14 days. One call with us, we build it, you review it, we launch it. Then you're answering every inbound conversation on day one.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

They get value before they care. The AI answers their question, books their callback, and tells them a technician will confirm everything tomorrow. Most customers don't ask. The ones who do are already sold on the service.
