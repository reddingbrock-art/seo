FAQ

Fire & Water Restoration AI Chat: Common Questions

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on your actual call recordings, your phrasing, your service areas, and how your team talks to customers. A customer calling won't know they're talking to AI unless you want them to.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It says so and transfers the call to a team member, or books a callback time and captures the full context so your crew knows what they're calling about.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It replaces the gaps. When your receptionist is busy or it's 2 a.m., the AI picks up. When you're in the field or between jobs, it qualifies leads. It makes your existing team more effective.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. Nights, weekends, holidays. It's always available. You control when calls route to your team and when callbacks are scheduled.

How long does setup take?

Live in 10-14 days. Discovery call, we build and test, then flip the switch. Your number routes to the agent. No downtime.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

Most won't notice. But if they ask, it says 'You're connected with our automated dispatch system—let me get your information so we can route this to the right crew.' It's honest and moves the call forward.
