Do I need technical skills to run this?

No. We build it, configure it, and hand it over. Your team logs in, sees incoming qualified leads, and books inspections. No coding, no platform navigation training needed.

The system doesn't create extra work—it removes it. Your team stops answering repetitive questions and coordinating callbacks. They see a cleaner calendar of pre-qualified jobs. Resistance usually disappears after the first week.

How is this different from buying software myself?
