FAQ

Google Review Automation for Locksmiths: Questions Answered

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests only to real customers you've already served. Google's system verifies the request came from an actual service interaction. All reviews are real or they don't count.

What if a customer is unhappy?

They're not forced to leave a review. The request is an invitation, not a demand. And unhappy customers—the rare ones—probably aren't going to review you anyway. This just removes friction for the happy ones.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google is the priority because that's where customers search for you. We also integrate with other review platforms you care about—just let us know during setup which ones matter for your market.

How does the timing work?

The request goes out automatically after job completion, when the experience is fresh but not so immediate that it feels pushy. You control when the request triggers during setup.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll map it out during the discovery call. If you're already collecting reviews manually, we'll automate and accelerate what you're doing. If you're not collecting them at all, we build from scratch.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Most of your customers are willing to leave a review—they just need to be asked. You'll see a significant increase in monthly review volume compared to whatever you're getting now by word-of-mouth alone.
