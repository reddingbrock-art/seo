FAQ

Common Questions About Lead Follow-Up Automation for Septic & Drain

How many follow-ups does it send?

That's your call. We typically set it up to send an initial message within 5 minutes, a second message 2-4 hours later, and a final check-in the next day. You control the sequence and timing.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. We write messages that sound like your business—your tone, your service area, your urgency. You approve every message before it goes live.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can opt out with one reply, and they're removed immediately. The system respects that, and you stay compliant.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Within 5 minutes of the lead coming in. Fast enough that you're often the first call or text they get.

What happens to leads that never respond?

After the sequence runs, they stay in your database for future campaigns. No lead gets permanently lost—you can re-engage them later with seasonal offers or service reminders.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. Your dispatcher still handles scheduling and dispatch. The system just handles the first touch and reminders while your team focuses on closing.
