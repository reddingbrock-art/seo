FAQ

Review Automation for Fire & Water Restoration: Common Questions

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests only to customers you've actually worked for, at the point where the job's complete. Google's system handles verification. Real work, real customers, real reviews.

What if a customer is unhappy?

The system sends requests after job completion. If a customer has a legitimate complaint, they're not obligated to leave a review. Unhappy customers vote with their silence, not with fake positives. That's how it should work.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google is the primary focus—that's where most customers are searching and filtering. We can also extend to other platforms depending on your market, but Google's the ROI driver for fire and water restoration.

How does the timing work?

Requests deploy shortly after a job status changes to complete in your system. The timing is configurable—some owners prefer same-day, others prefer 24 hours after completion. We set it based on what works for your workflow.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll integrate with what you're doing now. If you're already capturing reviews manually, we layer automation on top to catch the ones your team misses and reduce manual work. No conflict, just coverage.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

It depends on your job volume and customer base. Most owners see a significant uptick in review submissions within 30 days because requests aren't being dropped. More jobs completed = more customers asked = more reviews landed. The system removes the friction.
