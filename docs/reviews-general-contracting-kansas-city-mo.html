FAQ

Review Automation Questions for Kansas City Contractors

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We only send requests to customers who actually completed jobs with you. Google detects fake reviews and kills your profile—we're not doing that.

What if a customer is unhappy?

The system asks for feedback after job completion. If someone's not satisfied, that's not a review you want anyway. Better to catch concerns early when you can fix them.

Which platforms does this work on?

We focus on Google Business profiles because that's where homeowners search for contractors and make decisions. That's where your visibility matters most.

How does the timing work?

You define when a job is 'complete'—could be final walkthrough, sign-off, invoice sent. The request goes out automatically at that moment, every single time.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll talk through what you're doing and either integrate it or replace it with something more consistent. Most contractors ask sporadically—this makes it impossible to forget.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Depends on your job volume and how many customers you're currently asking. Most contractors see significantly more reviews within 60 days because the system doesn't drop the ball.
