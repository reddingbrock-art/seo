FAQ

Common Questions About AI Chat for Tree Service in Fort Worth

Will the chat widget actually sound like my business?

Yes. We build it around the way you talk to customers—your tone, your service specifics, your style. It doesn't sound corporate or robotic because we pull from how your team actually responds.

What happens when the chat can't answer something?

It tells the visitor exactly what to do next—'A crew lead will call you at this number within 2 hours' or 'I'll pass this to our scheduling team.' It never leaves someone hanging.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It handles the questions that come in when no one's available—nights, weekends, or while crews are out. Your team still closes deals and manages relationships. This just stops leads from disappearing in the gaps.

What hours does the chat widget work?

24/7. It answers questions whenever someone lands on your site, whether that's 9 AM or midnight. After hours, it can still qualify them and book them in for the next available slot.

How long does setup take?

From discovery call to live chat is typically 10–14 days. Most of that is us building and you reviewing. Installation itself takes less than an hour.

What if a customer wants to call instead of chat?

The chat gives them that option. If they prefer to talk, they can request a callback or you can display your number prominently. The chat is an alternative for people who don't want to call, not a replacement for your phone line.
