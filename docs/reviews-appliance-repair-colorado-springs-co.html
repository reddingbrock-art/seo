FAQ

Common Questions About Review Automation for Appliance Repair

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests only to customers with completed jobs in your system. Real customers, real jobs, real reviews.

What if a customer is unhappy?

We only send requests after job completion. If a customer had a bad experience, you'll know before the request goes out. You control whether the system sends or holds.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google, Yelp, and your other major review platforms. Requests route customers to whichever platforms matter most for your business.

How does the timing work?

The request fires automatically after a job is marked complete in your system. You choose the delay—usually a few hours to the same day, depending on your workflow.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll integrate with what you've got or replace it entirely. Either way, the goal is to make it automatic so it actually happens every time.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

That depends on your job volume and customer response rates. Most shops see significantly more reviews once requests happen automatically instead of sporadically. We'll show you what's possible during your discovery call.
