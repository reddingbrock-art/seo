FAQ

Lead Follow-Up System FAQs for Junk Removal in Tucson

How many follow-ups does it send?

Typically three to five over a seven-day window—initial contact within minutes, then touchpoints at strategic intervals. You control the exact sequence and timing.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. Every message is yours to write and approve before it goes live. It should sound like your business, not a template.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can opt out, and the system respects that immediately. You'll never contact them again. Compliance is built in.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Within minutes of them contacting you—whether by phone, text, or web form. The goal is to beat your competition's response time.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in your lead list so you can see the full history. You can decide whether to follow up manually or let them age out. No leads disappear.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Yes. It integrates with how you're already getting leads. Nothing changes in your workflow except you get faster, more consistent follow-up.
