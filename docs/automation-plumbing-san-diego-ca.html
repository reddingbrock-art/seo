Common Questions About Plumbing Automation in San Diego

What exactly gets automated?

Lead capture across phone, web forms, and text. Instant response with your information and availability. Qualification questions to identify service type and location. Online appointment booking tied to your real calendar. Automated follow-up for leads that don't book on first contact. Review requests after job completion.

Do I need technical skills to run this?

No. You manage it the same way you manage your calendar and customer list now. We handle the setup and technical side. Your team sees jobs coming in and handles the work—the system handles the noise.

What if my team resists new tools?

Most resistance comes from fear it'll add work. It doesn't. It removes the part they hate—answering phones at 7 p.m., calling people back, managing a form inbox. Jobs just show up on their schedule already booked. They see the relief in the first week.

How is this different from buying software myself?

You could buy it, but then you're configuring it, connecting it to your phone system and calendar, writing response messages, troubleshooting when something breaks. We do all that. You get a working system, not a tool you're responsible for learning and maintaining.

What does the setup process look like?

Discovery call to map your process. We build and test everything—usually takes 5-7 business days. You review it, make adjustments if needed. Go live. Total time in the chair for you is about two hours.

What happens if something breaks after launch?

You contact us, and we fix it. We monitor the system for problems, but if something stops working—a form stops submitting, a text response fails—we handle it same day. You're not managing a ticket with a vendor support queue.
