FAQ

Lead Follow-Up Automation: Common Questions

How many follow-ups does it send?

We set that with you during setup. Most crews do 2–3 touches over 48 hours: first message same-day, second next morning if no response, third after another 24 hours. You decide the cadence.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. We write your initial messages based on how you actually talk to customers, and you can adjust them anytime. The system sends them on your schedule, not ours.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can unsubscribe with one click, and they're out of the sequence immediately. The system respects that and logs it.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Within a few minutes of the lead coming in. If someone fills your form at 5 PM on Friday, they get a response that same evening.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in your system. You can see them, pull them back into a conversation manually, or let the sequence finish. No leads get lost or hidden.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. It runs in the background. Your team still takes phone calls, still sends quotes, still closes jobs the way you do now. The system just handles the part that was falling through the cracks.
