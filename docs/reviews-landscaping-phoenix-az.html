FAQ

Common Questions About Review Automation for Landscapers

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We only request reviews from customers you've actually completed work for. Google and other platforms can spot fake reviews instantly—we're not doing that. What you get are real feedback requests sent to people who just hired you.

What if a customer is unhappy?

The system requests a review. If they're unhappy, that's real feedback and that's on you to address—but you'll know about it fast instead of hearing complaints months later. Most crews find that getting honest feedback early is better than operating blind.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google, Facebook, Yelp, and industry-specific review sites. The system routes requests to wherever your customers spend time.

How does the timing work?

The request goes out within hours of job completion—that's when your work is freshest in their mind. Customers click a link, leave a review, done. No follow-up nagging required.

What if I already have a review process?

We work with what you've got or replace it entirely. Either way, automation removes the manual work so your process actually happens instead of competing with a hundred other tasks.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Depends on your current ask rate. Most crews who never asked for reviews or asked sporadically see a significant jump in the first month. Crews already asking manually usually see faster, more consistent results and fewer missed opportunities.
