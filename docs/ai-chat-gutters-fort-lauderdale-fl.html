FAQ

AI Chat Widget FAQs for Fort Lauderdale Gutter Companies

Will the chat widget actually sound like my business?

Yes. We write and train it on your exact language, service descriptions, and how you actually talk to customers. It won't sound like a robot or a generic AI.

What happens when the chat can't answer something?

It acknowledges the question and either escalates to you in real-time or offers to have you call them back. The conversation never dead-ends.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It handles the first conversation and booking. Your team still closes jobs, does the work, and builds relationships. It just means fewer 'sorry we missed your call' moments.

What hours does the chat widget work?

24/7. Prospects can ask questions and book at midnight on a Sunday. You respond during business hours or the system can schedule a callback.

How long does setup take?

The widget goes live in 10-14 days from your discovery call. We handle all the configuration and training.

What if a customer wants to call instead of chat?

The chat offers a phone option. They can always call if they prefer. You're not forcing anyone into chat—you're just giving them the choice.
