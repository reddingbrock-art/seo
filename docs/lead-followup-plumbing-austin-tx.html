FAQ

Lead Follow-Up Questions from Austin Plumbing Owners

How many follow-ups does it send?

It depends on your sequence. We typically start with same-day contact, then 24 hours, then 72 hours if there's no response. You control how many touches and how far apart they are.

Can I customize what it says?

Completely. We write the initial sequence with you, but you can adjust wording, add your service area details, or change messaging for seasonal campaigns anytime.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can reply STOP or let you know, and we remove them from future follow-ups. It's built in and respects their preference.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Within minutes of the lead contacting you. If they call or submit a form at 2 PM, the system's already sent the first message before 2:05 PM.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They cycle through your sequence and then stop. They stay in your CRM so your team can follow up manually later if you want, but the automation doesn't spam endlessly.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Yes. It plugs into your current phone system, CRM, and email. Your team still takes inbound calls and manages jobs normally—this just handles the background follow-up automatically.
