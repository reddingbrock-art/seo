FAQ

AI Chat for Irrigation Companies: Common Questions

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We feed it your past job descriptions, how you talk about systems, your service areas, and your pricing structure. It sounds professional but conversational—like someone who knows irrigation, not a robot reading a script.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It flags the conversation and sends it to your team. Complex design questions, warranty claims, technical troubleshooting—anything that needs a human expert—goes directly to the right person with full context already captured.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It replaces the part of their job that's answering phones and qualifying leads. Your admin moves to closing, follow-up, and the work that actually moves revenue forward.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. Calls at midnight, weekend forms, holiday messages—all get answered. You set which hours trigger instant scheduling vs. appointment confirmation, and which hours send a callback reminder instead.

How long does setup take?

Build and deployment takes 10–14 days after our discovery call. You're live and taking calls in two weeks. Training and refinement happen in real time after that.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

We train the system to be transparent if asked directly. Most customers don't care—they just want fast, accurate answers and a booked appointment. The ones who do prefer talking to AI appreciate the speed.
