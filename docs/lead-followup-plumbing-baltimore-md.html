FAQ

Lead Follow-Up System Questions for Plumbing Companies

How many follow-ups does it send?

It depends on your sequence. Most plumbing shops use 3 to 5 touchpoints over 10 to 14 days. We customize it based on your close rate and what your team can handle.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. Every message is built around your business, your service areas, and your voice. We don't use templates that sound like spam.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can opt out anytime, and the system respects that immediately. It's compliant with all messaging regulations, and we make sure it's easy for them to unsubscribe.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Depends on the source. Web forms and texts can go out within minutes. Voicemails get flagged immediately so your team can prioritize. We build it around how fast you want to move.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in the sequence until it ends, then the system marks them as complete. You can see which ones ghosted and decide if you want to re-engage them later.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Absolutely. It integrates with how you're already taking calls and managing dispatch. No ripping out your current system. It layers on top and fills the gaps.
