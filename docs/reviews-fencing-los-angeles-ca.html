FAQ

Review Automation Questions for Los Angeles Fencing Contractors

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests only to customers who've actually completed jobs with you. Whether they leave a review or not is entirely their choice. We're not filtering responses or suppressing negative ones—we're just making sure satisfied customers know you'd appreciate their feedback.

What if a customer is unhappy?

Happy customers review. Unhappy ones usually don't respond to review requests at all. If someone had a bad experience, an automated request won't change that. This system is built to capture the reviews you've already earned.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google reviews primarily, since that's where Los Angeles homeowners look when they're searching for fence companies. We can configure requests to direct customers to your Google Business profile.

How does the timing work?

The request goes out the day after job completion. That's when satisfaction is highest and the experience is still fresh. We can adjust the timing if your team prefers—same-day or two-day windows both work.

What if I already have a review process?

Most fence contractors either have no process or an inconsistent one. If you're already getting 15+ reviews a month consistently, this might not be urgent. If you're getting 4-6 a month despite doing solid work, you've got a capture problem we can fix.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

It depends on your job volume and current capture rate. A crew running 8-12 jobs a month with no review system might go from 2-3 reviews monthly to 6-8. The system doesn't create demand—it captures the reviews sitting on the table already.
