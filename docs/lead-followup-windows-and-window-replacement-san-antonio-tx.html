FAQ

Lead Follow-Up System Questions for Window & Window Replacement Businesses

How many follow-ups does it send?

That depends on your preference, but typically we set up an initial response, then 1–2 additional messages over 5–7 days if the lead doesn't book. You control the timing and messaging.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. We write the follow-up messages based on your voice and your business info, and you approve every word before it goes live.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can opt out at any time, and the system honors that request immediately. We stay compliant with text and email regulations.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

The first response goes out within minutes of the lead submitting a form—typically before they've even closed your website.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in the system and receive your scheduled follow-up messages. If they still don't engage, they sit in your lead database for you to review and reach out manually later if you choose.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Completely. It doesn't replace your team or your phone calls—it just handles the first response and qualification so your crew can focus on converting warm leads instead of chasing cold ones.
