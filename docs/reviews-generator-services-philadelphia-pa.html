FAQ

Common Questions About Review Automation for Philadelphia Generator Services

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests only to customers you've actually completed jobs for. They choose whether to review or not. We're just making sure the ask reaches them while they still remember the work.

What if a customer is unhappy?

We only send the request after the job's closed and marked complete in your system. If a customer had an issue, you'd handle that before closure. This system gets the satisfied customers to the review platforms faster.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google, Yelp, Home Advisor, and Facebook. The system can target one, multiple, or all depending on where you want your reviews to show up.

How does the timing work?

The request goes out the day the job closes—usually the same afternoon. You set the time window (morning, evening, etc.). Customers get it when they're most likely to respond.

What if I already have a review process?

Tell us what you're doing now. Most owners are either doing it inconsistently or not at all. If you've got something working, we audit it and either optimize it or replace it with something faster.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Most service owners get somewhere between 30–50% of satisfied customers to leave a review when asked properly and at the right time. You already have the satisfied customers. This just makes sure they convert.
