Common Questions About AI Chat for EV Charger Installation Companies

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We use your actual language from past customer conversations, your service area, real pricing, and your tone to build the agent. It sounds like someone from your team answering the phone.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It's honest. If a question's outside what it knows, it tells the prospect that a crew member will follow up with details, then flags that conversation for your team to handle personally.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It replaces the moments when your receptionist or crew is unavailable. That's most of the day—especially during installations. You're adding 24/7 availability without hiring anyone.

What hours does the AI work?

All of them. Nights, weekends, holidays. A prospect can inquire at 11 PM and get a response immediately—then your team picks it up first thing in the morning with a warm, pre-qualified lead.

How long does setup take?

10–14 days from our first call. We gather your business details, build the agent, train it on your real conversations, and deploy it. You're live and taking calls before the next wave of season hits.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

Some will figure it out. That's fine—we design the AI to be transparent about what it is while still being helpful. Most prospects don't care as long as they get their answer fast and a callback from a human happens on time.
