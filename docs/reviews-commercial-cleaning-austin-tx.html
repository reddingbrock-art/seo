FAQ

Common Questions About Review Automation for Commercial Cleaning in Austin

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests to actual customers who completed jobs with you. Whether they leave a review is up to them. We're asking real people at the right time, not generating fake feedback.

What if a customer is unhappy?

They don't have to leave a review. The request is an invitation, not a demand. Some unhappy customers won't respond at all. That's honest feedback, and it's better than pretending the issue didn't happen.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google, primarily. That's where commercial property managers and facility directors look first in Austin. We can configure requests for other platforms, but Google is the priority.

How does the timing work?

The system sends a request within a few hours of job completion—or the next business day if the job finishes after hours. The customer gets it while the work is still fresh in their mind.

What if I already have a review process?

We'll work around it. If you're already asking some customers manually, we'll make sure the automation doesn't duplicate the effort. We integrate with what you've got.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Most of our clients see significantly more reviews within the first 90 days because the automation catches customers in the right moment. The exact number depends on your current review rate and job volume. A discovery call will give you realistic expectations for your business.
