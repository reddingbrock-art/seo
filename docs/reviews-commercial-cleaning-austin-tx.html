FAQ

Google Review Automation Questions for Austin Commercial Cleaning Companies

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We only send requests to real customers who completed a real job. The system ties to your job completion data, so requests go out exclusively to work you actually did.

What if a customer is unhappy?

We only send review requests to jobs marked complete and satisfactory by your crew. If there's an issue, you resolve it before the request goes out. Your average stays strong.

Which platforms does this work on?

Google is the priority and the biggest driver of new leads in commercial cleaning. That's where your prospects are looking first.

How does the timing work?

Requests go out within 24 hours of job completion, when the customer still remembers the work clearly. That timing window is what drives response rates.

What if I already have a review process?

Most ops have a process on paper that doesn't run consistently. We automate what you're already trying to do, so nothing breaks—it just actually happens every time.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Most crews perform 15–30 completions per month. Once automation is live, you'll see significantly more of those customers leaving reviews. The exact number depends on your completion volume and customer base, which we'll map out on your discovery call.
