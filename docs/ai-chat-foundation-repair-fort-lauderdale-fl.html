AI Chat for Foundation Repair Companies: Common Questions

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. It's trained on how you talk to customers, your service areas, and your typical estimates. Customers interact with it the same way they'd interact with a sharp receptionist who knows foundation repair.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It escalates. If a customer asks something outside its scope, it acknowledges the question, collects the information, and routes it directly to you with full context so you can follow up informed.

Does this replace my receptionist?

Not if you have one. It removes the repetitive parts of their job—answering, qualifying, scheduling—so they can focus on customer relationships or other priorities. If you don't have a dedicated person, you're finally getting 24/7 intake.

What hours does the AI work?

All of them. It answers calls and chats at 2 AM the same way it does at 2 PM. You pick the hours it schedules appointments for—so if you don't take calls Sunday, the AI won't book those slots.

How long does setup take?

10-14 days from your discovery call to live. You're not waiting months or dealing with endless configuration meetings. We handle the heavy lifting; you start seeing booked appointments fast.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

It doesn't hide. It's transparent from the start, and for foundation repair—where speed and accuracy matter—customers appreciate an instant response that books their inspection in 90 seconds. Most don't care; they're just glad someone picked up.
