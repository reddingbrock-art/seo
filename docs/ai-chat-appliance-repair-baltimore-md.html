FAQ

AI Chat Agent for Appliance Repair: Common Questions

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on the way you talk to customers—your pricing structure, service areas, and how you handle common questions. It sounds like your business because it's using your voice and logic.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It collects the customer's information and flags the call for you to review. You'll see it tagged in your queue, and you can call them back with the full answer. No customer gets dropped.

Does this replace my receptionist?

It replaces the job of answering the phone and scheduling. If you have someone handling other office work—billing, parts ordering, customer follow-ups—they keep doing that. This just eliminates the phone-answering bottleneck.

What hours does the AI work?

Round the clock. Nights, weekends, holidays. Every call and text gets answered the same way, whether it's 10 a.m. Tuesday or 11 p.m. Friday.

How long does setup take?

Live in 10-14 days from your discovery call. We handle the technical integration. You don't need IT help on your end.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

Most don't. But if they ask, the system's honest about it. Customers care about getting their washer fixed, not which entity answers the phone. They'll stay on the call and get booked.
