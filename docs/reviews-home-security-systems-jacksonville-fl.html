FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions About Google Review Automation for Home Security Companies

Will this get me fake reviews?

No. We send requests only to customers whose jobs are actually complete in your system. Google verifies the customer exists and has a real account. You get legitimate reviews from real people or you get no reviews—there's no middle ground.

What if a customer is unhappy?

If they're unhappy, they probably won't leave a review—which is fine. Unhappy customers rarely review at all. Our process reaches everyone equally; happy customers are just more likely to respond. You're not filtering anyone out.

Which platforms does this work on?

We focus on Google Reviews because that's where your customers are looking first. Google is the listing that matters when someone searches 'home security near me' in Jacksonville.

How does the timing work?

The review request goes out after you mark the job complete—usually the same day or next morning. The window when they're most satisfied is the window where requests actually convert.

What if I already have a review process?

Tell us about it on your call. Most owners are doing it manually or sporadically. If you're already getting 20+ reviews a month consistently, you probably don't need this. Most aren't.

How many more reviews can I realistically expect?

Depends on your monthly install volume and your current request rate. Most security companies we talk to are surprised how many customers will leave a review when the ask actually happens. You'll know within your first month of live data.
