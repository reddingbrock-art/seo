FAQ

Lead Follow-Up System FAQs for Tucson Window Companies

How many follow-ups does it send?

It depends on your process. Most window companies use 3–5 touches over a week: first text or email within minutes, second reminder after 24 hours, third after 48 hours, then a final email. You decide the sequence.

Can I customize what it says?

Yes. You write the messages or we help you craft them. They reflect your voice and business, not ours.

What if a lead asks to stop receiving messages?

They can unsubscribe at any time. The system removes them from your follow-up sequence immediately and logs it.

How fast does the first follow-up go out?

Within 2–5 minutes of the lead arriving, depending on your setup. If it's a form submission, it's automatic. If it's a call, the text goes out as soon as the call ends.

What happens to leads that never respond?

They stay in your CRM. You can see who's been contacted and how many times. Some leads take longer to move, and that's fine—they're still being reminded you exist.

Does this work alongside my existing process?

Yes. It fills the gap between lead arrival and when you're ready to call. Your sales calls still happen exactly the same way, just with better-prepared leads on the other end.
