FAQ

AI Chat Agent FAQs for Plumbing Companies

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We listen to your team, learn how you talk about jobs, and build the agent to match your voice. It won't sound like a bot reading a script—it'll sound like someone who knows plumbing in Houston.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It tells the caller it'll get you the right answer and captures the question. You get the full context and can follow up directly. No dead ends.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It replaces the part of their job that's answering phones and taking intake. If they handle other work—scheduling follow-ups, managing customer relationships, admin—that stays. You just free up time for higher-value work.

What hours does the AI work?

24/7. Calls come in at midnight, 6 a.m., whenever—the agent answers them all and books what it can into your available slots. Urgent situations get flagged for you to see right away.

How long does setup take?

10-14 days from your discovery call to go-live. We handle everything—no technical work on your end. Your phone number doesn't change.

What if a customer realizes they're talking to AI?

It's built to be transparent. If they ask, it tells them they're talking to an AI assistant helping qualify and schedule. Most callers don't care as long as the job gets booked and you show up on time.
