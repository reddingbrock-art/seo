FAQ

AI Chat for Plumbing Companies: Common Questions

Will the AI actually sound like my business?

Yes. We train it on your service menu, pricing, service area, and how you talk to customers. It'll mention your brand, your neighborhoods, and your guarantees. Customers won't know it's not your receptionist until you tell them.

What happens when the AI can't answer?

It escalates to your team via text or email in real-time, with the full conversation history. You step in if needed, but most questions get resolved without you ever touching the phone.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. If you have one, the AI handles the after-hours overflow and routine stuff (appointments, service area confirmation, emergency triage). Your receptionist focuses on customer relationships and complex scheduling. If you don't have one, this handles all of it.

What hours does the AI chat work?

24/7/365. It answers calls and chats whenever someone reaches out—nights, weekends, holidays. You set office hours in the chat so customers know when you're available for emergency dispatch.
