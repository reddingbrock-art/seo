FAQ

Automation for Junk Removal: Common Questions About Setup and Results

What exactly gets automated?

Lead capture from all sources (phone, web form, text), initial qualification, appointment booking, calendar sync, crew dispatch, and review requests after completion. Your team stops manually managing the intake-to-dispatch handoff.

Do I need technical skills to run this?

No. You don't touch the backend. Your team interacts with the system the same way they do now—they just see cleaner data and faster updates. We train them in one session.

What if my team resists new tools?

Resistance usually drops when they see less manual work, not more. Your dispatcher stops writing things down twice. Your office staff stops chasing down missing details. The tool makes their day easier, which matters more than whether they 'like' it.

How is this different from buying software myself?

You can buy software. You still have to configure it, connect it to your other systems, train your team, and troubleshoot when something breaks. We do all that and live with the setup until it works. You go live and move forward.

What does the setup process look like?

Call with us, we map your workflow, you approve the design, we build it in your environment, we test it, your team gets trained, we flip the switch. Typically 10–14 days from call to live.

What happens if something breaks after launch?

It's our system, so it's our responsibility. We monitor it, fix it, and make sure your crew keeps moving. You're not on hold waiting for vendor support or watching a knowledge base article.
